## Sample Notes PDFs (Phase 2 deliverable)

Four branded sample PDFs (one per exam) demonstrate the full notes structure —
official-syllabus alignment, concept notes in Class 11–12-friendly points, LaTeX-quality
formulas, HTML-rendered diagrams and mindmaps, analogy boxes, formula cheatsheet,
exceptions & traps, 10 exam-pattern MCQs with worked solutions (inline answers).
Built with Typst + `@preview/machiatto:0.2.0` + the OurMocks brand kit (v2.1).

**Content rules:** `../contentInstructions.md` is the mandatory rulebook for every
topic written from now on (structure, tone, cheatsheet/exceptions sections, claims
policy, pre-publication checklist). Progress tracking: `../../worklog.md` checklist.

| Sample PDF | Exam · Subject · Topic | Pages |
|------------|------------------------|-------|
| `../notes-samples/OurMocks_CUET UG_Physics_Ray Optics.pdf` | CUET UG · Physics · Ray Optics & Optical Instruments | 16 |
| `../notes-samples/OurMocks_IISER IAT_Mathematics_Definite Integration.pdf` | IISER IAT · Mathematics · Definite Integration | 13 |
| `../notes-samples/OurMocks_NISER NEST_Biology_Cell Biology.pdf` | NISER NEST · Biology · Cell — The Unit of Life | 12 |
| `../notes-samples/OurMocks_COMEDK UGET_Chemistry_Chemical Bonding.pdf` | COMEDK UGET · Chemistry · Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure | 18 |

Reusable pipeline (for producing the remaining topics):
- `../contentInstructions.md` — read FIRST; the content rulebook (v2.1)
- `../typst-sources/<exam_topic>/main.typ` — the notes document (content pattern to copy)
- `../typst-sources/<exam_topic>/brand.typ` — OurMocks brand kit v2.1 (full-page cover,
  watermark, formula/analogy/exception/cheatsheet boxes, MCQ/solution blocks)
- `../diagrams/*.html` — HTML/SVG diagram + mindmap sources; render via `scripts/render_diagrams.py`
  or `scripts/render_mindmap.py` (Playwright, 2x scale) and embed the PNG with `#image("assets/<name>.png", width: 100%)`

Compile command: `typst compile main.typ "OurMocks_<Exam>_<Subject>_<Topic>.pdf"`
(brand assets must sit in `assets/` next to `main.typ`).
