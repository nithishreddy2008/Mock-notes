# IISER IAT — Exam Overview (IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) 2027 Cycle)

**Authority:** Joint Admissions Committee, IISERs (organizing IISER: IISER Berhampur for 2026)  
**Official site:** https://iiseradmission.in/

## Exam Pattern (from official document)

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Mode** | Computer-Based Test (CBT) at centres across India |
| **Total questions** | 60 — 15 each from Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics |
| **Duration** | 180 minutes (3 hours) |
| **Question type** | Multiple-choice, only one correct answer |
| **Marking** | +4 correct, −1 incorrect, 0 unanswered |
| **Maximum marks** | 240 |
| **Sections** | All four subjects are compulsory (PCB + PCM students have no choice since 2024) |
| **Admissions via IAT** | All 7 IISERs (BS-MS), IISER Bhopal B.Tech., IIFPT Thanjavur, IISCerB/other DARE institutes accept IAT (verify per brochure) |

## Syllabus Basis

IAT syllabus = NCERT Classes 11 and 12 (all four science subjects compulsory). The official IAT 2026 brochure publishes a chapter-wise syllabus with detailed sub-topics per subject (sections A. Biology, B. Chemistry, C. Mathematics, D. Physics). Saved verbatim from the official brochure; the chapter-wise syllabus has been stable since 2024 — verify the IAT 2027 brochure when released.
