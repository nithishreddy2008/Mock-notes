Industrial Synthesis from CumeneAcetone Byproduct
Cumene + O₂ → Cumene Hydroperoxide →[dil. H₂SO₄] Phenol + CH₃COCH₃ (Acetone)
Most economical industrial route. Isopropylbenzene (cumene) undergoes auto-oxidation to hydroperoxide, which rearranges in acid to yield equal molar quantities of phenol and marketable acetone.

Williamson Ether Synthesis & Acidic Cleavage

C-O-C Bond Construction
Williamson Synthesis Mechanism (S_N2)1° Halide Strict Rule
R-O⁻ Na⁺ + R'-X → R-O-R' + NaX   (e.g. C₂H₅O⁻Na⁺ + CH₃I → C₂H₅-O-CH₃)
Alkoxide acts as nucleophile attacking primary alkyl halide. CRITICAL LIMITATION: If a 3° alkyl halide is used, alkoxide acts as a strong base, causing exclusive E2 elimination to an alkene (e.g. CH₃O⁻Na⁺ + (CH₃)₃C-Br → 2-methylpropene (100%)).
Cleavage of Ethers by Hydrogen Halides (HI)S_N2 vs S_N1 Cleavage
R-O-R' + HI →[Cold/Equimolar] Smaller Alkyl Iodide + Larger Alcohol
(CH₃)₃C-O-CH₃ + HI →[3° Substrate] (CH₃)₃C-I (S_N1) + CH₃OH
With primary/secondary alkyls, I⁻ attacks the less sterically hindered carbon via S_N2. If one group is tertiary, cleavage proceeds via S_N1 through stable 3° carbocation → 3° alkyl iodide + alcohol!
Cleavage of Alkyl Aryl Ethers (Anisole)Ar-O Inviolable Bond
C₆H₅-O-CH₃ + HI → C₆H₅-OH (Phenol) + CH₃I (Methyl Iodide)
Oxygen lone pair delocalizes with benzene ring (+R effect), giving Ar-O partial double bond character. Attack by I⁻ occurs exclusively at methyl sp³ carbon → yields Phenol + CH₃I (never iodobenzene).

● Phenol vs Alcohol Acidity

Phenol (pKₐ ≈ 10) is 1 million times more acidic than alcohols (pKₐ = 16–18).
Resonance Stabilisation: The conjugate base phenoxide ion (C₆H₅O⁻) delocalises negative charge across ortho and para ring carbons over 5 resonance structures. Alkoxide (R-O⁻) has localized charge and is destabilized by +I alkyl groups.

▲ Substituent Effects on Acidity

Electron-Withdrawing (-NO₂): At ortho/para positions, -NO₂ pulls negative charge by -I and -R effects, drastically elevating acidity.
Picric acid (2,4,6-trinitrophenol, pKₐ = 0.71) is more acidic than acetic acid and decomposes aqueous NaHCO₃!
Electron-Donating (-CH₃, -OCH₃): Cresols are weaker acids than phenol.

■ Diagnostic Laboratory Tests

Neutral FeCl₃ Test: Phenol reacts with neutral ferric chloride solution to give a brilliant violet/purple complex [Fe(OC₆H₅)₆]³⁻. Alcohols show no reaction.
Bromine Water Test: Phenol instantly decolourizes bromine water, forming a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol.
Libermann's Nitroso Reaction: Phenol + NaNO₂ + conc. H₂SO₄ → red →[NaOH] deep blue.
