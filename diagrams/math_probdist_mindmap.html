PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTIONS
CUET UG MATHEMATICS

Discrete Distributions

Table: every value of X with its probability
Police: pᵢ in [0, 1], column sums to exactly 1
Story to table: two sixes-in-2-throws → 0, 1, 2 with 25/36, 10/36, 1/36

Expectation & Variance

E(X) = Σ xᵢpᵢ — the long-run average per trial
Var(X) = Σ xᵢ²pᵢ − (E(X))² — subtract the squared mean
SD = √Var — same units as X
Game sense: fair price = expected payout

Bernoulli & Binomial

Bernoulli trial: two outcomes, constant p, independent
P(X = r) = C(n, r) pʳ qⁿ⁻ʳ, q = 1 − p
Mean np · Variance npq — never λ
At least one: 1 − qⁿ — the complement shortcut

Poisson

Rare events per window: calls, accidents, typos
P(X = r) = e^(−λ) λʳ / r!
Mean = Variance = λ — the identifying signature
Bridge: λ = np when n is huge and p tiny

Normal Distribution

Continuous bell — two dials: μ (centre), σ (spread)
68 / 95 / 99.7 rule at 1σ, 2σ, 3σ
P(X = a) = 0 — only intervals carry area
Symmetric: half each side of μ

Standard Normal Variate

z = (x − μ)/σ — convert, then read the table
Example: x = 70, μ = 60, σ = 10 → z = 1
Tails: P(Z > 1) ≈ 0.1587 from the standard table
Sign discipline: below-mean x gives negative z

TOP TRAPS

λ vs np — Poisson mean is λ; binomial mean is np
Missing e^(−λ) — the classic Poisson formula slip
