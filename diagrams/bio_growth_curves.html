TWO WAYS A POPULATION GROWS
EXPONENTIAL J-CURVE · LOGISTIC S-CURVE

Exponential growth J-CURVE

Food and space assumed unlimited — nothing holds the line back.
No limits — crashes follow. Every pair of parents adds more breeders, so the curve doubles faster and faster. Real habitats run out of food or space, and the overshoot ends in a population crash.

Logistic growth S-CURVE

Resources are finite — K, the carrying capacity, sets the ceiling.
Growth slows near K, stops at K. As N climbs, free resources (K − N)/K shrink, so the same r delivers less and less growth — the curve levels off exactly at the ceiling.
ONE CURVE, TWO LIFESTYLES r-strategists: many offspring, no care — ride the J-curve K-strategists: few offspring, heavy care — live near K
