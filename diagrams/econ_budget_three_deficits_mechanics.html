THE THREE GOVERNMENT DEFICITS: REVENUE, FISCAL & PRIMARY

FIG 2: FORMULAS, INTERRELATIONSHIPS & MACROECONOMIC IMPACT
1. Revenue Deficit (RD) CONSUMPTION SHORTFALL
Mathematical Formula
RD = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts
Economic Diagnosis & Meaning
• Measures government dissaving (routine revenue does not cover routine daily operating costs).
• Government borrows or disinvests assets simply to finance consumption (salaries, subsidies).
Primary Warning Signal
A high RD forces the government to cut capital expenditure (schools, highways), starving the economy of long-term productive assets.
2. Fiscal Deficit (FD) BORROWING REQUIREMENT
Mathematical Formula
FD = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excl. Borrowings)
The Fundamental Equivalence
• FD = Total Gross Borrowings of Government.
• Shows total financial liabilities the state must incur from market, RBI, and external sources.
Macroeconomic Consequences
Debt Trap: Borrowing to pay interest.
Crowding Out: Mopping up private credit.
Inflation: If monetized by RBI money creation.
3. Primary Deficit (PD) CURRENT YEAR STANCE
Mathematical Formula
PD = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments
Economic Diagnosis & Meaning
• Isolates the borrowing requirement generated solely by current year's fiscal actions.
• Strips away the historical legacy debt burden of past interest liabilities.
When Primary Deficit = ZERO
FD = Interest Payments: The government borrows only to pay interest on past debt. Current revenue completely funds all non-interest expenditure!
DEFICIT HIERARCHY & FINANCING How Deficits Are Financed & Targeted under the FRBM Act

The Deficit Ladder Rule

In standard budgets: Fiscal Deficit ≥ Primary Deficit ≥ 0. Fiscal deficit can equal primary deficit only if interest payments on past debt are zero.

Two Deficit Financing Routes

1. Market Borrowings: Issuing bonds/T-bills to public & banks (causes crowding out).
2. Deficit Financing: Borrowing from RBI / printing currency (inflationary risk).

FRBM Act 2003 Targets

Enacted to instill fiscal discipline. Recommended targets (NK Singh Committee): 3% Fiscal Deficit to GDP and 60% General Government Debt to GDP (40% Centre, 20% States).
