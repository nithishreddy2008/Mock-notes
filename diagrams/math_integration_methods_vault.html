3. Integration by Parts & ILATE Strategy
Product Strategy
The By Parts Formula: ∫ u · v dx = u ∫ v dx − ∫ [u' · (∫ v dx)] dx
Rule: First function × Integral of second − Integral of [Derivative of first × Integral of second].
ILATE Priority Hierarchy for Choosing u (First Function):
I: Inverse Trig (sin⁻¹x) > L: Logarithmic (ln x) > A: Algebraic (xⁿ) > T: Trigonometric (sin x) > E: Exponential (eˣ).
Classic Exponential Master Shortcut:
∫ eˣ [f(x) + f'(x)] dx = eˣ f(x) + C
Example: ∫ eˣ [tan x + sec² x] dx = eˣ tan x + C; ∫ eˣ [(x−1)/x²] dx = eˣ(1/x) + C.
Tabular Method: Use when u is algebraic (e.g. x³) and v is easily integrated repeatedly. Speed Hack
4. Rational Functions & Partial Fractions
Decomposition
Degree Pre-Check (Proper vs Improper):
If deg(Numerator) ≥ deg(Denominator), perform polynomial long division first: P(x)/Q(x) = T(x) + R(x)/Q(x). Never apply partial fractions to improper fractions!
Non-Repeated Linear Factors:
px + q / [(x − a)(x − b)] = A / (x − a) + B / (x − b).
Cover-Up Trick: A = (p·a + q)/(a − b); evaluate directly by covering (x − a).
Repeated Linear & Irreducible Quadratic Factors:
1 / [(x − a)²(x² + bx + c)] = A/(x − a) + B/(x − a)² + (Cx + D)/(x² + bx + c).
