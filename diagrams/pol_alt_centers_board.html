Evolutionary Milestones of the European Union

Post-WWII Reconstruction: 1948 Marshall Plan provided US aid; OEEC established for economic cooperation.
Treaty of Rome (1957): Six founding nations created the European Economic Community (EEC) and Euratom.
Maastricht Treaty (1992): Formally established the European Union, single European citizenship, and justice union.
Euro & Schengen: Single currency (Euro) launched in 2002; passport-free Schengen travel zone established.
Limitations: Britain exited (Brexit 2020); EU Constitution draft failed in 2005 (voted down by France & Netherlands).

The Chinese Economic Miracle & Reforms

Maoist Insulation: Autarkic command economy; high social indicators but chronically low per-capita growth.
Four Modernisations (1973): Zhou Enlai proposed agriculture, industry, national defense, and science & technology.
Deng's Pragmatism (1978): "It doesn't matter if a cat is black or white, so long as it catches mice." Opened to foreign capital.
Step-by-Step Transition: Privatized agriculture in 1982, opened Special Economic Zones (SEZs), privatized industry in 1998.
Trade Dominance: Accession to WTO in December 2001; world's leading exporter; projected to surpass US GDP by 2040.

THE THREE PILLARS OF THE ASEAN COMMUNITY (ESTABLISHED 2003)

ASEAN Security Community (ASC) Committed to resolving territorial and sovereignty disputes through peaceful negotiations without resorting to armed confrontation. Supported by the ASEAN Regional Forum (ARF) founded in 1994 for Asia-Pacific security dialogue.
ASEAN Economic Community (AEC) Created a single market and production base across Southeast Asia. Encourages free movement of goods, services, investment, and skilled labor. Established Free Trade Areas (FTAs) with India, China, Japan, and Australia.
ASEAN Socio-Cultural Community (ASCC) Fosters people-to-people exchanges, environmental protection, disaster management, human resource training, and preservation of shared Southeast Asian cultural identity.

PITFALL 1: ASEAN Way vs Western Supranationalism

The 'ASEAN Way' is informal, non-confrontational, and consensus-driven. Unlike the European Union, ASEAN has NO common parliament, NO single currency, and NO binding supranational court.

PITFALL 2: Gradual Chinese Transition vs Shock Therapy

China did NOT adopt IMF Shock Therapy! It modernized through a gradual, controlled sequence: agriculture was privatized first in 1982, followed by industrial enterprises in 1998, while preserving the state apparatus.

PITFALL 3: Eurozone Opt-Outs

Not every EU member state uses the Euro. Denmark and Sweden famously resisted the Maastricht Treaty's monetary integration and chose to retain their national currencies through opt-out clauses.
