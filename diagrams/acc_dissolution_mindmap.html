Master Mindmap

1. Legal Framework & Modes

Firm vs Partnership: Dissolution of firm ends all business; dissolution of partnership only changes relations.
By Agreement (Sec 40): Unanimous consent or existing deed terms.
Compulsory (Sec 41): All/all-but-one insolvent; or unlawful business.
Contingencies (Sec 42): Term expiry, venture done, death, insolvency.
By Notice (Sec 43) & Court (Sec 44): Notice in partnership at will; court orders for insanity, misconduct, perpetual loss.
SECTIONS 39–44

2. Settlement Hierarchy (Sec 48 & 49)

Section 48(a) Losses: Paid 1st out of profits, 2nd out of capital, 3rd by partners individually in profit-sharing ratio.
Priority 1: Outside third-party debts (Creditors, Bank OD, Partner's Wife Loan).
Priority 2: Partner's loan to the firm (pro-rata if assets shortfall).
Priority 3: Repayment of partner capital credit balances.
Priority 4: Surplus split in profit-sharing ratio (never capital ratio!).
SECTIONS 48 & 49
Unit III Baseline

DISSOLUTION OF A PARTNERSHIP FIRM

Complete closure of business, realization of all assets, settlement of all debts, and closure of books via Realisation Account.

Core Exam Golden Rule:
Cash Account balance must automatically match Partner Capital balances to close the ledger!

3. Realisation Account Protocol

Transfer Debit: All tangible/intangible assets at BV (Gross Debtors, Stock, Goodwill). Cash/Bank/Fictitious excluded.
Transfer Credit: Outside liabilities + Provision for Doubtful Debts + IFR. Partner loan & reserves excluded.
Realisation: Asset sale: Bank Dr. to Realisation Cr. Asset taken: Partner Cap Dr. to Realisation Cr.
Discharge: Liability paid: Realisation Dr. to Bank Cr. Taken: Realisation Dr. to Partner Cap Cr.
Result: Net profit/loss to Old Partners in Old PSR.
NOMINAL ACCOUNT

4. Top High-Frequency CUET Traps

TRAP 1 Asset Given to Creditor: NO journal entry passed when asset settles liability!
TRAP 2 Partner's Wife Loan: Is a third-party outside debt—transferred to Realisation A/c!
TRAP 3 Partner's Own Loan: Paid directly via Bank A/c; NEVER enters Realisation A/c!
TRAP 4 Goodwill on Dissolution: Treated like any saleable asset; no separate AS-26 write-off!
TRAP 5 Realisation Expenses: Partner bearing expenses paid by firm debited to Partner Cap!
