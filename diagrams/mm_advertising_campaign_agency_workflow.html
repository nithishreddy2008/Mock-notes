Advertising Agency Operations & Comprehensive Campaign Lifecycle

End-to-end agency workflow: Client servicing, creative brief, media planning, production, and ASCI compliance

CUET Mass Media · Agency Workflow
1
Client Brief & Planning
1. Client Servicing Intake
Account Executive receives marketing objectives, product specs, competitor landscape, and budget parameters from client.
2. Account Planning Research
Qualitative focus groups, demographic & psychographic profiling; identifying core consumer insights.
3. The Creative Brief
One-page strategic contract guiding creative team: Single-minded proposition (SMP), target audience, tone of voice, mandatories.
➔
2
Creative Execution
Copywriting Craft
Crafting headlines (direct, blind, question), persuasive body copy, memorable taglines/slogans, and call-to-actions.
Art Direction & Visualization
Visual hierarchy, typography selection, color psychology, print scamps, and 24-frame storyboard for TVCs.
Internal Creative Review
Creative Director evaluates alignment against Creative Brief; pitches refined concepts to client brand managers.
➔
3
Media Planning & Buying
1. Media Strategy Formulation
Balancing Reach (% of target audience exposed) versus Frequency (average number of exposures per viewer).
2. Quantitative Analytics
Calculating Gross Rating Points (GRP), Cost Per Thousand (CPM), Cost Per Rating Point (CPRP), and target Dayparts.
3. Media Buying & Negotiation
Contracting prime-time television slots, print full pages, OOH billboard sites, and programmatic digital ad auctions.
➔
4
Production & Governance
1. Commercial Production
Filming live TV commercials (TVC), recording radio jingles, digital banners, and final print proofing.
2. ASCI Ethical Audit
Verifying adherence to Advertising Standards Council of India Code: Truthfulness, non-misleading, no surrogate alcohol/tobacco.
3. Post-Campaign Evaluation
Pre-testing and post-testing recall, brand lift studies, digital Click-Through Rates (CTR), and sales conversion ROI.
Key Exam Invariant: The Creative Brief bridges account management and creative; Media Planning balances Reach vs Frequency; ASCI enforces self-regulation.
