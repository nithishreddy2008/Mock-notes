GOVERNMENT BUDGET ARCHITECTURE: RECEIPTS & EXPENDITURE CLASSIFICATION

FIG 1: COMPLETE CLASSIFICATION & THE TWIN DIAGNOSTIC TESTS
1. REVENUE BUDGET (Daily Running Accounts)
REVENUE NATURE
A. Revenue Receipts Liability: NO | Asset: NO
Receipts that neither create a liability nor cause any reduction in government assets. Regular, non-redeemable earnings.
Tax Revenue: Direct Taxes (Income Tax, Corporate Tax) + Indirect Taxes (GST, Customs Duties, Excise Duties).
Non-Tax Revenue: Interest receipts on loans given, Dividends/Profits of PSUs, RBI dividend, Fees, Fines, Escheats, Grants-in-aid.
B. Revenue Expenditure Asset: NO | Liability: NO
Expenditure that neither creates physical/financial assets nor reduces any liabilities. Routine administrative and operational costs.
General Public Services: Salaries of government employees, pensions, maintenance of law, order, and administration.
Transfers & Subsidies: Food, fertilizer, and petroleum subsidies; old-age allowances; grants to State Governments.
Interest Payments: Servicing interest on accumulated past national debt (pure consumption cost).
2. CAPITAL BUDGET (Assets & Liabilities Accounts)
CAPITAL NATURE
A. Capital Receipts Liability: YES | Asset: REDUCED
Receipts that create a liability OR lead to a reduction in the assets of the government.
Debt Capital Receipts (Create Liability): Borrowings from public (bonds, G-Secs), borrowings from RBI, external loans from World Bank/IMF.
Non-Debt Capital Receipts (Reduce Assets): Recovery of loans advanced to State Governments, Disinvestment proceeds from sale of PSU equity, sale of government land.
B. Capital Expenditure Asset: CREATED | Liability: REDUCED
Expenditure that either creates physical/financial assets OR causes a reduction in liabilities. Long-term capital accumulation.
Creation of Public Capital Assets: Construction of highways, railways, bridges, airports, dams, educational institutions, hospitals.
Acquisition of Assets: Purchase of machinery, defense hardware (fighter jets, naval vessels), purchase of shares in companies.
Discharge of Liabilities: Repayment of borrowed loan principal (cancels sovereign liability); Loans disbursed to States.
The Twin Tests of Classification
For any budget item, ask: (1) Does it alter government liabilities? (2) Does it alter government assets? If YES $\implies$ Capital. If NO to both $\implies$ Revenue.
Fatal Exam Trap: Interest vs Principal
Interest payment is REVENUE expenditure (services past debt; creates no asset). Repayment of loan principal is CAPITAL expenditure (reduces liability).
Recovery vs Disinvestment
Both Recovery of Loans and Disinvestment (PSU share sale) are Non-Debt Capital Receipts because they reduce government financial assets.
