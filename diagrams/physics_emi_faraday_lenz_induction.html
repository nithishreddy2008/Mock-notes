Electromagnetic Induction: Faraday, Lenz & Inductive Coupling

NCERT Class XII Physics · Unit 4 (Chapter 6) · Flux Dynamics, Motional EMF & Inductance Theorems

CUET UG Physics · Unit 4
1. Faraday & Lenz's Law
Flux Opposition
Faraday's Law: e = - N · (dΦ_B / dt)
Magnetic Flux: Φ_B = B · A = B A cos θ
Induced Current: i = e / R = - (N / R) · (dΦ_B / dt)
  • Negative Sign (Lenz's Law): The polarity of induced EMF is always such that it opposes the change in flux that produces it.
  • Energy Conservation: External work done moving a magnet against induced repulsion accounts directly for Joule heating in the loop.
  • Charge Flow Independence: Total charge flown ΔQ = ΔΦ / R is strictly independent of the rate of flux change.
2. Motional EMF & Inductance
Dynamic Induction
Motional EMF: e = B v L | Power: P = (B² L² v²) / R
Self-Inductance: e = - L · (dI / dt) | L = μ₀ n² A l
Energy in Inductor: U = ½ L I² | Mutual: M = k √(L₁ L₂)
  • Motional EMF: Generated by Lorentz magnetic force on conduction electrons ($q v B$); polarity given by Fleming's Right-Hand Rule.
  • Self-Inductance (Electrical Inertia): Inductor opposes any rise or decay of circuit current; stored magnetic energy $U = 1/2 L I^2$.
  • Mutual Inductance: Coupling between concentric solenoids $M = mu_0 n_1 n_2 pi r_1^2 l$; coefficient $k = M / sqrt(L_1 L_2) <= 1$.
3. AC Generator Mechanics
Rotational Induction
Flux: Φ(t) = N B A cos(ωt)
Instantaneous EMF: e = - dΦ/dt = N B A ω · sin(ωt)
Peak EMF: e₀ = N B A ω = 2π f · N B A
  • Continuous Alternation: When loop normal is perpendicular to field ($theta = 90 degree$), flux is zero but rate of change $d Phi / d t$ and induced EMF are *maximum*.
  • Slip Rings Function: Maintain continuous electrical contact without twisting lead wires, preserving alternating polarity.
  • Frequency: In India, commercial AC is generated at $f = 50" Hz"$ ($omega = 100 pi approx 314" rad/s"$).
