Unit 1: Electrostatics

Forces, Fields, Potentials & Capacitance

1. Charges & Coulomb Force

Point Charges
  • Postulates of Charge: Quantization (q = ±ne, e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C), conservation of total charge, relativistic invariance.
  • Coulomb's Law: F = [1 / (4πε₀)] · [q₁ q₂ / r²] r̂. Central, conservative, obeys Newton's 3rd Law.
  • Dielectric Permittivity: Force in medium F_m = F₀ / K, where K = ε_r ≥ 1 (K = ∞ for ideal conductors).
  • Principle of Superposition: Net force on test charge is vector sum: F_net = Σ F_i. Two-body forces remain invariant.
Constants: ε₀ = 8.854 × 10⁻¹² C²·N⁻¹·m⁻² | 1/(4πε₀) = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²·C⁻²

2. Electric Fields & Dipoles

Field Topologies
  • Field Definition: Force per unit positive test charge: E = lim(q₀→0) [F/q₀] = [kq / r²] r̂ [N/C or V/m].
  • Field Lines: Continuous curves from +q to -q; never intersect; tangents give direction; dense lines denote strong E.
  • Electric Dipole Moment: p = q · 2a (directed strictly from -q to +q).
  • Dipole Fields: Axial: E_ax = 2kp / r³ (along p); Equatorial: E_eq = kp / r³ (antiparallel to p); E_ax = 2·E_eq.
Dipole Scaling: E ∝ 1/r³ (short dipole) vs E ∝ 1/r² (point charge monopole).

3. Dipole Dynamics in External Fields

Torque & Energy
  • Uniform Field Dynamics: Net translational force F_net = 0; pure torque τ = p × E = p E sin θ.
  • Non-Uniform Field: Experiences both net translational force F ≠ 0 and torque τ ≠ 0.
  • Electrostatic Potential Energy: U = -p · E = -p E cos θ.
  • Equilibrium States: θ = 0° (Stable, U_min = -pE); θ = 180° (Unstable, U_max = +pE).
Work of Rotation: W(θ₁ → θ₂) = -ΔU = p E (cos θ₁ - cos θ₂).

4. Gauss's Law & Symmetries

Flux Theorem
  • Gauss's Theorem: Φ_E = ∮ E · dA = q_enclosed / ε₀. Independent of Gaussian surface geometry or charge position.
  • Infinite Straight Wire: Coaxial cylinder ⟹ E = λ / (2 π ε₀ r) [E ∝ 1/r, radial].
  • Infinite Plane Sheet: Pillbox surface ⟹ E = σ / (2 ε₀) [Uniform, distance-independent].
  • Thin Spherical Shell: Inside (r < R): E = 0; Surface: E = σ / ε₀; Outside (r ≥ R): E = kq / r².
Boundary Jump: Normal component jumps by ΔE = σ / ε₀ across charged sheet.

5. Potential & Equipotential Planes

Scalar Potential
  • Conservative Line Integral: ∮ E · dr = 0 ⟹ V_B - V_A = -∫_A^B E · dr [1 V = 1 J/C].
  • Potential Formulas: Point charge: V = kq/r; Dipole: V = [kp cos θ / r²] (Equatorial V = 0).
  • Potential Gradient: E = -dV/dr. Field points along maximum potential descent.
  • Equipotential Surfaces: Surfaces of uniform V (ΔV = 0 ⟹ W = 0); E lines are always orthogonal (E ⊥ surface).
Conductor Rule: Volume is equipotential; E_inside = 0, E_surface = (σ/ε₀) n̂.

6. Capacitors & Dielectrics

Storage Networks
  • Parallel Plate: C₀ = ε₀ A / d; with dielectric slab (t): C = ε₀ A / [d - t(1 - 1/K)].
  • Polarization Mechanics: P = χ_e ε₀ E; bound surface charge σ_b reduces internal field: E = E₀ / K.
  • Capacitor Combinations: Series: 1/C_s = Σ (1/C_i); Parallel: C_p = Σ C_i.
  • Stored Energy & Density: U = ½ C V² = Q² / (2C); Energy density u = ½ ε₀ E² [J/m³].
Charge Sharing: V_c = (C₁V₁ + C₂V₂) / (C₁ + C₂) | Loss: ΔU = C₁C₂(V₁-V₂)² / [2(C₁+C₂)]
