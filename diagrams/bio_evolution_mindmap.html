Origin of Life (Miller)

Miller–Urey 1953: CH₄ + NH₃ + H₂ + H₂O vapour + electric spark (~800°C) → amino acids formed
Earth ~4.5 bya, no O₂ early; life appeared ~4 bya; first cells ~3.8 bya
Oparin–Haldane: chemical evolution — life rose step-by-step from non-living molecules
RNA was the first genetic material (RNA world)

Darwin & Natural Selection

Beagle voyage 1831–36, Galápagos finch beaks noticed
Selection needs: tiny heritable variations + struggle for existence + survival & reproduction of the fittest + branching descent
Wallace co-proposed selection (1858); Origin of Species 1859
Industrial melanism: dark Biston betularia moths won after soot darkened trunks

Evidences — 4 Kinds

Palaeontology: fossils; deeper rock = older; Archaeopteryx = missing link
Embryology: vertebrate embryos share gill pouches & tail early
Comparative anatomy: homologous (divergent) vs analogous (convergent)
Industrial melanism: selection happening live, in ecology

Adaptive Radiation

Idea: one ancestor → many forms, each fitted to a new niche
Darwin's finches: seed-eater → insect-eater, vegetation-eater, woodpecker-like beaks
Australian marsupials mirror placental look-alikes (mouse, mole, wolf) — parallel radiation

Hardy–Weinberg + 5 Factors

p² + 2pq + q² = 1 and p + q = 1; p² = AA · 2pq = Aa · q² = aa
Equilibrium: large population + random mating + no disturbing forces → frequencies constant
5 disturbing factors: gene migration, genetic drift, mutation, recombination, natural selection
Evolution = change in allele frequency between generations

Human Evolution Ladder

Dryopithecus & Ramapithecus ~15 mya — ape-like, first hominid hints
Australopithecus "Lucy" ~3–4 mya, upright walker → Homo habilis ~2 mya (tools) → H. erectus ~1.5 mya (meat)
Neanderthal 100,000–40,000 ya, buried dead → Homo sapiens ~75,000 ya; farming ~10,000 ya
A bush of relatives, not a tidy ladder

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Homology ≠ analogy. Test the structure, not the job: same plan = homologous
Convergent = analogous; divergent = homologous — students flip the pair
2pq = heterozygotes. Dominant phenotype = p² + 2pq — a classic numeric trap
