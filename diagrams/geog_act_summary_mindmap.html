Human Activities: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary & Quaternary — Comprehensive Mindmap

Six structural pillars of production, resource conversion, services, knowledge economy, and global labor dynamics

Class 12 Geography · Unit III Mindmap

Human Activities Hierarchy

Fundamentals · Unit III
1. Primary & Pastoralism Extractive
  • Hunting & Gathering: Oldest economic activities; high latitudes (N. Canada, Eurasia) and low latitudes (Amazon, Congo).
  • Nomadic Herding: Subsistence transhumance; Gujjars, Bakarwals, Gaddis, Bhotiyas in Himalayas; Kirghiz in Central Asia.
  • Commercial Ranching: Western organized, capital-intensive, permanent ranches (USA, Australia, New Zealand, Argentina).
  • Red-Collar Workers: Outdoor labor directly extracting raw nature.
2. Global Agriculture Cultivation
  • Primitive Subsistence: Shifting/slash-and-burn (Jhuming, Milpa, Ladang); low productivity.
  • Intensive Subsistence: Wet paddy dominated (high yield/hectare, low yield/worker) vs non-paddy (wheat, pulses).
  • Plantation Agriculture: European cash estates; Tea (India/Sri Lanka), Rubber (Malaysia), Coffee (Brazil Fazendas).
  • Extensive Commercial Grain: Continental grasslands (Prairies, Steppes); high mechanization, low per hectare, high per worker.
3. Mining & Geology Mineral Base
  • Surface / Open-Cast: Cheapest method; overhead costs low; extracted when ore lies close to surface.
  • Underground / Shaft Mining: Deep vertical shafts, elevators, ventilation; capital intensive, hazardous (fire, poisonous gas, flooding).
  • Economic Factors: Grade and size of deposit, global commodity prices, transport proximity, and technological readiness.
  • Katanga-Zambia Belt: Prominent copper mining zone in Central Africa.
4. Secondary & Manufacturing Value Addition
  • Size Classification: Cottage/household (local raw materials), small-scale (local labor, workshops), large-scale (assembly lines).
  • Raw Material Base: Agro-based (sugar, textiles), mineral-based (iron & steel), chemical-based (plastics, fertilizer), forest-based.
  • Basic vs Consumer Goods: Basic industries (steel feeds other factories); consumer goods (direct retail consumption).
  • Ruhr Coalfield (Germany): Transitioning from heavy smokestack "rust bowl" to advanced clean-tech manufacturing.
5. Tertiary & Commerce Services
  • Trade & Commerce: Rural periodic markets (weekly mandis), urban malls, wholesale commerce, retail chains.
  • Transport & Communication: Network of nodes (settlements) and links (routes); cyber-space and fiber-optics.
  • Services: Low-order everyday services vs high-order specialized legal, financial, and educational services.
  • Tourism & Medical Tourism: High employment multiplier; India, Thailand, and Singapore leading in world medical travel.
6. Quaternary & Quinary Knowledge & R&D
  • Quaternary: Knowledge-based, software coding, data research, financial analysis (white-collar specialists).
  • Quinary Activities: Highest executive decision makers, policy architects, legal minds ("Gold-Collar" workers).
  • Outsourcing / Offshoring: BPO (call centers) and KPO (high-skill research); India and Philippines leading global hubs.
  • Digital Divide: Wide gap in ICT infrastructure access between developed and developing nations and rural-urban areas.
Core Structural Synthesis: Human civilization advances as societies master nature (Primary), engineer goods (Secondary), coordinate exchange (Tertiary), and innovate knowledge (Quaternary/Quinary).
