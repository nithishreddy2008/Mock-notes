Quantitative Core Syllabus
1. Visual Modalities
Chart Topology
Pie Charts & Angles
Total circle = 360° = 100%. Convert angle to percentage: % = (Angle / 360°) × 100. 1% = 3.6°.
Value = (Angle / 360°) × Total Corpus
Stacked & Grouped Bars
Segment length reflects independent component; bar height reflects aggregated cumulative sum.
Component = Top Boundary - Bottom Boundary
Multi-Line Trend Graphs
Slopes indicate instantaneous growth velocity; intersection points represent parity between two series.
Growth Rate = (ΔY / ΔX)
Tabular Matrices
Precise row/column intersections. Locate unit scales (₹ Lakhs vs Millions) before executing arithmetic.
Watch Units: Millions = 10 Lakhs
2. Arithmetic Levers
Speed Calculations
Percentage Change Base
Percentage increase or decrease is always calibrated against the INITIAL/ORIGINAL base quantity.
% Change = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100%
Successive Changes Formula
Net percentage impact of two sequential variations a% and b% on a single base.
Net % = a + b + (ab / 100)%
Weighted Average Lever
Combined average of subgroups with distinct counts n₁, n₂ and means A₁, A₂.
A_avg = (n₁A₁ + n₂A₂) / (n₁ + n₂)
Alligation Cross Rule
Determines ratio of ingredients mixed at price c (cheap) and d (dear) to yield mean price m.
Ratio (Q_c / Q_d) = (d - m) / (m - c)
3. Statistical Measures
Descriptive Analytics
Arithmetic Mean
Strict sum of all observations divided by count. Highly sensitive to extreme outliers and asymmetric tails.
Mean (x̄) = (Σ x_i) / N
Median (Positional Average)
Central observation of SORTED array. Robust against severe outliers; indicates 50th percentile.
Sorted Middle: Pos = (N + 1) / 2
Mode & Empirical Link
Most frequently occurring score. Connects with Mean and Median in moderately skewed distributions.
Empirical: Mode ≈ 3×Median - 2×Mean
Range & Dispersion
Measure of data spread. Two datasets with identical means can display completely different dispersions.
Range = Maximum Value - Minimum Value

DI Solving Algorithm

4-Phase Protocol for Sub-Minute Data Mastery

PHASE 01
Frame Triage (10s)
Check graph title, axis labels, and metric units (e.g. ₹ Crores vs Lakhs) before looking at bars.
PHASE 02
Stem Targeting (15s)
Isolate the specific years and categories demanded. Pull only the exact two coordinates required.
PHASE 03
Fraction Arithmetic (20s)
Use fraction anchors (1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 ≈ 16.67%) instead of lengthy decimal division.
PHASE 04
Sanity Eye-Check (5s)
Verify that the calculated mathematical trend visually matches the physical proportions of the chart.
