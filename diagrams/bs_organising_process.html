THE ORGANISING PROCESS — four steps from tasks to a working structure
1

Identification & division of work

Split the total work into specific jobs according to pre-determined plans — each job gets a clear, manageable scope.
Example: a school divides work into teaching, accounts, sports and office jobs.
2

Departmentalisation

Group similar jobs into departments or divisions — by function, product or region — so related activities sit together.
Example: production, marketing, finance and personnel departments.
3

Assignment of duties

Each department head is allotted the work matching their skills and qualification — the right person gets the right job.
Example: a chartered accountant heads finance, a sales veteran heads marketing.
4

Establishing reporting relationships

Fix who reports to whom — authority and responsibility links — so everyone knows their boss and their duties.
Example: salespeople report to the sales manager, who reports to the director.
Exam anchor: the four steps come in a fixed order — divide work first, group into departments second, assign duties third, fix reporting links last. Questions shuffle them and ask for the correct order or the step a described action belongs to.
