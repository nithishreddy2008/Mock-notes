Money — Meaning & Functions

Money = anything accepted as medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value, standard of deferred payment
Barter's problems solved: double coincidence of wants, lack of common measure, indivisibility, hard future contracts
Fiat money = government order, no intrinsic value (notes); fiduciary issue = notes backed by trust, not gold

Money Supply (M1–M4)

M1 = C + DD + OD — currency with public + demand deposits + other RBI deposits; the narrowest, most liquid
M2 = M1 + savings with post office · M3 = M1 + time deposits with banks (the widely quoted aggregate)
M4 = M3 + post office total deposits
Liquidity falls, M1 → M4; note: currency with BANKS is never money supply

Credit Creation

Banks lend what they don't reserve; loans return as deposits — money MULTIPLIES
Money multiplier = 1/CRR (CRR 10% → ₹10,000 becomes ₹1,00,000)
Lower CRR → steeper creation; the whole banking SYSTEM multiplies, not one bank

Central Bank (RBI) Functions

Bank of issue: sole note printer (except one-rupee notes/coins)
Banker to government + bankers' bank + lender of last resort
Custodian of forex + controller of credit — the economy's monetary captain

Policy Tools (control panel)

Quantitative: CRR, SLR, repo & reverse repo, open market operations, bank rate
Selective: margin requirements, credit rationing, moral suasion
Tighten (inflation): raise CRR/SLR/repo, sell securities · Loosen (recession): the reverse

Commercial Bank Functions

Accepting deposits: demand (current, savings) vs time (fixed, recurring)
Advancing loans: cash credit, overdraft, loans, discounting bills
Credit creation is the profit engine — lend the deposit, earn the spread

Traps that catch most students

Currency held by banks is NOT money supply — only currency with the PUBLIC counts (M1's C)
Repo lends, reverse repo borrows — flip them and every tightening/loosening MCQ collapses
Multiplier 1/CRR needs 100% re-deposit — leakages (cash held back) shrink the real multiple
M3 ≠ most liquid — M1 is; time deposits in M3 lock money away
