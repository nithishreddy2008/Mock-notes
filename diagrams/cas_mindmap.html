COMPUTERISED ACCOUNTING SYSTEM
UNIT V · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Concept & Types of CAS

Definition: accounting done on a computer — vouchers in, ledgers and reports out, no manual posting
One database: an entry saved once updates every ledger and report it touches
Ready-made: off-the-shelf, cheap, quick · Tailor-made: built for one firm, costly
Customised: ready-made core + firm changes · Integrated: all modules share one database (vs standalone)

Features & Advantages

Four headline features: speed · accuracy · reliability · versatility
Scalability: more customers or branches add no manual workload
Instant reporting: trial balance, P&L, Balance Sheet — any date range, on demand
Built-in checks: an unbalanced voucher is refused, so ledgers always agree

Structure of a CAS

Chart of accounts: the numbered master list of every account head the firm uses
Codification: sequential 1, 2, 3 · block 1000–1999 assets · mnemonic FUR = furniture · combination FUR-01
Hierarchy: Assets → Current Assets → Sundry Debtors → Sharma & Co.
Only the lowest level (ledgers) carries transactions; groups hold the totals below them

Limitations of a CAS

Cost: hardware, software, upgrades and trained staff all cost money
Training & dependence: the system is only as good as its operator
Failure risk: power cuts, viruses, disk crashes — backups are a must
Data security: unauthorised access and tampering of electronic data is a real threat

AIS & Spreadsheets

AIS components: people · procedures · data · software · hardware · internal controls
Good information is: relevant, reliable, comparable, understandable, timely
Spreadsheet kit: cells, formulas (=), functions SUM · AVERAGE · IF · SLN · DB · PMT
Applications: reports, pivot tables, depreciation & loan schedules, payroll, charts

Graphs & Charts

Column: compare values (branch-wise sales) · Bar: rankings (top debtors)
Line: trend over time (monthly sales, Jan–Dec)
Pie: share of a whole (expense split) — keep to about 6 slices
Charts are live: edit one cell and the picture redraws

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Computerised ≠ error-free. Wrong entry in → wrong reports out — the software cannot judge
WDV never reaches zero: 40% a year still leaves a balance; only SLM lands exactly on salvage
EMI split: interest slice is largest first and shrinks — the principal slice grows
