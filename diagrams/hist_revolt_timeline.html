10 May 1857
The Spark in Meerut Cantonment
• 3rd Native Cavalry mutinies after 85 sepoys sentenced for refusing greased cartridges. • Broke open jail, seized the bell of arms (shastragaar), killed officers, and cut telegraph wires. • Marched through the night to Delhi along the Yamuna.
Initiating Action
Breaching Cantonment Lines
11–12 May 1857
Proclamation of Bahadur Shah
• Sepoys surrounded the Red Fort during Ramadan; entered the palace. • Persuaded the aged Bahadur Shah Zafar to accept leadership of the insurrection. • Bestowed sovereign legitimacy across regional rebellion commands.
Legitimizing Symbol
Shahenshah-i-Hindustan
June–Aug 1857
Popular Uprising in Awadh & Central India
• Mutinies spread to Kanpur, Lucknow, Jhansi, Bareilly, and Jagdishpur. • Joined by dispossessed taluqdars and peasants; civil rebellion overtook military mutiny. • Sieges laid to Lucknow Residency and Wheeler's entrenchment in Kanpur.
Rebellion Character
Broad Civil & Agrarian Insurrection
Sept 1857 – 1858
British Reconquest & Retribution
• British troops under John Nicholson stormed Delhi in Sept 1857; Zafar captured at Humayun's tomb. • Sir Colin Campbell relieved Lucknow; Hugh Rose defeated Lakshmibai at Gwalior. • Savage retaliation: blowing rebels from cannons; public hangings.
Military Operations
Systematic Imperial Pacification
1858–1859
Colonial Visual Propaganda
• Thomas Jones Barker painted "Relief of Lucknow" (1859) celebrating Campbell, Outram, Havelock. • Joseph Noel Paton painted "In Memoriam" (1859) depicting British women and children awaiting death. • Aroused British public demand for merciless imperial vengeance.
British Art Ideology
Saviors, Martyrs & Retribution
Post-1857
Nationalist Memory & Heroic Legacy
• Re-evaluated by V.D. Savarkar (1909) as the "First War of Indian Independence". • Rani Lakshmibai immortalized as a masculine national warrior fighting for *swaraj*. • Popularized in folklore and Subhadra Kumari Chauhan's epic verse.
Nationalist Symbol
Anti-Imperial Heroism & Unity
British Colonial Representation
Framed as a treacherous, ungrateful military mutiny against a benevolent civilizing power. Paintings highlighted the chivalry of British commanders, the vulnerability of British women, and the justice of ruthless retribution.
Indian Nationalist Representation
Celebrated as the foundational moment of pan-Indian anti-colonial resistance. Emphasized Hindu-Muslim solidarity, the defense of indigenous faith (*dharma* / *deen*), and the legendary heroism of leaders like the Rani of Jhansi.
