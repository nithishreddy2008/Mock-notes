Unit V: Cash Flow Statement (AS-3 Revised) Master Synthesis Mindmap

Three-activity classification, non-cash adjustments, working capital mechanics, and cash equivalents

AS-3 (REVISED) MINDMAP
1. Operating Activities Core Operations
  • ▸Net Profit before Tax: Closing Surplus − Opening Surplus + Proposed Dividend + Prov for Tax.
  • ▸Non-Cash Additions: Depreciation, Amortisation of Goodwill/Patents, Loss on Sale of Assets.
  • ▸Non-Operating Incomes: Deduct Interest/Dividend Received, Gain on Sale of Fixed Assets.
  • ▸Working Capital Shifts: Add Decrease in CA & Increase in CL; Less Increase in CA & Decrease in CL.
Subtract actual Income Tax Paid (net of tax refund) to find Net Cash from Operations!
2. Investing Activities Capital Deployment
  • ▸Cash Inflows: Proceeds from sale of Land, Building, Plant, Machinery, Intangible assets.
  • ▸Investment Sales: Sale of Non-Current (long-term) Investments.
  • ▸Returns Received: Interest Received and Dividend Received (for non-financial firms).
  • ▸Cash Outflows: Purchase of Fixed Assets and Non-Current Investments.
For financial companies (banks/NBFCs), interest and dividend received are OPERATING!
Cash Flow Statement
Accounting Standard 3 (Revised) Indirect Method Framework for Class 12 & CUET UG
Section A
Operating Cash
Core revenue operations, trading receipts, tax payments, and working capital.
Section B
Investing Cash
Acquisition and disposal of long-term assets and investments.
Section C
Financing Cash
Capital structure alterations: equity, debentures, loans, and dividends.
Reconciliation
Net Change
A + B + C + Opening Cash Equivalents = Closing Cash Equivalents.
Reconciliation Master Check: Net Cash Inflow/Outflow (A + B + C) + Opening Cash = Closing Cash!
3. Financing Activities Capital Structure
  • ▸Issue Proceeds: Cash received from issue of Equity/Preference shares and Debentures.
  • ▸Borrowings: Proceeds from Long-Term / Short-Term Loans, Bank Overdraft & Cash Credit.
  • ▸Redemption / Buyback: Redemption of Debentures/Preference shares, Buyback of shares.
  • ▸Dividends & Interest: Interim & Final Dividend Paid, Interest Paid on all borrowings.
Dividend Paid is ALWAYS Financing for ALL companies (financial & non-financial)!
4. Cash Equivalents & Traps High-Yield Traps
  • ▸Cash Equivalents: Highly liquid short-term investments with maturity ≤ 3 months (Treasury bills, Commercial paper).
  • ▸Bank Overdraft Rule: Bank OD / Cash Credit is a FINANCING activity, NOT a cash equivalent!
  • ▸Non-Cash Transactions: Conversion of debentures to shares or bonus shares are EXCLUDED from CFS!
  • ▸Proposed Dividend: Previous year's dividend paid in current year (Financing outflow + Operating addback).
Current year's Proposed Dividend is an unrecorded contingent liability under AS-4!
