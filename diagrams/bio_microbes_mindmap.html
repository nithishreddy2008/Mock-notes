1. Household Food Processing Dairy & Dough
• LAB (Lactic Acid Bacteria): Lactobacillus converts milk to curd; boosts Vitamin B12; checks gut pathogens.
• Dough Fermentation: Puffed appearance in dosa & idli is due to CO2 production by fermenting bacteria.
• Baker's Yeast: Saccharomyces cerevisiae ferments bread dough, making it soft and porous.
• Traditional Food & Toddy: Toddy made by fermenting sap from palms; fermented soybean, bamboo shoots, and fish.
• Swiss vs Roquefort Cheese: Swiss cheese has large holes via CO2 by Propionibacterium sharmanii; Roquefort ripened by fungus Penicillium roqueforti.
2. Fermented Beverages & Chemicals Industrial
• Brewer's Yeast: S. cerevisiae ferments malted cereals & fruit juices into ethanol.
• Distillation Rule: Wine and beer are non-distilled (low alcohol); Whisky, brandy, rum are distilled (high alcohol).
• Organic Acids: Citric acid (Aspergillus niger, fungus); Acetic acid (Acetobacter aceti, bacterium); Butyric acid (Clostridium butylicum); Lactic acid (Lactobacillus).
• Lipases: Added to laundry detergents to digest lipids and remove oily stains.
• Fruit Juice Clarifiers: Commercial bottled juices are cleared by Pectinases & Proteases.
3. Sacred Bioactive Drugs (NCERT Trinity) Medical
• Streptokinase (Clot Buster): Bacterium Streptococcus (genetic engineering); dissolves clots in blood vessels of myocardial infarction patients.
• Cyclosporin A (Immunosuppressant): Soil fungus Trichoderma polysporum; inhibits graft rejection in organ transplant patients.
• Statins (Cholesterol Lowering): Yeast Monascus purpureus; competitively inhibits HMG-CoA reductase for cholesterol synthesis.
• Penicillin (First Antibiotic): Discovered by Alexander Fleming from Penicillium notatum; therapeutic potential proven by Chain & Florey (WWII).
4. Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) Purification
• Primary Treatment (Physical): Sequential filtration (floating debris) + sedimentation (grit/sand); primary sludge settles, primary effluent passes.
• Aeration Tank (Biological): Constant air pumping; aerobic flocs (bacteria + fungal hyphae) consume organics, rapidly driving down BOD.
• BOD Rule: Higher BOD = more polluted water. Secondary treatment continues strictly until BOD is drastically reduced.
• Activated Sludge: Flocs settle in settling tank; a fraction is recycled as inoculum; major part goes to anaerobic digesters.
• River Clean-up: Ganga Action Plan & Yamuna Action Plan by Ministry of Environment.
5. Biogas & Methanogenic Digestion Energy
• Methanogens: Strictly anaerobic bacteria, notably Methanobacterium, growing on cellulosic matter.
• Gas Mixture: Produces combustible Biogas (50–70% CH4 + 30–40% CO2 + trace H2S/H2).
• Cattle Rumen & Gobar: Methanogens abundant in rumen to digest cellulose; cattle dung (gobar) feeds rural biogas digesters.
• IARI & KVIC Plant: 10–15 ft deep concrete tank, floating gas holder cover, slurry inlet, spent slurry outlet (used as fertilizer).
6. Biocontrol & Biofertilisers Sustainable Agri
• Predatory Biocontrol: Ladybird beetle controls aphids; Dragonflies eradicate mosquitoes.
• Bt Spores: Bacillus thuringiensis cry protein dissolves in alkaline caterpillar gut, punching fatal pores.
• Baculoviruses (NPV): Genus Nucleopolyhedrovirus; species-specific, narrow spectrum, zero non-target harm (IPM gold standard).
• Fungal Mycorrhiza: Genus Glomus; absorbs phosphorus from soil; confers drought/pathogen tolerance.
• Biofertilisers: Symbiotic Rhizobium; free-living Azotobacter & Azospirillum; Cyanobacteria (Anabaena, Nostoc) in paddy.
⚠️ High-Yield CUET Pitfalls & Traps to Avoid
1. Statins inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, NOT lipases! Lipases are laundry enzymes.
2. Trichoderma polysporum is a fungus, while Streptococcus is a bacterium.
3. Primary treatment is purely physical (no microbes); Secondary treatment is biological (flocs & BOD reduction).
4. Baculoviruses have zero impact on mammals, birds, or non-target insects.
