Fig 2: The 5-Stage Editorial Lifecycle & Newsroom Workflow

From Tip-Off to Inverted Pyramid Drafting, Copy Desk Gatekeeping, and Multiplatform Release

Workflow Lifecycle · CUET UG 318
1
Scouting & "Nose for News"
Reporter detects a potential story through beat monitoring, whistleblower leaks, press releases, or civic anomalies.
Core Standard: Filter via News Values: Impact, Timeliness, Proximity, Prominence, Conflict, and Novelty.
Fatal Trap: Blindly copying a PR agency handout without assessing genuine public interest.
2
Sourcing & Fact-Verification
Field investigation, witness interviews, RTI document retrieval, and seeking the subject's right of reply.
Sourcing Hierarchy: On-the-record (ideal) → On background → Deep background (off-the-record) needing cross-proof.
Fatal Trap: Single-source reliance or accepting unverified social media viral claims.
3
Inverted Pyramid Drafting
Constructing the report: Headline → Byline & Dateline → 5 Ws & 1 H Lead → Nut Graf → Descending Body Details.
Lead Principle: Answer Who, What, When, Where, Why, How in the opening 25–35 words with strong active verbs.
Fatal Trap: "Burying the lead" by placing the most critical, breaking revelation in the middle of the story.
4
Copy Desk & Sub-Editing
Sub-editor (copy editor) scrutinizes text for factual errors, grammar, stylebook compliance, and legal clearance.
Gatekeeping Duty: Trim from the bottom up without loss of core meaning; craft accurate, catchy headlines.
Fatal Trap: Sensationalizing the headline beyond the substantive facts supported by the reporter's body copy.
5
Publication & Accountability
Multiplatform dissemination across print, web portal, broadcast feed, and official verified social handles.
Ethical Obligation: Immediate, prominent correction or apology if an error is identified; ombudsman review.
Fatal Trap: Silent deletions of erroneous stories without editorial transparency (stealth editing).
