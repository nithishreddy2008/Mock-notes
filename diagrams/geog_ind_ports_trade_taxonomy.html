NCERT Class XII Geography

1. Western Coast Ports

Arabian Sea Hubs
Kandla (Deendayal) Tidal Port · Gujarat

Origin: Developed in Gulf of Kutch post-1947 to compensate for Karachi port loss. Serves broad NW hinterland (Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan).

Cargo: Heavy crude oil imports, fertilizers, foodgrains.

Mumbai Port Natural Harbour

Profile: Largest port of India; natural sheltered deep-water harbour. Gateway to Western/Central India.

Cargo: General cargo, petroleum products, engineering goods.

Jawaharlal Nehru (JNPT) Nhava Sheva

Profile: Built as a satellite port to decongest Mumbai port; now India's largest container port handling over 50% of container cargo.

Marmagao Port Estuarine · Goa

Location: Natural harbour situated at the entrance of Zuari estuary. Remodeled with specialized mechanised berths.

Specialization: Leading exporter of high-grade iron ore to Japan.

New Mangalore Karnataka Coast

Cargo Focus: Caters to export of iron ore concentrates (Kudremukh), coffee, tea, cashew, fertilizers, and petroleum products.

Kochi Port Vembanad Kayal

Layout: Natural harbour located on Willingdon Island. Known as "Queen of the Arabian Sea".

Exports: Spices, tea, coffee, coir; imports crude oil for Kochi refinery.

2. Eastern Coast Ports

Bay of Bengal Hubs
Kolkata Port Riverine · Hooghly

Location: Riverine port 128 km inland on Hooghly river. Siltation managed via Farakka Barrage water head flushing.

Hinterland: Entire Ganga-Brahmaputra plain, plus landlocked Nepal & Bhutan.

Haldia Port Downstream Satellite

Purpose: Constructed downstream at Hooghly-Haldi confluence to relieve heavy cargo congestion from Kolkata.

Cargo: Bulk coal, petroleum, chemicals, heavy machinery.

Paradip Port Deepwater · Odisha

Profile: Deepwater port on Mahanadi delta with artificial lagoon harbour. Handles mineral wealth of Odisha & Jharkhand.

Traffic: Bulk iron ore exports (Japan), thermal coal, and bauxite.

Visakhapatnam Port Landlocked Harbour

Harbour: Deep natural harbour protected by Dolphin's Nose hill & outer harbour built for large carriers.

Specialization: Exports iron ore from Bailadila mines (Chhattisgarh).

Chennai & Kamarajar Artificial Harbours

Chennai: One of oldest artificial harbours (1859); container & auto hub.

Ennore (Kamarajar): India's first corporate port, built north of Chennai to handle coal for Tamil Nadu power plants.

Tuticorin (V.O.C.) Gulf of Mannar

Profile: Artificial deep-sea harbour in southern Tamil Nadu. Handles coal, salt, foodgrains, and coastal trade with Sri Lanka.

3. Trade Composition

Structural Shift
Export Basket Transformation Manufactured Dominance

Shift: Post-independence shift from colonial raw materials to value-added manufactured goods (~70-75% share).

Key Exports: Engineering goods, Gems & jewellery, Petroleum products (Jamnagar/Mangalore refineries), Chemicals & Pharma.
Agri & Mineral Exports Niche Value-Add

Agri Share: Traditional bulk tea/jute reduced; replaced by marine products, Basmati rice, spices, organic vegetables.

Minerals: Iron ore, manganese, bauxite (regulated to ensure domestic steel security).

Import Basket Structure POL & Capital Goods

POL Dominance: Petroleum, Oil & Lubricants account for ~25-30% of total import expenditure (critical energy dependence).

Capital Goods: Industrial machinery, electrical & transport equipment, aircrafts, electronics, metallurgical ores.
Precious Metals & Raw Inputs Re-export Inputs

Gems & Metals: Uncut diamonds, gold, and silver imported for domestic fabrication and re-export in gems/jewellery cluster.

Fertilizers & Chemicals: Rock phosphate, potassic salts, and sulfur for domestic agricultural productivity.

4. Direction & Trade Balance

Global Links
Geographical Diversification Look East & West

Historical: UK and Commonwealth dominated pre-1970s; now replaced by diversified multi-polar trade partnerships.

Top Partners: USA (largest export destination), China (largest import source), UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Iraq, Germany.
Regional Trading Blocs Integration

Asia & ASEAN: India's largest trading regional bloc is Asia & ASEAN, followed by Europe and North America.

West Asia: Vital energy supplier (crude oil, LNG) and remittance source.

Merchandise Trade Balance Deficit Dynamics

Deficit Pattern: Chronic merchandise deficit driven by inelastic POL and electronics import bills.

Service Offset: Surpluses in IT software exports, business services, tourism, and diaspora remittances cushion current account.
Maritime Logistics Leverage 95% Volume

Volume vs Value: ~95% of foreign trade volume and ~68% of value moves through maritime gateways.

Air Cargo: Handles remaining ~30% value (high-value low-bulk: gems, electronics, lifesaving pharma, perishable floriculture).
