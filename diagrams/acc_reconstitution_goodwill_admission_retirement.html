Goodwill Valuation Triad Valuation Methods
Normal Profit: Capital Employed × NRR / 100
Super Profit: Actual Profit - Normal Profit
Capital Employed: Total Assets (excl. Fictitious & Non-trade) - Outside Liab.
  • Abnormal Items Adjustment: Deduct abnormal/non-recurring gains (lottery, gain on sale of asset); add back abnormal losses (fire, theft).
  • Identical Capitalisation: Capitalisation of Average Profit and Capitalisation of Super Profit yield the mathematically identical goodwill figure!
  • AS-26 Mandate: Internally generated goodwill can *never* be recognized in books; only purchased goodwill for which consideration is paid is recorded.
NRR = Normal Rate of Return Super Profit < 0 ➔ Goodwill = Nil
Admission & AS-26 Goodwill Compensating Sacrifice
Sacrificing Ratio: Used exclusively to credit Premium for Goodwill
Current A/c Debit: Preserves new partner's agreed capital intact
Hidden Goodwill: Firm Capital on New Partner Base - Actual Net Worth
  • Current A/c Preference: When incoming partner cannot bring goodwill in cash, debit their *Current A/c* (not Capital A/c) under AS-26.
  • Existing Partner Gain: If an old partner *gains* on admission ($New > Old$), they must also be *debited* for goodwill!
  • Existing Book Goodwill: Goodwill already appearing in Balance Sheet must be immediately written off to Old Partners in Old PSR.
Credit: Strictly Sacrificing Ratio Existing Goodwill ➔ Write Off
Retirement & Death Protocols Exit Valuation
Retirement Goodwill: Gaining Partners (GR) Dr. To Retiring Partner
Section 37 Option: 6% p.a. interest OR share of profit attributable to dues
Interim Profit: Time Basis: Last Profit × Share × (m/12) | Turnover Basis
  • P&L Suspense Rule: Interim profit is debited to P&L Suspense A/c only if PSR between continuing partners does not change!
  • Executor Status: The deceased partner's capital balance is transferred to the *Executor's Account*; deceased partner ceases to be a partner.
  • Section 37 Right: The executor has a statutory right to choose either 6% p.a. interest on unpaid dues or proportional profits earned.
Retirement: Gaining Ratio Executor: 6% p.a. or Profit share
