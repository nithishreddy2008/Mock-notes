How Bt Cotton Kills the Bollworm
Five stops of the cry-protein journey — plus two side notes that finish the story
1

Bt cotton carries cry genes

The plant was given cryIAc (bollworm) and cryIIAb (corn borer) from Bacillus thuringiensis.

2

Plant packs protoxin crystals

Inside the plant the toxin is stored as inactive protoxin crystals — harmless until the right gut arrives.

3

Insect eats — gut dissolves

The bollworm chews the leaf. Its alkaline gut (high pH) dissolves the crystal.

4

Protoxin activated — binds

The freed toxin becomes active and locks onto the midgut lining cells.

5

Gut wall breaks — insect dies

Pores form, the cells swell and burst; the bollworm stops feeding and dies.

SIDE NOTES
SIDE NOTE 1

Safe for humans

Our gut is acidic, not alkaline — the crystal never dissolves — and human gut cells carry no receptor for the toxin. The code never fits our lock.

SIDE NOTE 2

RNAi kills differently

No toxin at all: tobacco given nematode-specific genes makes dsRNA that silences the pest's own mRNA — Meloidogyne incognita cannot survive.
