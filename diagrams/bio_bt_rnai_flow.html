NCERT Class 12 Biology · Unit IX · Pest Resistance in Transgenic Crops (Bt Cotton & Tobacco)

Pathway 1: Bt Cotton & Crystal Protoxin Lysis

Insecticidal Protein
1
Inactive Crystal Protoxin Synthesis
Soil bacterium Bacillus thuringiensis forms protein crystal protoxins during sporulation. The protoxin does not harm bacteria because it remains in an inactive crystalline form.
2
Insect Ingestion of Transgenic Plant Tissue
Pest caterpillar (e.g., cotton bollworm) ingests leaves or bolls expressing the cloned bacterial cry gene. Inactive protoxin enters the insect gut tract.
3
Alkaline Midgut Solubilization & Activation
The alkaline pH of the insect midgut solubilizes the crystals. Midgut proteases cleave protoxin into its biologically active toxic form.
4
Epithelial Receptor Binding & Pore Formation
Activated toxin binds specifically to surface receptors of midgut epithelial cells, oligomerizes, and punches transmembrane pores.
5
Osmotic Lysis, Cell Swelling & Insect Death
Pores cause influx of ions and water, triggering cellular swelling and osmotic lysis of midgut cells. The caterpillar ceases feeding and dies within 48–72 hours.
Crucial High-Yield Cry Gene Distinctions
• cryIAc & cryIIAb: Control cotton bollworms (Lepidopterans).
• cryIAb: Controls corn borer (NTA's #1 favorite trap pair).
• Harmless to humans: Human stomach is acidic (pH ~1.5–2.0) and lacks midgut toxin receptors.

Pathway 2: RNA Interference (RNAi) Gene Silencing

Post-Transcriptional Silencing
1
Agrobacterium Ti Vector Transformation
Nematode-specific genes from Meloidogyne incognita (root-knot nematode) are introduced into host tobacco plants using Agrobacterium tumefaciens Ti plasmid vectors.
2
Transcription of Sense & Anti-Sense RNA
The engineered DNA construct is driven such that host cells produce both sense and anti-sense RNA transcripts simultaneously.
3
dsRNA Formation & Dicer Endonuclease Cleavage
Sense and anti-sense strands anneal to form double-stranded RNA (dsRNA). The host enzyme Dicer cleaves dsRNA into short interfering RNAs (siRNAs, 21–23 bp).
4
RISC Loading & Target mRNA Slicing
Single siRNA guide strand incorporates into RISC (RNA-Induced Silencing Complex). Activated RISC binds and selectively cleaves nematode mRNA.
5
Translation Arrest & Nematode Survival Failure
With target mRNA destroyed, the parasite cannot express vital survival proteins. The nematode fails to colonize roots, making transgenic tobacco fully immune.
Fundamental RNAi Principles
• Takes place in all eukaryotic organisms as a universal cellular defense mechanism.
• Mobile genetic elements (transposons) or RNA viruses also trigger natural RNAi.
• Key concept: Post-transcriptional gene silencing (PTGS) without altering genomic DNA.
