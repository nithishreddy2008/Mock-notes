CUET Statistical Blueprint
1
Box Plot & Tukey's 1.5 IQR Outlier Rule
Interquartile Range: IQR = Q3 - Q1
Lower Outer Fence: Q1 - 1.5 × IQR
Upper Outer Fence: Q3 + 1.5 × IQR
Extreme Outliers: > Q3 + 3.0 × IQR
Five-Number Summary: $[Min, Q_1, Q_2, Q_3, Max]$. Unaffected by extreme outliers because quartiles are non-parametric ordinal ranks.
2
The Normal Distribution (Empirical Rule)
Within μ ± 1σ: 68.27% of Data
Within μ ± 2σ: 95.45% of Data
Within μ ± 3σ: 99.73% of Data
Z-Score Normalization: Z = (X - μ) / σ
3
Skewness Relations & CUET Traps
Positive Skew
Tail extends Right
Mean > Median > Mode
Symmetric
Bell-shaped
Mean = Median = Mode
Negative Skew
Tail extends Left
Mean < Median < Mode
Variance (σ²): Σ(X - μ)² / N
Std Deviation (σ): √Variance
Coeff. of Variation: (σ / μ) × 100%
Trap 1: Standard Deviation Unit Variance is expressed in squared units of the original data ($kg^2$). Standard deviation restores the original measurement unit ($kg$).
Trap 2: Sensitivity to Outliers Mean and Standard Deviation are heavily affected by extreme outliers. Median and IQR are strictly robust.
