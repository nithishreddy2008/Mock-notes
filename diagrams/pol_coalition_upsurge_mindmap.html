DEMOCRATIC UPSURGE & COALITION POLITICS
POLITICS IN INDIA SINCE INDEPENDENCE · CH. 7 (TOPIC 8)

1. The 1990s Transition & 5 Shifts

End of Congress system: 1989 election defeat ushered in 25 years of multi-party coalition rule.
Mandal Commission: V.P. Singh implemented 27% OBC job quota (Aug 1990); transformed caste representation.
New Economic Policy: 1991 LPG reforms under P.V. Narasimha Rao and Dr. Manmohan Singh.
Ayodhya Dispute: Demolition of Babri structure (Dec 1992); BJP emerged as the primary national pole.

2. The Rise of the Janata Dal & NF

Janata Dal (1988): Merged Jan Morcha, Lok Dal, and Congress (S) under V.P. Singh against Bofors corruption.
National Front (1989–90): Formed government supported from the outside by BOTH BJP and Left Front!
Collapse: Fell when Advani was arrested during Rath Yatra; Chandra Shekhar took over with Congress support.
Mandalisation: Solidified OBC assertion; spawned regional leaders (Lalu Yadav, Mulayam Singh, Nitish Kumar).

3. United Front Era (1996–1998)

1996 Fractured verdict: BJP won 161 seats; Vajpayee's 13-day ministry fell for want of majority.
United Front: 13 regional/secular parties formed government backed from outside by Congress and CPI.
Two Prime Ministers: H.D. Deve Gowda (1996–97) followed by I.K. Gujral (1997–98).
Gujral Doctrine: Policy of unilateral non-reciprocal goodwill towards smaller South Asian neighbours.

4. NDA & Vajpayee Era (1998–2004)

National Democratic Alliance: Pre-poll coalition led by BJP; first stable non-Congress multi-party regime.
Vajpayee leadership: 13-month govt in 1998 (fell by 1 vote); returned with decisive majority in 1999 (full 5-year term).
Key Milestones: Pokhran-II nuclear tests (May 1998); 1999 Kargil War victory; Golden Quadrilateral highways.
Pragmatism over ideology: Hardline ideological planks kept aside to preserve coalition consensus with allies.

5. UPA Era & Rights Revolution (2004–14)

United Progressive Alliance: Congress-led coalition under Dr. Manmohan Singh and Sonia Gandhi (UPA Chair).
UPA-I (2004–09): Backed by Left; landmark laws: RTI Act (2005), MGNREGA (2005); Indo-US nuclear deal 2008.
UPA-II (2009–14): Returned with 206 Congress seats; passed Right to Education (2009) and Food Security (2013).
Anti-corruption surge: Anna Hazare movement (2011) and policy paralysis laid ground for political change.

6. NDA III & IV: Dominant Coalition (2014–19)

2014 Landmark: BJP under Narendra Modi won 282 seats — first single-party majority in 30 years!
2019 Expansion: Re-elected with 303 BJP seats (353 NDA); ended fragmented coalition instability.
Three Democratic Upsurges: 1st = Adult franchise; 2nd = Backward caste/OBC surge; 3rd = Vikas & governance delivery.
New Hegemony: Transition from Congress dominance to a BJP-centred national political architecture.
