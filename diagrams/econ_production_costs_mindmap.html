1. Short-Run Production & Variable Proportions
One Factor Variable
  • Production Function: Q = f(L, K̄). Variable factor is Labour ($L$), Fixed factor is Capital ($K̄$).
  • Total Product (TP): Total physical quantity produced. S-shaped curve starting at the origin.
  • Average Product (AP): $A P_L = T P / L$. Slope of ray from origin to the TP curve.
  • Marginal Product (MP): $M P_L = d(T P)/dL = T P_n - T P_{n-1}$. Slope of TP curve at that point.
  • The 3 Stages of Variable Proportions:
    • Stage I: Origin to $A P_{\max}$ (Increasing Returns). $M P > A P$.
    • Stage II: $A P_{\max}$ to $T P_{\max}$ (Diminishing Returns). $M P < A P$, $M P > 0$. Rational zone!
    • Stage III: Beyond $T P_{\max}$ (Negative Returns). $M P < 0$, $T P$ decreases.
Critical Intersection: $M P$ cuts $A P$ from above at the maximum point of $A P$. When $M P = 0$, $T P$ is at its maximum!
2. Long-Run Production & Returns to Scale
All Factors Variable
  • Returns to Scale: Proportionate change in output when ALL inputs are changed simultaneously in the same proportion.
  • Increasing Returns (IRS): Output expands more than proportionately ($% \Delta Q > % \Delta \text{Inputs}$). Arises from specialization and indivisibilities.
  • Constant Returns (CRS): Output expands in exact proportion ($% \Delta Q = % \Delta \text{Inputs}$). Linear homogeneous function.
  • Diminishing Returns (DRS): Output expands less than proportionately ($% \Delta Q < % \Delta \text{Inputs}$). Caused by managerial diseconomies.
  • Cobb-Douglas Production Function: Q = A · K^α · L^β
    • If $\alpha + \beta > 1 \implies$ IRS.
    • If $\alpha + \beta = 1 \implies$ CRS (Degree 1 homogeneous).
    • If $\alpha + \beta < 1 \implies$ DRS.
  • Isoquant & Isocost: Optimal factor choice where $M R T S_{L,K} = w / r$.
CUET Rule: Law of Variable Proportions operates in the short run (factor ratio changes). Returns to Scale operates in the long run (scale changes, ratio constant).
3. Short-Run Cost Structures & Geometry
TFC + TVC = TC
  • Economic Cost: $\text{Economic Cost} = \text{Explicit Cost} + \text{Implicit Cost} + \text{Normal Profit}$.
  • Total Fixed Cost (TFC): Constant at all output levels. Horizontal straight line. Incurred even at zero output!
  • Total Variable Cost (TVC): Starts at origin ($0$). Inverse S-shaped due to Law of Variable Proportions.
  • Total Cost (TC): Parallel to TVC, vertical gap equals constant TFC.
  • Average Fixed Cost (AFC): $A F C = T F C / Q$. Rectangular hyperbola ($A F C \times Q = \text{Constant}$). Asymptotic to both axes.
  • AVC and ATC (AC): Both are U-shaped curves. $A T C = A V C + A F C$. Gap between ATC and AVC narrows as $Q \to \infty$.
Dual Cut Rule: Marginal Cost (MC) cuts both AVC and ATC from below at their exact minimum points. Min AVC occurs before Min ATC.
4. Long-Run Costs & CUET Exam Traps
Envelope Curve & Traps
  • Long-Run Average Cost (LAC): "Envelope Curve" and "Planning Curve". Tangent to every Short-Run Average Cost (SAC) curve.
  • Flatter U-Shape: LAC is flatter U-shaped than SAC due to economies and diseconomies of scale.
  • Minimum LAC Point: At the lowest point of LAC, $L A C = L M C = S A C = S M C$. Optimal plant size!
  • Area Under MC Curve: The definite integral $\int M C\, dQ$ equals Total Variable Cost (TVC), NOT Total Cost!
  • MC Independence: $M C_n = T V C_n - T V C_{n-1}$. A change in fixed costs (TFC) has zero effect on MC!
  • Break-Even vs Shut-Down: Break-even where $P = A T C_{\min}$. Shut-down where $P = A V C_{\min}$.
Trap: LAC is NOT tangent to the minimum points of SAC curves, except at the absolute lowest point of LAC itself!
