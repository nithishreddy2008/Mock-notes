COALITION ARCHITECTURE & THREE DEMOCRATIC UPSURGES — THE TRANSITION MATRIX

FOUR HISTORIC COALITION FORMATIONS (1989–2024)

National Front (1989–90)
Led by V.P. Singh (Janata Dal); backed from outside by BOTH BJP and Left Front. Mandal quota enacted.
United Front (1996–98)
13 regional parties led by Deve Gowda & I.K. Gujral; outside support from Congress & CPI. First CMP.
NDA I & II (1998–2004)
BJP-led alliance under A.B. Vajpayee; first non-Congress coalition to finish a full 5-year term (1999–2004).
UPA I & II (2004–2014)
Congress-led coalition under Dr. Manmohan Singh; rights revolution (RTI, MGNREGA, RTE, Food Security).

Mandal Assertion & Other Backward Classes (OBC)

Mandal Commission: Appointed in 1978 under B.P. Mandal (Second Backward Classes Commission).
Recommendation: 27% reservations in central government jobs and educational institutions for OBCs.
Implementation: Announced on 7 August 1990 by PM V.P. Singh; triggered widespread anti-reservation protests.
Indra Sawhney Case (1992): Supreme Court upheld 27% quota subject to a 50% ceiling and exclusion of "creamy layer".
Political Transformation: Emergence of powerful regional OBC/caste parties (SP, RJD, BSP, JD(U), DMK).

Consensual Core of Coalition Era (1989–2014)

Economic Consensus: Agreement across all coalitions on New Economic Policy (liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation).
Federalization: Regional parties (AIADMK, DMK, TDP, TMC, BJD) became indispensable kingmakers in New Delhi.
Coalition Management: Governance mediated through Common Minimum Programmes (CMP) and coordination committees.
De-ideologisation: Regional and national parties formed pragmatic alliances across traditional ideological divides.
End of Congress System: Replaced by multi-party competitiveness centered on two poles (NDA vs UPA/Third Front).

CHRONOLOGY OF PRIME MINISTERIAL TRANSITIONS & COALITIONS (1989–2024)

1989–1990

V.P. Singh (NF): Janata Dal government supported by BJP + Left. Falls after Advani's Rath Yatra arrest.

1990–1991

Chandra Shekhar: SJP breakaway backed by Congress from outside. Fell after surveillance controversy.

1996–1998

UF Era: Vajpayee (13 days) → Deve Gowda (1996–97) → I.K. Gujral (1997–98). Supported by Congress.

1998–2004

A.B. Vajpayee (NDA): 13 months in 1998 → re-elected 1999–2004. Pokhran-II, Kargil victory, highway boom.

2004–2014

Dr. Manmohan Singh (UPA): UPA-I (Left supported) & UPA-II. Rights-based laws, high GDP growth, anti-graft protests.

2014–2024+

Narendra Modi (NDA III & IV): BJP won single-party majorities (282 in 2014, 303 in 2019); governed within NDA.

YOGENDRA YADAV'S THREE DEMOCRATIC UPSURGES IN POST-INDEPENDENCE INDIA

First Democratic Upsurge (1950s–1970s) Introduction of Universal Adult Franchise under the 1950 Constitution. Mass voter turnout of common citizens, peasants, and rural voters participating in national elections for the first time.
Second Democratic Upsurge (1980s–1990s) Assertion of Other Backward Classes (OBCs), Dalits, and marginalized communities. Triggered by the Mandal Commission and Bahujan assertion. Transformed politics from elite rule to caste representation.
Third Democratic Upsurge (2014 onwards) Mass mobilization of youth, aspirational voters, women, and neo-middle class. Focus shifted to "Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas", good governance delivery, welfare digitisation, and national pride.

PITFALL 1: Outside Support vs Coalition Ministry

In 1989, neither the BJP nor the Left Front joined the V.P. Singh cabinet — they gave outside support. Similarly, Congress gave outside support to Chandra Shekhar (1990) and the United Front (1996–98).

PITFALL 2: First and Second Backward Classes Commissions

Do NOT confuse: Kaka Kalelkar Commission was the First Backward Classes Commission (1953, submitted 1955). B.P. Mandal Commission was the Second (appointed 1978, submitted 1980).

PITFALL 3: One-Party Dominance vs One-Party Majority

In 2014 and 2019, BJP secured an absolute single-party majority (282 and 303 seats). However, it chose to form an NDA coalition cabinet, leading political scientists to label it the "dominant coalition system".
