AIDA Hierarchy (1898)
Origin & Author
E. St. Elmo Lewis. Foundational sales and advertising funnel model tracking consumer cognitive progression.
Sequential Stages
Attention Visual hook / headline.
Interest Engaging body copy / facts.
Desire Emotional / benefit longing.
Action Purchase / call-to-action (CTA).
CUET Examination Invariant
Linear Funnel Cognitive $->$ Affective $->$ Conative response hierarchy.
DA
DAGMAR Model (1961)
Origin & Author
Russell H. Colley. Defining Advertising Goals for Measured Advertising Results. Separates ad communication from sales.
Sequential Stages (ACCA)
Awareness Brand existence recognition.
Comprehension Understanding attributes.
Conviction Psychological preference.
Action Measurable consumer behavior.
CUET Examination Invariant
Measurable Target Ad goals must define specific target audience, benchmark, and time frame.
US
Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
Origin & Author
Rosser Reeves (Ted Bates agency, 1940s/1961 in Reality in Advertising). Hard-sell functional superiority principle.
Three Inviolable Rules
Specific Benefit "Buy X to get Y."
Unique Claim Competitor cannot/does not offer.
Mass Pull Strong enough to move millions.
CUET Examination Invariant
Functional Hook Classic example: M&Ms ("Melts in your mouth, not in your hand").
BP
Brand Positioning (1972/81)
Origin & Author
Al Ries and Jack Trout (Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind). Owning a singular perception in an over-communicated society.
Core Strategic Logic
Mental Ladder First brand on category rung.
Repositioning Redefining competitors.
Perceptual Space Owning a single word (e.g. Volvo = "Safety").
CUET Examination Invariant
Mind Over Product Positioning is not what you do to a product; it is what you do to the prospect's mind.
Core Invariant: AIDA tracks sales stages; DAGMAR measures communication goals (ACCA); Reeves coined USP; Ries & Trout pioneered Positioning.
