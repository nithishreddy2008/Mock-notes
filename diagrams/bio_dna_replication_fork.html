Enzymatic Machinery of DNA Replication in Escherichia coli

6 Master Enzymes

1. DNA Helicase Unwinding

Breaks hydrogen bonds between purines and pyrimidines, unwinding the double helix at the replication fork. Requires ATP.

2. DNA Gyrase (Topoisomerase) Tension

Cuts, swivels, and re-ligates DNA strands upstream of the fork, relieving positive torsional strain generated by helicase.

3. RNA Primase Initiation

Synthesizes short (~10 nucleotide) RNA primers providing free 3'-OH groups required for DNA Polymerase III to initiate chain growth.

4. DNA Polymerase III Main Pol

Primary catalytic enzyme. Polymerizes dNTPs strictly in the 5' → 3' direction. Ultra-fast (~2000 bp/s) with 3'→5' proofreading.

5. DNA Polymerase I Kornberg

Removes RNA primers via 5' → 3' exonuclease activity and fills gap with deoxyribonucleotides via 5' → 3' polymerase activity.

6. DNA Ligase Sealing

Catalyzes formation of phosphodiester bonds between adjacent Okazaki fragments, sealing nicks on lagging strand (requires NAD+/ATP).

Meselson & Stahl Experiment (1958)

CsCl Centrifugation

Grew E. coli in heavy isotope 15NH4Cl, then transferred to normal light 14NH4Cl. DNA analyzed via CsCl density gradient centrifugation:

Generation 0 (0 min)
100% Heavy
15N/15N
Gen 1 (20 min)
100% Hybrid
15N/14N
Gen 2 (40 min)
50% Light
50% Hybrid

NCERT Conclusion: In Gen 3 (60 min), ratio is 75% Light : 25% Hybrid (3:1). After n generations, fraction of hybrid DNA is 2/(2n). Proves semi-conservative replication unequivocally.

Taylor's Experiment on Vicia faba (1958)

Eukaryotes

Herbert Taylor and colleagues utilized radioactive tritiated thymidine (3H-thymidine) in root tip chromosomes of broad bean (Vicia faba). Autoradiography confirmed that chromosomes in eukaryotes also replicate semi-conservatively.

Dual Role of dNTPs: Deoxyribonucleoside triphosphates (dATP, dGTP, dCTP, dTTP) serve as both substrates for DNA synthesis AND provide energy via hydrolysis of two terminal phosphates (like ATP). Polymerization proceeds strictly 5' → 3'!
