Partnership Act 1932 Defaults Statutory Rules
PSR: Equal (1:1)  |  Loan Interest: 6% p.a. (Charge)  |  IOC/IOD: NIL
  • Equal Profit: Capital ratio is ignored; profits/losses split equally when deed is silent.
  • Loan Interest 6%: Compulsory charge on P&L A/c, payable even during net business losses.
  • No Remuneration: No partner is entitled to salary, commission, or bonus without deed clause.
Capital Account Architectures Ledger Dualism
Fixed: Capital + Current  |  Fluctuating: Single Capital A/c
  • Fixed System: Capital A/c stays intact; all operational items pass through Current A/c.
  • Debit Current Balance: Current A/c can show Dr balance; displayed on Asset side of B/S.
  • Default Mode: In absence of specification, Fluctuating Capital is always presumed.
Charges vs Appropriations Profit Duality
Charge ➔ P&L A/c  |  Appropriation ➔ P&L Appropriation A/c
  • Charges: Partner Loan Interest, Partner Rent, Manager Commission (Must be paid).
  • Appropriations: IOC, Partner Salary, General Reserve (Paid only out of profit).
  • Inadequate Profit: Distribute available profit in the ratio of respective appropriations!

IOC & Drawings Mechanics Time-Weighted
Monthly: 6.5 / 6.0 / 5.5  |  Quarterly: 7.5 / 6.0 / 4.5  |  Date silent: 6 mos
  • IOC Base: Calculated strictly on Opening Capital + time-weighted additions.
  • Average Period Rule: $T_{\text{avg}} = (\text{months left after 1st} + \text{after last})/2$.
  • "p.a." Importance: If "p.a." is omitted in rate, calculate flat without time weight!
Guarantee of Minimum Profit Deficiency Sharing
Deficiency = Guaranteed Profit - Actual Share  |  Borne in agreed ratio
  • By Firm: Deficiency borne by remaining partners in their mutual PSR.
  • By Partner: Deficiency recovered exclusively from the specific guarantor partner.
  • Higher Share Rule: If actual profit exceeds guarantee, partner keeps the higher share.
Past Adjustments & Rectification Single Entry
Analytical Table ➔ Net Effect ➔ Gaining Partner (Dr) to Sacrificing (Cr)
  • Analytical Table: Debit partners for excess received; Credit for omitted entitlements.
  • Single Entry: Passed through Capital/Current A/cs; closed final accounts are never reopened.
  • Fixed Distinction: In fixed capital system, adjustment entry must debit/credit Current A/cs!
