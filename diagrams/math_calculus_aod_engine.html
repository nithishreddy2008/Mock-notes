1. Rates of Change, Tangents & Normals
Geometry
Rate of Change of Quantities:
• Rate with respect to time t: dy/dt = (dy/dx) · (dx/dt).
• Spherical Balloon: V = 4/3 π r³ ⇒ dV/dt = 4π r² (dr/dt).
• Sliding Ladder: x² + y² = L² ⇒ x (dx/dt) + y (dy/dt) = 0.
Tangent Line Equation at (x₀, y₀):
Slope: m = [dy/dx]|₍ₓ₀, ᵧ₀₎.
Equation: y - y₀ = m (x - x₀).
• Horizontal Tangent: m = 0 ⇒ dy/dx = 0.
• Vertical Tangent: m → ∞ ⇒ dx/dy = 0.
Normal Line Equation at (x₀, y₀):
Perpendicular to tangent: m_N = -1 / m (for m ≠ 0).
Equation: y - y₀ = -(1/m) (x - x₀).
Two curves intersect orthogonally if their tangent slopes satisfy m₁ · m₂ = -1.
2. Monotonicity Engine
Inc / Dec
Strictly Increasing Function:
For all x ∈ (a, b):
f'(x) > 0
Graph strictly rises from left to right: x₁ < x₂ ⇒ f(x₁) < f(x₂).
Strictly Decreasing Function:
For all x ∈ (a, b):
f'(x) < 0
Graph strictly falls from left to right: x₁ < x₂ ⇒ f(x₁) > f(x₂).
Critical Points:
Points in the domain of f where:
f'(x) = 0  OR  f'(x) is undefined.
These partition the domain into test intervals.
Wavy Curve Method (Sign Scheme):
Factor f'(x) and determine the sign of f'(x) in each sub-interval to classify intervals of increase and decrease.
If f'(x) = 0 at isolated points, the function remains strictly increasing (e.g. y = x³).
3. Maxima & Minima Engine
Optimization
First Derivative Test (Sign Across c):
• + to - : f'(x) changes from positive to negative ⇒ Local Maximum at c.
• - to + : f'(x) changes from negative to positive ⇒ Local Minimum at c.
• No Sign Change: ⇒ Point of Inflection (e.g. x³ at 0).
Second Derivative Test (at f'(c) = 0):
• f''(c) < 0 ⇒ Local Maximum (Concave down ∩).
• f''(c) > 0 ⇒ Local Minimum (Concave up ∪).
• f''(c) = 0 ⇒ Test fails! Revert to 1st derivative test.
Absolute Extrema on Closed [a, b]:
1. Find all critical points in (a, b).
2. Evaluate f(x) at critical points AND at endpoints a and b.
3. Absolute Max = max {f(c), f(a), f(b)}.
Absolute extrema ALWAYS evaluate boundary values f(a) and f(b) in addition to critical points!
