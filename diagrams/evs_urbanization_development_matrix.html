Spatial Land Use Urban Sprawl & Fragmentation Pervious agricultural soil replaced by concrete; per capita land consumption surges. Compact city design, urban growth boundaries (UGB), infill redevelopment. Sprawl accelerates habitat fragmentation and motor vehicle dependency.
Microclimate Urban Heat Island (UHI) Low albedo asphalt/concrete absorbs solar flux; tall street canyons trap longwave IR radiation. High-albedo cool roofs, green vegetative roofs, urban shade tree canopy corridors. UHI temperature delta (ΔT) reaches 2°C to 8°C higher than surrounding rural plains.
Urban Hydrology Flash Floods & Depletion Impervious surface ratio surges; stormwater runoff fraction jumps from ~10% to over 55%. Sponge City design, Sustainable Drainage Systems (SuDS), rooftop rainwater harvesting. Runoff peaks dramatically; baseflow and groundwater recharge sharply collapse.
Transport & Air Street Canyon Smog & Noise Concentrated tailpipe combustion (PM2.5, NOx, CO); high building height-to-width ratio limits dispersion. Transit-Oriented Development (TOD), Bus Rapid Transit (BRTS), non-motorized lanes. TOD concentrates high-density zoning within 400-800m of mass rapid transit hubs.
Built Environment High Embodied Carbon Extensive energy and water footprints in HVAC, cement, glass, and artificial lighting. GRIHA (TERI/MNRE rating), LEED (USGBC), IGBC rating, ECBC building code. GRIHA is India's indigenous green building rating tool evaluating lifecycle energy.
Social Ecology Environmental Injustice Disproportionate siting of municipal landfills, waste incinerators, and polluting zones near slums. Inclusive decentralized civic infrastructure, community tenure, participatory zoning. Environmental racism / injustice identified in Warren County 1982 PCB protests.
