Ethical / Operational Domain Professional Norm & News Value Ethical Malpractice & Violation Legal Boundary & Regulatory Remedy
Paid News & Advertorials Objectivity & Independence: Editorial selection must rely purely on news value (Impact, Timeliness), strictly separated from advertising revenue. Publishing paid political or commercial propaganda disguised as independent news reportage ("Private Treaties"). Press Council of India (PCI) censure; Election Commission of India (ECI) expenditure accounting and disqualification of candidates.
Sensationalism & Yellow Journalism Proportion & Public Interest: Reporting facts accurately with sobriety, avoiding speculative exaggeration or hysteria. Lurid headlines, tabloidization, melodramatic music, trial by media, invading personal bereavement for TRP ratings. News Broadcasting & Digital Standards Authority (NBDSA) code of conduct; judicial contempt of court for interfering with trials.
Fake News & Disinformation Verification & Sourcing: The "Holy Trinity" of reporting: Multi-source cross-checking, fact-checking, and on-record attribution. Fabrication of synthetic stories, sharing unverified social media rumors, deepfakes, and clickbait hoaxes. Information Technology Act 2000 (Section 66D); Indian Penal Code (IPC 505: spreading rumors promoting public mischief).
Sting Operations & Privacy Investigative Scrutiny: Exposing high-level corruption or public danger where conventional documentary evidence is suppressed. Entrapment (inducing a crime that wouldn't otherwise occur), voyeurism, hidden cameras violating personal privacy for sensationalism. Supreme Court "Public Interest Test": Stings are lawful *only* as a measure of last resort for grave public interest, not commercial entrapment.
Defamation (Libel & Slander) Truth & Good Faith: Protecting individual reputation against malicious, unsubstantiated falsehoods. Publishing false allegations that lower a person's moral or professional reputation in the estimation of right-thinking citizens. IPC Section 499 (Criminal Defamation) & Civil Tort; Exceptions: Truth for public good, fair comment on public conduct of officials.
