1. EMF & Internal Resistance

Source Dynamics
EMF vs Terminal Potential (V) Open vs Closed

Electromotive Force (E): Potential difference across terminals when no current is drawn (open circuit, I = 0).

Terminal Voltage (V): Potential difference across terminals when current flows through closed circuit.

Operating Regimes of a Cell Discharge vs Charge

Discharging (supplying current): V = E - I r   (V < E).

Charging (driven by source): V = E + I r   (V > E).

Open Circuit: I = 0 ⟹ V = E.

Short Circuit: R = 0 ⟹ V = 0, I_max = E / r.

Internal Resistance (r) Electrolyte Factors
r = [(E - V) / V] · R = (E / V - 1) · R
Dependence: r increases with plate separation and electrolyte concentration; r decreases with immersed plate area and temperature.

2. Combination of Cells

Series / Parallel
Series Combination (n Cells) Additive EMF
E_eq = n E,   r_eq = n r

Current: I = (n E) / (R + n r).

Optimization: Advantageous when R >> n r (external load dominates internal resistance).

Reversed Cells: If m cells are connected in reverse: E_eq = (n - 2m) E, but total internal resistance remains r_eq = n r!
Parallel Combination (m Cells) Current Capacity
E_eq = E,   r_eq = r / m

Current: I = E / (R + r / m).

Optimization: Advantageous when R << r (internal resistance dominates external load).

Unequal Cells (E₁, r₁ and E₂, r₂):

E_eq = (E₁/r₁ + E₂/r₂) / (1/r₁ + 1/r₂)

3. Kirchhoff's Laws

Network Theorems
Junction Rule (KCL) Charge Conservation
Σ I_in = Σ I_out   ⟹   Σ I = 0

Physical Basis: Total current entering a junction equals total current leaving. Electric charge cannot accumulate at a junction.

Loop Rule (KVL) Energy Conservation
Σ ΔV = 0   ⟹   Σ E = Σ I R

Physical Basis: The algebraic sum of changes in electric potential around any closed loop is zero (conservative field).

Sign Conventions for Loop Traversal Consistent Analysis

Resistors: Moving in direction of current: ΔV = -I R (potential drop); moving opposite to current: ΔV = +I R.

EMF Sources: Moving from negative to positive terminal: ΔV = +E; moving positive to negative: ΔV = -E.

4. Wheatstone Bridge

Null Deflection
Bridge Balance Condition I_g = 0
P / Q = R / S   (or P / R = Q / S)

Zero Galvanometer Current: When potentials at bridge corners B and D equalize (V_B = V_D), zero current flows through galvanometer.

Conjugate Arms: If battery and galvanometer positions are interchanged, the balance condition remains completely unchanged!
Meter Bridge (Slide Wire) Null Method
X = R · [(100 - l) / l]

Operation: Uses 1-meter uniform resistance wire. Balancing length l from zero end yields unknown resistance X.

Max Sensitivity: Null point should be adjusted near the center (l ≈ 50 cm) by choosing R comparable to X.
