Moving Coil Galvanometer, Circuit Meters & Magnetic Materials

NCERT Class XII Physics · Unit 3 (Chapters 4 & 5) · Radial Field Torque, Meter Conversions & Magnetic Classification

CUET UG Physics · Unit 3
1. Moving Coil Galvanometer
Radial Field
Deflecting Torque: τ_d = N I A B (sin 90° = 1)
Restoring Torque: τ_r = C · θ
Equilibrium: θ = (N A B / C) · I ⟹ θ ∝ I
  • Radial Magnetic Field: Concave poles + soft iron core ensure field is always parallel to coil plane, yielding a strictly linear scale.
  • Current Sensitivity: I_s = θ / I = (N A B) / C [rad/A or div/A].
  • Voltage Sensitivity: V_s = θ / V = (N A B) / (C · R_g) [rad/V]. Note: Doubling N doubles R_g, leaving V_s unchanged.
2. Ammeter & Voltmeter Design
Instrument Conversion
Ammeter Shunt: S = (I_g · G) / (I - I_g) | R_A = (G·S)/(G+S) ≈ 0
Voltmeter Series: R_s = (V / I_g) - G | R_V = G + R_s ≈ ∞
Galvanometer Figure of Merit: k = 1 / I_s = I / θ
  • Ammeter Connection: Connected in *series* with circuit branch; ideal ammeter resistance R_A = 0 to avoid altering circuit current.
  • Voltmeter Connection: Connected in *parallel* across branch; ideal voltmeter resistance R_V = ∞ to draw zero current.
  • Range Extension: To increase ammeter range by factor n = I / I_g, choose shunt S = G / (n - 1).
3. Magnetic Materials & Hysteresis
Dia, Para & Ferro
B = μ₀ (H + M) = μ₀ μ_r H | χ = M / H
Curie's Law (Para): χ = C / T
Curie-Weiss Law (Ferro): χ = C / (T - T_c) [T > T_c]
  • Diamagnetism: Paired electrons, negative susceptibility independent of temperature, repelled from stronger to weaker fields.
  • Paramagnetism: Unpaired spins, weakly attracted, obeys Curie's law (χ ∝ 1/T).
  • Electromagnets vs Permanent: Soft iron (narrow loop, high retentivity, low coercivity) for electromagnets; Steel/Alnico (broad loop, high coercivity) for permanent magnets.
