Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 Article 48A & 51A(g); 2022 Amendment National Parks, Sanctuaries, Tiger Reserves; National Board for Wildlife (NBWL); CITES compliance. Strict protection of Schedule I fauna (hunting totally prohibited). 2022 amendment rationalized schedules to 4 (Sch I-II protected, Sch III plants, Sch IV CITES).
Water Act 1974 & Air Act 1981 Article 252 (Water) & Article 253 (Air) Established CPCB & SPCBs; Consent to Establish (CTE) / Consent to Operate (CTO); effluent standards. M.C. Mehta (Ganga Pollution): Mandated sewage treatment & tannery closure. Air Act 1987 amendment statutorily incorporated Acoustic Noise as an air pollutant.
Environment Protection Act, 1986 Article 253 (Enacted post-Bhopal 1984) Umbrella legislation; empowers Central Govt to set nationwide emission standards, CRZ rules, and hazardous waste handling. Absolute Liability Doctrine (Oleum Gas Leak 1987, Shriram Foods). Absolute liability has NO defense (unlike strict liability in Rylands v. Fletcher).
Forest Conservation Act, 1980 & FRA 2006 Article 48A (42nd Amendment 1976) Restricts diversion of forest land for non-forest purposes; CAMPA afforestation funds; FRA recognizes tribal rights. Godavarman Thirumulpad (1996): Expanded definition of forest across all land. FRA 2006 empowers Gram Sabha as the statutory body for individual and community forest rights.
Biological Diversity Act, 2002 Article 253 (CBD Rio 1992 ratification) 3-Tier structure: National Biodiversity Authority (NBA), State Boards (SBB), and local Biodiversity Management Committees (BMC). Access & Benefit Sharing (ABS); People's Biodiversity Registers (PBR). NBA headquarters in Chennai; foreign entities require prior approval from NBA to access bio-resources.
National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 Article 21 (Right to healthy environment) Specialized tribunal for multi-disciplinary environmental civil disputes; 6-month statutory disposal timeframe. Precautionary Principle & Polluter Pays (Vellore Citizens Forum 1996). Headed by retired Supreme Court judge; exercises original and appellate jurisdiction over 7 key environmental acts.
