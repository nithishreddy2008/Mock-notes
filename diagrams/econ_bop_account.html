THE BOP ACCOUNT FORMAT & THE FOREX MARKET THAT PRICES THE RUPEE

Balance of Payments — the account's actual shape

Credits (+) bring foreign currency IN (exports, FDI coming); debits (−) send it OUT — the statement reads like a bank passbook
CURRENT ACCOUNT (flow of goods, services, income)Credit (+)Debit (−)
1. Merchandise trade — goods (VISIBLE items)tea, textiles sold abroadcrude oil, phones bought
2. Services — software, transport, tourism (INVISIBLES)IT exportsstudents' fees abroad
3. Income — interest, profit, dividends on investmentsearnings from US assetsprofit repatriated by MNCs
4. Current transfers — remittances, gifts, donationsNRI remittancesgifts sent abroad
CAPITAL ACCOUNT (flow of ownership of assets)Credit (+)Debit (−)
Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) — factories, controlToyota builds a plant herean Indian firm buys abroad
Portfolio investment — shares, bonds (paper, no control)FII buys Indian equitiesFII pulls money out
Loans and banking capitalExternal commercial borrowingrepayment of loans
Official reserve transactions (RBI) — the ACCOMMODATING itemRBI draws down reservesRBI accumulates reserves
Read it once: the current account is about EARNING this year (things consumed within the period); the capital account is about CHANGING ownership of assets. Autonomous items happen for profit; accommodating items (RBI reserves) exist only to balance the book.

Forex market — where the rupee gets its price

Demand for $ = our payments abroad (imports, investing out) · Supply = our receipts (exports, FDI in)
Read it once: more dollars demanded than supplied → the dollar's price in rupees rises → DEPRECIATION (imports costlier, exports cheaper for the world). Under a fixed regime the RBI absorbs the pressure by selling reserves.

TRAP 1

Balance of trade ≠ balance of payments. BoT = goods (visibles) only, a current-account slice; BoP = the WHOLE account book.

TRAP 2

Depreciation ≠ devaluation. Depreciation is the MARKET letting the rupee slide (flexible regime); devaluation is a DELIBERATE government cut of a fixed peg.

TRAP 3

Autonomous vs accommodating: autonomous items happen for profit regardless of BoP; accommodating items (reserve transactions) exist ONLY to plug the gap.
