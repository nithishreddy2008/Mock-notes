INCOME DETERMINATION & THE TWO GAPS THE GOVERNMENT FIGHTS

Two-sector equilibrium — where AD meets the 45° line

AD = C + I meets the 45° line (Y = AD) at E — planned spending exactly equals output OY*
45° line: AD = Y C (consumption) AD = C + I
Read it once: the 45° line shows every point where spending = income. Wherever AD crosses it, planned spending equals output — firms' inventories are stable, nobody changes production. That Y* is equilibrium income.

Excess demand vs deficient demand — the two gaps

Compare AD with the full-employment output line: a vertical gap above = inflationary gap; below = deflationary gap
45° line AD at full employment AD with excess demand AD with deficient demand
Read it once: beyond full employment, extra demand cannot call out extra output — prices simply climb (inflationary gap). Below it, machines and workers sit idle — output and jobs shrink (deflationary gap). Policy exists to close both gaps.
