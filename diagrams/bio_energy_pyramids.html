Ecological Pyramids — Number, Biomass, and Energy

NCERT Class 12 Biology · Unit X · Ecosystem · Trophic Levels, 10% Energy Transfer Law & Classic Inversions

OurMocks CUET UG

1. Pyramid of Numbers

Upright / Inverted
Tertiary Consumers (TC: 3)
Secondary Consumers (SC: 354,000)
Primary Consumers (PC: 708,000)
Primary Producers (PP: 5,842,000)
What is Counted: The total number of individual organisms present at each successive trophic level per unit area.
Grassland Ecosystem: Strictly Upright. Millions of grass blades (producers) support herbivores, carnivores, and just a few apex predators.
Tree Ecosystem Exception: Can be Inverted or Spindle-Shaped. A single oak tree (1 producer) supports thousands of herbivorous insects, which host hyper-parasites.
CUET Rule: A single tree hosting hundreds of birds is spindle-shaped; if birds carry ticks/lice, it is completely inverted!

2. Pyramid of Biomass

Terrestrial vs Ocean
Fishes / Zooplankton (Biomass: 21 g/m²)
Phytoplankton (Biomass: 4 g/m²)
What is Weighed: Total dry weight (standing crop) of living organic matter at each trophic level ($g/m^2$ or $kg/ha$).
Terrestrial Ecosystems: Generally Upright. Huge vegetative biomass in forests/grasslands exceeds herbivore and carnivore mass.
Aquatic / Ocean Exception: Sharply Inverted! The standing crop biomass of microscopic phytoplankton is tiny compared to long-lived zooplankton and predatory fishes.
Why Inverted in Oceans: Phytoplankton have microscopic size and rapid life cycles (high turnover rate) that sustain massive fish biomass!

3. Pyramid of Energy

Always Upright
Tertiary Consumers: 10 J
Secondary Consumers: 100 J
Primary Consumers: 1,000 J
Producers (Sunlight: 10⁶ J): 10,000 J
What is Measured: The rate of energy flow through each trophic level over time ($J / m^2 / yr$ or $kcal / m^2 / yr$).
Lindeman's 10% Law: Only about 10% of chemical energy stored in biomass at one level is transferred and fixed into the next trophic level.
Universal Rule: The pyramid of energy is ALWAYS UPRIGHT, NEVER INVERTED in any ecosystem, because energy is irreversibly lost as heat (Thermodynamics).
Key Fact: Only 1–5% of incident solar radiation (2–10% of PAR) is captured by producers via photosynthesis!
Limitation 1: Food Web Ignored
Assumes a simple linear food chain, which almost never exists naturally in complex real ecosystems.
Limitation 2: Multi-Trophic Species
Cannot accommodate species that belong to multiple trophic levels simultaneously (e.g., sparrow eating seeds & insects).
Limitation 3: Saprophytes Excluded
Completely ignores saprophytes and decomposers, despite their vital role in biogeochemical cycling!
