Standard antiderivatives (+C always)

∫ dx/x = ln|x| + Cthe n = −1 rescue
∫ eˣ dx = eˣ + C · ∫ aˣ dx = aˣ/ln a + Cexponentials

Definite integrals → area (the 4-step pipeline)

Antiderivative F(x) by any method above — no +C needed for definite work
FTC: ∫ab f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a) — upper minus lower, in order
Root–Split–Flip for "area bounded": split at every x-axis crossing, take absolute values
Between curves: ∫ (upper − lower) dx — verify who is on top at a sample point
King's rule: ∫0a f(x) dx = ∫0a f(a−x) dx — reflecting the interval often collapses the integral in one line.
