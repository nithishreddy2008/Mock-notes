IN SITU v/s EX SITU — TWO WAYS TO SAVE A SPECIES
UNIT X · CONSERVATION METHODS

IN SITU“IN THE ORIGINAL PLACE” — PROTECT IT AT HOME

DEFINITION
The species is protected inside its natural habitat — the whole ecosystem is fenced, guarded and left alive.
MAIN TOOLS (WITH EXAMPLES)
National parks (strict protection) · wildlife sanctuaries (animal-focused) · biosphere reserves (whole ecosystems + research) · sacred groves (Khasi & Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya; Bishnoi villages, Rajasthan).
WHEN IT IS USED
The habitat can still support the species — this is always the first choice, the ideal method.
ONE-LINE ADVANTAGE
Saves the entire ecosystem with all its species at once — and is the cheaper, more natural route.

EX SITU“AWAY FROM THE ORIGINAL PLACE” — PROTECT IT IN HUMAN CARE

DEFINITION
The threatened species (or its seeds, gametes, tissues) is protected outside its natural habitat, under human care.
MAIN TOOLS (WITH EXAMPLES)
Zoological parks with captive breeding · wildlife safari parks · seed and pollen banks · tissue culture for plants · cryopreservation of gametes at −196°C in liquid nitrogen.
WHEN IT IS USED
The habitat is already gone, or the wild population is too small to survive alone — the backup plan.
ONE-LINE ADVANTAGE
Works even when there is no wild home left to return to — it banks life for a future release.
