HUMAN REPRODUCTION
UNIT VI · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

The Male Reproductive System

Testes in the scrotum: kept 2–2.5°C below body temperature — sperm need cooler air
Inside each testis: seminiferous tubules — the sperm factories
Three cell types: germ cells make sperm · Sertoli nurse them · Leydig cells make testosterone
Duct ladder: rete testis → vasa efferentia → epididymis → vas deferens → urethra

The Female Reproductive System

Paired ovaries: make the eggs plus oestrogen & progesterone
Tube parts in order: infundibulum (fimbriae catch the egg) → ampulla (fertilisation site) → isthmus
Uterus walls: perimetrium outer · myometrium muscle · endometrium lining that rebuilds monthly
Mammary glands: alveoli make milk; ducts carry it to the nipple

Gametogenesis — Making Gametes

Spermatogenesis: 1 spermatogonium → 4 sperm; starts at puberty (mitosis first, then meiosis)
Oogenesis: 1 oocyte → 1 ovum + tiny polar bodies; starts before birth
Two arrests: prophase-I before birth · metaphase-II until a sperm enters
Sperm parts: head with acrosome cap · middle piece (mitochondria) · tail

The Menstrual Cycle

28 days, 4 phases: menstrual (1–5) → follicular (5–13) → ovulation (day 14) → luteal (15–28)
Hormone logic: FSH grows follicles → oestrogen rebuilds the lining → LH surge → egg released
Luteal phase: corpus luteum makes progesterone, which keeps the lining ready
No pregnancy? Corpus luteum dies, progesterone falls, lining sheds = next period

Fertilisation & Implantation

Site: ampullary-isthmic junction of the fallopian tube — not the uterus
Sperm entry: acrosome enzymes cross the zona pellucida; cortical reaction blocks a second sperm
Counts: ovum 23 + sperm 23 = zygote 46; meiosis-II finishes only after entry
Cleavage: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16-cell morula → blastocyst → implants at ~6–7 days

Pregnancy, Parturition & Lactation

Placenta: chorionic villi + uterine tissue; does nutrition, respiration and excretion for the foetus
Placental hormones: hCG · hPL · oestrogens · progesterone (relaxin comes from the ovary)
Milestones: heart by month 1 · limbs by month 2 · organ systems by 12 weeks · hair by month 5
Birth: foetal ejection reflex → oxytocin → stronger contractions; colostrum = baby's first IgA

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Meiosis starts at puberty in males — spermatogonia divide by mitosis until then
Oogenesis arrests twice; polar bodies are discarded — they are not eggs
LH surge (day 14) causes ovulation — not FSH; fertilisation happens in the ampulla, not the uterus
Sertoli = nurse cells; Leydig = testosterone — students swap these roles
