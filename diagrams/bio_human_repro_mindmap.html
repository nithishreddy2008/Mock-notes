NCERT CORE BLUEPRINT

1. Male Reproductive System & Sperms

Testes & Spermatogenesis
Testicular Architecture Scrotum maintains 2–2.5°C lower temperature. 250 lobules, each with 1–3 seminiferous tubules containing Sertoli cells (nurse/FSH) & Leydig cells (testosterone/LH).
Duct Pathway Rete testis → Vasa efferentia → Epididymis (storage/maturation) → Vas deferens → Ejaculatory duct → Urethra.
Accessory Glands • Seminal vesicles (paired): Fructose, prostaglandins, clotting enzymes (60–70% semen).
• Prostate: Alkaline fluid, citrate.
• Bulbourethral: Mucus for lubrication.
Sperm Cytology Head (haploid nucleus + Acrosome), Neck (proximal centriole), Middle piece (mitochondrial Nebenkern, ATP), Tail (flagellum). 200–300M sperms per ejaculate.

2. Female Reproductive System & Ovum

Ovaries & Folliculogenesis
Ovarian Ducts & Uterus Fallopian tube: Fimbriae → Infundibulum → Ampulla (fertilization site!) → Isthmus. Uterus: Perimetrium, Myometrium (smooth muscle), Endometrium (sheds).
Oogenesis Timeline Prenatal origin (~2M oogonia); arrested at Prophase I (Diplotene) at birth. Only 60K–80K primary follicles remain at puberty.
Follicular Evolution Primary follicle → Secondary → Tertiary (fluid-filled Antrum) → Mature Graafian follicle. Meiosis I completes just before ovulation → Secondary oocyte + 1st polar body.
Ovulated Secondary Oocyte Arrested at Metaphase II until sperm entry. Enveloped by Zona Pellucida (glycoprotein coat) and outer Corona Radiata.

3. Menstrual Cycle & Fertilization

Endocrine Control & Syngamy
28-Day Cycle Phases • Menstrual (1–5): Bleeding, lowest steroids.
• Follicular (6–13): FSH & Estrogen rise, endometrium repairs.
• Ovulatory (14): LH Surge causes ovulation.
• Luteal (15–28): Corpus luteum secretes Progesterone.
Fertilization Mechanics Sperm capacitation in female tract → Acrosomal enzymes digest corona/zona → Depolarization & Cortical reaction permanently blocks polyspermy.
Completion of Meiosis II Sperm entry triggers secondary oocyte to finish Meiosis II → Haploid Ovum (Ootid) + 2nd Polar Body.
Syngamy & Cleavage Sperm & ovum pronuclei fuse forming Zygote (2n, 46). Rapid mitotic holoblastic cleavage begins in oviduct: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 cells (Morula).

4. Gestation, Placenta & Birth

Blastocyst to Parturition
Blastocyst & Implantation Comprises outer Trophoblast (implants into endometrium ~Day 7) + Inner Cell Mass (ICM) (embryo progenitor; pluripotent stem cells).
Placenta Endocrinology Interlocked chorionic villi + uterine wall. Secretes hCG, hPL, Estrogen, Progesterone, and Relaxin (hCG, hPL, Relaxin exclusive to pregnancy!).
Parturition Mechanism Fetal Ejection Reflex: Mature fetus & placenta induce mild uterine contractions → Maternal posterior pituitary releases Oxytocin → Powerful contractions expel fetus.
Lactation & Colostrum Prolactin drives milk synthesis; Oxytocin drives milk ejection. First milk (Colostrum) is rich in maternal secretory IgA antibodies.
FERTILIZATION SITE: Strictly at the Ampullary-isthmic junction / Ampulla of the fallopian tube CUET High-Frequency
EXCLUSIVE PREGNANCY HORMONES: hCG, hPL, Relaxin are produced in females only during pregnancy Diagnostic Marker
CENTRIOLE LEGACY: Sperm contributes proximal centriole; unfertilized egg lacks centriole for cleavage Cleavage Trigger
