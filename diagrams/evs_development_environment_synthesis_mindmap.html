1. Growth Limits & Steady State

  • Herman Daly: Steady-State Economics (constant physical capital stocks).
  • Club of Rome (1972): Limits to Growth World3 overshoot model.
  • Ecological Footprint: Rees & Wackernagel; Global Hectares (gha).
  • Overshoot Day: Consumption exceeds annual Earth biocapacity.

2. The Commons & Ostrom's Rules

  • Garrett Hardin (1968): Tragedy of open-access pasture.
  • Elinor Ostrom (1990): 8 Design Principles for enduring CPRs.
  • Economic CPR Matrix: High subtractability + high exclusion cost.
  • Polycentric Governance: Layered community self-organization.

3. Urbanization & Green Cities

  • Morphology: Push-pull migration, sprawl, impervious surfaces.
  • Urban Heat Island (UHI): ΔT up to 8°C; low albedo materials.
  • Sponge Cities & SuDS: Permeable paving, retention wetlands.
  • Green Building Standards: GRIHA (TERI/MNRE), LEED, ECBC.

4. Carrying Capacity & Eco-Equity

  • IPAT Equation: Impact = Population × Affluence × Technology.
  • Dual Capacities: Supportive (inputs) vs Assimilative (waste sink).
  • Planetary Boundaries: Rockström & Steffen (9 Earth systems).
  • Environmental Justice: Warren County 1982, Climate refugees.
