PART B: India's 3-Tier Grievance Redressal Architecture (IT Rules 2021)
Level I: Platform Self-Regulation I
Publisher / Intermediary
  • Appoint Resident Grievance Officer (RGO) based in India
  • Acknowledge user complaints within 24 hours
  • Resolve grievances within 15 days
  • Takedown non-consensual sexual content within 24 hours
Level II: Institutional Body II
Self-Regulating Council
  • Independent industry body registered with MIB
  • Headed by retired Supreme Court or High Court judge
  • Hears unresolved appeals from Level I decisions
  • Issues compliance orders, warnings, or censures
Level III: Oversight Committee III
Government of India (MIB / MeitY)
  • Inter-Departmental Committee headed by Joint Secretary
  • Issues directions for blocking content under Sec 69A IT Act
  • Emergency blocking powers during national security crises
  • Final appellate authority for regulatory non-compliance
