Life of a Share Issue
Follow one share from the boardroom decision to full payment — and what happens when money stops coming
1

Company decides the issue

Board and shareholders approve. The authorised capital in the Memorandum caps how much can be raised.

2

Application money invited

Public applies on a form. Application money is at least 5% of the issue price; allotment needs 90% minimum subscription.

3

Applications received

Applied shares vs offered shares decide the fate: over-subscribed (more demand) or under-subscribed (less).

4

Allotment — pro-rata logic

2 applied : 1 allotted means excess application money adjusts the allotment; rejected applicants get a refund.

5

Calls are made

Allotment, then first & final call. Each call is a debt due from the shareholder on demand.

6

Shares fully paid

The full ₹10 face value is collected — the holder is now a complete owner with all shareholder rights.

side path — only if a shareholder defaults
A

Default

After a 14-day notice, the money is still unpaid on allotment or a call.

B

Forfeiture

Shares are cancelled: Share Capital debited with called-up, Forfeited Shares A/c credited with money already received.

C

Re-issue

Sold again — discount cannot exceed the forfeited amount. What is left becomes a Capital Reserve.
