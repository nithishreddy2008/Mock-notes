PRINCIPLES OF INHERITANCE AND VARIATION

Mendel's Three Laws

Law of dominance: in a contrasting pair, one factor rules the F₁ — the other hides, unharmed
Law of segregation: paired factors separate at gamete formation — every gamete carries one
Law of independent assortment: one pair sorts freely of another pair — dihybrid 9:3:3:1
Monohybrid F₂: genotype 1 : 2 : 1 · phenotype 3 : 1 (tall vs dwarf pea)

Deviations from Mendelism

Incomplete dominance: F₁ is a blend — red × white snapdragon → pink; F₂ 1 : 2 : 1
Co-dominance: both alleles show side by side — blood group AB expresses IA and IB
Multiple alleles: one gene, many forms in the population — ABO carries IA, IB, i
Still two per person: any individual carries only 2 alleles — one from each parent

Test Cross & Dihybrid Cross

Test cross: F₁ × recessive parent — a 1 : 1 result proves the F₁ was heterozygous
Back cross: offspring taken back to its own parent or a parent-like plant
Dihybrid cross: RrYy × RrYy — 16-box Punnett grid, 4 gamete types RY Ry rY ry
Dihybrid test cross: F₁ × double recessive — 1 : 1 : 1 : 1, four phenotypes equal

Chromosomes Carry Genes

Sutton & Boveri (1902): chromosomes are the physical carriers of Mendel's factors
Linkage: genes on the same chromosome travel together — tight linkage, fewer recombinants
Recombination: crossing over swaps chromosome pieces and splits linked genes
Morgan's fly work: white eye in Drosophila sits on the X chromosome — X-linked

Polygenic Inheritance

Many genes + environment: each gene adds a small dose — continuous variation, no clean ratios
Examples: human skin colour, human height, wheat kernel colour (Nilsson-Ehle)
F₂ shape: a bell-shaped range of shades instead of a 3 : 1 split

Sex Determination & Disorders

Patterns: humans XX-XY · birds ZW (males ZZ) · grasshopper XO · honeybee haplo-diploid (16 · 32)
Mendelian: haemophilia, sickle-cell (GAG→GUG), thalassemia, PKU, colour blindness
Chromosomal: Down's trisomy 21 (47) · Klinefelter XXY (47) · Turner XO (45)
Pedigree: family tree of a trait — square = male, circle = female, shaded = affected

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Blend ≠ both-show: incomplete dominance mixes; co-dominance displays both alleles
1 : 2 : 1 shows as phenotype only in incomplete dominance — that coincidence is the trap
AB × O parents: children can be A or B only — never AB, never O
Swap-proof the karyotypes: Turner XO = female, 45 · Klinefelter XXY = male, 47
