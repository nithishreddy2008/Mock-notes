INHERITANCE & VARIATION

GENETICS CONCEPT HUB
Transmission of characters from parents to offspring (Heredity) and structural/functional differences exhibited by progeny (Variation).

1. Mendelian Genetics & Principles

CLASSICAL RULES
Gregor Johann Mendel (1856–1863) Conducted hybridization experiments on garden pea (Pisum sativum) for 7 years; statistical analysis of large sample size.
Seven Contrasting Character Pairs Stem height, flower color, flower position, pod shape, pod color, seed shape, seed color.
Monohybrid Cross Ratios Phenotypic: 3 Tall : 1 Dwarf; Genotypic: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt (1:2:1). Test cross: 1:1.
Dihybrid Cross & Independent Assortment Phenotypic: 9:3:3:1; Dihybrid Test Cross: 1:1:1:1. Unlinked genes assort independently in meiosis.

2. Post-Mendelian Gene Interactions

NON-MENDELIAN
Incomplete Dominance (1:2:1) Antirrhinum majus (snapdragon): Red ($R R$) $\times$ White ($r r$) $\to$ Pink ($R r$). Phenotypic and genotypic ratios coincide.
Co-Dominance & Multiple Alleles Human ABO blood groups (gene $I$ with $I^A, I^B, i$); both $I^A$ and $I^B$ co-dominant; 6 genotypes, 4 phenotypes.
Pleiotropy Single gene mutation triggers multiple phenotypic effects (Phenylketonuria, Sickle-cell anemia).
Polygenic Inheritance Continuous quantitative trait governed by 3+ cumulative additive genes (Human skin color $A A B B C C$ to $a a b b c c$).

3. Chromosomal Theory & Linkage

T.H. MORGAN & MAPPING
Sutton-Boveri Chromosome Theory (1902) Meiotic chromosome segregation parallels Mendelian factor segregation.
Morgan's Linkage in Drosophila Yellow-white (Cross A: 1.3% recomb, tight linkage) vs White-miniature (Cross B: 37.2% recomb, loose linkage).
Genetic Mapping (Sturtevant) Recombination frequency directly maps distance between genes (1% = 1 cM).
Sex Determination Systems $XX-XY$ (Human, Drosophila); $XX-XO$ (Grasshopper); $ZZ-ZW$ (Birds, female heterogamety); Haplodiploidy (Honeybee).

4. Human Genetic Pathology

MENDELIAN & ANEUPLOIDIES
Mendelian Disorders (Point Mutations) • Hemophilia (X-linked recessive; clotting defect).
• Sickle-Cell Anemia (Autosomal recessive; $\beta$-globin 6th codon GAG $\to$ GUG).
• Thalassemia (Quantitative globin deficiency; $\alpha$ on chr 16, $\beta$ on chr 11).
Chromosomal Aneuploidies • Down Syndrome: Trisomy 21 ($47, +21$; simian crease, furrowed tongue).
• Klinefelter Syndrome: Trisomy $47, \text{"XXY"}$ (masculine with gynaecomastia).
• Turner Syndrome: Monosomy $45, \text{"XO"}$ (sterile female, webbed neck).
Pedigree Analysis Autosomal dominant (no skips; Myotonic dystrophy) vs Autosomal recessive (skips; Sickle cell).
