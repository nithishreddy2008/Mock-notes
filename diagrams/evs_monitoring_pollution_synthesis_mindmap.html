1. Atmospheric Monitoring

• Particulates: PM2.5, PM10, smog, ESP (>99% removal), baghouse filters.
• Gases: SO2, NOx, CO, O3, photochemical smog (PAN).
• AQI Matrix: 8 pollutants, 6 health categories (Good to Severe).

2. Aquatic Diagnostics

• Organic Metrics: Dissolved Oxygen (DO), BOD5 (20°C), COD (dichromate).
• Pathologies: Cultural eutrophication, hypolimnetic anoxia, algal blooms.
• Toxicology: Biomagnification (DDT), Minamata (Hg), Itai-Itai (Cd).

3. Waste Management Systems

• Sewage (STP): Primary physical, secondary biological (ASP), tertiary UV.
• Biomedical (2016): Yellow (incinerate), Red (autoclave), White, Blue.
• E-Waste (2022): Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) targets.

4. Noise & Biomonitoring

• Noise: Leq, decibel scale, CPCB zones (Industrial to Silence).
• Bioindicators: Lichens (SO2), Tubifex (sludge), E. coli (fecal coliform).
• Radioactive: Vitrification, deep geological repositories.
