India: Urban Settlement Evolution & Functional Typologies

Three Historic Epochs of Urban Growth & The Census 9-Fold Functional Classification Matrix

CUET UG Geography · Unit 6

Part A: Historical Epochs of Urban Evolution in India

Ancient Epoch > 2,000 Years Old

Ancient Religious & Cultural Towns

Developed as prominent religious, pilgrimage, and cultural centers during antiquity. Sustained continuous urban life for millennia.

Key Towns: Varanasi, Prayag (Allahabad), Pataliputra (Patna), Madurai, Ujjain, Ayodhya.
Medieval Epoch 8th – 18th Century CE

Medieval Fort Capitals & Feudal Hubs

Emerged as fortified headquarters of princely kingdoms, sultanates, and Mughal subahs. Built around royal palaces and grand mosques.

Key Towns: Delhi, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Agra, Nagpur, Gwalior, Golconda.
Modern Epoch British Colonial & Post-1947

Colonial Seaports, Hill Sanatoria & Planned Cities

British/European footholds on coasts (Bombay, Calcutta, Madras, Goa, Pondicherry), sanatoria hill stations (Shimla, Darjeeling), and post-1947 planned state capitals & industrial steel towns.

Colonial/Planned: Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai; Planned Capitals: Chandigarh, Gandhinagar, Bhubaneswar; Industrial: Bhilai, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bokaro.

Part B: 9-Fold Functional Classification of Indian Towns (Census of India)

1. Administrative Towns

Governance

Headquarters of national or state governments supporting executive administration.

New Delhi, Chandigarh, Bhopal, Gandhinagar, Dispur, Imphal.

2. Industrial Towns

Manufacturing

Dominated by heavy manufacturing, steel mills, chemicals, and engineering plants.

Jamshedpur, Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela, Modinagar, Salem.

3. Transport Towns

Logistics

Major sea ports, break-of-bulk terminals, and cardinal railway junction hubs.

Kandla, Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Mughalsarai, Itarsi, Katni.

4. Commercial Towns

Trade & Finance

Specialized in wholesale trade, banking, financial exchanges, and agro-mandis.

Mumbai, Kolkata, Saharanpur, Satna, Muzaffarnagar.

5. Mining Towns

Resource Extraction

Developed directly on mineral ore fields, coal basins, and petroleum fields.

Raniganj, Jharia, Bokaro, Digboi, Singrauli, Ankleshwar.

6. Garrison / Cantt Towns

Defense

Established as permanent military bases, cantonments, and defense depots.

Ambala, Jalandhar, Mhow, Babina, Udhampur, Kamptee.

7. Educational Towns

Academia

Towns whose economic identity centers on premier universities and research institutes.

Roorkee, Varanasi, Aligarh, Pilani, Manipal, Vallabh Vidyanagar.

8. Religious / Cultural Towns

Heritage

Celebrated for famous temples, shrines, pilgrimage circuits, and historic culture.

Varanasi, Mathura, Amritsar, Puri, Tirupati, Ajmer, Haridwar.

9. Tourist Towns

Recreation

Pleasant hill stations and beach resorts attracting national & global visitors.

Nainital, Mussoorie, Shimla, Pachmarhi, Mount Abu, Ooty, Kodaikanal.
