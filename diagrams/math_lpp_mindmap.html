Vocabulary (serves A + B1 + B2)

Decision variables: x, y — the things you control
Objective function: Z = ax + by, maximise profit or minimise cost
Constraints + non-negativity: inequalities from the story, x, y ≥ 0
Formulation is itself a scored skill — name, then translate

Half-Planes & the Feasible Region

Each inequality shades a half-plane; test the origin for the side
Feasible region: the common overlap of ALL half-planes
Feasible point inside · infeasible outside
Bounded or unbounded — read the region's shape first

Corner Point Theorem

The optimum of Z lives at a vertex of the feasible region
List every corner — origin, axis hits, line intersections
Evaluate Z at all of them, pick the best
Ties: an iso-profit line along an edge = every edge point optimal

The Five-Step Pipeline

1 Formulate → 2 draw lines → 3 shade agreement → 4 corners → 5 evaluate Z
Simultaneous equations find the winning corner
Verify the winner obeys every constraint
Answer the asked thing: the point or the value of Z

Region Moods

Bounded: max and min both exist at corners
Unbounded: maximum needs proof — slide the iso-profit line
Empty region: constraints contradict → infeasible LPP
Minimisation often lives on unbounded regions

The Classic (know it cold)

Max Z = 3x + 2y; x + 2y ≤ 10, 3x + y ≤ 15, x, y ≥ 0
Corners: (0,0)→0, (5,0)→15, (4,3)→18, (0,5)→10
Max = 18 at (4,3) — the two-line intersection
Same skeleton under every cost/profit story
