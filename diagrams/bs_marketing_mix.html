THE MARKETING MIX — 4 Ps
EVERYTHING THE FIRM TUNES TO SERVE A NEED
P1

Product

WHAT do we offer?
Covers: the good or service plus brand, label, package, design and after-sale support
Classification: consumer goods (convenience, shopping, specialty) and industrial goods
Brand: name + mark that identifies the product; label describes it; package protects and sells it
P2

Price

WHAT does the customer pay?
Covers: list price, discounts, credit terms — the value exchange in money
Factors: cost of the product, demand and utility, competition, government regulation, pricing objectives, marketing method used
Price is the only P that earns revenue — the other three Ps spend it
P3

Place (Physical Distribution)

HOW does it reach the buyer?
Components: transportation, warehousing, inventory control, order processing
Channel types: zero-level (direct), one-level (retailer), two-level (wholesaler + retailer), three-level (agent too)
Choice factors: the product's nature, the company's resources, the market's size and spread, competition
P4

Promotion

HOW do customers learn and buy?
Promotion mix: advertising, personal selling, sales promotion, public relations
Advertising: paid, non-personal, mass — wide reach, low per-head cost; criticised for adding cost and tempting false claims
Personal selling persuades face to face; sales promotion offers short-run incentives; PR builds the image for free
Exam anchor: "Which element of the marketing mix does this action belong to?" — decide first whether the action concerns what is offered (product), what is charged (price), where it travels (place), or how people hear about it (promotion).
