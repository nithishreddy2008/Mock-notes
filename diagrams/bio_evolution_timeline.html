4.5 BYA
Earth forms from the cloud around the young Sun. No oxygen in the air yet.
3.8 BYA
First cells appear. Giant RNA molecules came before them — the RNA world.
2000 MYA
Invertebrates flourish — soft-bodied sea animals without backbones.
500 MYA
Jawless fish evolve — the first backboned animals swim the seas.
350 MYA
Giant ferns cover swampy forests; amphibians step onto land.
230 MYA
Dinosaurs rise — reptiles rule the land for over 160 million years.
CONTINUES ↓
65 MYA
Dinosaurs vanish suddenly. Mammals spread into the empty world.
3–4 MYA
Australopithecus ("Lucy") walks upright in East Africa and hunts.
2 MYA
Homo habilis — the "handy man" — makes the first stone tools.
1.5 MYA
Homo erectus — larger brain, almost certainly ate meat.
100,000–40,000 YA
Neanderthal man used hides for clothing and buried his dead.
~75,000 YA
Homo sapiens spreads out; agriculture begins ~10,000 years ago.
