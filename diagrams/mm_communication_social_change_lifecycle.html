Fig 2: Mass Media as a Catalyst for Social Change & Cultural Transformation

5-Stage Cognitive Process Flow: From Media Exposure to Policy Enactment & Institutional Reform

Process Flow · CUET UG 318
1
Agenda Setting & Exposure
Mass media spotlights marginalized issues, corrupt practices, or health crises, elevating them from private grief to public prominence.
Theoretical Premise: McCombs & Shaw Agenda-Setting Theory; Media gatekeepers establish issue salience and national focus.
Indian Benchmark: Investigative exposés on water scarcity, malnutrition, and environmental degradation in remote districts.
2
Diffusion & Opinion Leaders
Information travels through respected community intermediaries, village elders, civil society leaders, and specialized influencers.
Theoretical Premise: Two-Step Flow Model (Lazarsfeld) & Diffusion of Innovations (Rogers: Innovators to Early Majority).
Development Media: SITE (1975) & Kheda Communication Project using community television for agricultural adoption.
3
Public Sphere Deliberation
Citizens engage in rational-critical debate via prime-time talk shows, community radio, local press, and social media forums.
Theoretical Premise: Jürgen Habermas' concept of the Bourgeois Public Sphere — democratic deliberation outside state control.
Civic Media: Community radio stations like Radio Mewat empowering local communities to debate governance issues.
4
Grassroots Mobilization
Public awareness transforms into collective civic action, street protests, consumer boycotts, and viral digital petitioning.
Theoretical Premise: Networked Social Movements (Castells); Decentralized mobilization via mobile telephony and social feeds.
Historic Precedents: Chipko Movement folk communication; India Against Corruption (2011) anti-graft mobilization.
5
Structural & Legal Reform
Sustained media scrutiny forces democratic institutions and legislatures to enact new statutory frameworks and institutional safeguards.
Theoretical Premise: Media as the Fourth Estate; Democratic accountability and permanent sociological norm shift.
Legislative Impact: Enactment of the Right to Information (RTI) Act 2005 & Lokpal Act following sustained media campaigns.
