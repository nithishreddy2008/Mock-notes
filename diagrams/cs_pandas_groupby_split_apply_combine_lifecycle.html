Reshaping Dualism: df.pivot() vs df.pivot_table()
df.pivot() requires strictly unique index-column pairs (duplicate raises ValueError).
df.pivot_table() aggregates duplicate combinations automatically using aggfunc='mean' (or sum, max, count).
df.pivot_table(values='Sales', index='Region', columns='Product', aggfunc='sum', fill_value=0)
