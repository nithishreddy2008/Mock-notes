P&L Appropriation Account Ledger Schema
Divisible Profit: Net Profit (Cr) + IOD (Cr) - Appropriations (Dr)
Commission After Charging: Profit · R / (100 + R)
Commission Before Charging: Profit · R / 100
  • Nominal Nature: P&L Appropriation is a nominal extension of P&L A/c; debited with expenses to partners, credited with firm earnings from partners.
  • Net Profit Hygiene: If Net Profit includes partner rent or loan interest, deduct them before posting to the Credit side of Appropriation.
  • Loss Inadmissibility: If the firm operates at a Net Loss, P&L Appropriation A/c is not prepared (unless IOD exceeds loss or deed allows IOC during loss).
Cr: Net Profit + IOD Dr: IOC + Salary + Comm + Reserve
Interest on Drawings (IOD) Time-Weighted
Product Method: Total Products × (Rate / 100) × (1 / 12)
Rate "p.a." absent: Calculate WITHOUT time factor!
Flat 6 Months: Applied whenever withdrawal date is omitted
  • The "per annum" Trap: If rate is given as "10%" (without "p.a."), time factor is completely ignored: $\text{IOD} = \text{Total} \times 10/100$.
  • Product Method Applicability: Use product method when drawings are made at irregular intervals or with unequal amounts.
  • Capital vs Profit Drawings: Drawings against capital are debited to Capital A/c (no IOD); drawings against profit attract IOD!
Beginning: +0.5 mo End: -0.5 mo
Guarantees & Past Adjustments Correction Protocol
Guarantee Deficiency: Guaranteed Profit - Actual Share
Single Rectification Entry: Gaining Partner A/c (Dr.) to Sacrificing Partner A/c (Cr.)
  • Higher Share Rule: If guaranteed partner's actual share exceeds guaranteed minimum, they receive the actual higher share!
  • Two-Way Guarantee Order: First adjust partner's guarantee to firm (firm income rises), then distribute profits and meet guarantee to partner.
  • Single Entry Principle: Never reopen closed ledger accounts for past errors; pass one composite entry through Capital (or Current) A/cs.
