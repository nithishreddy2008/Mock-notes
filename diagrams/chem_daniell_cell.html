THE DANIELL CELL — Zn | Zn²⁺ (1 M) || Cu²⁺ (1 M) | Cu
A galvanic cell: the spontaneous reaction Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s) becomes a steady electric current
Why a salt bridge?
Keeps both half-cells electrically neutral and completes the circuit. Without it, charge piles up and the current stops within seconds.
Cell notation, one line
Anode | anode solution || cathode solution | cathode. Writing 1 M means standard conditions — the cell then delivers its standard potential E°.
