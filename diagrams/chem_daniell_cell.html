THE DANIELL CELL — GALVANIC REDOX ARCHITECTURE

Spontaneous chemical energy to electrical current: Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s) | Standard EMF E° = +1.10 V

Unit II: Electrochemistry E°cell = +1.10 V Galvanic vs Electrolytic

ANODE HALF-CELL

Negative Terminal
Electrode & Polarity
Zinc (Zn) Rod — Negative (−) Terminal
Electrolyte Solution
1.0 M ZnSO₄(aq) solution (colourless)
Oxidation Half-Reaction
Zn(s) → Zn²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻
Zinc rod undergoes dissolution; mass decreases over time. Standard reduction potential: E° = −0.76 V.
Electron Motion
Generates e⁻; electrons exit the anode and enter the external circuit towards cathode.
Mnemonic: LOAN
Left, Oxidation, Anode, Negative

CATHODE HALF-CELL

Positive Terminal
Electrode & Polarity
Copper (Cu) Strip — Positive (+) Terminal
Electrolyte Solution
1.0 M CuSO₄(aq) solution (deep blue)
Reduction Half-Reaction
Cu²⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → Cu(s)
Cu²⁺ ions deposit onto copper strip; electrode mass increases. Standard reduction potential: E° = +0.34 V.
Electron Motion
Consumes e⁻; incoming electrons reduce copper ions to neutral copper atoms.
Mnemonic: RED CAT
Reduction occurs at the Cathode
