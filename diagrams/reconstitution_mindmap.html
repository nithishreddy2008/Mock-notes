RECONSTITUTION OF A
PARTNERSHIP FIRM
UNIT II · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Change in PSR

New ratio decides everything: who gains, who sacrifices, who is untouched
Sacrificing ratio = old share − new share (the part given up)
Gaining ratio = new share − old share (the part picked up)
Only the gaining partners pay for goodwill — sacrificers never pay

Revaluation A/c

Why: bring assets & liabilities to true values on the reconstitution date
Credit: rise in assets, fall in liabilities; Debit: fall in assets, rise in liabilities
Profit or loss goes to OLD partners in the OLD ratio — never the incoming partner
A nominal account: opened, filled, closed on the same day

Reserves & Accumulated Profits

General Reserve · P&L balance · Workmen Comp Fund surplus belong to old partners
Shared in the OLD ratio at admission or retirement
Workmen Comp Fund: claim above the fund → extra debited to Revaluation A/c
Investment Fluctuation Fund: fall in value first met from the fund, rest via Revaluation

Goodwill & Valuation

Goodwill = the money value of a firm's reputation — power to earn extra profit
Average profit: goodwill = average profit × number of years' purchase
Super profit: normal = capital × NRR/100; super = avg − normal; goodwill = super × N
Capitalisation: goodwill = capitalised value of profits − net assets

Admission of a Partner

Admission needs consent of all partners — a new agreement begins
Premium for goodwill: his share × firm's goodwill, credited to old partners in the sacrificing ratio
Revaluation profit + reserves go to old partners only
Capitals adjusted to the new agreement; the new PSR begins

Retirement / Death

Claim = capital + goodwill share + revaluation profit + reserves − drawings
Continuing partners take the outgoing share in the gaining ratio (new − old)
On death, the balance moves to the Executor's Account, then to a Loan A/c
Loan instalments earn 6% p.a. interest when the deed is silent

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Revaluation profit never touches the new partner — old partners only, in the old ratio
Gaining ratio ≠ new ratio. It is new − old, partner by partner — never computed in bulk
Accumulated LOSSES also belong to old partners — debited, not credited, in the old ratio
