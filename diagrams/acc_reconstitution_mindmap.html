Ratio Dynamics Sacrifice & Gain
Sacrifice = Old - New  |  Gain = New - Old  |  New = Old - Sac.
  • Admission Default: If new share is $1/k$, old partners sacrifice in their old mutual PSR.
  • Retirement Default: Remaining partners gain in their old ratio unless a new ratio is stated.
  • Negative Sacrifice: If Old - New < 0, the existing partner *gains* and must pay for goodwill!
Revaluation Account Nominal Account
Dr: Assets ↓, Liab ↑, Unrec Liab  |  Cr: Assets ↑, Liab ↓, Unrec Asset
  • Old Ratio Rule: Net Revaluation Profit/Loss is transferred *strictly to Old Partners in Old PSR*.
  • New Partner Exclusion: Incoming partner receives zero share in revaluation balance.
  • Retirement Scope: In retirement/death, *all partners* (including exiting partner) share in old PSR.
Reserves & Specific Funds Balance Sheet Purge
General Reserve ➔ Old PSR  |  WCR Surplus ➔ Old PSR  |  Ad Suspense ➔ Dr.
  • Free Reserves: Transferred to Old Partners' Capital A/cs in Old PSR before reconstitution.
  • WCR Claim Rule: Fulfill actual liability first; only uncommitted excess is credited to partners.
  • Fictitious Assets: Deferred revenue expenditure (Ad Suspense) must be debited to Old Partners.

Goodwill Valuation Methods Valuation Triad
Super Profit = Actual - Normal  |  Normal = Cap. Emp × NRR / 100
  • Abnormal Items: Deduct abnormal gains (gain on sale of asset); add back abnormal losses.
  • Capitalisation Identity: Capitalisation of Average Profit equals Capitalisation of Super Profit!
  • AS-26 Compliance: Internally generated goodwill cannot be recorded; only purchased goodwill.
Admission of a Partner Goodwill Protocol
Premium for Goodwill (Dr) ➔ Sacrificing Partners' Capital (Cr) [In SR!]
  • Sacrificing Ratio Sole Purpose: Premium for goodwill is shared *strictly in Sacrificing Ratio*.
  • Not Brought in Cash: Debit New Partner's *Current Account* (preserves agreed capital).
  • Hidden Goodwill: Total Capital based on incoming partner - Total Adjusted Net Worth.
Retirement & Death Protocols Exit Settlement
Gaining Partners (Dr) ➔ Retiring Partner (Cr) [GR!]  |  Section 37: 6%
  • Retirement Goodwill: Retiring partner's goodwill is funded by Gaining Partners in Gaining Ratio.
  • Section 37 Dues: Retiring partner's unpaid loan attracts 6% p.a. interest or share of profit.
  • Death of Partner: Interim profit debited to P&L Suspense A/c; final dues transferred to Executor.
