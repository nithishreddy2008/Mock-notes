♂ Male Lineage: Microsporogenesis

Stamen → Anther → Pollen

1. Anther Wall Stratigraphy Somatic: 2n

Bilobed, dithecous anther contains 4 microsporangia. Wall consists of 4 distinct concentric layers:

Epidermis Single protective outer layer.
Endothecium α-cellulosic fibrous bands; hygroscopic for dehiscence.
Middle Layers 1–3 parenchymatous layers; crush at maturity.
Tapetum Innermost; dense cytoplasm, multinucleate/polyploid; nourishes spores, callase & sporopollenin precursors.

2. Microsporogenesis & Meiotic Reduction 1 MMC (2n) → 4 Spores (n)

Each cell of central sporogenous tissue acts as a Microspore Mother Cell (MMC / PMC, 2n). Undergoes meiosis to produce a Microspore Tetrad (n). As anther dehydrates, spores dissociate into 4 distinct pollen grains.

3. Mature Pollen Grain (Male Gametophyte) Haploid (n)

Exine (Outer Wall)

Made of Sporopollenin (most resistant organic material; resists high temp, strong acids, alkalis, enzymes). Contains Germ Pores where sporopollenin is absent.

Intine (Inner Wall)

Thin, continuous pectocellulosic layer (cellulose + pectin). Emerges as pollen tube during germination.

Vegetative Cell (Tube Cell)

Larger cell with abundant food reserves and large, irregular nucleus. Directs pollen tube growth.

Generative Cell (Sperm Cell)

Small, spindle-shaped with dense cytoplasm; floats in vegetative cell. Divides mitotically into 2 male gametes.

Shedding Stage: Shed at 2-celled stage in >60% angiosperms; in <40%, generative cell divides first, shedding at 3-celled stage.

♀ Female Lineage: Megasporogenesis

Pistil → Ovule → Embryo Sac

1. Anatropous Ovule Anatomy Maternal Tissue: 2n

Inverted (anatropous) ovule attached to placenta via Funicle. Junction is Hilum. Main body features:

Integuments 1–2 protective envelopes; enclose nucellus.
Micropyle Small pore at apex; site of pollen tube entry.
Chalaza Basal swollen region opposite micropyle.
Nucellus (2n) Central nutritive tissue; 1 cell at micropylar pole differentiates into MMC.

2. Megasporogenesis & Monosporic Selection 1 MMC (2n) → 1 Functional (n)

Single MMC (2n) undergoes meiosis to form a linear tetrad of 4 megaspores (n). 3 micropylar megaspores degenerate; only 1 chalazal megaspore remains functional (Monosporic development).

3. Polygonum-Type Mature Embryo Sac 7-Celled, 8-Nucleate

Functional megaspore nucleus undergoes 3 successive mitotic divisions without cytokinesis (free-nuclear: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 nuclei), followed by cellular reorganization:

Chalazal End (3 Cells)

3 Antipodal Cells (n). Vegetative nutritive cells; degenerate before or right after fertilization.

Central Cell (1 Cell, 2 Nuclei)

Largest cell; houses 2 Polar Nuclei (n + n). Fuses with 2nd male gamete to form PEN (3n).

Micropylar End (3 Cells)

Egg Apparatus: 1 Egg Cell (n) (female gamete) + 2 Synergids (n) with Filiform Apparatus.

Filiform Apparatus: Special finger-like cellular thickenings in synergids; guide pollen tube entry into embryo sac chemotropically.

MEGASPORE SELECTION: 1 Megaspore MMC yields 1 Functional Embryo Sac (3 micropylar degenerate) 1:1 Ratio
EMBRYO SAC BLUEPRINT: 8 Nuclei organized into 7 Distinct Cells (Monosporic Polygonum Type) 8 Nuclei / 7 Cells
