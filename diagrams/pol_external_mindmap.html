INDIA'S EXTERNAL RELATIONS
POLITICS IN INDIA SINCE INDEPENDENCE · CH. 3

Nehru's Foreign Policy

Non-alignment: no bloc membership, independent judgment on every issue
Panchsheel 1954: five co-existence principles — signed with CHINA
Anti-colonial voice: Bandung 1955, Afro-Asian solidarity, Commonwealth membership without dominion status
UN activism: Korea mediation, early peacekeeping voice

The China Collapse

1950-59 ladder: Tibet absorbed → Panchsheel → Dalai Lama's asylum (1959)
Two disputed fronts: Aksai Chin (Ladakh, China's road) + McMahon Line (NEFA)
Oct-Nov 1962: simultaneous attacks; defences collapse; Menon resigns
Aftermath: unilateral ceasefire; Aksai Chin held; defence budgets and grief both jump

The Pakistan Wars

1965: Kutch skirmishes → Operation Gibraltar → full war → Tashkent (Jan 1966); Shastri dies there
1971: East Pakistan crackdown → 80 lakh refugees → Indo-Soviet Treaty (Aug) → two-front war
16 Dec 1971: Dhaka falls; BANGLADESH born
1972: Shimla Agreement — ceasefire line becomes the Line of Control

Shifting Alliances

1950s: courted by both blocs; Pakistan signs US pacts (SEATO 1954, CENTO)
1962: US-UK emergency airlift during the China war
1965: US embargo bites India; USSR brokers Tashkent
Aug 1971: 20-year Indo-Soviet Treaty — the decisive shift

The Nuclear Programme

1948: Atomic Energy Commission; Homi Bhabha's peaceful-atom vision
18 May 1974: Pokhran-I "Smiling Buddha" — a "peaceful nuclear explosion"
NPT 1968 refused: the treaty freezes a have/have-not world — discriminatory
The posture: universal disarmament demanded; the option preserved

How the Syllabus Lines Map

"Nehru's foreign policy": non-alignment + Panchsheel + anti-colonial voice
"1962, 1965, 1971": three wars, three outcomes — learn cause-war-treaty chains
"Nuclear programme": 1948 commission → 1974 Pokhran → NPT refusal
"Shifting alliance": solo non-alignment → Indo-Soviet Treaty 1971

Traps that catch most students

Non-alignment ≠ neutrality: independent judgment per issue — India took sides (1971)
Tashkent ≠ Shimla: 1966 after 1965 (Shastri); 1972 after 1971 (Indira-Bhutto, Line of Control)
Panchsheel partner is China: 1954 — the treaty the 1962 war shamed
Pokhran-I is 1974: "peaceful nuclear explosion" — Pokhran-II belongs to 1998
