THE FOUR BIOMOLECULE FAMILIES — ONE CARD EACH
Sugars run on carbon · proteins do the work · vitamins regulate · nucleic acids remember

CARBOHYDRATES — fuel & frame

SugarsGlucose C₆H₁₂O₆ — aldohexose, pyranose ring, D-configuration; from cane sugar hydrolysis or starch
DisaccharidesSucrose (glucose + fructose, non-reducing, α1,2-link), maltose (2 glucose, α1,4), lactose (galactose + glucose, β1,4)
PolysaccharidesStarch (amylose helix + amylopectin), cellulose (β1,4 — wood, fibre), glycogen (animal storage, branched)
EvidenceGlucose: 5-OH + CHO (penta-acetate, Br₂ water), no free CHO in solution (hemiacetal ring) → pyranose

AMINO ACIDS & PROTEINS — the workers

Amino acidsH₂N–CH(R)–COOH; essential (10, diet-only: valine, leucine, lysine...) vs non-essential (body makes: glycine, alanine...)
ZwitterionIn water: ⁺H₃N–CHR–COO⁻ — amphoteric, high melting
StructuresPrimary (sequence) → secondary (α-helix, β-sheet, H-bonds) → tertiary (3-D fold, S–S bridges) → quaternary (subunit assembly, e.g. haemoglobin)
DenaturationHeat/pH breaks 2°/3° only (egg boiling) — primary chain survives; globular → fibrous, biological activity lost

ENZYMES & VITAMINS — regulate & protect

EnzymesGlobular proteins, catalysts: lock-and-key / induced-fit; named substrate + -ase (urease, invertase); extremely specific, mild conditions
Fat-solubleA (night blindness), D (rickets), E, K (clotting)
Water-solubleB-complex (beriberi = B₁, pernicious anaemia = B₁₂), C (scurvy) — not stored, daily diet

NUCLEIC ACIDS & HORMONES — memory & messengers

DNASugar = β-D-2-deoxyribose; bases A T G C; double helix (Watson-Crick), A=T, G≡C pairing; stores heredity
RNASugar = ribose; bases A U G C; single strand; runs protein synthesis (m/t/r-RNA)
UnitsNucleotide = base + sugar + phosphate; backbone = sugar-phosphate ester chain
HormonesSteroids (testosterone, cortisol), amines (adrenaline, thyroxine), peptides (insulin); deficiency: diabetes (insulin), goitre (thyroxine/iodine), dwarfism (growth hormone)
