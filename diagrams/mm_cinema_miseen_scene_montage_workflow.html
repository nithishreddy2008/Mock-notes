The Anatomy of Film Language: Mise-en-scène, Montage & Sound Design

End-to-end cinematic grammar: Pro-filmic space, cinematography, Eisensteinian montage, and sound design

CUET Mass Media · Film Grammar Pipeline
1
Mise-en-scène
1. Pro-Filmic Setting & Decor
Everything placed before the camera: studio soundstages, authentic outdoor locations, set dressings, and symbolic props.
2. Lighting Architecture
Chiaroscuro (extreme contrast); High-key (bright, even, comedy/musical); Low-key (dark, moody, noir mystery).
3. Costume, Makeup & Blocking
Character visual coding; spatial proxemics and actor choreography (blocking) within the camera frame.
➔
2
Cinematography & Framing
1. Aspect Ratio Standards
Academy Ratio (1.37:1); Flat Widescreen (1.85:1); Anamorphic CinemaScope (2.39:1); Panavision.
2. Lenses & Depth of Field
Wide-angle (deep focus, exaggerated depth); Telephoto (shallow focus, compressed space); Normal 50mm (natural perspective).
3. Camera Movement Mechanics
Pan & Tilt (tripod axis); Steadicam (fluid handheld stabilizer); Dolly/Tracking; Technocrane swings.
➔
3
Editing & Soviet Montage
Continuity (Invisible) Editing
180° Rule (Axis of action), Eyeline Match, Match-on-action, Shot-reverse-shot, Cross-cutting / Parallel editing.
Disjunctive Editing & Jump Cuts
French New Wave abrupt temporal jumps; violation of the 30° rule; jarring aesthetic self-reflexivity.
Eisenstein's 5 Montage Tiers
Metric (cut length), Rhythmic (action pace), Tonal (emotional light), Overtonal (synthesis), Intellectual (metaphor collision).
➔
4
Sound & Certification
1. Diegetic vs Non-Diegetic
Diegetic: originating inside story world (speech, radio, footsteps). Non-Diegetic: outside story world (score, VO).
2. Sound Design & Foley
ADR dialogue replacement; physical Foley props; ambient wild tracks; Dolby Atmos immersive surround panning.
3. CBFC Film Certification
Cinematograph Act 1952: U (Universal), U/A (7+, 13+, 16+ parental guidance), A (Adults only), S (Specialized audience).
Core Invariant: Mise-en-scène is what is put before the camera; Montage creates meaning across cuts; Diegetic sound belongs to the film's narrative world.
