Meaning, Role & Objective of FM

Business finance = the money and credit needed to carry out business activities — raised, used and managed
Role: no activity runs without money — fixed assets, stock, salaries, expansion all lean on finance
Objective of financial management: wealth maximisation — decisions should raise the long-term market value of shareholders' wealth (not just this year's profit)

Financial Planning

Meaning: decide how much money the firm needs and from where it will come
Importance: prepares for the future · avoids both shortage and surplus · coordinates departments · links present needs with future growth · prevents waste
Shortage stalls operations; surplus sits idle earning nothing — planning keeps both away

Capital Structure — The Mix

Capital structure = the proportion of debt and equity in the firm's long-term funds
Factors: cash flow position · interest coverage ratio (ICR) · control · cost of debt · floatation costs · state of capital markets · trading on equity · tax rate · risk consideration · flexibility
Trading on equity: using cheap debt to boost returns for shareholders — works only when earnings are stable

Three Financial Decisions

Investment: where to put money — long-term (capital budgeting) + short-term (current assets). Factors: cash flows, rate of return, risk
Financing: the equity–debt mix. Factors: cost, risk, control, floatation cost, capital market state
Dividend: distribute or retain profit. Factors: earnings, stability, growth prospects, cash flow, shareholder preference, taxation

Fixed Capital — Factors

Fixed capital = money locked in long-term assets (land, plant, machines) for years
Factors affecting requirement: nature of business (railway vs shop) · scale of operations · choice of technique (capital vs labour intensive) · growth prospects · technology upgradation · business cycles
Capital-intensive businesses need much more fixed capital than service firms

Working Capital — Factors

Working capital = money for day-to-day operations — current assets minus current liabilities
Factors affecting requirement: nature of business · scale of operations · credit policy (buying and selling) · availability of credit · seasonal factors · operating efficiency · growth and inflation
Trading firms need high working capital; service firms need low

Traps that catch most students

Objective = wealth maximisation, not profit maximisation — profit is this year, wealth is long-term value
Trading on equity works only when the rate of return exceeds the cost of debt
Fixed capital = long-term assets; working capital = day-to-day — do not swap their factors
Dividends need cash — a profitable firm with tight cash flow may still skip dividends
