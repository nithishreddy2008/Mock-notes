Balance of Payments (BoP) Architecture: Current & Capital Accounts

Comprehensive accounting of economic transactions between domestic residents and the rest of the world (Credit = Inflow / +, Debit = Outflow / -)

NCERT CLASS 12 · CUET UG 2027
1. Current Account (Flow of Goods & Services)
No Asset/Liability Impact
A. Visible Trade (Merchandise) Balance of Trade (BoT)
Physical goods cross customs frontier: exports of tea, software hardware, oil imports.
Merchandise Exports (Inflow of Forex)Credit (+)
Merchandise Imports (Outflow of Forex)Debit (-)
B. Invisible Trade (Services & Factors) Net Invisibles
1. Non-Factor Services (IT, BPO, shipping, tourism)Credit / Debit
2. Factor Income (Profits, dividends, interest)Credit / Debit
3. Unilateral Transfers (Gifts, NRI remittances, grants)Credit / Debit
Core Diagnostic Distinction:
• Balance of Trade (BoT) = Merchandise Exports - Merchandise Imports (Visibles Only).
• Current Account Balance (CAB) = BoT + Net Invisibles (Visibles + Invisibles). A BoT deficit can be cushioned by a surplus in services and remittances.
2. Capital Account (Assets & Liabilities)
Changes Sovereign Ownership
A. Foreign Investments Productive / Financial
Foreign Direct Investment (FDI - Control/Factories)Credit (+)
Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI / FII - Shares/Bonds)Credit (+)
Domestic Investment Abroad (Indian firms buying assets)Debit (-)
B. External Borrowings & Lending Debt Inflows/Outflows
Commercial Borrowings (ECBs at market rates)Credit (+)
External Assistance (Concessional soft loans: World Bank)Credit (+)
Repayment of Foreign Loans (Principal debt repayment)Debit (-)
C. Banking Capital & NRI Deposits Liabilities to Foreigners
Foreign currency non-resident bank balances and commercial banks' external holdings.
Capital Account Impact: Records all transactions that cause a change in the assets or liabilities of domestic residents or the government vis-a-vis the rest of the world.
3. Autonomous vs Accommodating & BoP Deficit
Settlement Mechanics
Autonomous Transactions ("Above the Line") Independent
undertaken strictly for independent economic considerations (e.g. commercial profits, trade in goods, individual investment). Cause the BoP deficit or surplus.
Accommodating Transactions ("Below the Line") Compensatory
Undertaken by the Central Bank (RBI) specifically to bridge the autonomous gap and restore balance to zero. Includes Official Reserve Transactions (ORT).
BoP Deficit / Surplus Determination:
• BoP Deficit: Autonomous Receipts < Autonomous Payments.
• Financed by Central Bank selling foreign exchange reserves (Decrease in Forex Reserves = Credit in ORT).
• BoP Surplus: Autonomous Receipts > Autonomous Payments (Forex Reserves increase).
Accounting BoP vs Operational BoP:
Accounting BoP always balances (Total Credits = Total Debits). Operational BoP deficit refers strictly to an autonomous net deficit.
CUET Golden Rule: An autonomous deficit forces official reserve drawdowns: Decrease in Central Bank Reserves = Credit (+) in BoP.
BoP Identity: Current Account + Capital Account + Errors & Omissions = 0
