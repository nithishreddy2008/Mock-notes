The Circular Flow Theorems

  • Real Flow vs. Money Flow: Real flow is physical exchange of factor services and goods. Money flow is counter-balancing monetary payments (wages, rent, prices).
  • Leakages & Injections: Leakages (Savings $S$, Taxes $T$, Imports $M$) pull money out. Injections (Investment $I$, Govt $G$, Exports $X$) inject money in. In equilibrium: $S + T + M = I + G + X$.

The 3 Golden Transformation Formulas

  • Gross to Net: $\text{Net} = \text{Gross} - \text{Depreciation (Consumption of Fixed Capital)}$.
  • Domestic to National: $\text{National} = \text{Domestic} + \text{NFIA}$ (Where $\text{NFIA} = \text{Factor Income from Abroad} - \text{To Abroad}$).
  • Market Price to Factor Cost: $\text{Factor Cost (FC)} = \text{Market Price (MP)} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)}$ (Where $\text{NIT} = \text{GST/Taxes} - \text{Subsidies}$).

CUET Trap: True National Income

Fatal Trap: Believing GDP is National Income. Reality: $G D P_{M P}$ is Gross Domestic Product at Market Price. True *Domestic Income* is strictly $N D P_{F C}$, and true *National Income* is strictly $N N P_{F C}$!
