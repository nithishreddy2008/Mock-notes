FIGURE 2: LIFECYCLE & AUDIT WORKFLOW
STAGE 1 Screening (Cat B)
Screening: B1 vs B2
Applies only to Category 'B' projects at State level (SEAC). Category 'A' projects bypass screening (mandatory EIA). Divides Category B into B1 (requires full EIA report) and B2 (exempt from EIA).
Authority: State Expert Appraisal Committee (SEAC) via Form 1.
STAGE 2 Scoping & ToR
Scoping & Terms of Reference
Formulates comprehensive, site-specific Terms of Reference (ToR) addressing all key environmental impacts. Baseline data collected for one season (other than monsoon) across air, water, ecology, and noise.
Output: Draft EIA Report & Environmental Management Plan (EMP).
STAGE 3 Public Hearing
Public Consultation
Mandatory democratic participation. SPCB conducts Public Hearing on-site within 45 days, presided by District Magistrate, plus invites written responses from all stakeholders. Videographed and minuted.
Exemptions: National defense, border roads, small unit modernization.
STAGE 4 Appraisal & EC
Appraisal & Grant of EC
Detailed scrutiny of final EIA, EMP, and public hearing proceedings by EAC (Category A) or SEAC (Category B). Recommends grant of Environmental Clearance (EC) with binding conditions or rejection within 45 days.
Compliance: Half-yearly compliance reports submitted to MoEFCC.
AUDIT Rule 14 EPA
Environmental Auditing
Systematic, periodic, and objective evaluation of compliance. Rule 14 mandates submission of annual Environmental Statement (Form V) by September 30. Conducted across 3 phases: Pre-Audit, On-Site, and Post-Audit.
Types: Compliance, Liability, Waste Minimization, Energy Audits.
ISO 14001 PDCA Cycle
EMS & Life Cycle Assessment
ISO 14001 specifies Environmental Management Systems based on Deming's Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) continual improvement cycle. ISO 14040/14044 governs Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) from cradle to grave.
Eco-Mark: Indian Matka logo (1991) for eco-friendly consumer goods.
