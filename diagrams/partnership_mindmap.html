ACCOUNTING FOR PARTNERSHIP
UNIT I · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Partnership & Its Deed

Definition: relation between persons agreed to share profits of a business carried by all or any acting for all (Indian Partnership Act, 1932)
Features: 2 or more persons · agreement · profit sharing · mutual agency
Deed = written rulebook: ratio, salaries, IoC & IoD rates, drawings limits
Deed silent? Apply the Act: equal shares, no IoC, no salary, no IoD, loan interest 6% p.a.

Capital Accounts

Fixed: two accounts — Capital stays constant, Current absorbs everything
Fluctuating: one account — balance changes every year (default system)
Current A/c can show a debit balance; capital (fixed) cannot
All yearly adjustments (IoC, salary, drawings) land in Current A/c when fixed

Past Adjustments

Error found after accounts closed — do not reopen the books
4 steps: what was done → what should be done → net effect per partner → one combined entry
Entry passes through partners’ Current A/cs (or Capital when fluctuating)
One partner’s loss = the others’ gain, in the profit-sharing ratio

P&L Appropriation A/c

Extension of P&L — shows how profit is shared, not how it is earned
Credit: net profit b/d + interest on drawings
Debit: IoC · partner salary · commission · general reserve
Balance left = divisible profit → shared in the agreed ratio

Profit Division Items

IoC: opening capital × rate × time (extra capital counted month-wise)
IoD: equal monthly drawings — average period 6.5 / 6 / 5.5 months; quarterly — 10.5 / 7.5 / 4.5
Product method: Σ (amount × months) × rate% × 1/12
Commission: before charge = rate × profit / 100; after charge = rate/(100+rate) × profit

Guarantee of Profit

A partner is assured a minimum profit — by the firm, some partners, or an outsider
Deficiency = guaranteed amount − actual share
Guarantors bear it in their agreed ratio (or the sacrificing ratio, if given)
Remaining partners’ shares reduce; total distributed stays the same

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Deed silent ≠ 6% IoC. 6% p.a. applies only to a partner’s loan to the firm
Salary to a partner is an appropriation, not a charge — it never reduces profit before appropriation
Fixed system: never touch the Capital A/c — adjustments go to Current A/c
