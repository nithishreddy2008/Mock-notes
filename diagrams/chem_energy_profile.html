ARRHENIUS EQUATION & ENERGETICS

Quantitative Laws
Arrhenius Exponential Formulation
k = A · e^(−Ea / RT)
• k: Rate constant | A: Frequency / Pre-exponential factor.
• e^(−Ea / RT): The Boltzmann fraction of colliding molecules with kinetic energy $\ge E_a$.
Logarithmic Form & Two-Temperature Equation
log(k₂ / k₁) = (Ea / 2.303R) · [(T₂ − T₁) / (T₁ · T₂)]
Plotting $\log k$ vs $1/T$ yields a straight line with slope $= -E_a / (2.303 R)$ and $y$-intercept $= \log A$.
TEMPERATURE COEFFICIENT RULE
For most chemical reactions, a 10°C rise in temperature doubles or triples the reaction rate:
Temperature Coefficient = k_(T + 10) / k_T ≈ 2 to 3
Reason: The fraction of molecules having energy $\ge E_a$ nearly doubles, drastically multiplying effective collisions.
