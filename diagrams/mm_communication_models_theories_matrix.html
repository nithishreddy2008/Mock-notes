Aristotle's Rhetoric
Classical (300 BCE)		 Speaker → Speech → Occasion → Audience → Effect.
Three proofs: Ethos, Pathos, Logos.		 Speaker-centric linear model designed for public speaking; persuasion is the paramount goal. Trap: No feedback loop and no noise factor. Primarily for interpersonal/public rhetoric, not electronic mass media.
Lasswell's Formula
Harold Lasswell (1948)		 Who → Says What → In Which Channel → To Whom → With What Effect. Unilinear transmission mapping five distinct research areas: Control, Content, Media, Audience, Effects. Trap: Assumes a direct, unmediated effect (Hypodermic Needle Theory lineage). Omits noise and reciprocal feedback.
Shannon & Weaver
Mathematical (1949)		 Info Source → Transmitter → Channel (Noise) → Receiver → Destination. Engineered for telecommunications; introduced the concept of Noise (mechanical, semantic, psychological). Trap: Mathematical/engineering model originally devoid of human feedback (added later by Weaver as secondary loop).
Berlo's SMCR Model
David Berlo (1960)		 Source → Message → Channel → Receiver.
5 sensory channels (hearing, seeing, touching, etc.).		 Focuses on psychological variables: communication skills, attitude, knowledge, social system, culture. Trap: Completely linear with no provision for feedback or noise. Requires source and receiver encoder/decoder parity.
Osgood-Schramm
Circular Model (1954)		 Encoder, Interpreter, Decoder ↔ Message.
Overlapping Field of Experience.		 Circular and interactive; both parties simultaneously act as transmitters and receivers. Communication is dynamic. Trap: Communication fails if there is zero overlap between the Source and Receiver's Field of Experience.
Agenda Setting & Cultivation
McCombs-Shaw / Gerbner		 Media Agenda → Public Agenda.
Heavy TV viewing → "Mean World Syndrome".		 Media tells audiences what to think about (salience), not what to think. Television cultivates perceptions of reality over time. Trap: Agenda setting does not dictate opinion valence; Cultivation applies to long-term cumulative TV exposure, not single broadcasts.
