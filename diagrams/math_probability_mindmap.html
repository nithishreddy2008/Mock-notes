1. Conditioning & Independence Foundations
Conditional Probability P(A|B)
$P(A|B) = P(A \cap B) / P(B)$. Sample space reduces from $S$ to $B$. $P(S|B) = 1$.
Multiplication Theorem Joint Rule
$P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B|A)$. Product rule across sequential trial branches.
Independent Events P(A ∩ B) = P(A)·P(B)
Occurrence of $B$ gives zero info on $A$. If $A, B$ independent $\implies A', B'$ independent.

2. Partitions & Bayes' Inversion Inference
Sample Space Partition {E₁, ..., Eₙ}
Pairwise disjoint events whose union forms universe $S$ ($E_i \cap E_j = \emptyset, \bigcup E_i = S$).
Theorem of Total Probability Law of Total P
$P(A) = \sum_{i=1}^n P(E_i) \cdot P(A|E_i)$. Total probability via all causal paths.
Bayes' Posterior Rule P(Eᵢ|A)
Inverse probability calculation: $P(E_i|A) = [P(E_i)P(A|E_i)] / \sum [P(E_j)P(A|E_j)]$.
3. Random Variables & Moments Distributions
Discrete Random Variable X: S → ℝ
Maps sample space outcomes to real numbers. Normalization: $p_i \ge 0, \sum p_i = 1$.
Mean / Expectation μ = E(X)
$E(X) = \sum x_i p_i$. Scaling property: $E(aX + b) = a E(X) + b$. Center of mass.
Variance & Std Dev σ² = Var(X)
$\text{Var}(X) = E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2 \ge 0$. Scaling: $\text{Var}(aX + b) = a^2 \text{Var}(X)$.
4. Bernoulli Trials & Binomial Model Binomial
Bernoulli Trials 4 Axioms
Finite $n$ trials, independent, only 2 outcomes ($p + q = 1$), constant success $p$.
Binomial PMF B(n, p)
$P(X = r) = \binom{n}{r} p^r q^{n-r}$. At least 1 success: $P(X \ge 1) = 1 - q^n$.
Binomial Parameters Mean > Var
$\text{Mean} = np$, $\text{Variance} = npq$. Since $q < 1$, $\mathbf{\text{Var} < \text{Mean}}$ universally!
