PROBABILITY — CONDITIONING & BAYES
CUET UG MATHEMATICS

Foundations (the A5 refresher)

P(E) = favourable / total, always in [0, 1]
Complement: P(not E) = 1 − P(E) — the rescue move
Certain + impossible: P = 1 and P = 0 are the boundary posts
Simple sets: dice (36 ordered pairs), cards (52), coins (2ⁿ)

Addition Rule

P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)
Mutually exclusive: overlap is 0, so just add
Exhaustive: the parts together cover everything
Trap: forgetting the subtracted overlap

Conditional Probability

P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B) — the universe shrinks to B
Card reflex: given red, P(king) = 2/26 = 1/13
Arrow discipline: P(A|B) and P(B|A) are different animals
Read the words: "given that" names the new universe

Multiplication & Independence

Chain: P(A∩B) = P(B) · P(A|B) — and along the tree for 3+
Independent: P(A∩B) = P(A) · P(B)
Not exclusive: disjoint ≠ independent — the classic confusion
At least one: 1 − P(none) — compute the back door

Total Probability

Doors E₁…Eₙ partition the space; A arrives through one
P(A) = Σ P(Eᵢ)·P(A|Eᵢ) — weighted door sum
Bulb story: three machines, one defect rate each
Discipline: the door weights must sum to 1

Bayes' Theorem

P(Eᵢ|A) = P(Eᵢ)·P(A|Eᵢ) / Σ P(Eⱼ)·P(A|Eⱼ)
One door on top, all doors on the bottom
Tree habit: draw branches, multiply along, add across, divide
NCERT classic: defective bulb → 25/69 for machine A

TOP TRAPS

Flipped conditional — P(A|B) answered as P(B|A)
Bayes denominator — one door used instead of the full sum
Replacement ignored — second-draw probabilities shift without it
