Reproductive Health: Meaning & Why

WHO: total well-being — physical, emotional, behavioural, social — in everything about reproduction
Why society cares: family planning · safe motherhood · STI control
India: first family-planning programme 1951 → RCH programmes from 1997
Awareness: sex education in schools, posters, audio-visual media, fertile-window knowledge

Amniocentesis & Population

Amniocentesis: test on amniotic fluid (fluid around the foetus) to read foetal chromosomes
Detects: chromosomal disorders (Down's) + metabolic disorders via enzyme checks
Misuse: sex determination → female foeticide → banned (PCPNDT Act, 1994)
Stabilise population: incentives + education; a growing population strains food, homes, jobs

MTP — Medical Termination of Pregnancy

What: intentional/voluntary abortion — legal under the MTP Act, 1971 (amended 2017, 2021)
Windows: up to 12 weeks safely · 12–24 weeks with RMP opinion · beyond 24 only special cases
Methods: suction/vacuum aspiration, medicines
Caution: risks rise after 12 weeks — qualified doctors only; unsafe abortions harm

Contraception Families

Natural: periodic abstinence (days 10–17) · withdrawal · lactational amenorrhoea (~6 months)
Barrier: condoms (also block STIs) · diaphragms · cervical caps + spermicides
IUDs: Lippes loop · Cu-T, Cu-7, Multiload 375 · Progestasert, LNG-20
Pills & surgery: Norethindrone + ethinylestradiol, Saheli · vasectomy vs tubectomy

Sexually Transmitted Infections

Bacterial (curable): gonorrhoea · syphilis · chlamydiasis
Viral (treatable, not curable): genital herpes · genital warts (HPV) · hepatitis-B · HIV
Protozoan: trichomoniasis (Trichomonas)
Often silent: discharge, itching, sores, pain may never show; condoms + screened blood prevent

Infertility & ART

Infertility: no pregnancy after 1–2 years of trying — the cause can sit with either partner
IVF: fertilisation outside; embryo at 8+ blastomeres into the uterus (test-tube baby)
ZIFT zygote ≤ 8 blastomeres into tube · GIFT gametes into tube · ICSI sperm into egg · IUI semen into uterus
Complete answers: counselling, surrogacy, adoption — ART Act 2021 keeps it regulated

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Condoms are the only family that also blocks STIs — pills and IUDs do not
Cu-T is copper-releasing; LNG-20 is hormone-releasing — do not mix the IUD families
Route rule: ZIFT puts ≤ 8 blastomeres in the TUBE; IVF puts 8+ blastomeres in the UTERUS
Viral STIs (herpes, HIV) are treatable — antibiotics never cure them
