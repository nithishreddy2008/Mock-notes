REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH

WHO GLOBAL DEFINITION
A total state of physical, emotional, behavioral, and social well-being in all aspects of human reproduction, not merely the absence of disease.

1. Demographics & Public Health

RCH & STATUTORY LAWS
National Family Planning (1951) India was the 1st country globally to launch nationwide reproductive action plans, expanded into Reproductive & Child Health Care (RCH) programs.
Demographic Growth Drivers Rapid population explosion fueled by sharp decline in Maternal Mortality Rate (MMR), Infant Mortality Rate (IMR), and overall death rate.
Amniocentesis Statutory Ban Prenatal foetal karyotyping from amniotic fluid; statutory ban enacted strictly to curb rampant female foeticide.
Legal Marriageable Age Statutory minimum age for marriage: 18 years for females, 21 years for males.

2. Contraception Modalities

FAMILY PLANNING ARSENAL
Natural Methods: Periodic abstinence (Day 10–17 fertile window), coitus interruptus, and lactational amenorrhea (up to 6 months post-partum).
Barrier Devices: Male/female condoms (protect against STIs/AIDS); diaphragms, cervical caps, and vaults (cervical covers paired with spermicides).
IUDs (Most Widely Accepted): Non-medicated (Lippes loop), Copper-releasing (CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375), and Hormone-releasing (Progestasert, LNG-20).
Oral & Injectable Hormones: Combined daily pills (Mala-D); once-a-week non-steroidal pill (Saheli by CDRI Lucknow); subdermal implants (Norplant).
Permanent Sterilization: Vasectomy (vas deferens cut/tied) in males; Tubectomy (fallopian tubes cut/tied) in females; irreversible terminal methods.

3. Sexually Transmitted Infections

STIs / VDs / RTIs
High Vulnerability Demographic Highest incidence reported among sexually active youth in the 15 to 24 years age bracket.
Bacterial & Protozoan STIs (Curable) Gonorrhoea (Neisseria), Syphilis (Treponema pallidum), Chlamydiasis, Trichomoniasis; curable with early antibiotics.
Incurable Viral STIs (High-Yield Trap) Hepatitis B, Genital Herpes, and HIV/AIDS cannot be cured completely; management is lifelong anti-retroviral therapy.
Chronic Complications Untreated STIs precipitate Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID), stillbirths, ectopic pregnancies, abortions, and tubal infertility.

4. MTP & Infertility Management

CLINICAL PROTOCOLS
MTP Act (1971 & 2017 Amendments) Safest during 1st trimester (up to 12 weeks); 1 RMP needed up to 12 wks; 2 RMPs for 12–20 wks (up to 24 wks for vulnerable cases).
IVF-ET (In Vitro Fertilization) "Test-tube baby"; fertilization in glassware. ZIFT: $\le 8$ blastomeres $\to$ Fallopian Tube. IUT: $> 8$ blastomeres $\to$ Uterus.
GIFT (In Vivo Technique) Donor ovum transferred into fallopian tube of female who cannot produce gametes but can nurture pregnancy.
ICSI & Insemination (AI / IUI) Single sperm microinjected directly into ovum (ICSI). Semen introduced into uterus (IUI) for male factor oligospermia.
