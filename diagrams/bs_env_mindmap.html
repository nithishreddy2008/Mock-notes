Meaning & Features

Business environment = the sum of all individuals, institutions and forces outside a business that affect its performance
Features: totality of external forces · specific + general forces · inter-relatedness · dynamic · uncertainty · complexity · relativity
Specific = customers, suppliers, competitors (affects one industry); general = government, social trends (affects all)

Importance (5 Points)

First mover advantage — spot opportunities before rivals do
Early warning — identify threats before they strike
Tapping useful resources — environment supplies inputs; the firm returns goods and services
Coping with rapid changes · planning and policy — plans are built on environment scanning

Demonetisation — One Event, Five Dimensions

Economic: ₹500 and ₹1000 notes cancelled overnight
Political: government's move against black money and tax evasion
Legal: RBI notification under the RBI Act, 1934
Technological: surge in digital payments and wallets
Social: people's payment habits changed within months

Five Dimensions (1)

Economic: interest rates, inflation, income, stock indices — the money climate
Social: customs, traditions, values, trends, literacy, demographics
Technological: new techniques and innovations in products and processes
Example: rising literacy → more demand for books; smartphone replaced cameras

Five Dimensions (2)

Political: stability, attitude of government towards business, party ideology
Legal: legislation, administrative orders, court judgments
Example: statutory warnings on tobacco packs = legal; investor-friendly government = political

Impact of Environment on Business

Environment supplies inputs (men, money, material, power); business returns outputs (goods, services, taxes)
Inter-related forces: soft-drink demand → glass and refrigerator demand rise too
The same event is read differently by different firms — that is relativity

Traps that catch most students

Specific ≠ general forces: customers/suppliers are specific; government/social trends are general
GST or demonetisation span several dimensions at once — expect multi-dimension answers
Political (attitude, stability) ≠ legal (actual laws, courts, orders)
Uncertainty is hard to predict — fashion, technology; complex = many forces easy to understand but hard to grasp together
