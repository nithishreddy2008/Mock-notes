1. New Media Nature & Characteristics
Digital Convergence (Negroponte: 'bits vs atoms'); Interactivity (user control over choice and pace); Hypertextuality (Ted Nelson, non-linear node hopping); Virtuality (simulated immersion); Networkability (dispersed nodes, peer-to-peer).
Digitization Hypertext Interactivity Convergence
2. Web Evolution & Participatory Culture
Web 1.0 (Static, Read-Only, webmaster-curated); Web 2.0 (Read-Write, Tim O'Reilly 2004, wikis, social feeds); Web 3.0 (Semantic, decentralized, machine-readable); Henry Jenkins (Participatory Culture); Alvin Toffler ('Prosumer').
Web 2.0 O'Reilly Prosumer Participatory Culture Semantic Web
3. Social Networks & Algorithmic Reality
Microblogging (X), Social networking (LinkedIn), Short-form video (Reels, Shorts); Algorithmic recommendation feeds; Eli Pariser's 'Filter Bubble' (isolation in tailored content); Echo Chambers; Virality metrics and dopamine loops.
Filter Bubble Echo Chamber Algorithms Microblogging
4. Citizen Journalism & Disinformation
Citizen Journalism (smartphones, live streaming, eyewitness reports, Arab Spring, Nirbhaya); UGC crowdsourcing vs editorial gatekeeping; Verification deficits; Misinformation (unintentional) vs Disinformation (malicious) vs Deepfakes; Fact-checking.
Citizen Journalism UGC Crowdsourcing Disinformation Deepfakes
5. Sociological Impacts & Net Neutrality
Digital Divide (access, literacy, urban-rural, gender divides); Net Neutrality (equal treatment of data packets; TRAI 2016 ban on discriminatory zero-rating like Free Basics); Shoshana Zuboff's Surveillance Capitalism (behavioral data extraction).
Digital Divide Net Neutrality TRAI 2016 Surveillance Cap.
6. Cyber Law & Governance in India
IT Act 2000 (Sec 43A data, Sec 66A struck down in Shreya Singhal 2015, Sec 69A blocking, Sec 79 safe harbor); IT Rules 2021 (3-tier grievance mechanism, 24-hr takedown, RGO); Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
IT Act 2000 Shreya Singhal 2015 IT Rules 2021 DPDP Act 2023
