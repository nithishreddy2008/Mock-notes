Everyday Science Anchors

Light beats sound: lightning first, thunder after — light outruns sound
Ice floats: frozen water is LESS dense than liquid water
Cooker logic: higher pressure raises water's boiling point
Sweat cools: evaporation carries heat away from skin

The Observation Method

List the givens: what is present, what changed, what stayed fixed
One clear question — turn the puzzle into "why did X happen?"
Sketch it: a 10-second drawing exposes what prose hides

The Claim Checklist

Evidence first: real data or just big promises?
Mechanism: can it say HOW, step by step, in plain words?
Alarm words: "instant", "cures everything", "no side effects"
Follow the gain: who profits if you believe it?

Problem-Solving Loop

Define: one clear sentence for the actual goal
Simplest first: test the cheapest approach before fancy ones
Verify: does the result satisfy EVERY condition given?

Correlation vs Causation

Together ≠ because of: ice-cream sales and drownings rise together — summer causes both
Ask for the how: no mechanism, no conclusion
Hunt the hidden cause: one third factor often explains the pair

Health & Environment Basics

Vaccines train immunity — a rehearsal, not an illness-giver
Vitamin D from sunlight; iron fights anaemia; iodised salt protects the thyroid
Standing water breeds mosquitoes — the post-rain disease link

Traps that catch most students

Heat confused with temperature — heat is energy flowing; temperature is a reading
Weight confused with mass — mass never changes; weight bends with gravity
Believing confident claims — confidence is not evidence; mechanisms are
Jumping to drama — the boring mechanism-supported answer is usually right
