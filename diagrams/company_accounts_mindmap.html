Companies: Features & Types

Separate legal entity: the company owns assets and owes debts in its own name, not the members'
Perpetual succession: members may come and go — the company lives on; also an artificial person created by law
Limited liability: a member loses only what he agreed to pay on his shares
Private vs Public: private — 2 to 200 members, no public invitation; public — min 7, no maximum

The Share Capital Ladder

Authorised = maximum allowed by the Memorandum (the ceiling)
Issued = offered to the public; Subscribed = actually taken up
Called-up = demanded so far; Paid-up = money truly received
Paid-up = called-up less calls in arrears; forfeited amount is added back in the note

Forfeiture & Re-issue

Forfeit = cancel shares after due notice for non-payment of calls
Debit Share Capital with called-up; credit Forfeited Shares with money received; debit Securities Premium only if premium is unpaid
Re-issue discount per batch cannot exceed the forfeited amount
Leftover forfeited money after re-issue → Capital Reserve

Issue of Shares

Money timeline: Application → Allotment → Calls (min application 5% of issue price)
At premium: extra over face value goes to Securities Premium Reserve — never to P&L
Over-subscription: excess application money adjusts allotment; rejected applicants refunded
Calls in arrears = not yet paid (deduct); calls in advance = paid early (liability till due)

Balance Sheet Presentation

Schedule III: Equity & Liabilities → Shareholders' Funds → (a) Share Capital
Note shows Authorised / Issued / Subscribed, then "fully paid" and "not fully paid" lines
Less calls in arrears from called-up; add Forfeited Shares A/c to paid-up
Debentures sit under Long-term Borrowings

Debentures

An IOU of the company: fixed interest, fixed repayment, holder is a creditor, not an owner
Issued at par / premium / discount; discount is a capital loss written off over the life
Interest is a charge — paid even in a loss year, before any dividend
Collateral security: note only, or Debentures Suspense A/c entry; write off discount by fixed or floating instalments

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Shares cannot be issued at discount (Sec 53) — only sweat equity is allowed; debentures CAN be
Securities Premium never touches P&L — only the four uses listed in Sec 52
Debenture interest is a charge, not an appropriation of profit
Re-issue discount cap: never more than the amount forfeited on those very shares
