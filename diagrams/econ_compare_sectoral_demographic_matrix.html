2. Sectoral Structural Trajectories (GDP Contribution vs Employment Absorption)
Factory of the World vs Service Leapfrog
India (Service-Led Leapfrog) Tertiary Boom
Agriculture Share:16% GDP | 43% Jobs
Industry Share:28% GDP | 25% Jobs
Services Share:56% GDP | 32% Jobs
Key Trajectory: Bypassed manufacturing-led growth. Direct shift from agriculture into services (IT, software, finance). Severe disguised unemployment persists in agriculture.
China (Manufacturing Powerhouse) Classical Path
Agriculture Share:7% GDP | 25% Jobs
Industry Share:38% GDP | 28% Jobs
Services Share:55% GDP | 47% Jobs
Key Trajectory: Followed textbook historical structural transition: Agriculture → Heavy Industrial Manufacturing ("Factory of the World") → Services.
Pakistan (Agricultural & Services) Premature Transition
Agriculture Share:24% GDP | 37% Jobs
Industry Share:19% GDP | 24% Jobs
Services Share:57% GDP | 39% Jobs
Key Trajectory: High agricultural GDP dependence (24%) with weak domestic manufacturing. Heavy reliance on services, informal activities, and Gulf worker remittances.
CUET Core Takeaway: China leads in urbanisation (65%) and industry share (38% of GDP). India leads in services growth. Pakistan records highest population growth (1.98%).
