1. Series LCR & Phasors
Impedance Analysis
Z = √(R² + (XL - XC)²) = √(R² + (ωL - 1/ωC)²)
tan ϕ = (XL - XC) / R | cos ϕ = R / Z
  • Inductive Circuit (XL > XC): Current lags voltage by phase angle ϕ (0 < ϕ < π/2).
  • Capacitive Circuit (XC > XL): Current leads voltage by phase angle ϕ.
  • Peak vs RMS: Irms = I₀ / √2 ≈ 0.707 I₀; Vrms = V₀ / √2.

2. Resonance & Q-Factor
Frequency Response
ωr = 1 / √(LC) ⟹ fr = 1 / (2π√(LC))
Q = ωr·L / R = (1/R)·√(L/C) = ωr / Δω
  • Resonance Condition: XL = XC ⟹ Z_min = R. Impedance is purely resistive.
  • Sharpness of Tuning: Higher Q factor means narrower bandwidth Δω, resulting in superior selectivity in radio receivers.
  • Acceptor Circuit: Series LCR admits maximum current at ωr; acts as a band-pass filter.

3. Power & Transformers
Energy & Transfer

P_avg = Vrms · Irms · cos ϕ (Power Factor = cos ϕ)
Vs / Vp = Ns / Np = Ip / Is = k | η = (Vs·Is)/(Vp·Ip)
  • Wattless Current: Component I sin ϕ has zero average power dissipation (cos(90°) = 0); seen in pure L or pure C circuits.
  • Step-Up vs Step-Down: Step-up (k > 1, Ns > Np ⟹ Vs > Vp, Is < Ip); Step-down (k < 1, Ns < Np).
  • Transformer Losses: Copper loss (I²R heating), Iron/Eddy loss (minimized by laminations), Hysteresis (soft iron core), Flux leakage.
