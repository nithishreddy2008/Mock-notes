1. Indefinite Integration Techniques
Antiderivative Vault
Substitution Rule: ∫ f(g(x)) g'(x) dx = ∫ f(t) dt. Standard forms: ∫ f'/f = ln|f|; ∫ [f]ⁿ f' = [f]ⁿ⁺¹/(n+1).
By Parts (ILATE): ∫ u v dx = u ∫ v − ∫ [u' ∫ v]. Priority: Inverse Trig > Log > Algebraic > Trig > Exponential.
Exponential Shortcut: ∫ eˣ [f(x) + f'(x)] dx = eˣ f(x) + C. Instantly collapses nested products.
Partial Fractions: Factorable polynomials. Check deg(Num) < deg(Den) before setting up linear/quadratic terms.
Special 9 Forms: Quadratic integrals without radicals (1/a tan⁻¹), radical denominators (sin⁻¹, ln), and product radicals.
2. Definite Integral Symmetries & Rules
Master Properties
The King's Rule: ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = ∫ₐᵇ f(a + b − x) dx. Adding I + I = 2I collapses symmetric fractions to (b − a)/2.
Queen's Property: ∫₀^(2a) f(x) dx = 2 ∫₀ᵃ f(x) dx if f(2a − x) = f(x); equals 0 if f(2a − x) = −f(x).
Even/Odd Symmetry: ∫₋ₐᵃ f(x) dx = 2 ∫₀ᵃ f(x) dx (even) or 0 (odd). Immediate zero for odd integrands on [-a, a].
Interval Splitting: ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = ∫ₐᶜ + ∫꜀ᵇ. Crucial for modulus |x − c| and greatest integer [x] functions.
Leibniz Integral Rule: d/dx [∫_{u(x)}^{v(x)} f(t) dt] = f(v(x)) v'(x) − f(u(x)) u'(x). Used in L'Hôpital limit questions.
3. Applications of Integrals (AOI Engine)
Bounded Geometry
Area Under Simple Curves: Area = ∫ₐᵇ |y| dx. If curve crosses x-axis at c, compute |∫ₐᶜ| + |∫꜀ᵇ|. Never allow net cancellation.
Area Between Two Curves: Area = ∫ₐᵇ [y_upper(x) − y_lower(x)] dx. Solve f(x) = g(x) to locate boundary x-limits.
Standard Conic Areas: Circle x² + y² = r² ⟹ π r²; Ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 ⟹ π a b.
Trigonometric Waves: Bounded area of 1 loop of sin x or cos x over interval of length π is exactly 2 sq units.
