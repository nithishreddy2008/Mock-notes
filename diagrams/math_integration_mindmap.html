INTEGRATION & ITS APPLICATIONS
CUET UG MATHEMATICS

Antiderivatives (+C)

Reverse differentiation: ∫xⁿ = xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1), ∫dx/x = ln|x|
+C is not decoration — a whole antiderivative family
Linearity: sums split, constants come out
Verification: differentiate your answer back

The Four Methods (in order)

1 · Standard form — match the table, write, add C
2 · Substitution — inner derivative visible outside
3 · Partial fractions — factorable rational denominator
4 · By parts — two different families multiplied; ILATE picks u

Special Standard Integrals

Arctan family: dx/(x²+a²) → (1/a)tan⁻¹(x/a)
Arcsin family: dx/√(a²−x²) → sin⁻¹(x/a)
Log family: dx/(x²−a²) → (1/2a)ln|(x−a)/(x+a)|
Square-root family: √(a²−x²) → x/2·√ + a²/2·sin⁻¹(x/a)

Definite Integrals & FTC

FTC: ∫ₐᵇ f = F(b) − F(a) — upper minus lower
Properties: reversal flips sign; additivity splits intervals
King's rule: ∫₀ᵃ f(x) = ∫₀ᵃ f(a−x) — the reflect trick
Even–odd: symmetric limits kill odd integrands, double even

Area Under Curves

Root–Split–Flip: roots first, split, absolute values
Quarter circle: ∫₀ᵃ√(a²−x²)dx = πa²/4 — know it cold
y = x² cut by y = 4: area 32/3 between x = −2, 2
Geometric area is never negative — the integral can be

Choosing the Method (reflexes)

See x·(x²+1)-shaped pair? substitution
Rational + factorable bottom? partial fractions
x, ln x, or inverse-trig factor? by parts (ILATE)
Stuck after one by parts? the integral can reappear — solve for it
