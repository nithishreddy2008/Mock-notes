NATURAL / BEHAVIORAL

Natural & Traditional Methods

Periodic Abstinence Avoid coitus from Day 10 to 17 of menstrual cycle (fertile window / ovulatory peak).
Coitus Interruptus Withdrawal of penis from vagina immediately before ejaculation; prevents insemination.
Lactational Amenorrhea Intense post-partum suckling elevates prolactin, suppressing GnRH & ovulation; valid for max 6 months.
Clinical Profile: Zero medicinal side effects; high failure / user-error rate; zero STI protection.
MECHANICAL BARRIER

Barrier Contraceptives

Condoms (Male / Nirodh) Thin latex/polyurethane sheath covering penis; prevents sperm deposition into cervix.
Female Condoms (Femidom) Polyurethane sheath lining vagina & cervix; dual-ring anchor.
Diaphragms, Vaults & Caps Rubber domes fitted over cervix; reusable. Paired with spermicidal creams, jellies & foams.
Dual Advantage: Condoms uniquely confer protection against STIs & HIV/AIDS transmission.
INTRAUTERINE DEVICES

IUDs (Most Popular in India)

Non-Medicated IUDs Lippes Loop: Double-S polyethylene loop; induces local sterile inflammatory response; phagocytoses sperms.
Copper-Releasing IUDs CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375: Ionized $Cu^{2+}$ suppresses sperm motility and fertilizing capacity.
Hormone-Releasing IUDs Progestasert, LNG-20: Slow levonorgestrel release; makes uterus hostile to implantation & cervix viscous.
Target Demographic: Ideal method for women wishing to delay pregnancy or space children.
ORAL CONTRACEPTION

Oral Steroidal & Non-Steroidal

Daily Combined Pills Progesterone + Estrogen (e.g., Mala-D). Taken daily for 21 days starting within first 5 days of cycle.
Mechanism of Action Negative feedback suppresses pituitary FSH & LH $\to$ inhibits ovulation and blastocyst implantation.
Saheli (Centchroman) World's 1st non-steroidal oral pill developed by CDRI Lucknow; taken once-a-week with few side-effects.
Cervical Barrier: Alters cervical mucus consistency, severely impairing sperm penetration.
EXTENDED RELEASE

Injectables & Subdermal Implants

Injectables (Depo-Provera) Intramuscular injection of depot medroxyprogesterone acetate (DMPA); effective for 3 months.
Norplant Implants 6 flexible silastic capsules containing levonorgestrel implanted subdermally under inner upper arm skin.
Emergency Contraception Progestogen-only or combined pills / LNG-IUD administered within 72 hours of coitus (rape/unprotected sex).
Long-Acting: Imparts continuous micro-dose contraception lasting up to 5 years.
PERMANENT / SURGICAL

Terminal Sterilization Methods

Vasectomy (Male) Small scrotal incision; small portion of each vas deferens cut and tied off; prevents sperm transport into semen.
Semen Post-Vasectomy Semen volume & hormones normal; ejaculate contains seminal fluid but strictly lacks spermatozoa.
Tubectomy (Female) Abdominal incision or laparoscopy; small section of both fallopian tubes cut and tied; blocks ovum transit.
Irreversible: Extremely high efficacy (>99.5%); surgical reversibility is extremely poor.
NTA CUET UG High-Yield Trap: Surgical sterilization halts gamete transit but does not affect endocrine hormone secretion, secondary sexual characters, or sexual libido. IUD insertion is executed strictly by doctors or trained medical professionals.
