Share Forfeiture Entry Anatomy & Premium Rules Capital Cancellation
Share Capital A/c (No. of shares × Called-up value) Dr.
Securities Premium A/c (If NOT received) Dr.
  To Calls-in-Arrears A/c (Total unpaid call dues)
  To Share Forfeiture A/c (Actual face value paid)
Case 1: Premium Already Received
NEVER debit Securities Premium. Sec 52(2) protects it. Do NOT include premium inside Share Forfeiture!
Case 2: Premium Unreceived
MUST debit Securities Premium A/c to reverse the uncollected charge created on allotment!
CUET Golden Rule: Share Capital is ALWAYS debited with CALLED-UP value, never full Face Value (unless fully called)!
Reissue Economics & Capital Reserve Formula Capital Gain Transfer
• Max Permissible Discount: Exactly equal to the amount forfeited per share.
• Min Reissue Price: Face Value minus Forfeited Amount per share.
• Reissue at Premium: Reissue price > Paid-up value; excess is credited to Securities Premium A/c.
Capital Reserve = [ (Total Forfeited Amt / Total Shares) × Reissued Shares ] − Reissue Discount
Partial Reissue Rule: If 1,000 shares forfeited and only 600 reissued, the capital reserve is calculated strictly on the 600 reissued shares. The forfeiture balance on the remaining 400 shares stays in Share Forfeiture Account!
Balance Sheet Disclosure: Unreissued Share Forfeiture is added to Subscribed Capital in Note 1.

Debentures as Collateral Security & Interest Mechanics Secondary Pledge
First Method (No Journal Entry)
No accounting entry is recorded in the books. Only a parenthetical note / footnote is added below the primary bank loan in Note to Accounts.
Second Method (With Journal Entry)
Record: Debenture Suspense A/c Dr. to ...% Debentures A/c Cr. Deduct Debenture Suspense from Debentures under Non-Current Liabilities.
• No Interest on Collateral: No interest is payable on debentures issued as collateral security until the company defaults on the primary debt!
• Debenture Interest: Charge against profit (paid semi-annually or annually) irrespective of profit or loss.
Disclosure: Premium on Redemption is shown under Other Long-Term Liabilities (Non-Current Liabilities).
