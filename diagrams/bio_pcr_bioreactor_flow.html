Step 3: Primer Extension (Taq Polymerase)

Thermostable Taq DNA polymerase (isolated from bacterium Thermus aquaticus) synthesizes complementary DNA strands in the 5' → 3' direction using free dNTPs and Mg2+.

Simple Stirred Tank Type A

Sparged Tank Type B

• Sterile air bubbles sparged from below.
• Dramatic increase in gas-liquid surface area.
• Maximizes dissolved oxygen for high cell density.

Optimal Bioreactor Control Systems

Vessels (100–1,000 L) equipped with automated control loops: temperature cooling jacket, pH monitoring probe, foam sensor & mechanical foam breaker, and sterile sampling ports for withdrawal of culture aliquots.

Downstream Processing Workflow

Following the biosynthetic stage, products undergo: (1) Cell separation via centrifugation/filtration; (2) Purification of the protein product; (3) Formulation with suitable preservatives; (4) Strict clinical trials (for drugs); and (5) Exhaustive Quality Control (QC) testing before commercial market release.

Separation + Purification + Preservatives

Taq Polymerase Source

Isolated from the thermophilic bacterium Thermus aquaticus inhabiting hydrothermal geysers. Remains enzymatically active and stable during repeated 95°C denaturation stages.

PCR Primer Requirements

Requires two sets of primers (forward and reverse oligonucleotides) complementary to the 3' termini of both template strands, plus all four deoxynucleoside triphosphates (dATP, dCTP, dGTP, dTTP) and Mg2+.

Upstream vs Downstream

Upstream: Strain selection, media optimization, fermentation in bioreactor.
Downstream: Separation, purification, quality control, and formulation. Downstream varies strictly from product to product.
