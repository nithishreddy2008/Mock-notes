AMINES
UNIT IX · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Structure, Class & Names

N is sp³ with a lone pair — pyramidal, ~108°; that lone pair IS the basicity story
1°/2°/3° amines (alkyl on N: one/two/three), plus quaternary (R₄N⁺X⁻, no lone pair, not basic)
Names: alkanamine / N-methylmethanamine / N,N-dimethyl... ; benzene ring: aniline (benzenamine)
Amide vs amine: CONH₂ (acid derivative, neutral) vs NH₂ (basic) — never confuse the two

Preparation Board

Alkylation: NH₃ + R–X gives 1° then 2° then 3° then quaternary (over-alkylation mess)
Reduction: nitro (Sn/HCl, Fe/HCl, H₂/Ni), nitriles (LiAlH₄), amides (LiAlH₄), oximes
Gabriel phthalimide: clean 1° aliphatic amines only (no 2°/3°, no aniline)
Hofmann bromamide: amide + Br₂/KOH → amine with ONE carbon fewer

Basicity Ladders (the famous ladders!)

Gas phase: 3° > 2° > 1° > NH₃ (alkyl push)
Aqueous 1°/2°/3°: 2° > 1° > 3° > NH₃ (solvation vs inductio­n tug-of-war)
Substituted anilines: p-toluidine > aniline > p-nitroaniline (donors up, EWG down)
All amines beat ammonia's ring cousins: aliphatic >> aniline > ... (lone pair asleep in the ring)

Chemistry of Amines

H-bonding: 1°/2° boil high (N–H donors); 3° has none — 3° boils lowest of the trio
Salt formation: amines dissolve in acid (C₂H₅NH₂ + HCl → salt) — the separation trick
Acylation/alkylation: acyl chloride + amine → amide (Hinsberg's playground)
Carbylamine test: 1° only + CHCl₃/KOH → foul isocyanide (2°/3° silent)
Hinsberg: benzenesulphonyl chloride sorts 1°/2°/3° by solubility

Reactions on the Ring

Aniline is a hyper-activator: bromine water → 2,4,6-tribromoaniline (white ppt) instantly
To control it: acylate first (acetanilide), react, deprotect — NH₂ tamed into NHCOCH₃
Nitration: needs protection too (oxidation + meta-mess otherwise); protected route gives p-nitroaniline
Sulphonation: sulphanilic acid — the zwitterion

Diazonium Salts & Uses

Diazotisation: aniline + NaNO₂/HCl at 273–278 K → benzenediazonium chloride
Seven exits: Sandmeyer (Cl/Br/CN), Balz-Schiemann (F), KI, H₂O (phenol), H₃PO₂ (H), coupling (azo dyes)
The logic: nitration → reduction → diazotisation → exit — the universal conversion route
Uses: azo dyes, sulpha drugs, quaternary salts as detergents, adrenalin/serotonin are amines

WATCH-OUTS THAT SET MCQ TRAPS

Basicity order flips between gas and water: memorise BOTH ladders and read which is asked
3° amines fail carbylamine and Hinsberg — no N–H at all
Gabriel cannot make aniline — aryl halides refuse S⁽2
Hofmann bromamide LOSES one carbon — the amide carbonyl leaves as carbonate
