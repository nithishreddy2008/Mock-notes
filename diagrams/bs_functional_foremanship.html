FUNCTIONAL FOREMANSHIP — one worker, eight specialist bosses (Taylor's technique)
PLANNING INCHARGE
PLANS THE WORK — DOES NOT EXECUTE
PRODUCTION INCHARGE
EXECUTES THE PLAN ON THE FLOOR
PLANNING FOREMEN
PRODUCTION FOREMEN
Route Clerk
Decides the route — the sequence of steps for each job
Instruction Card Clerk
Writes the instructions for each worker on cards
Time & Cost Clerk
Fixes time limits and cost targets for every job
Disciplinarian
Keeps order, rules and discipline in the factory
Speed Boss
Keeps the work moving at the promised speed
Gang Boss
Keeps machines, tools and materials ready
Repair Boss
Keeps machines and equipment in working order
Inspector
Checks the quality of everything produced
THE WORKER
receives day-to-day orders from all eight specialists
Why Taylor designed it: no single foreman can master every skill — so each boss gives orders only in his own speciality.
The trade-off: specialisation gained, but unity of command is violated — one worker answers to eight bosses. That tension is a favourite question.
