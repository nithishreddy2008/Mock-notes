Television Production Lifecycle & Studio PCR Operational Pipeline

End-to-end broadcast workflow: Pre-production planning, multi-camera studio floor, PCR switching & playout

CUET Mass Media · Lifecycle Architecture
1
Pre-Production Phase
1. Ideation & Research
Concept proposal, target audience analysis, legal clearance, talent casting & budget approval.
2. Two-Column Scripting
Split-page script format: Left Column (Video/Shots/Graphics) and Right Column (Audio/Dialogue/FX).
3. Storyboard & Floor Plan
Visual framing cards, lighting grid setup, camera placement arcs, sets, and call-sheet scheduling.
➔
2
Production: Floor & PCR
Studio Floor Operations
Floor Manager directs talent; 3-point lighting (Key, Fill, Back light); Teleprompter; Lavalier lapel audio mics.
PCR (Production Control Room)
Director calls camera cuts; Technical Director operates Vision Mixer (cuts, dissolves, wipes, keys); CCU adjusts video levels.
Intercom / Talkback System
Director, Floor Manager, Camera Operators, and Audio Engineer communicate via closed-circuit headsets.
➔
3
Post-Production Phase
1. Non-Linear Editing (NLE)
Rough cut to fine cut assembly, multi-camera sync, transition effects, timecode EDL conforming.
2. Sound Design & Dubbing
Foley sound effects, dialogue equalization, voice-over (VO) narration, and final master stereo/5.1 audio mix.
3. Graphics, VFX & Color Grading
Chroma-key matte extraction, lower-third supers, Aston tickers, color timing, broadcast safe-luminance check.
➔
4
Playout & Transmission
1. MCR (Master Control Room)
Ingest, broadcast schedule automation, commercial ad insertion, continuous signal QC compliance.
2. Distribution Uplink
Satellite transponder uplink (C-Band / Ku-Band), DTH multiplexing, HFC Cable networks & OTT live CDN.
3. Audience Metric (BARC)
Inaudible audio watermarking detected by BAR-O-meters in sample households to generate weekly TRP ratings.
Key Exam Invariant: The Vision Mixer lives in the PCR; the Master Control Room (MCR) handles transmission playout; BARC uses audio watermarking for TRPs.
