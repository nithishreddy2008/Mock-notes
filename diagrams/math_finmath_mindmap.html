FINANCIAL MATHEMATICS
CUET UG MATHEMATICS

Perpetuity & Sinking Fund

Perpetuity: endless payment A → price A/i
Sinking fund: equal deposits to hit a future target
Fund ≠ savings account: locked to one liability (debt, machinery)
Same engine: annuity accumulation, reversed

EMI

One flat payment covering interest + principal
Reducing balance: interest on what remains
i per period: 12% p.a. → 1% per month
Early EMIs pay mostly interest — the amortisation fact

Returns & Nominal Rate

RoR = gain ÷ cost × 100, gain includes dividends
Nominal rate: the stated headline rate
Before inflation and before compounding effects
Compare honestly: same period, same basis

CAGR vs AGR

CAGR = (End/Start)^(1/n) − 1 — one smoothed rate
AGR: simple average of yearly % changes
Volatility gap: +50%, −50% → AGR 0%, CAGR −29.3%
n = years of growth between the endpoints

Linear Depreciation

D = (Cost − Residual) ÷ Life — equal slices
Book value: cost minus accumulated depreciation
Schedule habit: one table row per year
Never depreciate below the residual value

Bond Valuation

V = coupons' PV + face value's PV at the MARKET rate
Coupon is fixed; market rate moves — hence prices move
Market > coupon → discount below face
Market < coupon → premium above face
