Themes in Indian History Part II · Chapter 7
The Spatial Organization of Vijayanagara: Three Concentric Urban Zones
Structural layer-stack mapping the Sacred Centre, the Royal Centre, and the Urban Core across Hampi’s topography
OurMocks Study Visuals CUET UG History · Vijayanagara Zones
Zone 1 · The Sacred Centre (Northern Riverfront & Rocky Hills)
Gods, Rituals & Dynastic Legitimacy
Virupaksha Temple Complex
Ancient shrine of Shiva (Virupaksha), guardian deity of the kingdom. Krishnadeva Raya added the grand Eastern Gopuram and pillared central hall in 1509 CE.
Vitthala Temple & Stone Chariot
Dedicated to Vishnu (Vitthala) from Maharashtra; features an iconic monolithic Stone Chariot sanctum and 56 musical pillars (*sa-re-ga-ma* acoustic resonance).
Raya Gopurams & Bazaar Streets
Colossal monumental gateway towers (*Raya Gopurams*) visible from miles away; broad paved temple avenues served as bustling diamond and spice markets.
Zone 2 · The Royal Centre (Southwestern Administrative & Imperial Enclave)
Power, Ceremonies & Statecraft
Mahanavami Dibba & King's Hall
Massive 40-ft high stone ceremonial platform (11,000 sq ft) for the 10-day Navaratri festival; military reviews, animal sacrifices, wrestler bouts, and tribute presentation.
Lotus Mahal & Council Pavilion
Exquisite Indo-Islamic pavilion named by British officers; features cusped arches and multi-tiered vaults, likely used as an executive council chamber for confidential statecraft.
Hazara Rama & Elephant Stables
Hazara Rama was reserved exclusively for the royal family with sculpted Ramayana scenes; adjacent grand arched stables housed eleven royal state elephants.
Zone 3 · The Urban Core & Fortified Agricultural Valleys (Outer Perimeter)
Civilian Habitats, Guilds & Hydraulic Defense
Seven Concentric Fort Walls
Persian envoy Abdur Razzaq noted seven concentric rings of walls constructed from dry interlocking granite blocks, enclosing agricultural fields, gardens, and forests.
Hiriya Canal & Kamalapuram Tank
Dam across Tungabhadra diverted water via the Hiriya Canal to cultivate the river valley; Kamalapuram tank piped fresh water directly to royal complexes.
Residential Quarters & Shards
Excavations revealed fine Chinese porcelain in affluent merchant sectors, tombs and mosques in Muslim quarters, and simple mud-thatch homes of peasant artisans.
Key Architectural Insight: Vijayanagara rulers styled themselves Hindu Suratrana ("Sultans among Hindu Kings"), synthesizing indigenous Dravidian and Indo-Islamic styles.
