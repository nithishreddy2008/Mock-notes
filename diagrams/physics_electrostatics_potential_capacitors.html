1. Electrostatic Potential

Scalar Energy
Potential & Potential Difference Work / Charge
V_B - V_A = -∫_A^B E · dr = W_ext / q₀

Scalar Nature: 1 Volt = 1 Joule / Coulomb. Independent of path (conservative field, ∮ E · dr = 0).

Point Charge & Dipole Potential Radial Decay

Point Charge: V(r) = [1 / (4πε₀)] · [q / r] (retains sign of q; falls as 1/r).

Short Dipole: V(r, θ) = [1 / (4πε₀)] · [p cos θ / r²].

Equatorial Plane (θ = 90°): V = 0 everywhere, but E ≠ 0! Potential decays as 1/r².
Potential Gradient & Field Gradient Vector
E = -dV/dr ⟹ E = -(∂V/∂x î + ∂V/∂y ĵ + ∂V/∂z k̂)

Directional Rule: Electric field points strictly in direction of maximum potential decrease.

2. Equipotential & System Energy

Surfaces & Energy
Equipotential Surfaces Zero Work

Core Properties: Potential is identical at all points (ΔV = 0 ⟹ W = q·ΔV = 0).

Orthogonality: Electric field lines are always mutually perpendicular (normal) to equipotential surfaces.

Surface Geometries: Point charge: concentric spheres; Line charge: coaxial cylinders; Uniform field: parallel equidistant planes.
System Potential Energy (U) Assembly Work
U = [1 / (4πε₀)] · [q₁ q₂ / r₁₂]

Multi-Charge System: Total energy is algebraic sum over all distinct pairs: U_total = Σ_(i<j) [k q_i q_j / r_ij].

Like Charges (q₁q₂ > 0): U > 0 (repulsive work); Unlike Charges (q₁q₂ < 0): U < 0 (bound state).

Dipole in Electrostatic Field Orientational U

Potential Energy: U = -p · E = -p E cos θ.

Rotation Work: Work to rotate dipole from θ₁ to θ₂: W = p E (cos θ₁ - cos θ₂).

3. Dielectrics & Polarization

Material Response
Polar vs Non-Polar Dielectrics Molecular Dipoles

Non-Polar: Centers of + and - charges coincide (H₂, O₂, CO₂). Permanent dipole moment = 0. Acquire induced dipole in E.

Polar: Centers are separated (H₂O, HCl, NH₃). Possess permanent dipoles; torque aligns them against thermal agitation.

Polarization Vector & Bound Charge P = χ_e ε₀ E
E_net = E₀ - E_p = E₀ / K

Polarization (P): Dipole moment per unit volume [C/m²]. Induces bound surface charge σ_b = P · n̂.

Dielectric Constant (K): K = ε_r = 1 + χ_e. Internal field is reduced by factor K, where K ≥ 1 (K = ∞ for metals).
Dielectric Strength & Breakdown Field Limits

Definition: Maximum electric field a dielectric withstands without ionization breakdown (Air: ~3 × 10⁶ V/m).

4. Capacitors & Energy Dynamics

Storage Networks
Parallel Plate Capacitor C = Q / V

Vacuum: C₀ = ε₀ A / d. Isolated spherical conductor: C = 4πε₀ R.

Dielectric Slab (thickness t): C = ε₀ A / [d - t(1 - 1/K)].

Conducting Slab (t): K = ∞ ⟹ C = ε₀ A / (d - t).

Series & Parallel Combinations Network Rules

Series (Equal Q): 1/C_s = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... (C_s < smallest C).

Parallel (Equal V): C_p = C₁ + C₂ + ... (C_p > largest C).

Energy Stored & Density U = ½ C V²
U = ½ C V² = Q² / (2C) = ½ Q V

Electrostatic Energy Density: u = ½ ε₀ E² [J/m³].

Dielectric Insertion Impact:
• Battery Disconnected (Q const): C ↑ K, V ↓ V₀/K, U ↓ U₀/K.
• Battery Connected (V const): C ↑ K, Q ↑ KQ₀, U ↑ KU₀.
