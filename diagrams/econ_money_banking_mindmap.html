PILLAR 1
Money & Money Supply
Barter & Functions of Money
• Primary: Medium of Exchange & Unit of Account (Measure of Value).
• Secondary: Store of Value & Standard of Deferred Payment.
• Eliminates double coincidence of wants.
Measures of Money Supply
Stock of money held by public at a point in time:
M1 = C + DD + OD (Most liquid)
M2 = M1 + PO Savings
M3 = M1 + Net Time Dep.
M4 = M3 + PO Tot. Dep. (excl. NSC)
High Powered Money (Base)
Monetary Base H = C + R.
C = Currency with public, R = Cash reserves of banks with RBI + vault cash.
PILLAR 2
Commercial Banking
Two Core Functions
• Accepting Deposits: Demand (Current, Savings) vs Time (Fixed, Recurring).
• Advancing Loans: Cash Credit, Overdraft, Term Loans, Discounting Bills.
Credit Creation Engine
Banks expand primary deposits into secondary (derivative) credit:
Multiplier k = 1 / LRR
Total Dep = Primary Dep × (1/LRR)
LRR = Legal Reserve Ratio (CRR + SLR). Higher LRR cuts credit creation.
Crucial Distinctions
• A single bank lends only its excess reserves.
• The entire banking system multiplies credit.
• Cash drain & excess reserves act as leakages.
PILLAR 3
The Central Bank (RBI)
Apex Institution Profile
Established April 1, 1935 (RBI Act 1934); nationalized Jan 1, 1949. Sole regulator of currency & credit.
Six Cardinal Functions
1. Bank of Issue: Sole monopoly on currency notes (except ₹1 & coins by MoF).
2. Banker to Government: Manages state accounts, public debt, advances.
3. Bankers' Bank: Custodian of cash reserves & clearing house.
4. Lender of Last Resort: Emergency liquidity to solvent banks in distress.
5. Custodian of Forex: Manages gold & foreign exchange reserves.
6. Controller of Credit: Steers economy's money supply.
PILLAR 4
Monetary Control & Policy
Quantitative Instruments
• Repo / Bank Rate: Cost of RBI lending.
• Reverse Repo / SDF: Drains liquidity.
• CRR & SLR: Minimum statutory reserve requirements.
• OMOs: Sale vs Purchase of G-Secs.
Qualitative Instruments
• Margin Requirement: Collateral haircut.
• Credit Rationing: Sector quotas.
• Moral Suasion & Direct Action: Persuasion & sanctions.
Demonetisation (8 Nov 2016)
₹500 & ₹1000 notes ceased legal tender. Aims: curb black money, terror funding, counterfeit currency; promote digital economy & tax compliance.
Alert 1: What is NOT Money Supply
Vault cash kept by commercial banks and cash balances of Central/State Governments are strictly excluded from money supply.
Alert 2: Multiplier vs LRR Direction
Money multiplier k has an inverse relationship with LRR. Doubling LRR cuts money creation in half.
Alert 3: Repo vs Reverse Repo Doors
Repo = RBI lends to banks (injects liquidity). Reverse Repo = RBI borrows from banks (absorbs liquidity).
