REDRESSAL MACHINERY UNDER THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019 — three tiers, one ladder
NATIONAL COMMISSION
claims above ₹2 crore
sits at New Delhi · appeal to the Supreme Court
STATE COMMISSION
claims of ₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore
sits in each state capital · appeal to the National Commission
DISTRICT COMMISSION
claims up to ₹50 lakh
the entry door for most complaints · appeal to the State Commission

Filing the complaint

Who can file: the consumer, a registered consumer organisation, the Central or State Government, or consumers on behalf of many
Time limit: within 2 years of the cause of action
Relief sought: state the defect, the loss, and the remedy you ask for

The ladder rule

Complaint goes to the tier matched to the claim's value
Unhappy with a tier's order? Appeal upward — District $arrow$ State $arrow$ National $arrow$ Supreme Court
Limits revised in 2021 — ₹50 lakh and ₹2 crore are the two walls
Exam anchor: value-of-claim questions and appeal-route questions come straight from this ladder — memorise the two money walls and the order of appeal, and every machinery question falls out.

Remedies the commissions can order

Remove the defects Replace the goods Refund the price paid Pay compensation for loss Punitive damages Discontinue unfair / restrictive practice Cease manufacture of hazardous goods Withdraw hazardous goods from sale Issue corrective advertisement Provide adequate costs to the complainant
