Unit 6 Core Architecture

1. Data Taxonomy & Lifecycle

  • Structured: Tabular relational model with fixed rigid schema (RDBMS, CSV, SQL tables, DataFrames).
  • Semi-Structured: Self-describing schema using hierarchical tags or key-value trees (JSON, XML, YAML, NoSQL).
  • Unstructured: Lacks predefined model; binary or freeform (audio, images, video, text documents, sensor logs).
  • Processing Pipeline: Ingestion → Storage (Lake/Warehouse) → Sanitization → Transformation → Descriptive Analytics.

2. Measures of Central Tendency

  • Arithmetic Mean: $\bar{X} = \sum X_i / N$. Sensitive to extreme values; pulled toward the long tail.
  • Median: Middle value in sorted series (Rank $\frac{N+1}{2}$ for odd, avg of $\frac{N}{2}$ and $\frac{N}{2}+1$ for even). Robust to outliers.
  • Mode: Most frequently occurring value. Can be unimodal, bimodal, multimodal, or non-existent. Sole central metric for categorical data.
  • Skewness Order: Right (Positive): $\text{Mean} > \text{Median} > \text{Mode}$. Left (Negative): $\text{Mean} < \text{Median} < \text{Mode}$.

3. Measures of Dispersion & Spread

  • Range: $\text{Max} - \text{Min}$. Extreme sensitivity to outliers; ignores intermediate distribution.
  • Quartiles & IQR: $Q_1$ (25th), $Q_2$ (50th, Median), $Q_3$ (75th). $\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1$. Represents middle 50% spread.
  • Variance & Std Dev: $\sigma^2 = \sum (X_i - \mu)^2 / N$; $\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2}$. Measures average dispersion from mean.
  • Coeff. of Variation: $\text{CV} = (\sigma / \mu) \times 100\%$. Dimensionless metric comparing variability across different scales.

4. Normal Distribution & Outliers

  • Gaussian Normal Curve: Symmetric, bell-shaped ($\text{Mean} = \text{Median} = \text{Mode}$). Total area $= 1.0$.
  • Empirical Rule: $68.27\%$ within $\mu \pm 1\sigma$; $95.45\%$ within $\mu \pm 2\sigma$; $99.73\%$ within $\mu \pm 3\sigma$.
  • Tukey's Outlier Fences: Lower: $Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$; Upper: $Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$. Extreme outliers: $3.0 \times \text{IQR}$.
  • Data Sanitization: Drop duplicates (`drop_duplicates`), impute missing numericals with Median (skewed) or Mean (symmetric), Mode for categoricals.
