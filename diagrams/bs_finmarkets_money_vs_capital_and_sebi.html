5 Money Market Instruments (NCERT)

1. Treasury Bills (T-Bills)

14 to 364 Days
Issued by RBI on behalf of Central Govt. Also called Zero Coupon Bonds. Issued at discount and redeemed at par. Min lot: ₹25,000. Zero default risk.

2. Commercial Paper (CP)

15 Days to 1 Year
Unsecured promissory note issued by highly rated corporates. Used for seasonal working capital & Bridge Financing (meeting floatation costs of equity).

3. Call Money

1 to 15 Days
Short-term inter-bank borrowing utilized by commercial banks to maintain mandatory Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) with RBI. Highly volatile call rates.

4. Certificate of Deposit (CD)

91 Days to 1 Year
Unsecured negotiable bearer instruments issued by commercial banks and DFIs to mobilize large institutional deposits during periods of tight liquidity.

5. Commercial Bill (Trade Bill)

Up to 90 Days
Bill of exchange drawn by a seller on buyer to finance credit sales of goods. Accepted by buyer; can be discounted with a commercial bank before maturity.
Stock Exchange Trading Sequence
1

Select Broker & Client Registration

Investor selects SEBI-registered broker. Mandatorily submits PAN card, bank details, and opens a Demat Account with a Depository Participant (DP).

2

Placement of Order

Investor instructs broker specifying security name, quantity, and target limit price (or market price). Broker generates an order confirmation slip.

3

Order Execution on Automated Terminal

Electronic trading terminal automatically matches matching buy and sell orders. Trade is executed; broker sends trade confirmation slip.

4

Issue of Contract Note

Issued by broker within 24 hours of execution. Contains Unique Client Code (UCC), transaction details, and statutory brokerage charges.

5

Settlement (Rolling T+1)

Pay-in occurs when cash/shares are delivered before T+1. Pay-out releases funds/shares on T+1 day directly to investor's Demat / Bank account.

SEBI Functions & Depositories
● 1. Protective Functions
  • Prohibiting Price Rigging (manipulating stock prices).
  • Prohibiting Insider Trading using non-public price-sensitive info.
  • Promoting fair practices and issuing investor protection guidelines.
● 2. Regulatory Functions
  • Registration of stock brokers, sub-brokers, and merchant bankers.
  • Registration & regulation of Collective Investment Schemes / Mutual Funds.
  • Conducting inquiries, audits, and inspections of stock exchanges.
● 3. Development Functions
  • Training intermediaries and promoting investor education campaigns.
  • Conducting research and publishing market survey data.
  • Facilitating electronic screen trading and internet broking channels.
Depository Architecture: NSDL (NSE, IDBI promoted) & CDSL (BSE, SBI promoted). Investors interact solely via Depository Participants (DPs). Demat converts physical certificates into electronic ledger balances.
CUET GOLDEN RULES: Commercial Paper (CP) funds Bridge Financing; Call Money maintains CRR; T-Bills are Zero Coupon Bonds issued at discount; Primary Market creates securities; Secondary Market provides liquidity.
