What Happens When a New Partner Joins
The admission journey — from consent to the new profit-sharing ratio
1

New partner admitted

With the consent of all partners the old agreement ends and a new agreement begins.

2

Old PSR surrendered

Old partners agree on a new ratio; each old share is compared with the new share.

3

Sacrificing ratio found

Sacrifice = old share − new share, partner by partner. Gainers pick up the slack.

4

Goodwill + revaluation + reserves

Premium is credited to old partners in the sacrificing ratio; revaluation profit and reserves also go to old partners in the old ratio.

5

Capitals adjusted

New capital is fixed as agreed — cash brought in or balances equalised through current accounts.

6

New PSR begins

From the admission date, profit is shared in the new ratio — the firm runs on fresh terms.
