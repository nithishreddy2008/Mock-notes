Ratio Mechanics Sacrifice vs Gain
Sacrificing Ratio: Old Share - New Share
Gaining Ratio: New Share - Old Share
New Share: Old Share - Sacrifice OR Old Share + Gain
  • Negative Result Sign: A negative sacrifice ($Old < New$) indicates a *gain*; a negative gain ($New < Old$) indicates a *sacrifice*!
  • Admission Default: If new partner acquires $1/k$ share and question is silent, old partners sacrifice in their *Old Profit-Sharing Ratio*.
  • Retirement Default: If partner retires and question is silent, continuing partners gain in their *Old Mutual Ratio*.
Admission: Sacrificing Ratio Retirement: Gaining Ratio
Revaluation Account P&L Adjustment
Profit on Reval: Cr Side > Dr Side ➔ Credit Old Partners (Old PSR)
Loss on Reval: Dr Side > Cr Side ➔ Debit Old Partners (Old PSR)
New Partner Share: Strictly NIL (No share in past revaluation)
  • Nominal Principle: Revaluation Account is a nominal account recording unearned past value changes prior to reconstitution.
  • Old Ratio Invariance: Both profit and loss on revaluation are transferred strictly to Old Partners in their Old PSR.
  • Retirement/Death Scope: On retirement/death, *all partners* (including the retiring/deceased partner) share revaluation profit/loss in old PSR.
Old Assets ➔ Old Partners Old Ratio Mandatory
Reserves & Specific Funds Balance Sheet Purge
Accumulated Losses (P&L Dr): Debit Old Partners in Old PSR
IFR Fall in Value: Deducted from IFR; excess to partners; shortfall to Reval
Advertisement Suspense: Fictitious asset written off to Old Partners
  • Past Earnings Purity: Accumulated reserves belong to past efforts; they must be cleared before admitting new partners.
  • WCR Claim Priority: Always fulfill the actual liability for compensation first; only the uncommitted surplus is shared.
  • Fictitious Asset Write-off: Deferred revenue expenditure (Ad Suspense) appearing on Asset side must be debited to Old Partners in Old PSR.
