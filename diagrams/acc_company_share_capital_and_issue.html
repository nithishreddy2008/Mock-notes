Share Capital Architecture & Issue Mechanics

Classification hierarchy, Section 52(2) securities premium applications, Table F rules, and pro-rata workflows

UNIT IV · SHARES & DEBENTURES
Share Capital Classification Hierarchy (Schedule III) Statutory Structure
1. Authorised Capital Max nominal capital stated in Memorandum of Association (MOA Cl. V).
2. Issued Capital Portion of authorised capital offered to public for subscription.
3. Subscribed Capital Shares applied for and allotted by the company.
A. Subscribed & Fully Paid Entire face value called up AND received in cash/consideration.
B. Subscribed Not Fully Paid Company hasn't called full face value OR calls-in-arrears exist.
CUET Trap: Reserve Capital (Sec 99) can ONLY be called up upon winding up; it is NEVER shown in the Balance Sheet!
Section 52(2) Permissible Uses of Securities Premium Strict Statutory List
1
Issue Fully Paid Bonus Shares: Capitalised to provide free bonus shares to existing equity shareholders.
2
Write off Preliminary Expenses: Formation and promotional expenses incurred before incorporation.
3
Write off Issue Expenses / Discount: Underwriting commission, brokerage, discount on issue of debentures.
4
Premium on Redemption: Provide for premium payable on redemption of redeemable preference shares or debentures.
5
Buyback of Own Shares (Sec 68): Utilised for purchase of own shares or specified securities.
Zero Dividends: Securities Premium CANNOT be distributed as cash dividend under ANY circumstances!
Table F Statutory Provisions: Arrears vs Advance Companies Act 2013
Calls-in-Arrears (Unpaid Dues)
• Table F Max Interest: Up to 10% p.a. from due date to actual payment date.
• Accounting: Kept in separate account; shown as deduction from Subscribed Capital.
• Dividend Effect: Shareholder loses dividend on unpaid portion unless Articles permit.
• Board Discretion: Directors have right to waive interest on calls-in-arrears.
Calls-in-Advance (Prepaid Dues)
• Table F Max Interest: Up to 12% p.a. from deposit date to call due date.
• Accounting: Shown under Other Current Liabilities in Balance Sheet.
• No Voting Rights: No voting rights on advance money until call becomes due.
• Winding Up Priority: Repaid prior to share capital, but after external debt.
Rule: Interest on Calls-in-Advance is a charge against profits — payable even if company earns no profit!
Pro-Rata Over-Subscription Settlement Protocol Allotment Flow
Step 1
Application Pool
Total apps received × App money per share = Total Bank Dr.
Step 2
Share Capital Cr.
Shares allotted × App money per share transferred to capital.
Step 3
Allotment Offset
Surplus app money adjusted against Allotment Due on pro-rata shares.
Step 4
Call Advance / Refund
Remaining surplus credited to Calls-in-Advance (if permitted) or Refunded.
Default Rule: Unless stated otherwise in the question, surplus application money is adjusted only up to Allotment. Any remaining balance is promptly refunded via Bank!
Section 39 Minimum Subscription: 90% of issued capital must be subscribed within 30 days, else entire sum refunded in 15 days!
