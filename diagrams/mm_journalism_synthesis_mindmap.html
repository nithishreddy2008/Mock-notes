Fig 3: Master Synthesis Mindmap — Journalism, News Values & Editorial Ethics

Comprehensive synoptic map covering News Values, Writing Architecture, Professional Ethics, and Legal Frameworks

Master Mindmap · CUET UG 318
📰 1. Journalism & The Fourth Estate
  • Democratic Mandate: The Fourth Estate acting as an adversarial watchdog holding public power accountable.
  • Qualities of a Journalist: "Nose for News", persistent curiosity, linguistic clarity, empathy, skepticism.
  • Verification Principle: "If your mother says she loves you, check it out" — on-record attribution.
  • Objectivity vs Neutrality: Impartial reporting of factual evidence without taking false moral equivalence.
🎯 2. News Values & Determinants
  • Impact / Consequence: Scale and gravity of citizens affected by the reported event.
  • Timeliness & Currency: Immediacy of breaking events; relevance to prevailing public discourse.
  • Proximity: Geographical distance and cultural relevance to local readership.
  • Prominence & Conflict: Public figures, political controversy, institutional struggles, and human interest.
📐 3. Inverted Pyramid & Story Anatomy
  • The Lead (Lede): 5 Ws & 1 H (Who, What, When, Where, Why, How) encapsulated in the opening 30 words.
  • Nut Graf: The paragraph establishing context, background, and the broader societal significance.
  • Body Copy: Supporting evidence, quotes, and secondary facts in strict descending order of importance.
  • Headline & Byline: Snappy active headline, author credit, and dateline indicating location of origin.
✍️ 4. Journalistic Forms & Genres
  • Hard News: Time-sensitive, factual, objective, breaking coverage (accidents, parliamentary votes).
  • Soft News & Features: Evergreen, narrative, emotional, lifestyle, and character profiles.
  • Investigative Reporting: Long-term, in-depth unearthing of concealed public corruption or crime.
  • Opinion & Editorial: Editorials (institutional voice of paper), Op-Eds, signed columns, and letters.
⚠️ 5. Ethical Breaches & Pitfalls
  • Paid News: Covert editorial coverage bought with commercial or political cash payments.
  • Sensationalism / Yellow Journalism: Tabloid hysteria, melodramatic speculation, and media trials.
  • Disinformation Triad: Misinformation (false/no harm), Disinformation (false/harm), Malinformation (true/leaked to harm).
  • Sting Operations: Entrapment hazards; legally permissible only as last resort for grave public interest.
⚖️ 6. Indian Press Law & Regulation
  • Article 19(1)(a): Fundamental right to freedom of speech and expression (incorporates press freedom).
  • Article 19(2) Restrictions: Sovereignty, security of state, public order, decency, defamation, contempt.
  • Defamation Law: IPC Section 499/500 criminal defamation & civil torts; truth exception.
  • Regulatory Bodies: Press Council of India (PCI - statutory quasi-judicial) & NBDSA (self-regulatory TV body).
