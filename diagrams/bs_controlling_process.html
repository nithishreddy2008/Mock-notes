THE CONTROL PROCESS — four steps that never stop circling
1

Setting performance standards

Fix the benchmarks — targets in quantity, cost, time, quality — usually grown out of the plan. Standards can be quantitative (10,000 units) or qualitative (courtesy scores).
2

Measuring actual performance

Collect real results — samples, reports, inspections — accurately and on time. Good measurement uses objective, reliable data.
3

Comparing with standards

Put actual beside standard and find the deviations — which targets were met, which missed, and by how much.
4

Analysing deviations & taking corrective action

Focus on critical deviations (key result areas, big exceptions) and act — fix the process, revise the standard, or retrain the team.
THE LOOP
Corrective action taken today becomes the improved process — and next period's new standards come from the plan again
Two exam-favourite ideas inside step 4: Critical Point Control — watch the key result areas, not everything equally; Management by Exception — small deviations are tolerated, only significant ones climb to the top.
