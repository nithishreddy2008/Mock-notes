Electromagnetic Induction & AC
Time-varying magnetic flux, inductive reactance, phasor analysis & power grids
1. Faraday's Laws & Magnetic Flux
Flux Dynamics
Φ = B·A·cos θ | e = -N(dΦ/dt) | q = ΔΦ/R
  • Magnetic Flux: Maximum when B ⊥ surface (θ = 0°); zero when B ∥ surface.
  • Induced EMF: Proportional to time-rate of change of magnetic flux linkage.
  • Induced Charge: q = ΔΦ/R depends ONLY on net flux change, independent of time!
2. Lenz's Law & Motional EMF
Energy Conservation
e = B·v·L | e = ½·B·ω·L² (Rotating Rod)
  • Lenz's Law: Direction of induced current opposes the cause producing it; consequence of conservation of energy.
  • Translational Motional EMF: Rod moving with speed v across field B creates potential difference e = B v L.
  • Eddy Currents: Circulating currents in bulk conductors; minimized via slotted/laminated cores.
3. Self & Mutual Induction
Inductive Inertia
L = μ₀·n²·A·l | M = μ₀·n₁·n₂·A·l | U = ½·L·I²
  • Self Inductance (L): Inertia of electricity opposing current growth/decay; e = -L(dI/dt).
  • Mutual Inductance (M): e₂ = -M(dI₁/dt); Coupling coefficient k = M/√(L₁·L₂).
  • Magnetic Energy Density: uB = B² / (2μ₀) stored inside magnetic field volume.
4. AC Generator & RMS Values
AC Generation
e = NBAω·sin(ωt) | Irms = I₀/√2 | Vrms = V₀/√2
  • AC Alternator: Mechanical rotation converts flux variation into sinusoidal EMF e₀ = NBAω.
  • RMS (Effective) Value: Produces identical Joule heat as equivalent DC in same time.
  • Mean Value (Half Cycle): I_avg = (2/π)·I₀ ≈ 0.637 I₀; zero over full cycle.
5. Series LCR & Resonance
Circuit Dynamics
Z = √(R² + (XL-XC)²) | ωr = 1/√(LC) | Q = (1/R)√(L/C)
  • Impedance: XL = ωL (leads current by 90°), XC = 1/(ωC) (lags current by 90°).
  • Resonance (XL = XC): Z_min = R, current reaches peak I₀ = V₀/R; power factor cos ϕ = 1.
  • Q-Factor: Sharpness of tuning curve; Q = ωr / Δω = (1/R)·√(L/C).
6. AC Power & Transformers
Power Distribution
P_avg = Vrms·Irms·cos ϕ | Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = Ip/Is = k
  • Power Factor: cos ϕ = R/Z; Pure L or C has cos ϕ = 0 (Wattless current).
  • Step-Up / Step-Down: k > 1 steps voltage up (Is decreases); k < 1 steps voltage down.
  • Losses in Transformer: Copper (I²R), Iron/Eddy (lamination reduces), Hysteresis (soft iron), Flux leakage.
