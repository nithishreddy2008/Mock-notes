The Making of the Indian Constitution: Five Historic Milestones

The journey from the Cabinet Mission plan to the inauguration of the Sovereign Democratic Republic

CUET UG History · Process Timeline
Stage 1 Dec 1946

Inaugural Session & Objectives Resolution

  • 9 Dec 1946: First meeting of Constituent Assembly; Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha interim President.
  • 11 Dec 1946: Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected permanent President.
  • 13 Dec 1946: Jawaharlal Nehru moved historic Objectives Resolution; adopted unanimously 22 Jan 1947.
Pillar: Defined India as an "Independent Sovereign Republic" with justice, equality, and minority safeguards.
Stage 2 Aug 1947

Partition & Drafting Committee

  • 14–15 Aug 1947: Partition reduced Assembly membership from 389 to 299 members.
  • 29 Aug 1947: Drafting Committee appointed with Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as Chairman.
  • Civil servants Sir B.N. Rau (Advisor) and S.N. Mukherjee (Chief Draftsman) prepared foundational text.
Pillar: Ambedkar's legal brilliance provided the structural framework of constitutional morality.
Stage 3 Feb – Oct 1948

Draft Publication & Public Consultation

  • Feb 1948: First Draft of Constitution published by Drafting Committee.
  • Subjected to 8 months of open public scrutiny, bar associations, and press criticism.
  • Special Committee refined provisions on citizenship, language, and emergency powers.
Pillar: Embodied deliberative democracy with extensive grassroots feedback.
Stage 4 1948 – 1949

Clause-by-Clause Readings & Debates

  • Assembly sat for 11 sessions over 165 days across 2 years, 11 months, 18 days.
  • Out of 7,635 amendments tabled by members, 2,473 were vigorously debated.
  • Intense deliberation on Untouchability (Art 17), Federal Lists, and Munshi-Ayyangar language formula.
Pillar: Compromises negotiated by the triumvirate: Nehru, Patel, and Prasad.
Stage 5 Nov 1949 – Jan 1950

Final Adoption & Republic Enforcement

  • 26 Nov 1949: Final Constitution adopted; signed by 284 members (Constitution Day / Samvidhan Diwas).
  • 24 Jan 1950: Final session; signed text; "Jana Gana Mana" adopted as National Anthem.
  • 26 Jan 1950: Constitution fully came into effect, commemorating the 1930 Purna Swaraj Day.
Pillar: Transformed India into the world's largest constitutional republic with universal adult franchise.
Key Exam Statistic: Exactly 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days elapsed between the first sitting (9 Dec 1946) and final adoption (26 Nov 1949).
