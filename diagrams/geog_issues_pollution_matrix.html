India: Environmental Pollution Typology & Ecological Impact Matrix

Source Classifications, Contaminant Chemistry, Biological Vectors & National Remediation Frameworks

NCERT Class XII Geography · Unit 9

1. Water Pollution

Aquatic Crisis
Point vs Non-Point Sources Emission Vectors

Point Sources: Specific identifiable discharge conduits (industrial effluent pipes, municipal sewage outfalls).

Non-Point Sources: Diffuse agricultural runoff (fertilizers, pesticides) and surface urban storm runoff without single entry.

Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) Oxygen Depletion

Mechanism: Organic waste decomposition exhausts Dissolved Oxygen (DO), triggering anaerobic conditions and massive fish mortality.

Key Metrics: High BOD signifies severe organic pollution; pristine river water maintains BOD under 2 mg/L.
Critical River Reaches Severe Degradation

Ganga Hotspots: Kannauj to Varanasi stretch (Kanpur tanneries, untreated civic sewage, toxic chromium).

Yamuna Hotspot: Delhi (Wazirabad to Okhla) — receives 70% of city's untreated wastewater; virtually zero DO in dry season.

Remediation Initiatives Namami Gange

Evolution: Ganga Action Plan (GAP, 1985) expanded into Namami Gange integrated mission (2014) for sewage treatment, river-front development, and afforestation.

2. Air Pollution

Atmospheric Toxicity
Combustion & Industrial Sources Primary Vectors

Thermal Power Plants: Coal combustion releases fly ash, sulfur dioxide (SO₂), and nitrogen oxides (NOₓ).

Vehicular Emissions: High-density urban traffic produces carbon monoxide (CO), lead, and unburnt hydrocarbons.

Particulate Matter (PM) Inhalable Aerosols

PM2.5 & PM10: Microscopic particles penetrating deep into bronchial passages and alveoli.

Health Toll: Chronic bronchitis, asthma, emphysema, reduced lung capacity, and cardiovascular mortality.
Urban Smog & Acid Rain Atmospheric Hazards

Winter Smog: Smoke + fog inversion in Indo-Gangetic plains aggravated by post-harvest agricultural stubble burning in Punjab & Haryana.

Acid Rain: SO₂ and NO₂ reacting with moisture to form sulfuric and nitric acids, corroding monuments and acidifying soils.

Clean Air Interventions NCAP & Standards

Measures: National Clean Air Programme (NCAP), Bharat Stage VI (BS-VI) fuel norms, CNG public transit, and solar energy substitution.

3. Noise Pollution

Acoustic Stress
Acoustic Thresholds (Decibels) Measurement Unit

Decibel (dB): Logarithmic scale of sound pressure level. Permissible residential day limit: 55 dB; commercial: 65 dB; industrial: 75 dB.

Hazard Threshold: Continuous exposure above 85 dB induces permanent hearing loss and acoustic trauma.
Major Urban Sound Sources Noise Vectors

Transportation: Heavy vehicular horns, express railways, aircraft jet engines during landing and take-off near metropolitan airports.

Industrial & Civic: Construction machinery, pneumatic drills, diesel generators, loudspeakers at socio-religious gatherings.

Physiological & Psychological Toll Health Impact

Somatic Stress: Hypertension, elevated heart rate, constriction of blood vessels, and chronic sleep deprivation (insomnia).

Mental Fatigue: Anxiety, reduced cognitive focus, behavioral irritability, and impaired scholastic performance in children.

Regulatory Framework Noise Rules 2000

Statutory Zoning: Demarcation of "Silence Zones" within 100 metres of hospitals, educational institutions, and courts.

4. Soil & Land Degradation

Edaphic Decline
Agrochemical Toxicity Excessive Inputs

Intensive Agriculture: Over-application of chemical nitrogenous fertilizers (urea) and organophosphate pesticides disrupts soil biomes.

Biomagnification: Persistent chemical toxics enter food chains, concentrating in higher trophic levels and human milk.
Salinization & Alkalinization Canal Over-Irrigation

Mechanism: Excessive canal irrigation in arid/semi-arid plains (Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan) creates waterlogging and capillary action.

Result: Dissolved mineral salts rise to surface, forming toxic white efflorescence (Kallar/Reh), rendering soils sterile.

Industrial Leachates & Mining Toxic Slag

Heavy Metal Infiltration: Lead, cadmium, and arsenic leaching from industrial sludge into groundwater tables.

Mining Debris: Open-cast coal and iron mines leaving unreclaimed overburden and topsoil erosion.

Reclamation Practices Edaphic Restoration

Remedies: Gypsum application on alkaline soils, organic compost, balanced NPK ratios, and afforestation shelterbelts.
