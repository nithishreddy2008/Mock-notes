The 28-Day Menstrual Cycle
Four phases on repeat — the uterus prepares a fresh bed every month
1

Menstrual phase

DAYS 1–5

The endometrium sheds out of the body as the monthly flow. Progesterone and oestrogen sit at their lowest.

2

Follicular phase

DAYS 5–13

FSH grows ovarian follicles; the endometrium rebuilds. Oestrogen rises steadily to a peak.

3

Ovulation

DAY 14

An LH surge bursts the Graafian follicle; the secondary oocyte is released and caught by the fimbriae.

4

Luteal phase

DAYS 15–28

The empty follicle becomes the corpus luteum, making progesterone to keep the lining ready. No fertilisation → it fades and day 1 restarts.

BREAKS IN THE RHYTHM
Pregnancy and lactational amenorrhoea (no periods while the baby is fully breast-fed) pause the cycle — no menstruation while the lining has a job to do.
