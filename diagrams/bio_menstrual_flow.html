Synchronous 28-day dynamics: Pituitary gonadotropins (LH, FSH), ovarian steroids (Estrogen, Progesterone), and endometrial remodeling

CUET UG Biology
Unit VI Reproduction
28-Day Rhythm
PHASE 1 DAYS 1–5
Menstrual (Bleeding) Phase
Endocrine Status: Basal Minimum Estrogen and Progesterone crash to lowest levels due to regression of corpus luteum. Negative feedback released → pituitary begins slow FSH secretion.
Endometrial Breakdown Spiral arterioles undergo intense vasoconstriction, causing tissue ischemia. Stratum functionale sloughs off, discharging 40–80 mL of blood, mucus, and tissue debris.
CUET Trigger: Menstruation occurs only if released ovum is not fertilized. Absence of menstruation is first presumptive sign of pregnancy.
Ovarian state: Primary follicles recruited by rising FSH.
PHASE 2 DAYS 6–13
Follicular / Proliferative Phase
Pituitary & Ovarian Steroids FSH stimulates follicle growth (Primary → Secondary → Tertiary → Graafian). Developing theca interna secretes escalating quantities of Estrogen.
Endometrial Proliferation Estrogen stimulates rapid mitotic proliferation of uterine stroma, tubular glands, and spiral arteries; thickness reaches 3–5 mm.
Cervical Mucus: High estrogen makes cervical mucus thin, watery, alkaline, and stretchy (spinnbarkeit), optimizing sperm transport.
Ovarian state: Mature Graafian follicle bulges from ovarian surface.
PHASE 3 DAY 14 (MID-CYCLE)
Ovulatory Surge Phase
The Dual Gonadotropin Peak High estrogen threshold switches feedback from negative to positive, triggering dramatic LH Surge (and concomitant FSH peak) at mid-cycle.
Follicular Rupture LH surge induces collagenases to dissolve ovarian stigma. Mature Graafian follicle ruptures, discharging the Secondary Oocyte (n, arrested at Metaphase II)!
Basal Body Temperature: Progesterone initiation causes a distinct 0.3°C to 0.5°C rise in morning temperature post-ovulation.
Viability: Ovulated secondary oocyte viable for ~24 hours in ampulla.
PHASE 4 DAYS 15–28
Luteal / Secretory Phase
Corpus Luteum Endocrinology Ruptured follicle transforms into the Corpus Luteum under LH. Secretes massive quantities of Progesterone (pregnancy hormone) and moderate estrogen.
Secretory Transformation Endometrium reaches peak thickness (6–8 mm); glands become corkscrew-tortuous, secreting glycogen-rich "uterine milk" for blastocyst implantation.
Dual Fate Rule:
• No Fertilization: Corpus luteum degenerates into Corpus Albicans; progesterone plunges → Menstruation.
• Fertilization: Trophoblast hCG maintains corpus luteum.
Fixed duration: Luteal phase duration is constant at exactly 14 days.
LH SURGE CRITICAL WINDOW: Peak LH at mid-cycle (Day 14) triggers ovulation of Secondary Oocyte arrested in Metaphase II Essential Trigger
PROGESTERONE BED: Corpus luteum progesterone is absolute requisite for endometrial maintenance; plummeting level starts bleed Pregnancy Hormone
CYCLE CONSTANT: Follicular phase varies in length, but the post-ovulatory Luteal Phase is strictly fixed at 14 days 14-Day Law
