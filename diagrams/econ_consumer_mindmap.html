2. Cardinal Utility Approach Marshall
Law of Diminishing MU Gossen 1st Law
As consumption increases continuously, $MU = \Delta TU / \Delta Q$ declines. TU reaches max when $MU = 0$.
Single Good Equilibrium MUₓ = Pₓ · MUₘ
Equilibrium where marginal benefit equals marginal cost ($MU_x / P_x = MU_m$).
Equi-Marginal Principle Gossen 2nd Law
$MU_x / P_x = MU_y / P_y = MU_m$. Rebalancing via Law of DMU restores parity if violated.
3. Ordinal Indifference Analysis Hicks-Allen
Diminishing MRS Convexity Invariant
$MRS_{xy} = - \Delta y / \Delta x = MU_x / MU_y$. Consumer willing to give up less $Y$ for each extra $X$.
Budget Line Pₓ·x + Pᵧ·y = M
Slope $= -P_x / P_y$. Income change produces parallel shift; price change produces rotational pivot.
Consumer Equilibrium Tangency & Convexity
Dual conditions: 1. $MRS_{xy} = P_x / P_y$, and 2. IC must be strictly convex to origin at tangency point.
