Where Does the Year's Profit Actually Go?
The Profit & Loss Appropriation journey — from the P&L account into the partners' pockets
1

Net profit arrives

P&L A/c closes and hands its balance to the P&L Appropriation A/c (credit side).

2

Add interest on drawings

Money partners took out earns interest for the firm — credited here.

3

Deduct deed items

Debit side: IoC · partner salary · commission · transfer to reserve.

4

Divisible profit

Whatever remains is the profit the partners share between themselves.

5

Share in the agreed ratio

Divisible profit is split in the profit-sharing ratio of the deed (equally if silent).

6

Credit each partner

Each share is credited to the partner's Current A/c (fixed system) or Capital A/c (fluctuating).
