The Eight Common Diseases

Pathogen = a disease-causing organism (bacteria, viruses, protozoa, fungi, worms)
TyphoidPneumoniaCommon coldMalaria AmoebiasisAscariasisFilariasisRingworm
Spread by: contaminated food & water, infected droplets, insect vectors

Immunity — Innate vs Acquired

Innate: inborn, non-specific, attacks any pathogen — no memory
4 barriers: physical (skin) · physiological (HCl, lysozyme) · cellular (phagocytes) · cytokines
Acquired: pathogen-specific, has memory — remembers every attacker
Humoral (B cells → antibodies) vs cell-mediated (T cells)

Vaccination & Immunisation

Vaccine: weakened/dead antigen → body makes antibodies without the disease
Active: own body makes antibodies (infection or vaccine) — slow, long-lasting
Passive: ready-made antibodies given — instant, short-lived (colostrum IgA, antivenom)

Allergy & Autoimmunity

Allergy: exaggerated immune response to a harmless substance (pollen, dust mites)
Chemistry: IgE antibodies trigger histamine — sneezing, watery eyes; treated with antihistamines
Autoimmunity: body attacks its own cells — e.g. rheumatoid arthritis

AIDS & Cancer

HIV: retrovirus, attacks helper T cells — tests ELISA (screen) → Western blot (confirm)
Spread: sexual contact, infected blood, shared needles, mother to child — not by handshakes or food
Cancer: cells lose contact inhibition; malignant cells spread = metastasis
Treatment: surgery · radiation · chemotherapy · immunotherapy (α-interferon)

Drugs & Alcohol Abuse

Opioids: heroin from Papaver somniferum — depressant (binds opioid receptors)
Stimulants: cocaine from Erythroxylum coca — excites the CNS
Cannabinoids: from Cannabis sativa — hallucinogen (perception distorts)
Help: counselling, de-addiction centres, sports, refusing peer pressure

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Malaria — female Anopheles; filariasis — Culex. Do not swap the mosquitoes
Innate immunity has no memory — only acquired immunity remembers
Benign tumours do not metastasise — only malignant (cancer) cells spread
HIV is not spread by insect bites, hugs or sharing meals — body fluids only
