CUET Mass Media · Technical Matrix
AM
Amplitude Modulation (AM)
Spectrum & Physics
Medium Wave (526–1606 kHz) & Short Wave (3–30 MHz). Constant frequency; audio modulates carrier amplitude.
Propagation & Range
Skywave Ionospheric reflection at night.
Massive Reach Hundreds of kilometers across national borders.
Audio Fidelity
Narrow bandwidth (5 kHz); mono audio; highly vulnerable to electrical static and lightning interference.
Exam Significance
AIR Backbone Primary medium for national news and agricultural outreach to remote rural zones.
FM
Commercial FM Radio
Spectrum & Physics
VHF Band II (88–108 MHz). Constant carrier amplitude; audio signal modulates carrier frequency directly.
Propagation & Range
Line-of-Sight Ground wave propagation.
Local Radius 50–70 km urban metro coverage from hilltop/tower antennas.
Audio Fidelity
Wide bandwidth (15 kHz audio, 200 kHz channel); high-fidelity stereo; totally immune to electrical static.
Exam Significance
Private FM Auctions Phase I-III licensing; restricted from producing independent news.
CR
Community Radio (CRS)
Spectrum & Physics
Dedicated low-power FM frequencies (88–108 MHz) assigned by WPC wing of DoT under MIB guidelines.
Propagation & Range
100W Transmitter Max ERP limit.
10–15 km Radius Hyper-local geographic or campus community footprint.
Philosophy & Format
Non-profit, community-owned and operated. Local dialects, indigenous folk arts, maternal health, local farming.
Exam Significance
Pioneers Anna FM (2004, Campus); Sangham Radio (2008, Rural NGO); 5 min/hr ad cap.
POD
Podcasting & Digital Audio
Spectrum & Physics
Internet protocol audio packets (MP3/AAC); syndicated via RSS feeds over cellular networks and broadband.
Propagation & Range
Global Reach On-demand asynchronous streaming.
Time-Shifted Non-linear listener control.
Audio Fidelity
Variable/Lossless bitrates (128–320 kbps); binaural 3D spatial audio; pristine multi-track studio mastering.
Exam Significance
Niche Culture Intimate long-form talk; true crime; bypasses terrestrial spectrum licensing.
Key Invariant: AM reflects off the ionosphere (long distance); FM is line-of-sight (static-free); Community Radio is restricted to 100W and 15 km.
