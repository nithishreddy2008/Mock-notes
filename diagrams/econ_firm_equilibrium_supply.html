The Supply Curve Identification Rules

  • Short-Run Supply Curve: The rising segment of the Short-Run Marginal Cost ($S M C$) on or above the minimum $S A V C$ point. Output is 0 for $P < A V C_{\min}$.
  • Long-Run Supply Curve: The rising segment of the Long-Run Marginal Cost ($L M C$) on or above the minimum $L A C$ point. Output is 0 for $P < L A C_{\min}$.

Shutdown vs. Break-Even Decision Matrix

  • Break-Even Point ($P = A T C_{\min}$): Firm covers ALL economic costs (explicit + implicit). Economic profit = 0; earns Normal Profit.
  • Shutdown Point ($P = A V C_{\min}$): Firm only covers variable costs. Loss equals exactly $T F C$. If $P < A V C_{\min}$, operating increases losses beyond $T F C \implies$ Immediate Shutdown!
  • Operating at a Loss ($A V C_{\min} < P < A T C_{\min}$): Firm operates in short run because revenue covers all TVC and contributes partially toward paying TFC.

CUET Trap: Free Entry & Exit

Fatal Trap: Believing firms earn supernormal profits in long-run perfect competition. Reality: Free entry eliminates supernormal profit; free exit eliminates losses. Long-run equilibrium strictly forces $P = LMC = \min LAC$, yielding strictly Zero Economic Profit (Normal Profit).
