CUET UG · MATHEMATICS · FINANCIAL MATHEMATICS

PERPETUITY

PV = A / i
Forever payments A at rate i. ₹5,000/yr at 8% → ₹62,500.
Read: the price of an endless income = payment ÷ rate.

SINKING FUND

Deposit = Target × i / ((1+i)ⁿ − 1)
Save up to a target with equal deposits. ₹10 L in 10 yrs at 8% → ≈ ₹69,030/yr.
vs savings account: earmarked for ONE future liability, not general saving.

EMI (reducing balance)

EMI = P·i·(1+i)ⁿ / ((1+i)ⁿ − 1)
i is the PERIOD rate — monthly loan: annual ÷ 12.
₹1.2 L, 1%/month, 12 EMIs → ≈ ₹10,662.

RATE OF RETURN

RoR = (gain ÷ cost) × 100
Gain = selling price − cost + dividends.
₹50,000 → ₹55,000 + ₹2,000 dividend = 14%.

CAGR

CAGR = (End/Start)^(1/n) − 1
One smoothed yearly rate. ₹1 L → ₹1.44 L in 2 yrs → 20%.
AGR averages yearly % changes; CAGR compounds — different numbers by design.

LINEAR DEPRECIATION

D = (Cost − Residual) ÷ Life
Same slice every year. ₹5 L → ₹50 L? No: ₹5,00,000 to ₹50,000 over 5 yrs = ₹90,000/yr.
Book value falls in a straight line.

Bond valuation (present-value approach)

Bond terms: face value F, coupon rate (fixed), maturity, market rate (moves daily).
Value = PV of all coupons + PV of face value at the MARKET rate:
V = C · [1 − (1+i)⁻ⁿ]/i + F/(1+i)ⁿ
₹1,000 bond, 8% coupon, 3 yrs, market 10% → 80 × 2.487 + 1000 × 0.751 ≈ ₹950 — below face.
Rule: market rate > coupon → discount; market < coupon → premium.

The rate dictionary (never mix these)

Nominal rate: the STATED rate — before inflation, before compounding frequency.
Real feel: nominal minus inflation ≈ what your money actually earned.
Period vs annual: 12% annual = 1% per month — EMI maths uses the MONTHLY one.
CAGR vs AGR: 50% then −50% has AGR 0% but CAGR −29.3% — the order of % changes is not symmetric.
Compounding steps: align (1+i)ⁿ's i with the payment period, every time.

TRAPS THE OPTIONS ARE BUILT AROUND

Perpetuity PV = A/i, not A·i — divide, always
EMI's i is per period — 12% annual enters as 1% monthly
CAGR's n counts YEARS of growth, not the number of values
Depreciation uses Cost − RESIDUAL, never cost alone
Bond PV at MARKET rate, not the coupon rate
Sinking fund ≠ savings account — purpose-locked by definition
