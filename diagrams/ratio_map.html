Liquidity Ratios

Can the firm pay its short-term dues on time?
Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities — covers dues payable within a year; commonly quoted ideal 2:1
Quick ratio = (CA − Inventory − Prepaid) ÷ Current liabilities — the same test without slow-moving stock; commonly quoted ideal 1:1

Solvency Ratios

Can the firm carry its long-term debt safely?
Debt-Equity ratio = Long-term debt ÷ Shareholders' funds — how heavily the firm borrows; lower is safer
Proprietary ratio = Shareholders' funds ÷ Total assets — the owners' slice of the assets
Total assets to debt = Total assets ÷ Long-term debt — the asset cushion standing behind the debt
Interest coverage = Profit before interest & tax ÷ Interest on long-term debt — how many times profit can pay the interest bill

Activity (Turnover) Ratios

How fast do assets turn into sales and cash?
Inventory turnover = Cost of revenue ÷ Average inventory — how many times stock is sold in a year
Receivables turnover = Net credit revenue ÷ Average trade receivables — collection speed; days = 365 ÷ ratio
Payables turnover = Net credit purchases ÷ Average trade payables — how fast the firm pays suppliers
Working capital turnover = Revenue from operations ÷ Working capital — sales squeezed out of each rupee of working capital

Profitability Ratios

Is every rupee of sales leaving a profit behind?
Gross profit ratio = Gross profit ÷ Revenue × 100 — margin before operating costs
Net profit ratio = Net profit ÷ Revenue × 100 — the final margin after everything
Operating ratio = (Cost of revenue + Operating expenses) ÷ Revenue × 100 — the cost slice; lower is better
Operating profit ratio = Operating profit ÷ Revenue × 100 — the operating margin; it and the operating ratio always total 100%
Ground rules: "Average" = (opening + closing) ÷ 2 when both are given · "Revenue" means revenue from operations · every ratio gains meaning only when compared — with past years, other firms or a quoted benchmark.
