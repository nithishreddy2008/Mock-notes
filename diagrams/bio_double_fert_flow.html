From Pollen to Seed
The double fertilisation journey — seven steps from a landing pollen grain to a finished seed
1

Pollination

Pollen lands on the stigma, the sticky landing pad at the top of the pistil.

2

Pollen tube grows

A tube pushes down through the style, carrying the two male gametes at its tip.

3

Through the gate

The tube slips into a synergid through the micropyle, the ovule's tiny doorway.

4

SYNGAMY

One sperm + the egg → zygote (2n), the first cell of the new plant.

5

TRIPLE FUSION

The second sperm + the 2 polar nuclei → primary endosperm nucleus (3n).

6

Feeding the embryo

Endosperm stores food while the zygote divides and builds the embryo.

7

Seed and fruit

The ovule matures into the seed; the ovary swells into the fruit.

Steps 4 and 5 together = DOUBLE FERTILISATION — found only in angiosperms
