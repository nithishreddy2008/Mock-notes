Unit IV: Company Accounts Master Synthesis Mindmap

Holistic structural blueprint connecting shares, debentures, forfeiture mechanics, and Schedule III reporting

MASTER SYNTHESIS MINDMAP
1. Capital Structure (Sch. III) Balance Sheet
  • ▸Authorised Capital: Max nominal capital stated in MOA. Shown as footnote total.
  • ▸Issued Capital: Offered to public. Par value × issued shares.
  • ▸Subscribed & Fully Paid: 100% face value called AND received.
  • ▸Subscribed Not Fully Paid: Uncalled face value OR calls-in-arrears deducted.
  • ▸Reserve Capital: Sec 99 uncalled capital only available on winding up.
Balance Sheet Total includes ONLY Subscribed Capital + Forfeited Shares A/c!
2. Issue & Pro-Rata Allotment Issue Dynamics
  • ▸Par vs Premium: Premium to Sec 52(2) Securities Premium (Reserves & Surplus).
  • ▸Table F Interest: Calls-in-Arrears up to 10% p.a.; Advance up to 12% p.a.
  • ▸Sec 39 Minimum: 90% subscription mandatory within 30 days of prospectus.
  • ▸Pro-rata Surplus: Adjusted 1st against Allotment, then Calls/Refund.
  • ▸Vendor Issue: Shares issued for assets. Asset − Liab = Net Assets.
Purchase Consideration > Net Assets = Goodwill; Consideration < Net Assets = Capital Reserve!

3. Forfeiture & Reissue Rules Forfeiture Logic
  • ▸Share Capital Debit: Debited with Called-up amount, NEVER nominal face value!
  • ▸Securities Premium: Debited ONLY if unreceived. Once received, do NOT touch!
  • ▸Forfeited Shares Cr.: Holds face value actually received from shareholder.
  • ▸Max Reissue Discount: Capped at amount forfeited per share.
  • ▸Capital Reserve: Net gain on reissued shares transferred to Capital Reserve.
Unreissued shares balance stays in Share Forfeiture A/c until future reissue!
4. Debentures & Redemption Debt Instruments
  • ▸Types: Naked/Secured, Registered/Bearer, Convertible/Non-convertible.
  • ▸6 Redemption Cases: Premium on redemption recognized at issue as Loss on Issue.
  • ▸Write-Off Hierarchy: 1st Securities Premium, 2nd Statement of Profit & Loss.
  • ▸Collateral Security: Method 1 (Footnote) vs Method 2 (Debenture Suspense).
  • ▸Debenture Interest: Fixed charge against profits, subject to TDS.
Premium on Redemption is a liability under Other Long-Term Liabilities!
