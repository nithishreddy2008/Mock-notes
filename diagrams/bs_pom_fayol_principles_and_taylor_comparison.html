Principles of Management: Classical vs Scientific Comparative Framework

Nature & Significance of Principles, Henri Fayol's 14 Administrative Laws, and F.W. Taylor's Scientific Techniques

CUET Business Studies · Unit 2
Henri Fayol
Administrative Top-Down Perspective: Mining engineer; focus on overarching administrative efficiency, 14 universal principles, general theory of management, and functional processes.
F.W. Taylor
Scientific Bottom-Up Shop-Floor: Mechanical engineer; focus on factory floor productivity, eliminating physical waste, standardizing tools, work-study experiments, and differential wages.

1. Nature of Principles

Core Essence

Universal & General Guidelines Scope

Applicable to all enterprises regardless of size, ownership, or culture. Provide practical guidance rather than rigid, ready-made solutions.

Formed by Observation & Trial Empirical

Derived through decades of empirical observation in real factories and validated through continuous workplace experimentation.

Flexible & Dynamic Adaptation Modifiable

Not rigid mathematical formulas. Managers have the discretion to modify them according to changing organizational contingencies.

Behavioral & Cause-Effect Human Focus

Aims primarily to influence human behavior at work. Establishes predictive relationships: if action X is taken, consequence Y tends to follow.

CUET Crucial Fact: Principles are contingent. Their application depends entirely on prevailing workplace situations at a given point in time.

2. Fayol's 14 Principles

Administrative

Division of Work & Discipline Specialization

Division of Work: Specialization increases output.
Discipline: Obedience to organizational rules and fair penalties.
Authority & Responsibility: Parity between the two.

Unity of Command & Direction Chain

Unity of Command: One subordinate receives orders from ONE superior only.
Unity of Direction: One head and one plan for a group of activities with the same objective.

Scalar Chain & Gang Plank Communication

Formal line of authority from top to bottom. Gang Plank: Direct cross-communication between employees of equal rank in emergencies.

Order, Equity & Esprit de Corps Human Spirit

Order: Right place for everything and everyone.
Equity: Fair, kind, and just treatment.
Esprit de Corps: Replaces 'I' with 'We' for team spirit.

Key Distinction: Unity of Command prevents dual subordination; Unity of Direction prevents overlapping activities.

3. Taylor's Principles

Scientific Base

Science, Not Rule of Thumb Standardization

Replace trial-and-error intuition with scientific analysis of every job element to discover the "one best method" of performing work.

Harmony, Not Discord Mental Revolution

Complete mental revolution between workers and management. Realization that prosperity for employer cannot exist without prosperity for worker.

Cooperation, Not Individualism Involvement

Extension of Harmony. Management should take workers into confidence before setting standards and welcome constructive worker suggestions.

Development of Each Person Efficiency

Scientific selection of workers, systematic training in the best methods, and assignment of tasks matching physical and mental capabilities.

Mental Revolution: Transformation of mutual mindset from fighting over division of surplus to cooperating to maximize the surplus.

4. Taylor's Techniques

Shop Floor

Functional Foremanship 8 Bosses

Separates Planning from Execution. Planning Incharge: Instruction Card, Route, Time & Cost, Disciplinarian.
Production Incharge: Speed, Gang, Repair, Inspector.

Work-Study Experiments Time & Motion

Method Study: Finding the one best way.
Motion Study: Eliminating unproductive gestures.
Time Study: Standard time & workforce size.
Fatigue Study: Rest intervals frequency & length.

Differential Piece Wage System Incentive

Higher wage rate for workers who achieve or exceed standard output; lower wage rate for workers falling below standard (sharp incentive).

Foremanship Violation: Functional Foremanship deliberately violates Fayol's Unity of Command by placing 8 bosses over 1 worker!
CUET EXAM PRINCIPLE: Fayol builds an administrative architecture applicable to any organization; Taylor builds operational precision engineering on the factory floor.
