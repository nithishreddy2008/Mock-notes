Structure & Function

Definition: organisms + their physical surroundings, interacting as one working system
Pond example: water + dissolved nutrients feed producers → herbivores → carnivores → decomposers
Species composition = which species live there; stratification = vertical layers
Four roles: producers make food · herbivores eat · carnivores hunt · decomposers recycle

Productivity — GPP & NPP

GPP: rate at which producers build organic matter (g/m²/yr — a rate, not a stock)
NPP = GPP − R: R = producers’ respiration; NPP is the only share consumers can eat
Standing crop = mass of living matter now; standing state = nutrients in soil now
Oceans: ~70% of Earth’s surface yet a smaller share of output — light + nutrients limit them

Decomposition — 5 Steps

Detritus = dead leaves, bodies, droppings; eaten by detritivores (earthworm)
Order: fragmentation → leaching → catabolism → humification → mineralisation
Humus: dark, amorphous, decomposes very slowly — a reservoir of nutrients
Fast when warm + moist + oxygen-rich; slow when waterlogged; sugars fast, lignin slow

Energy Flow — One Way

Source: the Sun; plants can use only PAR — less than 50% of sunlight
Trophic level = one step of the chain; producers sit at level 1
10% rule (Lindeman): each level hands on about 10% of its energy
Never cycles back: the lost part leaves as heat at every step

Food Chains & Food Web

GFC: starts with living producers — grass → rabbit → fox
DFC: starts with detritus → decomposers → predators; joins the GFC where its animals become prey
Food web: many chains interlinked — one organism, many eaters
Chains stay short (3–4 levels): energy runs out

Ecological Pyramids

Producers form the base; layers stacked by trophic level — 3 kinds
Number: upright in grassland; inverted for a tree (one tree → many insects)
Biomass: upright on land; inverted in the ocean (small phytoplankton, big zooplankton)
Energy: ALWAYS upright — heat is lost at every transfer

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

NPP ≠ GPP − decomposition. NPP = GPP − respiration of producers
Productivity is a rate (per year) — never a stored pile of matter
Energy pyramid never inverts; number and biomass can
Don’t skip leaching when listing the 5 decomposition steps in order
