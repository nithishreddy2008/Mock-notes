THE STAFFING PROCESS — eight steps from counting needs to paying and growing people
1

Estimating manpower requirements

Count how many people of which skills the firm needs — workload analysis (work to be done) and workforce analysis (skills available now).
2

Recruitment

Search for suitable candidates and stimulate them to apply — through internal sources (transfer, promotion) and external sources (ads, campuses, portals).
3

Selection

Choose the most suitable from the applicants — tests, interviews, references — rejecting those who do not fit. Selection is a negative process.
4

Placement and orientation

Put the selected person on the right job (placement) and introduce them to colleagues, rules and workplace (orientation).
5

Training and development

Systematically improve skills for the present job (training) and overall growth for future roles (development).
6

Performance appraisal

Measure how well each employee performs against the standards of the job — strengths, weaknesses and potential.
7

Promotion and career planning

Reward better performers with higher posts and pay, and plan each person's growth path in the firm.
8

Compensation

Fix and pay fair remuneration — wages, salaries, benefits — matched to the job's worth and the person's performance.
