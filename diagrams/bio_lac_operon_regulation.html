Key Rule: Gene i is constitutively active (transcribed continuously at low basal rate). Structural genes z, y, a are transcribed as a single continuous polycistronic mRNA transcript under coordinate control.

State A: Absence of Inducer (Lactose Absent)

Operon Switched OFF
1
Constitutive Transcription: Gene i produces repressor mRNA → translated into an active homotetrameric repressor protein.
2
Operator Binding: In the absence of inducer, active repressor binds with high affinity to the Operator (O) region.
3
Steric Hindrance: Bound repressor physically blocks RNA Polymerase from moving downstream from promoter (P).
4
No Transcription: Structural genes z, y, a cannot be transcribed into mRNA.
OPERON OFF: No β-Galactosidase, Permease, or Transacetylase Synthesized

State B: Presence of Inducer (Lactose / Allolactose)

Operon Switched ON
1
Lactose Entry & Isomerization: Basal permease allows lactose entry → converted by basal β-gal into Allolactose (real inducer).
2
Allosteric Inactivation: Allolactose binds repressor tetramer → induces conformational change, releasing it from Operator.
3
Uninhibited Transcription: RNA Polymerase freely transcribes structural genes into a single polycistronic mRNA.
4
Enzymatic Translation: Translates 3 enzymes: β-gal (z), Permease (y), and Transacetylase (a).
OPERON ON: Rapid Hydrolysis of Lactose into Glucose + Galactose

1. Tri-Enzyme Biochemical Roles

gene z (β-Galactosidase): Hydrolyzes lactose into β-D-galactose + D-glucose.
gene y (Permease): Enhances membrane permeability to lactose.
gene a (Transacetylase): Transacetylates non-metabolizable β-galactosides using Acetyl-CoA.

2. Negative vs Positive Control

Negative Control (Default): Repressor halts transcription until inducer arrives.
Positive Control (Catabolite Repression): High glucose → low cAMP → CAP inactive → transcription low. Low glucose → high cAMP → CAP-cAMP binds promoter → maximal expression!

3. Essential CUET Traps

A very low (basal) level of lac operon expression must ALWAYS be present in E. coli; otherwise lactose could never enter the cell initially!
IPTG: Gratuitous inducer (binds repressor but is NOT degraded by β-gal).
