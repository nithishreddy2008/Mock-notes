NCERT & NTA CUET 2027 SPECIFICATION
Policy Foundation 1991 New Economic Policy (LPG)
1. Liberalisation (Freedom to Operate)
• Abolition of Industrial Licensing: Abolished for all except 6 strategic/hazardous industries (alcohol, cigarettes, hazardous chemicals, industrial explosives, electronic aerospace & defense, pharmaceuticals).
• Freedom of Expansion: Removal of MRTP limits and price controls.
• Operational Flexibility: Free movement of goods, import duty slashing, and simplified tax structures.
2. Privatisation (Rollback of State)
• Disinvestment: Sale of state-owned equity in Public Sector Enterprises (PSEs) to private investors and public.
• De-reservation: Exclusive PSE sectors reduced from 17 down to only 3 (Atomic energy, Defense equipment, Railway operations).
• Navratna / Miniratna Autonomy: Granting financial and operational autonomy to profit-making state firms.
3. Globalisation (Global Integration)
• Tariff Rationalization: Phasing out quantitative import restrictions (quotas) and reducing astronomical tariff walls.
• FDI Liberalization: Automatic route approvals for FDI up to 51% (now 100% in many sectors).
• Currency Convertibility: Partial convertibility of Rupee on trade account leading to market-determined forex rates.
Corporate Realignment Seven Impacts on Business & Industry
1. Increasing Competition Global Entry
Indian firms face severe competition from agile multinational corporations, resulting in domestic mergers, acquisitions, and restructuring.
2. More Demanding Customers Buyer's Market
Shift from a supply-constrained seller's market to a hyper-competitive buyer's market; customers demand superior quality and competitive pricing.
3. Rapidly Changing Technology Shorter Lifecycles
Technological obsolescence forces massive capital allocation toward automated machinery, process innovations, and continuous digital modernization.
4. Necessity for Change Agile Operations
Static long-term blueprints obsolete; enterprise survival demands agile flexibility, continuous environmental scanning, and swift strategic pivots.
5. Need for Developing Human Resources Talent Upskilling
High-tech automation and intense competition necessitate highly competent, specialized, multi-disciplinary personnel capable of critical execution.
6. Market Orientation Needs-First Shift
Shift from 'Production Concept' (produce then sell) to 'Marketing Concept' (identify consumer demands, design tailored products, then market).
7. Loss of Budgetary Support to PSEs Fiscal Discipline
Central government ceased automatic financial bailouts; public enterprises must generate internal profits or face privatization/closure.
Monetary Restructuring 2016 Demonetisation & Formalization
1. The Policy Shock (Nov 8, 2016)
• Legal Tender Withdrawal: ₹500 and ₹1,000 currency notes ceased to be legal tender, invalidating 86% of currency in circulation overnight.
• Deposit Mandate: Public given 50 days to deposit invalidated notes into banking channels, subject to strict KYC and tax declarations.
2. Core Stated Objectives
• Curbing Black Money: Trapping unaccounted, untaxed physical cash reserves.
• Eliminating Counterfeit Notes: Neutralizing high-quality fake Indian currency notes (FICN).
• Choking Terrorist Financing: Paralyzing illicit cash pipelines.
• Channelizing Household Savings: Converting physical cash into formal bank deposits.
3. Structural Environmental Impacts
• Short-Term Shock: Sharp liquidity contraction in cash-reliant informal sectors (unorganized retail, real estate, agriculture).
• Tax Base Expansion: Substantial surge in direct personal income tax returns filed and tax compliance.
• Digital Payments Surge: Massive institutional adoption of UPI, POS terminals, net banking, and cashless fintech ecosystems.
CUET 2027 Key Distinction
Core Conceptual Anchors: De-licensing eliminates red-tape barriers for private entry; Disinvestment transfers public ownership to private hands; Market Orientation means consumer satisfaction precedes production; Demonetisation represents a tax administration and formalization intervention, fundamentally transforming financial habits from cash-hoarding to digital banking.
