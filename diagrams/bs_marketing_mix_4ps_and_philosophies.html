The Five Marketing Management Philosophies (NCERT)

1. Production Concept

Starting Point: Factory
• Core Belief: Consumers favor products that are widely available and inexpensive.
• Focus: Large-scale production, reducing unit costs, extensive distribution.
• Ends: Profits through high production volume.

2. Product Concept

Starting Point: Factory
• Core Belief: Consumers favor products offering superior quality, performance, and features.
• Focus: Continuous product improvement and engineering innovations.
• Ends: Profits through product quality. (Risk: Marketing Myopia).

3. Selling Concept

Starting Point: Factory
• Core Belief: Consumers will not buy enough without aggressive selling and promotion.
• Focus: Existing products; "sell what you make" via aggressive sales pressure.
• Ends: Profits through sales volume.

4. Marketing Concept

Starting Point: Target Market
• Core Belief: Customer satisfaction is the key to achieving organizational goals.
• Focus: Customer needs; "make what you can sell" via integrated marketing.
• Ends: Long-term profits through customer satisfaction.

5. Societal Marketing Concept

Market + Society
• Core Belief: Balance customer satisfaction with long-term societal well-being.
• Focus: Customer needs + ethical environmental stewardship (curbing pollution, deforestation).
• Ends: Profits through customer satisfaction & social welfare.
The Four Elements of Promotion Mix

1. Advertising

Paid · Impersonal
  • Characteristics: Paid form, identified sponsor, mass reach, builds brand legitimacy.
  • Merits & Demerits: Expressive and economical per contact; but inflexible and lacks direct feedback.

2. Personal Selling

Direct · Oral
  • Characteristics: Face-to-face oral communication with prospective buyers for making sales.
  • Merits: Highly flexible, direct immediate feedback, creates long-term personal relationships.

3. Sales Promotion

Short-Term Incentives
  • Techniques: Rebates, Discounts, Refunds, Product Combinations, Quantity Gifts, Lucky Draws.
  • Role: Stimulates immediate trial and spikes short-term sales volume during off-peak periods.

4. Public Relations (PR)

Corporate Image
  • Tools: Press releases, press conferences, event sponsorships, lobbying, public service.
  • Role: Protects corporate reputation against negative rumors and builds positive goodwill.
CUET GOLDEN AXIOM: Selling focuses on the Seller's Needs (converting goods into cash); Marketing focuses on the Buyer's Needs (delivering customer satisfaction). 4Ps = Product, Price, Place, Promotion.
