Master Synthesis Mindmap: Societal Impacts, Cyber Law & Digital Ethics

Comprehensive knowledge taxonomy across Digital Footprints, IPR, Cyber Crime, E-waste, and Accessibility

CUET Code 308 · Syllabus Mindmap
1. Digital Footprint & Netiquette
  • Active Footprint: Deliberately shared data (posts, emails, blog comments).
  • Passive Footprint: Involuntary data trails (IP logs, cookies, browsing history).
  • Netiquette: Respect copyright, avoid flaming/trolling, verify sources.
  • Digital identity curation & long-term online reputation management.
2. IPR & Software Licensing
  • IPR Triad: Copyright (code), Patent (invention), Trademark (brand).
  • Plagiarism: Passing another's work as one's own without attribution.
  • FOSS vs Proprietary: 4 freedoms; GPL copyleft requires open derivative code.
  • Creative Commons: Flexible modular public attribution licenses (BY, NC, ND, SA).
3. Cyber Crimes & Threat Vectors
  • Phishing: Fraudulent emails imitating banks to steal credentials.
  • Cyberstalking & Bullying: Harassment, intimidation, defamation online.
  • Malware Spectrum: Ransomware, Spyware, Trojan Horses, Worms, Adware.
  • DDoS Attack: Botnet overwhelming target server with packet flood.
4. Indian IT Act (2000 & 2008)
  • Sec 43: Civil penalty for unauthorized computer damage/extraction.
  • Sec 66: Criminal hacking penalty (up to 3 years imprisonment).
  • Sec 66C: Identity theft (stolen passwords, biometric tokens).
  • Sec 66D: Cheating by personation using computer resource.
  • CERT-In: National agency for cyber security incident response.
5. E-Waste Management
  • Toxic Heavy Metals: Lead (brain/nerves), Mercury, Cadmium, Chromium (Cr-VI).
  • Environmental Impact: Groundwater contamination via landfill leaching.
  • Sustainable Lifecycle: Reduce, Reuse, Refurbish, Certified Recycling.
  • E-Waste Management Rules, 2016 / 2022: Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).
6. Digital Divide & Accessibility
  • Digital Divide: Infrastructure, socio-economic, linguistic, gender gap.
  • Assistive Tech: Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS), Braille displays, speech input.
  • Accessibility Guidelines: WCAG (Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust).
  • Universal design for equitable access to digital government services.
