NUMBERS & NUMERICAL APPLICATIONS
CUET UG MATHEMATICS

Modulo Arithmetic

a mod n = remainder; 17 mod 5 = 2
Reduce first: (a·b) mod n from the reduced factors
Powers: chase the cycle — 2ⁿ mod 5 cycles 2, 4, 3, 1
Clock logic: 3 h after 10 o'clock = 1 o'clock (mod 12)

Congruence Modulo n

a ≡ b (mod n) ⇔ n divides (a − b)
Same remainders on division by n
Properties: reflexive, symmetric, transitive — an equivalence relation
Calendar use: day after 100 days = 100 mod 7 steps ahead

Allegation & Mixture

Criss-cross: cheap : dear = (dear − mean) : (mean − cheap)
Mean price always sits between the two component prices
Verify by weighted average — one line, always
Two-cheap mixtures: apply the rule pairwise

Boats & Streams

Downstream: d = b + s · Upstream: u = b − s
Rescue pair: b = (u + d)/2, s = (d − u)/2
Same distance: time ratio = inverse speed ratio
Read: "in still water" = b; "stream" = s

Pipes & Cisterns

Every pipe = a rate in tank/hour: 6 h filler → 1/6
Fillers add, empties subtract
Net rate → time = 1 / net rate
Part-full checks: rate × hours = fraction filled

Races, Games & Inequalities

"Gives 10 m in 100 m" = A:100 while B:90 → speed ratio 10:9
"Gives 10 s" = time handicap on the shared clock
Inequalities: flip only on multiplying by a negative
Reciprocal (positives): a > b ⇒ 1/a < 1/b
