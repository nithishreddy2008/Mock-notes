Relational Algebra Operations: Unary, Set & Join Lifecycle

Mathematical Signatures, Degree and Cardinality Transformations, and Union Compatibility Invariants

CUET Relational Algebra Blueprint
1
Unary Relational Operators

Selection (Horizontal Filter) σ

Filters rows satisfying predicate condition $C$: $\sigma_C(R) = \{ t \mid t \in R \text{ and } C(t) = \text{True} \}$.

Degree: Identical ($= n$) Cardinality: $\le m$

Projection (Vertical Filter) π

Selects specified columns and strictly eliminates duplicate tuples: $\pi_{A_1, \dots, A_k}(R)$.

Degree: Number of cols ($k$) Cardinality: $\le m$

Rename Operator ρ

Renames relation or its attributes: $\rho_{S(B_1, \dots, B_n)}(R)$ without altering data or structure.

Degree: Unchanged ($= n$) Cardinality: Unchanged ($= m$)
2
Binary Set Theoretic Operators
Union Compatibility Rule:
Two relations $R$ and $S$ are union-compatible if and only if: 1. Both have identical degree: $\text{deg}(R) = \text{deg}(S)$. 2. Corresponding attribute domains are compatible: $\text{dom}(A_i) = \text{dom}(B_i)$.
Operator Notation Result Degree & Cardinality
Union $R \cup S$ Deg: $n$ • Card: $\le m_1 + m_2$ (no duplicates)
Intersection $R \cap S$ Deg: $n$ • Card: $\le \min(m_1, m_2)$
Difference $R - S$ Deg: $n$ • Card: $\le m_1$ (tuples in $R$ only)
Cartesian Product $R \times S$ Deg: $n_1 + n_2$ • Card: $m_1 \times m_2$
Note: Cartesian Product does NOT require union compatibility! It pairs every tuple of $R$ with every tuple of $S$.
3
Relational Join & CUET Invariants

Theta Join (θ-Join) ⋈θ

A Cartesian product followed by a Selection condition $\theta$: $R \bowtie_\theta S = \sigma_\theta(R \times S)$.

Degree: $n_1 + n_2$ Card: $\le m_1 \times m_2$

Natural Join ⋈

Equi-join over all identically named common attributes, automatically projecting out duplicate column copies.

Degree: $n_1 + n_2 - k$ Card: $0$ to $m_1 \times m_2$
CUET Fatal Trap: Cartesian Degree vs Cardinality!
Students constantly confuse Degree with Cardinality in $R \times S$: • Degree is ADDITIVE: $\text{Deg} = n_1 + n_2$.
• Cardinality is MULTIPLICATIVE: $\text{Card} = m_1 \times m_2$.
Example: If $R$ has $(3 \text{ cols}, 4 \text{ rows})$ and $S$ has $(2 \text{ cols}, 5 \text{ rows})$, $R \times S$ has 5 cols and 20 rows!
