1. Land Use & Cropping Seasons

Agro-Ecological
  • Land Classification: 9 reporting categories; Net Sown Area (~45.5%), Forests (~23.3%), Current Fallow (<1 yr), Fallow other than current, Culturable Waste (>5 yrs).
  • Kharif Season (Jun–Sep): Coincides with SW Monsoon; Rice, Cotton, Jute, Jowar, Bajra, Groundnut.
  • Rabi Season (Oct–Mar): Sown in autumn, harvested in spring; Wheat, Gram, Rapeseed/Mustard, Barley.
  • Zaid Season (Apr–Jun): Short summer cropping; Watermelon, cucumber, vegetables, fodder.
Cropping Intensity: (Gross Cropped Area ÷ Net Sown Area) × 100.

2. Major Crops & Green Revolution

Food & Cash Crops
  • Rice & Wheat: Rice leading in WB, UP, Punjab; Wheat leading in UP (yield in Punjab/Haryana).
  • Coarse Cereals & Pulses: Jowar (Maharashtra), Bajra (Rajasthan), Pulses (MP leading; nitrogen fixation).
  • Cash & Fibres: Cotton (Gujarat, Maharashtra regur soils), Jute (WB delta), Sugarcane (UP, Maharashtra).
  • Plantations: Tea (Assam, Darjeeling), Coffee (Karnataka Arabica/Robusta), Rubber (Kerala 90%).
Green Revolution: HYV Mexican wheat seeds + canal irrigation + chemical fertilizers.

3. Water Resources & Watersheds

Hydrological Matrix
  • Surface Water (1,869 BCM): Only 690 BCM (32%) utilisable; Ganga & Brahmaputra basins carry 60% runoff.
  • Groundwater (433 BCM): Stage of development >100% in Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan (over-exploited).
  • Sectoral Utilization: Agriculture consumes 89% surface water and 92% groundwater.
  • Watershed Management: Haryali (Central Govt), Neeru-Meeru (Andhra Pradesh), Arvary Pani Sansad (Alwar, Rajasthan).
Mandate: Rainwater harvesting mandatory in Tamil Nadu; National Water Policy 2012.

4. Mineral Resources & Belts

Metallic / Non-Metallic
  • Iron Ore (Hematite/Magnetite): Odisha (Badampahar), Jharkhand (Noamundi), Chhattisgarh (Bailadila), Karnataka (Kudremukh).
  • Manganese & Chromite: Manganese (MP Balaghat, Odisha Sundargarh); Chromite (Odisha Sukinda >90%).
  • Bauxite & Copper: Bauxite (Odisha Panchpatmali in Koraput); Copper (MP Balaghat, Rajasthan Khetri).
  • Non-Metallic Minerals: Mica (Jharkhand Koderma, AP Nellore); Limestone (cement industry base).
Mineral Belts: North-Eastern Plateau, South-Western Plateau, North-Western Region.

5. Conventional & Renewable Energy

Power Matrix
  • Coal Reserves: Gondwana coal (98% of reserves, Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro) vs Tertiary coal (Neyveli lignite, Assam).
  • Petroleum & Natural Gas: Digboi (oldest), Mumbai High (largest offshore), Ankleshwar (Gujarat), KG Basin (gas).
  • Nuclear Energy: Uranium (Jaduguda) & Thorium (Monazite sands); Tarapur, Rawatbhata, Kudankulam, Narora.
  • Renewable Surge: Solar (Bhadla Park, Rajasthan), Wind (Muppandal cluster, Tamil Nadu), Bio-energy, Geothermal (Puga/Manikaran).
Clean Transition: PM-KUSUM, International Solar Alliance (ISA), Green Hydrogen.

6. 8 Major Industrial Regions

Manufacturing Nodes
  • Mumbai-Pune: Textiles, chemicals, engineering, petrochemicals, IT hubs.
  • Hugli Region: Jute, tea packaging, engineering, chemicals along river Hugli.
  • Chotanagpur: Heavy metallurgy, iron & steel (Jamshedpur, Bokaro, Durgapur).
  • Bengaluru-Tamil Nadu: Electronics, aeronautics, textiles (Coimbatore), automotives (Chennai).
  • Gujarat, Delhi-Meerut, Vizag-Guntur & Kollam: Petrochemicals, consumer goods, pharmaceuticals, coir.
Industrial Policy: Liberalization (1991), delicensing, Make in India, DMIC corridor.
