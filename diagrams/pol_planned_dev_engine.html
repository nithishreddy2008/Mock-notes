PLANNED DEVELOPMENT — THE MACHINERY, THE PLANS, THE NEW INTERESTS

THE PLANNING MACHINERY — three facts to carry

Planning Commission — 1950
Set up by an executive resolution, NOT by law; the Prime Minister chairs it — Nehru was the first chairman
Advisory, not executive
It recommends; the Union and state governments execute. Extra-constitutional by design
NDC — 1952
National Development Council: PM + Chief Ministers — the federal table where plans get approved

Plan I (1951-56) vs Plan II (1956-61)

First Plan — agriculture first: Harrod-Domar growth arithmetic; food, irrigation, community development. Target ~2.1% — achieved ~3.6%. A success.
Why it worked: good monsoons + modest targets + the room Partition's food crisis left for quick wins.
Second Plan — the industrial turn: P.C. Mahalanobis's two-sector model — machines that make machines; heavy industry over quick consumer goods.
Its price: imports of machinery and food soared; a foreign-exchange crisis hit by 1957-58.

Expanding the state sector

Avadi 1955: the Congress adopted a "socialistic pattern of society" as the official goal — planning got an ideology.
IPR 1956: Schedule A — 17 industries EXCLUSIVE to the state; Schedule B — state-led with private entry; Schedule C — private under licence.
Steel with many flags: Bhilai (USSR), Rourkela (West Germany), Durgapur (UK) — non-alignment turned into steel plants.
State takeovers: Imperial Bank → SBI (1955); life insurance nationalised → LIC (1956).

THE FOOD CHAIN — how the plan crisis became a farm-policy revolution

1956-57

Food output dips; industry imports surge — foreign-exchange crisis within two years of the Second Plan.

Response

America's PL-480 supplies wheat against rupee payments — food imports keep the republic fed.

1960s

"Grow More Food" plus irrigation and fertiliser packages — but land reforms stall in the states.

1966

The Green Revolution: HYV seeds + assured irrigation break the famine fear — in Punjab, Haryana, west UP first.

Political result

Prosperous farmers emerge as a new economic interest — the countryside becomes a lobby the plans must answer.

THE RISE OF NEW ECONOMIC INTERESTS — who organised as planning grew the pie

Big industryEarlier than the state: the Bombay Plan (1944-45, Tata-Birla circle) itself ASKED for state-led heavy industry — protection and licences later built the "permit raj" the same class complained about.
Rich farmersIrrigation, subsidies and HYV packages created a prosperous agrarian class that pushed its demands through state politics — land reforms died where these interests ruled.
Organised workersPublic-sector expansion multiplied unions in railways, steel and banks — strikes became a normal instrument of bargaining with the plan state.

TRAP 1

Planning Commission ≠ constitutional body. A 1950 executive resolution created it; the Finance Commission is the constitutional one. The NCERT stresses this contrast.

TRAP 2

Model-plan pairing: Harrod-Domar → First Plan (agriculture); Mahalanobis → Second Plan (industry). Swapping them is the classic MCQ.

TRAP 3

Steel plants map to blocs: Bhilai-USSR, Rourkela-West Germany, Durgapur-UK. Non-alignment funded BOTH blocs' presence — do not mis-assign.
