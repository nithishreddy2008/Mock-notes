p-n Junction & Complete I-V Curve
Barrier Potential & Dynamic Resistance
Si: V₀ ≈ 0.7 V  |  Ge: V₀ ≈ 0.3 V
r_d = ΔV / ΔI  (Forward: 1–100 Ω, Reverse: ~ MΩ)
  • Forward Bias: p to $(+)$, n to $(-)$. Depletion width narrows, barrier drops to $(V_0 - V)$, current flows easily.
  • Reverse Bias: p to $(-)$, n to $(+)$. Depletion width widens, barrier rises to $(V_0 + V)$, only minority drift current $I_0$ flows.

Zener & Optoelectronics
Zener Voltage Regulator Design
R_s = (V_i - V_Z) / I = (V_i - V_Z) / (I_Z + I_L)
Heavily doped; operates strictly in reverse breakdown!
Optoelectronic Devices Summary:
Device Bias Mode Working Principle
Photodiode Reverse Bias Photons generate e⁻-hole pairs in depletion layer; photocurrent ΔI ∝ Light Intensity.
LED Forward Bias Spontaneous recombination emits photons across bandgap: hν ≈ E_g (visible: GaAsP).
Solar Cell Unbiased (0 V) Photovoltaic generation, separation & collection. I-V curve lies in 4th quadrant!
Why Photodiode Operates in Reverse Bias:
The fractional change in minority carrier density ($Δn_p / n_p$) is vastly greater than in majority carriers ($Δp_p / p_p$), allowing high-sensitivity optical detection!
