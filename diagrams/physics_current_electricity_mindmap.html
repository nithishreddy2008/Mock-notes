Unit 2: Current Electricity

Transport, Power, Cells & Kirchhoff's Networks

1. Microscopic Conduction

Drift Dynamics
  • Thermal vs Drift: Random thermal speed ⟨v_th⟩ ~ 10⁵ m/s (zero net current); applied E induces drift velocity v_d = (e E τ) / m (~10⁻⁴ m/s).
  • Relaxation Time (τ): Average collision interval (~10⁻¹⁴ s) with vibrating lattice ions.
  • Current & Current Density: I = n e A v_d; Vector current density J = I / A = n e v_d.
  • Mobility: μ = v_d / E = (e τ) / m [m²·V⁻¹·s⁻¹], strictly positive quantity.
Signal Propagation: EM signal travels at ~10⁸ m/s, despite mm/s electron drift.

2. Resistivity & Temperature

Material Physics
  • Microscopic Ohm's Law: J = σ E, where conductivity σ = (n e² τ) / m.
  • Resistivity Formula: ρ = 1/σ = m / (n e² τ); Resistance R = ρ L / A [Ω].
  • Temperature Dependence: ρ(T) = ρ₀ (1 + α ΔT).
  • Material Classes: Metals: α > 0 (τ drops with T); Standard alloys: α ≈ 0 (Nichrome, Manganin); Semiconductors: α < 0 (n grows exponentially).
Non-Ohmic Devices: Diodes, thyristors, electrolytes show non-linear V-I profiles.

3. Electrical Energy & Power

Joule Heating
  • Joule's Law of Heating: Power dissipated in resistance P = V I = I² R = V² / R.
  • Commercial Energy Unit: 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J (1 Board of Trade unit).
  • Rated vs Actual Bulb Power: Fixed internal resistance R = V_rated² / P_rated.
  • Bulb Brightness Rules: In series: lowest wattage glows brightest (P ∝ R); In parallel: highest wattage glows brightest (P ∝ 1/R).
Max Power Transfer: P_max = E² / (4 r) when load matches internal resistance (R_L = r).

4. EMF & Terminal Voltage

Source Mechanics
  • Electromotive Force (E): Potential difference across open terminals (I = 0).
  • Discharging Regime: Cell supplies current: V = E - I r < E.
  • Charging Regime: External source drives current into cell: V = E + I r > E.
  • Internal Resistance: r = [(E - V) / V] · R; Short circuit current I_sc = E / r.
Cell Properties: r drops with plate area & temp; grows with plate separation.

5. Combination of Cells

Network Grouping
  • Series (n cells): E_eq = n E, r_eq = n r; Current I = nE / (R + nr) (optimum when R >> nr).
  • Reversed Cells: If m cells reversed: E_eq = (n - 2m)E, but internal r_eq = nr.
  • Parallel (m cells): E_eq = E, r_eq = r/m; Current I = E / (R + r/m) (optimum when R << r).
  • Mixed Grouping: Max current delivered to load R when R = n r / m.
Unequal Cells: E_eq = (E₁/r₁ + E₂/r₂) / (1/r₁ + 1/r₂) | 1/r_eq = 1/r₁ + 1/r₂

6. Kirchhoff's Laws & Bridge

Circuit Analysis
  • Junction Rule (KCL): Σ I = 0 (*Conservation of Electric Charge*).
  • Loop Rule (KVL): Σ ΔV = 0 ⟹ Σ E = Σ I R (*Conservation of Energy*).
  • Balanced Wheatstone Bridge: Galvanometer I_g = 0 ⟺ P / Q = R / S.
  • Meter Bridge: Unknown resistance X = R · [(100 - l) / l] (null point near 50 cm).
Conjugate Arms: Interchanging battery & galvanometer preserves balance condition.
