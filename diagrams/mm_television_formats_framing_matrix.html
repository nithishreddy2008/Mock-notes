Comparative Matrix: Television Formats, Camera Framing & Studio Architecture

Systematic classification of program genres, multi-camera setups, shot grammar, and control room parameters

CUET Mass Media · Concept Matrix
TV
Live News & Bulletins
Production Paradigm
Multi-camera studio setup (3-4 cameras), teleprompter sync, real-time live graphics, PCR switcher routing, and satellite live links (OB van / DSNG).
Dominant Shot Grammar
Mid Shot (MS) Anchor presentation.
Close Up (CU) Emotional intensity.
Two-Shot Co-anchors & debates.
Scripting & Audio
Two-column TV script (video/audio), run-down software, lavalier lapel mics, ear-piece IFB talkback.
Key Exam Metric
Zero Delay PCR vision mixer switches cut-to-cut; lowest post-production latency.
SO
Soap Operas / Daily Serials
Production Paradigm
Multi-camera indoor sound stage sets with controlled grid lighting, fixed floor plans, continuous shooting, fast turnaround for daily broadcasting.
Dominant Shot Grammar
Over-The-Shoulder Dialogues.
Extreme CU (ECU) Dramatic gasps/eyes.
Three-Shot / Master Family scenes.
Scripting & Audio
Episodic screenplay, heavy background score, dramatic stings, boom microphones and hidden wireless mics.
Key Exam Metric
Emotional Hook Formulaic cliffhangers, non-linear editing (NLE), high character continuity.
DC
Documentaries & Docudramas
Production Paradigm
Single-camera setup (EFP - Electronic Field Production), natural location lighting, extensive b-roll capture, observational or participatory style.
Dominant Shot Grammar
Extreme Long Shot Geography/Context.
POV Shot Eyewitness perspective.
Tracking / Pan Dynamic environment.
Scripting & Audio
Post-shoot scripting, voice-over (VO) narration, ambient audio (wild track), directional shotgun mics.
Key Exam Metric
Investigative Rigor High post-production ratio (30:1 footage-to-cut), archival research.
RY
Reality & Game Shows
Production Paradigm
Massive multi-camera arrays (20–40 fixed robotic, crane & handheld cameras), isolated recording (ISO tracks), live audience floor management.
Dominant Shot Grammar
Jib / Crane Shot Sweeping arena views.
Reaction Shots Judges/Contestants.
Wide Master Stage choreography.
Scripting & Audio
Semi-scripted format bible, host teleprompter, multi-track audio mixing, extensive audience microphones.
Key Exam Metric
Format Licensing Global syndicated templates (e.g. KBC / Idol); high commercial TRP index.
CUET Invariant: Camera framing determines emotional proximity; Multi-camera PCR allows real-time switching; Single-camera EFP offers post-production flexibility.
