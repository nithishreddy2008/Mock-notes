Pollution Domain Diagnostic Parameter Analytical Methodology Standard Norms & Thresholds Control Technologies
Air Quality (Particulate) PM2.5 & PM10 Gravimetric / Beta-attenuation NAAQS 24-hr: PM2.5 = 60 µg/m³; PM10 = 100 µg/m³. AQI > 400 is "Severe". Electrostatic Precipitator (>99%), Fabric Baghouse, Cyclones.
Air Quality (Gaseous) SO2, NOx, CO, O3 UV fluorescence, Chemiluminescence, NDIR NAAQS 24-hr: SO2 = 80 µg/m³; NOx = 80 µg/m³; CO (8-hr) = 2 mg/m³. Flue Gas Desulfurization (FGD), Wet Lime Scrubbers, Catalytic Converters.
Water Quality (Organic) BOD5 & COD 5-day incubation at 20°C; K2Cr2O7 digestion Pristine stream: BOD < 2 mg/L; Untreated sewage: BOD 200-400 mg/L; COD/BOD > 2. Activated Sludge Process (ASP), Trickling Filters, Moving Bed Biofilms.
Aquatic Oxygenation Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Winkler titration / Membrane probe Critical threshold: > 4 to 6 mg/L required for healthy teleost fish. < 2 mg/L = Hypoxia. Mechanical Aeration, Cascade weirs, Algal bloom harvesting.
Acoustic Noise Leq (A-weighted decibels) Sound Level Meter (Fast/Slow response) CPCB Limits (Day/Night): Industrial: 75/70 dB; Commercial: 65/55 dB; Residential: 55/45 dB; Silence: 50/40 dB. Acoustic baffles, Green Belts (Ashoka, Neem), Mufflers.
Bioindicators Biological Sentinel Species Field surveying & saprobic index Lichens absent = SO2 pollution; Tubifex / Chironomus = high organic sludge; E. coli MPN. Bioremediation, Constructed wetlands, Riparian buffers.
