THE FIVE DIMENSIONS OF BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT — everything outside the firm's walls that shapes it
THE BUSINESS
SURROUNDED · SHAPED · LIMITED BY ITS ENVIRONMENT

Economic

MONEY CONDITIONS
Interest rates, inflation, income levels, stock market indices — the cost of money and the size of demand
Lower interest rates → cheaper loans → firms expand; inflation raises input costs

Social

PEOPLE & VALUES
Customs, traditions, values, social trends, literacy, demographics — how society lives and what it expects
Demand for fast food, festivals, clothing follows social customs; literacy raises demand for books

Technological

NEW WAYS TO MAKE & SELL
New techniques, innovations, scientific improvements in products and processes
Smartphones replaced cameras and torches; UPI changed how shops collect money

Political

GOVERNMENT & STABILITY
Political stability, attitude of government towards business, ideology of parties in power
A government that welcomes industry signals calm for investors; sudden policy shifts unsettle plans

Legal

LAWS & COURTS
Legislation passed by Parliament, administrative orders, court judgments — the rules every firm must obey
Compulsory statutory warnings on tobacco packs; the Companies Act; consumer protection law
