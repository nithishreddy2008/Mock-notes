DISEASE QUICK-MAP
Eight common human diseases — pathogen · how it spreads · the one fact exams check

Typhoid

Bacterial
Pathogen: Salmonella typhi
Spread: contaminated food and water
Key fact: confirmed by the Widal test; high fever weeks 1–2

Pneumonia

Bacterial
Pathogen: Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae
Spread: inhaling infected droplets/aerosols
Key fact: alveoli fill with fluid — breathing turns hard

Common cold

Viral
Pathogen: Rhinoviruses (a group of viruses)
Spread: cough/sneeze droplets, contaminated objects
Key fact: nasal congestion, sore throat — clears in 3–7 days

Malaria

Protozoan
Pathogen: Plasmodium vivax, P. falciparum
Spread: bite of infected female Anopheles mosquito
Key fact: recurring fever with chills every 3–4 days

Amoebiasis

Protozoan
Pathogen: Entamoeba histolytica
Spread: faecal contamination of food and water
Key fact: constipation, abdominal pain, cramps, mucus-stool

Ascariasis

Helminthic
Pathogen: Ascaris lumbricoides (round worm)
Spread: water/vegetables soiled with worm eggs
Key fact: internal bleeding, muscular pain, gut blockage

Filariasis

Helminthic
Pathogen: Wuchereria bancrofti (filarial worm)
Spread: bite of infected female Culex mosquito
Key fact: elephantiasis — slow swelling of limbs/genitals

Ringworm

Fungal
Pathogen: Trichophyton rubrum, T. mentagrophytes
Spread: shared towels, clothes, combs (warm, moist skin)
Key fact: dry, scaly, itching lesions with ring edges
