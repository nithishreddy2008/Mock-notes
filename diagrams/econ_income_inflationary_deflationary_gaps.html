MACROECONOMIC DISEQUILIBRIUM: INFLATIONARY & DEFLATIONARY GAPS

FIG 2: FULL-EMPLOYMENT BENCHMARK & POLICY REMEDIES
EXCESS DEMAND Inflationary Gap (Planned AD > Full Employment AS)
Economic Diagnosis: At full employment $Y_F$, planned spending exceeds potential real capacity. Since physical resources are 100% utilized, output cannot expand; excess demand exhausts itself in pure demand-pull inflation.
DEFICIENT DEMAND Deflationary Gap (Planned AD < Full Employment AS)
Economic Diagnosis: At full employment $Y_F$, planned spending falls short of full capacity. Equilibrium establishes at $Y_U < Y_F$, resulting in involuntary unemployment, unsold stocks, and deflationary slump.
REMEDIAL POLICY TOOLKIT Comprehensive Fiscal & Monetary Interventions
CORRECTING EXCESS DEMAND / INFLATIONARY GAP (CONTRACTIONARY)
Fiscal Policy: Cut Government Spending ($G \downarrow$), Raise Taxes ($T \uparrow$), Squeeze Public Borrowings.
Monetary Policy (RBI): Raise Repo Rate ($\uparrow$), Raise CRR & SLR ($\uparrow$), Sell G-Secs (OMOs), Hike Margin Requirements ($\uparrow$).
Macro Outcome: Drains excess purchasing power and credit $\implies AD$ shifts down to $AD_F \implies$ Price stability restored.
CORRECTING DEFICIENT DEMAND / DEFLATIONARY GAP (EXPANSIONARY)
Fiscal Policy: Increase Public Expenditure ($G \uparrow$), Slash Taxes ($T \downarrow$), Borrow to finance deficits.
Monetary Policy (RBI): Lower Repo Rate ($\downarrow$), Lower CRR & SLR ($\downarrow$), Buy G-Secs (OMOs), Reduce Margin Requirements ($\downarrow$).
Macro Outcome: Pumps fresh liquidity and cheap credit $\implies AD$ shifts up to $AD_F \implies$ Full employment restored.
