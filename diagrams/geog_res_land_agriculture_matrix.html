NCERT Class XII Geography

1. Food Grains

Core Staples
Rice (Oryza sativa) Kharif · Aus/Aman/Boro
Thermal/Moisture: 20°C–27°C; >100 cm rainfall; deep clayey/loamy alluvium.
Key States: West Bengal (leading producer), Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar.
Wheat (Triticum) Rabi Season
Thermal/Moisture: 10°C–15°C (sowing), 21°C–26°C (harvest); 75 cm rain / canal irrigation.
Key States: Uttar Pradesh (highest output), Punjab & Haryana (highest per-hectare yields).
Millets (Jowar / Bajra) Kharif / Dryland
Jowar: Rainfed coarse grain; Maharashtra (leading).
Bajra: Drought-tolerant on sandy soils; Rajasthan (leading).
Key States: Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Gujarat, MP.
Pulses (Gram / Tur) Rabi & Kharif
Ecological Role: Leguminous nitrogen-fixers; vital protein staple; dryland rainfed regimes.
Key States: Madhya Pradesh (leading pulses hub), Rajasthan, Maharashtra, UP.

2. Cash & Commercial Crops

Industrial Raw Material
Cotton (White Gold) Kharif · 210 Frost-Free Days
Pedology: Deep moisture-retentive black Regur soils; short-staple (Indian) & long-staple (American/Narma).
Key States: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab.
Jute (Golden Fibre) Kharif · Delta Floodplains
Conditions: Hot & humid climate (>150 cm rain); annual silt-replenished fertile floodplains.
Key States: West Bengal (produces 3/4th of national total), Bihar, Assam (Brahmaputra Valley).
Sugarcane Long Duration (10–12 Mo)
Thermal/Moisture: 21°C–27°C; 75–100 cm rain; high sucrose yield in maritime peninsular climate.
Key States: Uttar Pradesh (highest acreage), Maharashtra & Karnataka (higher recovery rate).
Oilseeds (Groundnut/Mustard) Kharif (Groundnut) · Rabi (Mustard)
Groundnut: Gujarat leading producer.
Rapeseed & Mustard: Rajasthan & MP leading.
Key States: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh.

3. Plantation Crops

Export & Estate Agriculture
Tea (Camellia sinensis) Perennial Shrub
Conditions: Sub-tropical climate; 150–200 cm rain; undulating well-drained slopes; acidic humus soil; shade trees.
Key States: Assam (Brahmaputra & Surma valley — over 50% output), West Bengal (Darjeeling/Jalpaiguri), Tamil Nadu.
Coffee (Arabica & Robusta) Shade Plantation
Varieties: High-grade Arabica (global demand) & hardy Robusta; grown on shaded highland slopes (900–1,800 m).
Key States: Karnataka (over 70% national output: Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan), Kerala, Tamil Nadu.
Natural Rubber Equatorial Plantation
Requirements: Moist & humid climate; >200 cm rainfall; temperatures above 25°C; deep porous red loams.
Key States: Kerala (accounts for ~90% of total Indian production), Tamil Nadu, Tripura.
Spices & Horticulture Diverse Agro-Climates
Spices: Cardamom, black pepper, ginger (Malabar coast).
Fruits: Mango (UP/AP), Apple (J&K/HP), Orange (Nagpur).
Key States: Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra.

4. Agrarian Strategy & Crises

Policy & Transformation
The Green Revolution 1960s Package Technology
Triad Inputs: High Yielding Variety (HYV) Mexican semi-dwarf seeds, assured canal/tubewell irrigation & chemical NPK fertilizers.
Core Impact Belt: Punjab, Haryana, Western Uttar Pradesh; turned India self-sufficient in wheat and rice.

Ecological Degradation

Over-irrigation in Punjab and Haryana caused widespread waterlogging, soil salinization (reh/kallar), and acute depletion of groundwater aquifers.

Structural Vulnerabilities

Extreme landholding fragmentation (>85% small and marginal farmers with <2 hectares), high dependence on unpredictable monsoon, and lack of cold storage chains.

Sustainable Transitions Modern Missions
Initiatives: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (Per Drop More Crop), Soil Health Card, Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF).
Target: Climate-resilient agriculture & income stability.
