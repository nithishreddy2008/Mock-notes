1. Household Food & Fermented Beverages Food Processing

LAB (Lactic Acid Bacteria) Curd

Inoculum of Lactobacillus coagulates milk proteins, increases Vitamin B12, and checks disease-causing microbes in stomach.

Yeasts & Dough Dough / Bread

Saccharomyces cerevisiae produces CO2 puffs in dosa/idli dough. Baker's yeast makes fluffy bread; Brewer's yeast ferments malted cereals.

Swiss & Roquefort Cheese Cheese

Swiss Cheese: Large holes by CO2 from Propionibacterium sharmanii.
Roquefort Cheese: Ripened by fungus Penicillium roqueforti.

Beverage Distillation Rule Ethanol

Non-distilled: Wine & Beer (lower alcohol percentage).
Distilled: Whisky, Brandy, Rum (higher alcohol concentration via fractional distillation).

2. Organic Acids & Industrial Enzymes Biochemicals

Citric & Acetic Acids Acids

Citric Acid: Produced by the fungus Aspergillus niger.
Acetic Acid (Vinegar): Produced by the bacterium Acetobacter aceti.

Butyric & Lactic Acids Acids

Butyric Acid: Bacterium Clostridium butylicum.
Lactic Acid: Bacterium Lactobacillus. Used in food preservation and polymers.

Lipases in Laundry Enzyme

Formulated into commercial detergents to enzymatically hydrolyze lipid fats and remove oily stains from clothes during washing.

Bottled Juice Clarification Enzymes

Commercial bottled fruit juices appear crystal-clear compared to cloudy homemade juice because juices are treated with Pectinases and Proteases.

3. The Sacred Bioactive Molecules (NCERT Trinity) Therapeutics

Streptokinase Clot Buster

Produced by bacterium Streptococcus (genetically modified). Acts as a vital clot buster to dissolve intravascular blood clots in myocardial infarction patients.

Cyclosporin A Immunosuppressant

Extracted from the soil fungus Trichoderma polysporum. Essential immunosuppressive agent that prevents graft rejection in organ transplant patients.

Statins Cholesterol Lowering

Produced by the yeast Monascus purpureus. Competitively inhibits HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme responsible for de novo cholesterol synthesis.

Penicillin Discovery Antibiotic

Discovered accidentally by Alexander Fleming from mold Penicillium notatum inhibiting Staphylococcus. Full clinical potential established by Chain & Florey.

4. Biocontrol Agents & Biofertilisers Ecology & Farming

Natural Predators Biocontrol

Ladybird Beetle: Controls aphids.
Dragonflies: Eradicate mosquito vectors.
Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt): Cry toxin kills butterfly caterpillars upon gut ingestion.

Baculoviruses (NPV) Species-Specific

Genus Nucleopolyhedrovirus. Narrow-spectrum, species-specific insecticidal viruses with zero non-target effects on birds, mammals, or beneficial insects (ideal for IPM).

Nitrogen-Fixing Bacteria Biofertilisers

Symbiotic: Rhizobium in legume root nodules.
Free-Living: Azotobacter (aerobic) and Azospirillum enrich soil nitrogen pool.

Mycorrhiza & Cyanobacteria Biofertilisers

Mycorrhiza (Glomus): Fungal symbiont absorbs phosphorus; confers pathogen/drought resistance.
Cyanobacteria: Anabaena, Nostoc fertilize paddy fields.
