Vocabulary That Runs the Unit

Coordination entity: central metal + ligands in brackets; counter ions sit outside
Ligands: mono (NH₃, Cl⁻), bidentate (en, oxalate), polydentate (EDTA⁴⁻); ambidentate (NO₂⁻, SCN⁻) bite two ways
Coordination number: ligand donor atoms bound — 4 or 6 in this course
Homoleptic vs heteroleptic: one ligand kind vs mixed ([Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ vs [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺)

Naming & Isomerism

Name order: ligands (alphabetical, di/tri/tetra prefixes) + metal + oxidation number in ( ) — cation first, anion last
Anionic metal endings: ferrate, cuprate, zincate, argentate
Structural isomers: ionisation, hydrate, linkage, coordination
Stereo: cis/trans for Ma₄b₂ octahedral + square planar Ma₂b₂; optical for [M(en)₃]³⁺ and cis-Ma₂b₂(en)

Valence Bond Theory

Inner orbital (d²sp³): uses (n−1)d — strong-field ligands, low spin: [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺, [Fe(CN)₆]³⁻
Outer orbital (sp³d²): uses n d — weak-field ligands, high spin: [CoF₆]³⁻
Test: count unpaired electrons magnetically, then match the hybridisation
Limitations: no colour explanation, no Δ₀, treats all ligands alike, clumsy for geometry prediction

Crystal Field Theory

Idea: ligands are point charges; their field splits the d orbitals into low t₂g and high e₂g sets, gap = Δ₀
Strong field: pair up first — low spin; weak field: spread out — high spin
Spectrochemical series: I⁻ < Br⁻ < Cl⁻ < F⁻ < H₂O < NH₃ < en < CN⁻ ≈ CO
Colour: absorbed light = Δ₀; ligand change shifts the colour of [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺
Limitations: ignores covalency, no explanation of ligand order fully, silent on π-bonding

Metal Carbonyls

Ni(CO)₄: tetrahedral, sp³, diamagnetic — Ni(0) d¹⁵
Fe(CO)₅: trigonal bipyramidal, dsp³, Fe(0)
Synergic bond: CO gives σ (carbon lone pair → metal) AND takes π back-donation (metal → CO π*) — a two-way handshake that strengthens both
M–C bond is shorter/stronger than a plain single bond because of that back-donation

Why Coordination Chemistry Matters

Extraction: Ag and Au dissolve in dilute cyanide (Mac-Arthur–Forrest), then displace with Zn
Medicine: cis-platin fights cancer; EDTA treats lead poisoning (chelation therapy)
Life itself: haemoglobin (Fe), chlorophyll (Mg), vitamin B₁₂ (Co)
Analysis: Ni²⁺ confirmed with DMG (bright red), Fe³⁺ with SCN⁻ (blood red), water hardness by EDTA titration

WATCH-OUTS THAT SET MCQ TRAPS

Oxidation number vs coordination number: charge of the metal vs count of donor atoms — not the same
Bidentate counts twice: en or oxalate gives 2 each — [Co(en)₃]³⁺ has CN = 6 with 3 ligands
Linkage isomerism needs ambidentate ligands — NO₂⁻ (nitro/nitrito), SCN⁻ (thiocyanato/isothiocyanato)
