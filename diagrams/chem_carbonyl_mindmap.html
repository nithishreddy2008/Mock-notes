1. Aldehyde & Ketone Syntheses
Selective Routes
Rosenmund Reduction: R-COCl + H₂ over Pd/BaSO₄ (poisoned with S/quinoline) → RCHO. Prevents further reduction to alcohol.
Stephen Reaction: R-CN + SnCl₂/HCl → R-CH=NH·HCl → (H₃O⁺) RCHO.
Etard & Gattermann-Koch: Toluene + CrO₂Cl₂/CS₂ → Benzaldehyde; Benzene + CO/HCl + anhy. AlCl₃/CuCl → Benzaldehyde.
2. Nucleophilic Additions & Derivatives
Reactivity Series
Reactivity: HCHO > CH₃CHO > CH₃COCH₃ > C₆H₅CHO (Steric crowding + +I alkyl electron donation reduce electrophilicity).
Reversible Additions: HCN gives cyanohydrin; NaHSO₃ gives crystalline bisulphite adduct for aldehyde/methyl ketone purification.
Acetals & Ketals: 1 eq ROH gives hemiacetal; 2 eq ROH gives stable acetal. Ethylene glycol gives cyclic ketal (protecting group).
Ammonia Derivatives (H₂N-G): Hydroxylamine → Oxime; Hydrazine → Hydrazone; 2,4-DNP → Brady's hydrazone (Yellow/Orange/Red ppt).
3. Hallmark Name Reactions
Aldol / Cannizzaro / Reductions
Aldol Condensation: Needs ≥ 1 α-H + dil. NaOH. Enolate attack gives β-hydroxyaldehyde; heating eliminates H₂O yielding α,β-unsaturated carbonyl.
Cannizzaro Reaction: Lacks α-H (HCHO, PhCHO) + 50% NaOH. Disproportionates: 1 mol reduced to alcohol, 1 mol oxidized to carboxylate salt.
Clemmensen vs Wolff-Kishner: Zn-Hg/conc. HCl (acidic) vs NH₂NH₂/KOH/glycol (basic). Both deoxygenate C=O directly to -CH₂-.
4. Diagnostic Distinction Tests
CUET Fingerprints
Tollens' Test: [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺ OH⁻ reduces to shiny Silver Mirror (Ag↓). Positive for ALL aldehydes (aliphatic + aromatic) + HCOOH! Ketones fail.
Fehling's Test: Alkaline Cu²⁺ tartrate reduces to red-brown Cu₂O↓. Positive for aliphatic aldehydes ONLY. Benzaldehyde fails!
Iodoform Test: I₂ + NaOH gives yellow CHI₃↓ (antiseptic odour). Positive for CH₃C=O and CH₃CH(OH)- groups (Ethanal, methyl ketones, ethanol).
5. Carboxylic Acid Syntheses & Acidity
pKa & Resonance
Synthesis: 1° Alcohol / Aldehyde oxidation (KMnO₄/acid); Nitrile hydrolysis (R-CN + 2H₂O/H⁺ → RCOOH + NH₄⁺); Grignard + CO₂ (dry ice).
Carboxylate Resonance: RCOO⁻ has 2 strictly equivalent resonance structures with negative charge on electronegative oxygens (equal C-O lengths: 127 pm).
Acidity Trends: -I groups increase acidity; +I alkyl groups decrease acidity:
CF₃COOH > CCl₃COOH > CHCl₂COOH > FCH₂COOH > ClCH₂COOH > HCOOH > C₆H₅COOH > CH₃COOH.
6. Acid Reactions & Decarboxylation
HVZ & Soda-Lime
Derivative Formations: + PCl₅ / SOCl₂ → Acyl Chloride (RCOCl); + R'OH/H⁺ → Ester (Fischer); + Δ/P₂O₅ → Acid Anhydride.
HVZ α-Halogenation: R-CH₂-COOH + X₂ / red P → R-CH(X)-COOH. Replaces α-H with halogen. Formic and benzoic acids fail (no α-H).
Decarboxylation: R-COONa + Soda-lime (NaOH + CaO, 3:1), Δ → R-H + Na₂CO₃. Yields alkane with one fewer carbon atom. Kolbe electrolysis gives dimer alkane.
MASTER VERDICT Rosenmund makes aldehydes; Aldol requires α-H; Cannizzaro lacks α-H; Benzaldehyde fails Fehling's; Carboxylic acids release CO₂ from NaHCO₃.
