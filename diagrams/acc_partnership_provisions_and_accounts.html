Partnership Fundamentals: Statutory Defaults, Capital Architectures & Accounting Duality

Indian Partnership Act 1932 Rules • Fixed vs Fluctuating Ledger Systems • Charges vs Appropriations of Profit

CUET UG Accountancy • Unit I
Indian Partnership Act 1932 Deed Silence
PSR Default: Shared Equally (regardless of capital)
Partner's Loan Interest: Strictly 6% p.a. (Charge)
IOC / IOD / Salary: Strictly NIL
  • Capital Irrelevance: Profits are divided equally even if Partner A invested ₹10 Lakhs and Partner B invested ₹10,000.
  • The 6% Misattribution Trap: The 6% default rate applies only to partner loans; it never applies to partner capital balances.
  • Inspection Rights: Every partner has an absolute right to access, inspect, and copy all books of account of the firm.
Min Partners: 2 Max Partners: 50 (Companies Act)
Capital Account Systems Ledger Architecture
Fixed: Capital A/c (Fixed) + Current A/c (Variable)
Fluctuating: Combined Capital A/c for all adjustments
Default System: Fluctuating Capital (unless specified)
  • Fixed Integrity: Partner's Capital A/c never changes unless fresh capital is introduced or capital is permanently withdrawn against capital.
  • Balance Signs: Under fixed system, Current Account can show a Debit balance (if drawings & losses exceed entitlements).
  • Balance Sheet Placement: Debit balance of Partner's Current A/c is shown on the Asset side of the firm's Balance Sheet.
Drawings: Out of Capital vs Profit IOC on Opening Capital
Charge vs Appropriation Accounting Duality
P&L Account: Houses all Charges (Net Profit outcome)
P&L Approp. Account: Houses all Appropriations
Insufficient Profit: Distribute in ratio of appropriations!
  • Charge Primacy: Net Profit transferred to P&L Appropriation must be calculated after deducting all charges (Loan Interest, Rent, Manager Commission).
  • Inadequate Profit Rule: If net profit is less than total appropriations, profit is distributed among partners in the ratio of their respective appropriations!
  • Net Loss Scenario: In case of net loss, NO appropriations (IOC, Salary) are provided unless deed explicitly makes them a charge.
