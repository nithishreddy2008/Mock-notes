Percentages — the Foundation

Fraction anchors: 50% = 1/2 · 25% = 1/4 · 20% = 1/5 · 12.5% = 1/8 — fractions are faster than decimals
Change formula: % change = (change ÷ ORIGINAL) × 100 — the base is always the old value
Successive changes: +20% then −20% is NOT zero — it is a 4% loss

Ratios & Averages

Ratio division: total ÷ sum of parts = one part; multiply back for shares
Average: total ÷ count; totals move by the value added or removed
One-out trick: new average = (old total − removed) ÷ remaining count

Reading Tables & Charts

Frame first: title, axes, units — before any bar or cell
Question next: it names the rows and columns you need
Then compute with exact values; sanity-check against the picture

Statistics Interpretation

Mean: the balance point — dragged by outliers
Median: the middle of the SORTED list — outlier-proof
Mode: the most frequent value; range = max − min

The Speed Toolkit

10% trick: 10% of N = N ÷ 10; build 5%, 15%, 20% from it
Ratio share check: shares must sum to the total — instant verification
Sorted before median: sorting is compulsory; the middle of an unsorted list means nothing

Scoring Habits

Estimate first: know roughly where the answer must land, then compute
Units and bases: every % question names its base — find it before dividing
Marking maths: +5 right, −1 wrong — computed answers only, no number-hope

Traps that catch most students

Successive percentage netting to zero — +20% then −20% loses 4%
Dividing the change by the NEW value — the base is the original
Median without sorting — 3, 9, 5 has median 5, not 9
Chart questions answered by eye — exact questions need exact values
