NATIONAL INCOME ACCOUNTING
COURSE II MACRO · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Macroeconomics & Key Pairs

Macro studies economy-wide totals: GDP, inflation, unemployment — born from Keynes and the Great Depression
Stock vs flow: wealth, capital, water in a tank = STOCK (a moment); income, investment, rainfall = FLOW (a period)
Final vs intermediate: bread is final; flour inside the bread is intermediate — only final goods enter GDP
Circular flow: producer's expense = household's income = spending on goods — one loop, three views

Domestic vs National; the NFIA Bridge

Domestic (D): produced INSIDE the territory — by anyone, Indian or not
National (N): produced by NORMAL RESIDENTS — anywhere on earth
NFIA = factor income earned abroad − factor income paid to abroad
GNP = GDP + NFIA; for India NFIA is slightly negative, so GNP < GDP

Three Methods (one answer)

Product/Value added: Σ GVA = output − intermediate inputs; kills double counting
Income: rent + wages + interest + profit (COMPENSATION of employees, operating surplus, mixed income); NO transfer payments
Expenditure: GDP = C + I + G + (X − M); final spending only — no second-hand goods, no shares

The Aggregates Ladder

Net = Gross − depreciation: NDP = GDP − dep; NNP = GNP − dep
Factor cost vs market price: FC = MP − indirect taxes + subsidies (NIT)
National Income (NI) ≡ NNP at FC — the exam's headline identity
Real vs nominal: real = current output × BASE-year prices; nominal uses current prices; GDP deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100

Precautions (method-specific)

Product: no intermediate goods; own-account production counts; imputed rent of owner-homes counts
Income: no transfers, no windfalls (lottery), no sale of second-hand goods or shares
Expenditure: no intermediate spending, no second-hand purchases, no pure financial deals

GDP and Welfare — the fine print

GDP misses: non-market work (home cooking), distribution (who got it), externalities (pollution), leisure, underground activity
Real GDP growth ≠ welfare growth — clean air and free time never enter the ledger
Externalities: a factory's output counts, its smoke does not — GDP can rise while welfare falls

Traps that catch most students

National Income = NNP at factor cost — every conversion question is a two-step bridge (depreciation, then NIT)
Transfers and second-hand sales never enter any method — they reshuffle money, they create nothing
GDP deflator moves with prices only — real GDP moves with quantities; the deflator is the price index linking them
Domestic ≠ national: an Indian worker's salary in Dubai is national but not domestic; a Japanese car plant in Gujarat is the reverse
