Comprehensive synoptic map covering Communication Levels, Theoretical Frameworks, Cultural Dynamics, and Evolutionary History

Master Mindmap · CUET UG 318
📡 1. Communication Levels & Foundations
  • Intrapersonal: Cognitive internal monologue, self-concept, sensory processing.
  • Interpersonal: Dyadic dialogue, immediate bidirectional feedback, non-verbal richness.
  • Group Communication: Cohesion, collective decision-making, groupthink vulnerabilities.
  • Mass Communication: Institutional sender, mediated transmission, vast heterogeneous audience, delayed/indirect feedback.
📐 2. Transmission & Linear Models
  • Aristotle (300 BCE): Speaker → Speech → Occasion → Audience → Effect (Ethos, Pathos, Logos).
  • Lasswell (1948): 5 Ws unilinear formula; foundation of media effects and propaganda analysis.
  • Shannon-Weaver (1949): Mathematical information theory; introduced physical/mechanical Noise.
  • Berlo's SMCR (1960): Source-Message-Channel-Receiver; focuses on human senses and socio-cultural parity.
🔄 3. Circular & Interactive Paradigms
  • Osgood-Schramm (1954): Circular reciprocity; dual roles of Encoder/Decoder; Field of Experience.
  • Newcomb's ABX Model: Co-orientation and psychological equilibrium between two communicators about topic X.
  • Westley & MacLean Model: Gatekeeper role (C) filtering sensory environment (X) for consumer (B).
  • Gerbner's Model: Dual dimension (Perceptual and Operational); subjective reality vs. media message.
🎭 4. Culture, Ideology & Representation
  • Cultural Transmission: Media as vehicle reproducing cultural hegemony, values, and socialization.
  • Stuart Hall's Model: Three decoding positions: Dominant-Hegemonic, Negotiated, Oppositional.
  • Stereotyping & Tropes: Reductionist framing of gender, caste, minorities, and marginalized bodies.
  • Cultural Imperialism: Asymmetric flow of western media programming altering indigenous cultural identities.
🌱 5. Media & Social Change Dynamics
  • Diffusion of Innovations: Rogers' 5 adopter categories: Innovators, Early Adopters, Early/Late Majority, Laggards.
  • Development Support Comm: Daniel Lerner, Wilbur Schramm; media as national development accelerator.
  • SITE (1975-76): NASA ATS-6 satellite beaming education to 2,400 Indian villages; rural empowerment benchmark.
  • Kheda Project (1975): Decentralized community participatory television in Gujarat; rural empowerment.
🇮🇳 6. Evolutionary History of Indian Media
  • Print Press: James Hicky's Bengal Gazette (1780); Raja Ram Mohan Roy's socio-religious reform press.
  • Radio Broadcasting: Radio Club of Bombay (1923), AIR (1936), Akashvani nomenclature (1956), Vividh Bharati (1957).
  • Television History: Experimental Delhi launch (1959), Krishi Darshan (1967), DD autonomy/split (1976), Color TV (1982).
  • Digital Dawn: VSNL internet rollout (1995), Cable TV explosion (1991), OTT streaming and social media convergence.
