UNIT VI · CAS MASTER MINDMAP
1. Electronic Spreadsheets Spreadsheet Engine
  • ▸Cell Referencing: Relative (A1), Absolute ($A$1), Mixed ($A1 or A$1).
  • ▸Financial Functions: PMT(rate, nper, pv), PV, FV, RATE, NPER.
  • ▸Depreciation Functions: SLN(cost, salvage, life); DB(cost, salvage, life, period).
  • ▸Logic & Lookups: IF(cond, val_true, val_false), VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP.
Monthly Loan Rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12; Total Periods = Years × 12!
2. Payroll & Amortisation Financial Modeling
  • ▸Gross Earnings: Basic Pay + DA + HRA + Transport + Special Allowances.
  • ▸Statutory Deductions: PF (12% of Basic + DA) + ESI + Professional Tax + TDS.
  • ▸Net Salary: Gross Salary − Total Deductions. Paid via Bank Transfer.
  • ▸Loan EMI: Fixed PMT = Interest Component (IPMT) + Principal (PPMT).
In loan amortisation, interest decreases while principal repayment increases each month!

3. DBMS & SQL Accounting Relational Engine
  • ▸Tables & Tuples: Tables (Entities), Rows (Tuples/Records), Columns (Fields/Attributes).
  • ▸Key Constraints: Primary Key (Unique, Not Null); Foreign Key (Referential integrity).
  • ▸DDL Commands: CREATE TABLE, ALTER TABLE, DROP TABLE (Structure).
  • ▸DML Commands: SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE (Data rows).
WHERE filters individual records; HAVING filters aggregated groups!
4. Security & Audit Trail Internal Controls
  • ▸Access Controls: Segregation of duties: Data Entry Clerk vs Supervisor vs Auditor.
  • ▸Audit Trail: Immutable log tracking who created, approved, or edited each voucher.
  • ▸Data Vault & Encryption: Passwords and sensitive ledger balances stored encrypted.
  • ▸Backups & Disaster Recovery: Offsite cloud storage to guard against hardware crashes.
Companies Act mandates an unalterable audit trail feature in accounting software!
