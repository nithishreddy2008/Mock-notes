The Big Four Abiotic Factors

Temperature: eurythermal = wide tolerance · stenothermal = narrow (both names explained in S1)
Water: salinity ≈ 5‰ inland waters vs 30–35‰ sea — halophiles handle salt
Light: photoperiod times flowering & migration; deep sea lives without it
Soil: texture + composition decide which plants, and so which animals, live there

Responses & Adaptations

Regulate: hold a steady inside state — sweat, shiver (mammals, birds)
Conform: body follows the outside temperature (most invertebrates)
Migrate: move away for the season — Siberian birds fly to Bharatpur
Suspend: pause life — hibernation (winter), aestivation (summer), diapause (insects)

Population Attributes

Birth & death rate: per-capita numbers — 20 births per 1,000 → 0.02 per year
Sex ratio: the females-to-males share in the population
Age pyramids: wide base = growing · even sides = stable · narrow base = declining
Size when counting fails: use % cover or biomass instead of head-counts

Growth Models

Exponential: dN/dt = rN → J-curve, no limits — crashes follow
Nt = N0 × ert — r = intrinsic rate of natural increase
Logistic: dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K → S-curve capped by K (carrying capacity)
Fastest at K/2; r-strategists vs K-strategists live by different rules

Life History Variation

Breed once (bamboo, Pacific salmon) vs many times (birds, mammals)
Age at first breeding: earlier or later, set by habitat pressures
Clutch size trade-off: few-precious young vs many-cheap eggs
Energy budget decides every trade-off a species makes

Population Interactions — 6 Ways

Mutualism +/+ (lichen, fig–wasp) · Competition −/− (Gause's Paramecium)
Predation +/− (tiger–deer) · Parasitism +/− (lice, tapeworm)
Commensalism +/0 (cattle egret + cattle) · Amensalism −/0 (Penicillium)
Same niche → exclusion; partition the niche → coexist

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Conformers do NOT hold a steady body temperature — regulators do
Exponential ignores K; logistic slows near K and stops at K
Commensalism = +/0, not +/− — and amensalism = −/0 (causer gains nothing)
Cuckoo–crow is brood parasitism (+/−), never commensalism
