1. Continuity & Diff
Foundations
Continuity at x = c:
• f(c) is defined.
• limx→c⁻ f(x) = limx→c⁺ f(x) = f(c).
Graph is unbroken without lifting pen.
Differentiability at c:
Left derivative = Right derivative:
Lf'(c) = Rf'(c).
Smooth tangent; no sharp corner/kink.
Fundamental Theorem:
Diff ⇒ Continuous (True!)
Continuous does NOT imply differentiable (e.g. |x| at 0).
Mean Value Theorems:
• Rolle's: f(a)=f(b) ⇒ f'(c) = 0
• LMVT: f'(c) = [f(b)-f(a)]/(b-a)
Algebra: sum, product, quotient of continuous functions are continuous.
2. Derivative Toolkit
Rules
Chain Rule:
dy/dx = (dy/du) · (du/dx)
Implicit Differentiation:
Differentiate term-by-term with respect to x, applying d/dx[yⁿ] = n yⁿ⁻¹ (dy/dx).
Logarithmic Differentiation:
For y = u(x)v(x):
Take ln y = v ln u, then differentiate.
Parametric & 2nd Derivative Trap:
• dy/dx = (dy/dt) / (dx/dt)
• d²y/dx² = [d/dt(dy/dx)] / (dx/dt)
(Always multiply by dt/dx!).
Inverse trig: d/dx[tan⁻¹ x] = 1/(1+x²); d/dx[sin⁻¹ x] = 1/√(1-x²).
3. Rates & Tangents
Geometry
Rate of Change:
dy/dt = (dy/dx) · (dx/dt)
• Sphere: dV/dt = 4π r² (dr/dt)
• Circle: dA/dt = 2π r (dr/dt)
Tangent Line at (x₀, y₀):
Slope: m = [dy/dx]|₍ₓ₀,ᵧ₀₎
Equation: y - y₀ = m (x - x₀)
• Horizontal: dy/dx = 0
• Vertical: dx/dy = 0
Normal Line at (x₀, y₀):
Slope: m_N = -1 / m
Equation: y - y₀ = (-1/m)(x - x₀)
Orthogonal Curves:
Intersect at right angles ⇔ m₁ · m₂ = -1.
Parallel to x-axis ⇒ slope m = 0; parallel to y-axis ⇒ 1/m = 0.
4. Monotonicity & Max/Min
Optimization
Strictly Increasing / Decreasing:
• f'(x) > 0 on (a, b) ⇒ Strictly Increasing
• f'(x) < 0 on (a, b) ⇒ Strictly Decreasing
Critical points: f'(x) = 0 or undefined.
First Derivative Test:
• + to - ⇒ Local Max
• - to + ⇒ Local Min
• No sign change ⇒ Point of Inflection
Second Derivative Test:
• f'(c) = 0 & f''(c) < 0 ⇒ Local Max
• f'(c) = 0 & f''(c) > 0 ⇒ Local Min
Absolute Extrema on [a, b]:
Test critical points AND endpoints a, b.
Word problems: express objective in ONE variable before differentiating!
