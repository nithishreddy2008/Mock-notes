Continuity & Differentiability

Continuous at c: f(c) exists, the limit exists, and they are equal
Differentiable at c: left derivative = right derivative — no corner
The arrow: differentiable ⇒ continuous, never the reverse
Exhibit A: |x| at 0 — continuous, undifferentiable, unforgettable

The Toolkit (six ways in)

Chain rule: peel outside-in, dy/dx = dy/du · du/dx
Inverse trig: sin⁻¹x → 1/√(1−x²), tan⁻¹x → 1/(1+x²)
Implicit: differentiate both sides, collect dy/dx
Exp / log: eˣ → eˣ, ln x → 1/x, aˣ → aˣ ln a
Log diff: ln both sides first — the xˣ move
Parametric: dy/dx = (dy/dt) / (dx/dt)

Second-Order Derivatives

Notation: d²y/dx² = f''(x) — differentiate the derivative again
Signature fact: y = A sin x + B cos x always satisfies y'' + y = 0
Use: concave up (f'' > 0) vs concave down (f'' < 0)

Rate of Change

Meaning: dy/dx is how fast y answers to x
Time chain: dA/dt = (dA/dr) · (dr/dt)
Balloon: dA/dt = 2πr · dr/dt — r = 10, dr/dt = 2 gives 40π

Increasing & Decreasing

f' > 0 climbing · f' < 0 descending on the interval
Routine: domain → f' → critical points → sign chart
x³ − 12x: up on (−∞,−2], down on [−2,2], up on [2,∞)

Maxima & Minima

Candidates: f'(x) = 0 or f' undefined — points of suspicion only
First-derivative test: watch f' change sign across the point
Second-derivative test: f'' < 0 peak, f'' > 0 valley, f'' = 0 undecided
Word problems: turn the sentence into one function, then optimise

TOP TRAPS

f'(c) = 0 alone proves nothing — inflections pass the test too
Chain rule: the inner derivative is the most-forgotten factor in the paper
cos⁻¹x flips the sign: −1/√(1−x²)
