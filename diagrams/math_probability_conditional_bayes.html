Foundation
Conditional Probability
Sample Space Reduction
P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
Occurs given that event $B$ has already transpired ($P(B) > 0$). Event $B$ replaces universe $S$ as the new reduced sample space.
Multiplication Theorem
P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B|A) = P(B) · P(A|B)
Generalizes to 3 events: $P(E \cap F \cap G) = P(E) \cdot P(F|E) \cdot P(G|E \cap F)$. Fundamental rule for sequential multi-stage experiments.
Core Conditional Invariants
• Certain Event: $P(S|B) = 1$ and $P(B|B) = 1$.
• Complement Rule: $P(A'|B) = 1 - P(A|B)$.
• Additive Law: $P((E \cup F)|B) = P(E|B) + P(F|B) - P((E \cap F)|B)$.
Independence
Independence vs Mutually Exclusive
Definition of Independence
P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B)
Occurrence of $B$ gives zero information about occurrence of $A$: $P(A|B) = P(A)$ and $P(B|A) = P(B)$.
Companion Independence Theorems
If $A$ and $B$ are independent, then each of the following pairs is also strictly independent:
1. A and B': $P(A \cap B') = P(A) \cdot P(B')$.
2. A' and B: $P(A' \cap B) = P(A') \cdot P(B)$.
3. A' and B': $P(A' \cap B') = P(A') \cdot P(B')$.
Probability of At Least One
P(A ∪ B) = 1 - P(A') · P(B')
Supreme speed shortcut: For $n$ independent events, the probability that at least one occurs is $1 - \prod_{k=1}^n P(E_k')$.
Inversion Engine
Total Probability & Bayes' Rule
E₁ (Prior) ── P(A|E₁) ──▸ P(E₁) · P(A|E₁)
E₂ (Prior) ── P(A|E₂) ──▸ P(E₂) · P(A|E₂)
E₃ (Prior) ── P(A|E₃) ──▸ P(E₃) · P(A|E₃)
Theorem of Total Probability
P(A) = ∑ P(Eᵢ) · P(A|Eᵢ)
Computes total likelihood of event $A$ by summing over all mutually disjoint hypothesis pathways $E_1, E_2, \dots, E_n$.
Bayes' Posterior Rule
P(Eᵢ|A) = [P(Eᵢ)P(A|Eᵢ)] / ∑ [P(Eⱼ)P(A|Eⱼ)]
Calculates reverse probability: Given that outcome $A$ has occurred, what is the probability that it arose from hypothesis $E_i$?
Mutually Exclusive vs Independent
• Mutually Exclusive: $A \cap B = \emptyset \implies P(A \cap B) = 0$. They cannot occur together.
• Independent: $P(A \cap B) = P(A)P(B) \ne 0$.
• Two events with $P(A) > 0, P(B) > 0$ can NEVER be both mutually exclusive and independent!
Partition of Sample Space
• Events $E_1, E_2, \dots, E_n$ form a valid partition of S if:
1. Pairwise Disjoint: $E_i \cap E_j = \emptyset$ for all $i \ne j$.
2. Exhaustive: $\bigcup_{i=1}^n E_i = S$.
3. Non-Zero Likelihood: $P(E_i) > 0$ for all $i$.
Bayes' Problem Identification
• Look for: "Given that the patient tested positive...", "Knowing that the defective bolt was drawn...".
• Numerator: Weight of the specific pathway asked for.
• Denominator: Sum of weights of ALL plausible pathways producing $A$.
