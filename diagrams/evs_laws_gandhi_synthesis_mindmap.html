CONSTITUTIONAL & STATUTORY ACTS
  • Constitutional Mandates: Art 48A (State duty), Art 51A(g) (Citizen duty), Art 21 (Clean environment as Fundamental Right).
  • Wildlife Act (WPA 1972): 6 schedules (rationalized to 4 in 2022); NTCA, CZA, strict anti-poaching penalties.
  • Pollution Acts: Water Act 1974 & Air Act 1981 established CPCB/SPCBs; noise added to Air Act in 1987.
  • Environment (Protection) Act 1986: Umbrella Act enacted post-Bhopal (1984); power to set discharge standards.
MULTILATERAL GLOBAL ACCORDS
  • Stockholm 1972: Birth of UNEP; Principle 21 state responsibility for transboundary environmental damage.
  • Montreal 1987 & Kigali 2016: Phased out CFCs; Kigali targets 80–85% cut in warming HFCs by 2045.
  • Rio 1992 Earth Summit: Agenda 21, Rio Declaration; spawned UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD conventions.
  • Climate Regimes: Kyoto 1997 (Annex I binding targets); Paris 2015 (NDCs to limit warming well below 2°C).
JUDICIAL DOCTRINES & NGT
  • Absolute Liability: MC Mehta v UOI (1987, Oleum Gas); no Rylands v Fletcher exceptions for hazardous industries.
  • Public Trust Doctrine: MC Mehta v Kamal Nath (1997, Span Motels); State holds natural resources in trust for citizens.
  • Precautionary & Polluter Pays: Vellore Citizens (1996); lack of full scientific certainty cannot excuse inaction.
  • National Green Tribunal (NGT Act 2010): Fast-track environmental justice (mandated disposal within 6 months).
GANDHIAN ETHICS & MOVEMENTS
  • Core Philosophy: "Earth satisfies need, not greed"; Sarvodaya (uplift of all), Ahimsa towards all living beings.
  • Trusteeship & Swaraj: Decentralized village autonomy (Gram Swaraj); humans as caretakers, not exploiters.
  • Chipko Movement (1973): Chamoli (Uttarakhand); hugging trees; led by Sunderlal Bahuguna, Gaura Devi, Chandi Prasad Bhatt.
  • Appiko (1983) & NBA: Western Ghats tree protection; Narmada Bachao Andolan led by Medha Patkar on dam rehabilitation.
