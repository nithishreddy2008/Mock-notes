Jammu & Kashmir Theatre
Accession, Special Autonomy & Cross-Border Militancy
Accession & Article 370 1947–1953
  • Instrument of Accession (26 Oct 1947): Signed by Maharaja Hari Singh following Pakistani tribal raider invasion.
  • Article 370: Granted special constitutional status; Parliament's jurisdiction restricted to Defense, External Affairs, and Communications.
  • Sheikh Abdullah Reforms: Land to the tiller without compensation; dismissed and arrested in August 1953 over autonomy disputes.
Hari Singh Article 370
Accords & Rise of Insurgency 1974–1989
  • Indira-Sheikh Accord (1974): Sheikh Abdullah renounced plebiscite demand; became Chief Minister with National Conference.
  • 1987 Assembly Elections: Widespread allegations of electoral rigging against the National Conference-Congress coalition.
  • Cross-Border Insurgency (1989): Eruption of armed militancy supported by Pakistan; exodus of Kashmiri Pandits from the Valley.
1974 Accord 1989 Militancy
Core Fact: Sheikh Abdullah signed the 1974 Accord with Indira Gandhi, dropping plebiscite demands to join democratic governance.
2
The Punjab Crisis & Accords
Anandpur Resolution, Operation Blue Star & Peace
From Autonomy to Extremism 1973–1984
  • Anandpur Sahib Resolution (1973): Akali Dal demanded federal restructuring, river water redistribution, and greater autonomy.
  • Rise of Extremism: Militant faction led by Jarnail Singh Bhindranwale fortified the Golden Temple complex.
  • Operation Blue Star (June 1984): Military assault to flush out militants; caused severe damage to Akal Takht and deep emotional trauma.
  • 1984 Aftermath: Indira Gandhi assassinated (31 Oct 1984) by Sikh bodyguards; widespread anti-Sikh violence in Delhi and northern India.
Anandpur 1973 Blue Star June 1984
Rajiv-Longowal Punjab Accord July 1985
  • Signatories: PM Rajiv Gandhi and Akali Dal President Sant Harchand Singh Longowal.
  • Key Terms: Transfer of Chandigarh to Punjab, tribunal for Ravi-Beas river waters, rehabilitation of military detainees.
  • Assassination: Longowal was assassinated by extremists a month later; full peace restored in mid-1990s under KPS Gill.
July 1985 Accord Nanavati Commission
CUET Distinction: The Punjab Accord was signed with moderate leader Harchand Singh Longowal, NOT with Bhindranwale.

North-East Autonomy & Accords
Tribal Reorganisation, Mizo Peace & Assam Accord
Tribal Reorganisation & Nagas 1963–1987
  • Composite Assam: Reorganised into Nagaland (1963), Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura (1972), and Mizoram, Arunachal (1987).
  • Naga Struggle: Naga National Council (NNC) under Angami Zapu Phizo demanded independent sovereignty from 1947.
  • Mizo Accord (1986): 1959 Mautam famine triggered insurgency by Mizo National Front (MNF) under Laldenga; signed 1986 peace pact.
Phizo (NNC) Laldenga (MNF) 1986
Assam Movement & 1985 Accord AASU to AGP
  • Anti-Foreigner Agitation (1979–85): All Assam Students Union (AASU) led mass stir against illegal Bangladesh migrants.
  • Assam Accord (15 Aug 1985): Rajiv Gandhi & AASU; cutoff date of 24 March 1971 for detection and deportation.
  • Birth of AGP: AASU student leaders formed Asom Gana Parishad (AGP) in 1985, sweeping state assembly elections under Mahanta.
