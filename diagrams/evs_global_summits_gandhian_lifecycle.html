FIGURE 2: POLICY & PHILOSOPHY TIMELINE
MILESTONE 1 1972
Stockholm Conference (UNCHE)
First major UN conference on environment. Established UNEP and coined "Only One Earth". Directly led to India's 42nd Amendment (Arts 48A & 51A(g)) and Wildlife Act.
Key Output: Principle 21 (State Sovereignty + No Harm Rule) & UNEP formation.
MILESTONE 2 1987 / 2016
Montreal Protocol & Kigali
Most successful environmental treaty. Phased out CFCs/halons (ODS). Kigali Amendment (2016) mandates phase-down of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) to avert 0.5°C warming.
Core Principle: Common But Differentiated Responsibilities (CBDR).
MILESTONE 3 1992
Rio Earth Summit (UNCED)
Seminal Rio Declaration (27 principles) and Agenda 21. Birthed the Rio Triple Conventions: UNFCCC, CBD (Biodiversity), and UNCCD (Desertification). Formulated Sustainable Dev.
Doctrines: Precautionary Principle (Principle 15) & Polluter Pays (Principle 16).
MILESTONE 4 1997 / 2015
Kyoto Protocol to Paris Agreement
Kyoto: Legally binding emission reduction targets for Annex I (CDM, carbon trading). Paris 2015: Bottom-up NDCs targeting well below 2°C, pursuing 1.5°C limit above pre-industrial levels.
India NDC: 50% non-fossil electric capacity & Net-Zero target by 2070.
ETHICS 1 1909–1948
Gandhian Ecological Philosophy
"Earth provides enough for every man's need, but not for every man's greed." Rooted in Ahimsa (non-violence), Sarvodaya (welfare of all), Trusteeship, and decentralized Gram Swaraj.
Philosophy: Voluntary simplicity, cyclical resource use, inter-species ethics.
ETHICS 2 1970s–1980s
Grassroots Eco-Movements
Chipko Movement (1973, Chamoli, Sunderlal Bahuguna, Gaura Devi) & Appiko (1983, Western Ghats, Pandurang Hegde). Narmada Bachao Andolan (Medha Patkar, Baba Amte) on dams & displacement.
Core Model: Non-violent satyagraha defending community common property (CPR).
