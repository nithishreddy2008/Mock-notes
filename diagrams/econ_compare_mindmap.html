INDIA · CHINA · PAKISTAN
DEVELOPMENT EXPERIENCES COMPARED · COURSE III IED

Why Compare at All?

Shared starting line: all three began low-income, agrarian and poor — with planning and public sectors
Divergent roads: democratic mixed economy (India), one-party market transition (China), mixed with aid and remittances (Pakistan)
The lesson: outcomes diverge because POLICY diverges — timing, speed and people-focus

Strategies — Three Roads

India: planning + import substitution; reforms in 1991 under crisis; LPG gradualism
China: central planning and communes; 1978 reforms — agriculture decollectivised, SEZs, FDI, fast industrialisation
Pakistan: agriculture-centred mixed economy; foreign aid and remittance support; IMF adjustment in the late 1980s

Growth & Sectoral Engines

China: manufacturing-led — the export factory; fastest sustained growth of the three
India: services-led — IT and services jumped; manufacturing skipped a stage
Pakistan: agriculture historically dominant, then services; industry remains thin

Population & Demographics

China: strict one-child policy tamed growth fast — now ageing
India: voluntary family planning — still a young country (demographic dividend)
Pakistan: faster population growth keeps straining resources

Human Development Indicators

China leads: highest life expectancy, literacy and income among the three
India middle: ahead of Pakistan, behind China; gender gaps persist
Pakistan weakest: lowest literacy and life expectancy of the three

Lessons for India

From China: invest early in people; SEZs and infrastructure; speed with delivery
From Pakistan's cautionary tale: don't lean on remittances; build industry and education
India's edge to keep: democracy, services strength, demographic dividend

Traps that catch most students

Reform order: China (1978) → Pakistan (~1988) → India (1991) — India was LAST, not first
China's engine is manufacturing, India's is services — never swap them
One-child policy = China, not a general South Asian story
Similar starts ≠ similar fates — strategy choices made the divergence
