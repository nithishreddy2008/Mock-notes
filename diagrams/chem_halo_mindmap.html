HALOALKANES & HALOARENES
UNIT VI · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Classification & the C–X Bond

By carbon type: 1°/2°/3° (sp³), allylic–benzylic, vinylic–aryl (sp²)
C–X bond: polar (X more electronegative) but not ionic enough to dissolve in water
Bond length grows, enthalpy shrinks: C–F strongest/shortest → C–I weakest/longest — RI is the most reactive
Dipole quirk: CH₃Cl > CH₃F (C–F's shortness offsets its polarity)

Preparation Toolkit

From alcohols: HX (Lucas: ZnCl₂+HCl fastest for 3°), PX₃/PX₅, and best: SOCl₂ (clean gases escape)
From hydrocarbons: UV halogenation (mixture), electrophilic substitution on arenes (o/p mix, FeX₃ catalyst)
Halide exchange: Finkelstein (NaI in acetone: RCl → RI), Swarts (AgF/Hg₂F₂: RCl → RF)
From amines (arenes): Sandmeyer (Cu₂X₂/diazonium), Balz-Schiemann (HBF₄ for ArF), Gattermann (Cu powder)

Reactions of Haloalkanes

S⁽2: one concerted backside step; rate = k[RX][Nu]; inversion; methyl > 1° > 2° > 3°
S⁽1: carbocation first; rate = k[RX]; racemisation; 3° > 2° > 1°
Elimination: alcoholic KOH, Saytzeff's alkene; competes with S⁽
With metals: Grignard RMgX in dry ether; Wurtz (Na, R–R), Fittig + Wurtz-Fittig for arenes

Physical Properties

Boiling point: RI > RBr > RCl > RF (mass + London forces); isomers: 1° > 2° > 3° (branching shrinks the surface)
Density: bromides/iodides heavier than water; density falls as the chain grows
Solubility: insoluble in water — they cannot H-bond; soluble in organic solvents
Melting point oddity: para-isomers melt HIGHER than ortho (symmetry packs better)

Haloarenes: The Reluctant Ones

Why aryl C–X refuses S⁽: resonance gives double-bond character; sp² carbon holds s-character tightly; phenyl cation is unstable; the ring repels backside attack
Still do EAS: halogen is o,p-directing though deactivating (its resonance donates, its −I withdraws)
Dow process: chlorobenzene + NaOH at 623 K / 300 atm → phenol (harsh conditions prove the reluctance)
Nitro rescues S⁽Ar: o/p-NO₂ groups pull electrons out — 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene hydrolyses gently

Polyhalogen Compounds

CH₂Cl₂ (DCM): paint remover, degreaser; CHCl₃: anaesthetic of the past → phosgene COCl₂ + HCl in air (store in dark bottles)
CHI₃ (iodoform): yellow antiseptic powder; CCl₄: fire extinguisher, cleaning fluid (toxic → phosgene on hot metal)
Freons (CFCs): refrigerants that shred ozone — banned by the Montreal Protocol
DDT: malaria-fighting insecticide, now banned for persistence and biomagnification

WATCH-OUTS THAT SET MCQ TRAPS

SN1 vs SN2 reactivity orders are OPPOSITE — 3° wins SN1, methyl wins SN2; check which is asked
Racemisation ≠ inversion: SN1 gives partial racemisation (not 50:50), SN2 gives full inversion
Haloarenes vs haloalkanes: same nucleophiles, wildly different rates — resonance is the culprit
o,p-directing ≠ activating: halogens direct o,p yet DEACTIVATE the ring — both facts in one option pair
