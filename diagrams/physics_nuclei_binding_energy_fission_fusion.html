Nuclear Size, Density & Force
Nuclear Radius Scaling Law
R = R₀ A^(1/3)
R₀ ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ m = 1.2 fm (Fermi). Mass number A = Z + N.
Constant Nuclear Density (Universal)
ρ = Mass / Volume = (A · m_u) / ((4/3)π R₀³ A)
ρ ≈ 2.3 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³ = const!
Completely independent of mass number A! (All nuclei have identical density).
Classification Condition Example
Isotopes Same Z, diff A ¹₁H, ²₁H, ³₁H
Isobars Same A, diff Z ⁴⁰₁₈Ar, ⁴⁰₁₉K, ⁴⁰₂₀Ca
Isotones Same N = A - Z ³₁H (N=2), ⁴₂He (N=2)
Key Properties of Nuclear Strong Force:
  • Strongest Force: ~ 100× stronger than Coulomb repulsion at ~1 fm.
  • Short-Range: Drops sharply to zero beyond ~ 2 to 3 fm.
  • Charge-Independent: F_nn = F_pp = F_pn.
  • Saturation Property: Each nucleon interacts only with nearest neighbors.
  • Repulsive Core: Strongly repulsive at r < 0.8 fm (prevents nuclear collapse).
Binding Energy per Nucleon Curve (E_bn vs A)
Thermodynamic Drive of Nuclear Reactions:
1. Nuclear Fission (Heavy Nuclei, A > 200):
A heavy nucleus (BE ~ 7.6 MeV/nuc) splits into two medium nuclei (BE ~ 8.5 MeV/nuc). Gain in stability: ~ 0.9 MeV per nucleon! Energy released per fission ≈ 235 × 0.9 ≈ 200 MeV!
2. Nuclear Fusion (Light Nuclei, A < 20):
Two very light nuclei (e.g. ²H, BE = 1.1 MeV/nuc) fuse to form ⁴He (BE = 7.1 MeV/nuc). Dramatic gain of ~ 6.0 MeV per nucleon, releasing immense energy per unit mass!
Mass Defect & Reactions
Mass Defect & Binding Energy
Δm = [Z m_p + (A-Z) m_n] - M_nucleus
E_b = Δm · c² = Δm (in u) × 931.5 MeV
Canonical Fission of ²³⁵U:
²³⁵₉₂U + ¹₀n ⟶ ²³⁶₉₂U* ⟶ ¹⁴⁴₅₆Ba + ⁸⁹₃₆Kr + 3 ¹₀n + Q (≈ 200 MeV)
Stellar Thermonuclear Fusion (Proton-Proton):
4 ¹₁H ⟶ ⁴₂He + 2 e⁺ + 2 ν_e + 26.7 MeV
  • Fission vs Fusion Comparison: Fission releases ~ 200 MeV per event (~ 0.85 MeV/amu). Fusion releases ~ 26.7 MeV per event, but yields ~ 6.7 MeV/amu (~ 8× higher energy density per kg)!
  • Coulomb Barrier in Fusion: Positively charged nuclei require extreme temperature (T ~ 10⁷–10⁸ K) to overcome Coulomb repulsion, hence termed thermonuclear reactions.
