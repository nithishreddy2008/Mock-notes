The 3 Definitive Cost Identites

  • Total Cost Identity: $T C = T F C + T V C$. At zero output, $T V C = 0 \implies T C = T F C$.
  • Unit Cost Identity: $A T C = A F C + A V C = (T F C / Q) + (T V C / Q)$.
  • Marginal Cost Formula: $M C_n = T C_n - T C_{n-1} = T V C_n - T V C_{n-1}$ (Independent of TFC!).

The Dual Intersection Geometry Rules

  • MC cuts AVC at its lowest point: Output level $Q_1$ is strictly less than $Q_2$.
  • MC cuts ATC at its lowest point: Output level $Q_2$ represents the optimum capacity output.
  • Why ATC & AVC never meet: Vertical distance is $A F C = T F C / Q > 0$. Even as $Q \to \infty$, $A F C$ approaches 0 asymptotically, but never equals 0.

CUET Trap: Area Under MC Curve

Fatal Trap: Believing the definite integral of MC gives Total Cost. Reality: $\int M C\, dQ = T V C$ (Total Variable Cost), NOT TC! To obtain TC, one must add TFC: $T C = \int M C\, dQ + T F C$.
