Master Synthesis Mindmap: Advertising Concepts, Agency Operations & Ethics

Six-pillar conceptual synthesis covering nature of advertising, models, agency departments, creative strategy, media & regulation

CUET Mass Media · Unit 6 Synthesis
1. Nature & Agency History
AMA / Kotler definition: paid, non-personal promotion by identified sponsor; Volney B. Palmer (1841, first agency); B. Dattaram & Co. (1902, oldest Indian agency); Full-service vs Boutique vs In-house agencies.
Paid Promotion Palmer 1841 B. Dattaram 1902 Full-Service
2. Advertising Theories & Models
AIDA (E. St. Elmo Lewis, 1898: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action); DAGMAR (Russell Colley, 1961: ACCA hierarchy, measurable goals); Unique Selling Proposition (Rosser Reeves); Brand Positioning (Ries & Trout).
AIDA 1898 DAGMAR 1961 Reeves USP Positioning
3. Agency Departmental Structure
Client Servicing / Account Management (Creative Brief); Account Planning (Consumer insights, demographics, psychographics); Creative Dept (Copywriter + Art Director); Media Dept (Planning, Buying, GRP, CPM).
Creative Brief Copy & Art Media Planning Account Planner
4. Creative Strategy & Appeals
Rational / Informational (features, price, health); Emotional (humor, fear appeals, nostalgia, warmth); Moral / Ethical (civic duty); Endorsements: Celebrity, Expert testimonial, Average consumer; Headline & Tagline anatomy.
Rational Appeal Fear Appeal Celebrity Tagline / Slogan
5. Classifications & Media Channels
Target: B2C vs B2B; Geography: Local, National, Global; Media: Print, Television (TVC), Radio jingles, Out-of-Home (OOH, billboards, transit), Digital programmatic; Public Service Advertising (PSA / social campaigns).
B2B vs B2C OOH Billboards PSA Campaigns Digital Ads
6. Advertising Ethics & ASCI Code
Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI, 1985); Consumer Complaints Council (CCC); Self-regulation; Surrogate advertising (alcohol, tobacco brands promoted as soda/music CDs); CCPA Guidelines 2022 on Dark Patterns.
ASCI Code 1985 Surrogate Ads Self-Regulation CCPA 2022
