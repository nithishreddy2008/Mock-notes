CURRENT CHALLENGES — INDIAN ECONOMY
COURSE III IED · COMPLETE CHAPTER MAP

Human Capital Formation

People become resource through education, health, training, migration and information spending
Payoff: higher earnings, innovation, growth with equity — knowledge stays with its owner
India's education arc: literacy 18% → 74%+, institutes multiplied; quality and vacancies still bite

Rural Credit & Marketing

Institutional credit: cooperatives, banks, RRBs, SHGs — cheap and safe; non-institutional (moneylenders) costly and exploitative
Marketing hurdles: intermediaries, distress sales, no storage, no grading
Fixes: regulated markets (APMC), MSP, cooperatives, warehousing, grading

Diversification & Organic Farming

Three lanes: crop diversification, allied activities (dairy, poultry, fisheries, horticulture), non-farm employment (food processing, rural tourism)
Organic farming: chemical-free, export premiums, sustainability — vs lower early yields and certification costs

Employment Structure

Three worker types: self-employed (largest), regular salaried (fewest), casual wage (most insecure)
Formal vs informal: contract + benefits + security vs none — most workers are informal
Women's participation lags sharply; jobless growth is the standing worry

Employment Policies

MGNREGA 2005: right to work — 100 days guaranteed wage employment per rural household
Self-employment & skill programmes layered on top for the youth

Environment & Sustainability

Four environment functions: resource supply, waste sink, life support, quality of life
India's strain: air and water pollution, deforestation, soil erosion, falling groundwater
Sustainable development: present needs without killing future options (Brundtland)
Strategies: solar, wind, CNG/LPG, bio-composting, mini-hydel, traditional practices

Traps that catch most students

Human capital ≠ physical capital — knowledge cannot be sold apart from its owner
Institutional ≠ non-institutional credit — moneylenders are the NON-institutional (exploitative) side
Formal ≠ government job only — the test is written contract + benefits, in any firm size
Sustainable development ≠ stopping growth — it means growing WITHOUT exhausting the base
