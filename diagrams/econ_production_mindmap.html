The Production Function

Input → output rule: the maximum output from each combination of inputs, with current technology
Short run: at least ONE factor fixed (land, machines); change output only by adding labour
Long run: ALL factors variable — build the second factory if you wish
Three product measures: TP (total), MP = ΔTP/ΔL (extra unit), AP = TP/L (per worker)

Law of Variable Proportions (short run)

Phase I: TP rises at an increasing rate; MP rises then peaks; AP rising (MP > AP)
Phase II: TP rises at a DIMINISHING rate; MP falls but stays positive; AP falls after MP cuts it — the rational zone
Phase III: TP falls; MP negative — overcrowding; nobody produces here
Reason: fixed factor gets crowded; factor proportions become unbalanced

Returns to Scale (long run)

Increasing: output rises MORE than inputs (doubling inputs more than doubles output) — specialisation, big machines
Constant: output rises in the SAME proportion
Decreasing: output rises LESS than inputs — management coordination limits

The Cost Family

TFC: fixed cost — same at every output, even at zero (rent, salaries); a horizontal line
TVC: varies with output (materials, casual labour); starts at 0
TC = TFC + TVC — runs parallel above TVC, exactly TFC above it
AFC = TFC/Q falls forever, never touches the axis · AVC = TVC/Q and AC = TC/Q = AFC + AVC are U-shaped
MC = ΔTC/ΔQ — the fastest mover; cuts AVC and AC at their minima

Revenue (perfect competition)

TR = P × Q — a straight line from the origin
AR = TR/Q = P — the demand curve for the firm
MR = ΔTR/ΔQ = P = AR — the firm is a price taker; all three collapse into one horizontal price line

Producer's Equilibrium (profit max)

Condition 1: MC = MR — the last unit adds exactly as much cost as revenue
Condition 2: MC must be RISING at that point (beyond it MC > MR, before it MC < MR)
Condition 3 (shutdown guard): price ≥ AVC in the short run — else produce nothing

Traps that catch most students

MC cuts AVC and AC at their MINIMA — never anywhere else; check every diagram against this
AFC never touches the x-axis — TFC spread thinner and thinner, but never zero
AP rising while MP > AP; AP falls once MP < AP — MP is the thermometer, AP follows it
Short run ≠ fixed time, long run ≠ years — they are about which factors are variable, not calendar time
