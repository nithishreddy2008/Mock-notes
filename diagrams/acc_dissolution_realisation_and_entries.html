Realisation Account: Transfer Protocols, Settlement Mechanics & Expense Rules

NTA CUET(UG) Accountancy 301 · Unit III Architecture · Section 48 Realisation T-Account Rules

Accounting Protocols
1. Balance Sheet Transfers

Assets Transferred DEBIT REALISATION

All tangible & intangible assets at book value: Debtors (GROSS), Stock, Plant, Goodwill, Patents, Prepaid expenses.

Excluded Assets NEVER IN REALISATION

Cash & Bank balances, Fictitious Assets (P&L Dr., Advertisement Suspense), Partner's Loan A/c (Dr.), Partner Capital Dr.

Liabilities Transferred CREDIT REALISATION

Third-party liabilities: Creditors, Bills Payable, Bank OD, Partner's Relative Loan, Provision for Doubtful Debts, IFR.

Excluded Liabilities NEVER IN REALISATION

Partner Capital & Current A/cs, Reserves (General Reserve, P&L Cr.), Partner's Loan to Firm (paid directly!).

2. Asset & Liability Settlement
Event Debit Credit
Asset sold for cash Bank A/c Realisation A/c
Asset taken by partner Partner's Capital Realisation A/c
Asset given to creditor in full settlement ★ NO JOURNAL ENTRY PASSED! ★
Asset given to creditor + cash balance paid Realisation A/c Bank A/c (cash paid only)
Liability paid in cash Realisation A/c Bank A/c
Liability taken by partner Realisation A/c Partner's Capital
Unrecorded asset sold Bank A/c Realisation A/c
The Golden Rule: Once an asset or liability is transferred to Realisation Account, its individual ledger account is CLOSED. Any future cash movement passes strictly through Realisation A/c!
3. Expenses & Partner's Loan

Realisation Expenses Matrix

• Firm bears & Firm pays: Realisation A/c Dr. to Bank A/c.
• Firm bears & Partner pays: Realisation A/c Dr. to Partner Cap.
• Partner bears & Partner pays: NO ENTRY in firm books!
• Partner bears & Firm pays: Partner Cap Dr. to Bank A/c.
• Fixed Remuneration: Realisation A/c Dr. to Partner Cap with fee; actual expenses paid by firm debited to Partner Cap!

Partner's Loan to Firm (Credit Balance)

Settled through Bank directly: Partner's Loan A/c Dr. to Bank A/c Cr. If settled at a discount, discount credited to Realisation A/c.

Partner's Loan by Firm (Debit Balance)

Asset owed by partner. Directly transferred to the DEBIT of Partner's Capital Account (NOT Realisation A/c!).
