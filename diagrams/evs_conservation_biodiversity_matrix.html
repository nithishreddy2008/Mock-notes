National Park In-Situ (IUCN Cat II) Wildlife Protection Act (1972) Highest protection; zero human habitation, private rights, timber logging, or livestock grazing allowed. Focus on total ecosystem integrity. Jim Corbett (Uttarakhand), Kaziranga (Assam), Keoladeo Ghana (Rajasthan).
Wildlife Sanctuary In-Situ (IUCN Cat IV) Wildlife Protection Act (1972) Species-oriented protection; limited human activities (regulated livestock grazing, traditional firewood/NTFP collection) permitted under DC control. Gir (Gujarat), Bharatpur, Periyar (Kerala), Chilika Nalaban (Odisha).
Biosphere Reserve In-Situ (MAB UNESCO) UNESCO MAB Protocol Tri-zonal structure: Core (strictly protected), Buffer (research/education), Transition zone (sustainable human settlements & agriculture). 18 in India (12 in WNBR): Nilgiri (1st, 1986), Nanda Devi, Sundarbans, Gulf of Mannar.
Sacred Groves In-Situ (Community-led) Customary Law / WPA (2002) Community-managed forest patches protected through spiritual taboos and cultural veneration; zero commercial exploitation; micro-refugia. Orans (Rajasthan), Devarakadu (Karnataka), Law Kyntang (Meghalaya), Sarnas (Jharkhand).
Botanical Garden & Zoo Ex-Situ (Captive Care) Central Zoo Authority (CZA) Off-site maintenance, pedigree breeding programs, captive propagation of endangered flora/fauna, public environmental education. Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Indian Botanic Garden (Howrah), Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoo.
Cryopreservation & Seed Bank Ex-Situ (Germplasm Storage) National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (NBPGR) Ultra-low temperature storage (-196°C in liquid nitrogen) of gametes, seeds, pollen, tissue cultures; prevents extinction of landraces. National Gene Bank (NBPGR, New Delhi), Svalbard Global Seed Vault (Norway).
