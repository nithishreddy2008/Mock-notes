The Three Measurement Methods of National Income

Comprehensive Architecture: Value Added Method (Output), Income Method (Factor Incomes), and Expenditure Method (Final Outlay)

3 Measurement Pillars GDP_MP vs NDP_FC Avoid Double Counting
1. Value Added Method (Product)
Production Phase

Core Mathematical Identity:

GVA_MP = Value of Output - Intermediate Cost
Value of Output = Sales + ΔStock

  • Value of Output: Total market value of all goods & services produced.
    Sales = Domestic Sales + Exports
    ΔStock = Closing Stock - Opening Stock
  • Intermediate Consumption: Value of non-factor inputs (raw materials, fuel, power) used up in production.
    Intermediate Purchases = Domestic + Imports
  • Double Counting Solution:
    Method A: Take ONLY Final Goods value.
    Method B: Take Gross Value Added (GVA) at each stage.
Direct Aggregation Yields: Σ GVA_MP = GDP_MP
Convert: NNP_FC = GDP_MP - Dep + NFIA - NIT
2. Income Method (Factor Incomes)
Distribution Phase

Core Mathematical Identity:

NDP_FC = COE + Operating Surplus + Mixed Income

  • 1. Compensation of Employees (COE):
    • Wages and Salaries in Cash
    • Wages in Kind (free house, medical)
    • Employer's Contribution to Social Security
  • 2. Operating Surplus (OS):
    • Rent (including imputed rent of owner homes)
    • Royalty (mining, copyright rights)
    • Interest (production loans only)
    • Profit = Corp Tax + Dividend + Undistributed Profits
  • 3. Mixed Income of Self-Employed:
    Unincorporated own-account workers (farmers, doctors)
Direct Aggregation Yields: NDP_FC (Domestic Income!)
Convert: NNP_FC = NDP_FC + NFIA
3. Expenditure Method (Outlay)
Disposition Phase

Core Mathematical Identity:

GDP_MP = C + G + I + (X - M)

  • Private Final Consumption Expenditure (C): Consumer spending by households and private non-profit institutions.
  • Government Final Consumption (G): Government spending on collective services (defense, roads, police).
  • Gross Domestic Capital Formation (I):
    • Gross Domestic Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF)
    • Inventory Investment (Change in Stock)
    Note: GDCF = GFCF + ΔStock!
  • Net Exports (X - M): Exports ($X$) minus Imports ($M$).
Direct Aggregation Yields: GDP_MP
Convert: NNP_FC = GDP_MP - Dep + NFIA - NIT

Mandatory Inclusion Rules

  • Imputed Rent: Always INCLUDED in National Income for self-occupied properties (factor service rendered by capital).
  • Production for Self-Consumption: INCLUDED (farmer consuming own wheat adds to economic output).
  • Brokerage on Second-Hand Goods: INCLUDED (productive service rendered currently).

Strict Exclusion Precautions

  • Transfer Payments: EXCLUDED! (Old-age pensions, scholarships, gifts, unemployment doles represent unrequited unilateral transfers with zero current output).
  • Sale of Second-Hand Goods: EXCLUDED (already counted in the year of initial production).
  • Financial Transactions: EXCLUDED (Shares, bonds, debentures are mere claims on paper).

CUET Trap: GDCF vs. GFCF

Fatal Trap: If question gives Gross Fixed Capital Formation (GFCF), students forget to add Change in Stock! Correction: $\text{GDCF} = \text{GFCF} + \Delta\text{Stock}$. But if question directly gives Gross Capital Formation (GDCF), $\Delta\text{Stock}$ is ALREADY inside it—do not add it twice!
