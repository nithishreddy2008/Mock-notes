Historiography & Economic Exchange · 4,500-Year Chronology

From Bronze Age Maritime Trade to Stratigraphic Discovery

A 4,500-year historical trajectory: from third-millennium BCE Mesopotamian trade links and urban disintegration to 20th-century stratigraphic excavations that placed the Indus Civilisation on the global map.

Trade Geography

The Mesopotamian Trade Triangle

Cuneiform tablets name Meluhha as the land of seafarers exporting carnelian, lapis lazuli, gold, and timber. Magan was Oman (nickel copper); Dilmun was Bahrain serving as an intermediate Persian Gulf entrepôt.

Methodology Shift

Marshall's Scraping vs Wheeler's Stratigraphy

John Marshall dug along arbitrary horizontal depths, grouping objects from different centuries together. In 1944, R.E.M. Wheeler introduced military precision and scientific stratigraphy along natural cultural mound layers.

Historiographical Critique

The 'Aryan Massacre' Myth Refuted

Wheeler linked Rigvedic Indra (Purandara - 'fort destroyer') to skeletons in Deadman Lane. Anthropologist George Dales proved the skeletons came from different chronological strata and showed no unhealed battle trauma.
