HARAPPAN ECONOMIC LIFE & ARCHAEOLOGICAL HISTORIOGRAPHY — THE EVIDENCE MATRIX

FOUR PILLARS OF HARAPPAN MATERIAL EXCHANGE & COGNITIVE ARTIFACTS

1. Material Sourcing
Steatite (Rajasthan/Gujarat), Lapis Lazuli (Shortughai), Carnelian (Bharuch/Lothal), Copper (Khetri/Oman), Gold (Kolar, S. India).
2. Maritime Trade (Mesopotamia)
Cuneiform texts cite Meluhha (seafarers/Harappa), Magan (Oman - nickel traces in copper), and Dilmun (Bahrain island).
3. Script & Sealing System
~375–400 pictographic signs; written Right-to-Left; longest text has 26 signs; stamped wet clay tags on tied trade bundles.
4. Metrology & Weights
Cubical chert weights; binary lower series (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 up to 12,800); decimal system for higher values & jewel scale pans.

Social Differences & Burials

Burial Practices: Dead laid in simple pits; oriented North-South; occasionally lined with fired bricks.
Modest Grave Goods: Pottery, shell bangles, bone beads & rare copper mirrors; no vast gold hordes like Egyptian Pharaohs.
Belief in Afterlife: Goods placed in graves intended for use in the next world; Harappans did not entomb heavy wealth.
Utilitarian vs Luxury: Daily stone querns & needles vs rare, costly miniature faience perfume pots concentrated in major cities.

Religious Evidence & Interpretation

"Mother Goddess": Elaborate terracotta female figurines with heavy necklaces & fan-shaped headdresses.
"Priest-King": Seated stone sculpture at Mohenjodaro draped in an embroidered cloak with trefoil pattern.
"Proto-Shiva" Seal: Yogic figure in cross-legged posture surrounded by elephant, tiger, rhino, buffalo & two deer below seat.
Ritual Installations: Conical stones classified as lingas; brick fire altars excavated at Kalibangan and Lothal.

HISTORIOGRAPHY: FIVE MILESTONES IN EXCAVATION & DISCOVERY

1875 · Alexander Cunningham

1st Director-General of ASI ("Father of Indian Archaeology"). Confused by Harappan seal; mistakenly believed Indian urbanization began with 6th c. BCE Ganga valley cities.

1921–22 · Sahni & Banerji

Daya Ram Sahni found seals at Harappa (1921); Rakhal Das Banerji found identical seals at Mohenjodaro (1922), proving a unified civilization across Punjab and Sindh.

1924 · John Marshall

DG of ASI announced Indus Civilisation to the world. Excavated horizontally along regular levels; ignored stratigraphy, blending artifacts from different time horizons.

1944 · R.E.M. Wheeler

DG of ASI; brought military precision. Rectified Marshall's flaw by excavating strictly along stratigraphic mound layers rather than arbitrary horizontal lines.

Post-1947 · Indian Archaeology

Partition left Harappa & Mohenjodaro in Pakistan. Extensive surveys in India revealed major sites: S.R. Rao (Lothal), B.B. Lal & B.K. Thapar (Kalibangan), R.S. Bisht (Dholavira).

THEORIES OF COLLAPSE & DEBUNKING THE "MASSACRE" MYTH

1. Ecological & River Factors: Climatic drying, deforestation from brick-firing, river shifts (drying of Saraswati/Ghaggar-Hakra system) led to gradual abandonment of cities.
2. Transformation to Late Harappan: Around 1800 BCE, mature urban markers (weights, seals, standardized bricks, drainage) vanished; population migrated to Gujarat, Haryana & Western UP.
3. Debunking the Aryan Invasion: Wheeler cited Rigvedic Indra (Purandara - "fort destroyer") & Deadman Lane skeletons. George Dales demonstrated skeletons showed no unhealed battle wounds or mass massacre.

⚠ TRAP 1: MARSHALL VS WHEELER

Marshall excavated horizontally regardless of strata; Wheeler introduced scientific stratigraphy (following soil layers).

⚠ TRAP 2: MESOPOTAMIAN PLACE NAMES

Meluhha = Harappan region; Magan = Oman (nickel copper); Dilmun = Bahrain island. Meluhha was called "land of seafarers".

⚠ TRAP 3: SCRIPT DIRECTION & METROLOGY

Harappan script is written Right-to-Left (cramping on left edge). Lower weights are binary; higher weights are decimal.
