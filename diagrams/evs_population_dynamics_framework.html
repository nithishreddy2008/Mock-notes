1. Growth Models
dN/dt = rN · (K-N)/K
  • Exponential (J-Curve): Unlimited resources; dN/dt = rN; crashes when threshold exceeded.
  • Logistic (S-Curve): Verhulst-Pearl model; environmental resistance slows growth as N approaches K.
  • Carrying Capacity (K): Maximum sustainable density an environment supports indefinitely.
Mathematical Ecology
2. r vs. K Selection
Life History Evolution
  • r-Strategists: Small size, rapid maturation, high fecundity, low parental care, unstable environments (insects, weeds).
  • K-Strategists: Large body, slow maturation, low reproductive output, high parental investment, stable habitats (elephants, humans).
Evolutionary Adaptation
3. Survivorship Curves
Type I, II, & III Curves
  • Type I (Convex): High juvenile survival; mortality concentrated in old age (humans, large mammals).
  • Type II (Diagonal): Constant mortality rate throughout life span (songbirds, hydra).
  • Type III (Concave): High juvenile mortality; survivors enjoy long longevity (oysters, trees, pelagic fish).
Cohort Demography
4. Demographic Transition
5-Stage Population Model
  • Stage 1: High CBR, High CDR (Stable).
  • Stage 2: High CBR, Plummeting CDR (Explosion).
  • Stage 3: Falling CBR, Low CDR (Deceleration).
  • Stage 4: Low CBR, Low CDR (Stabilization).
  • Stage 5: Sub-replacement fertility (Decline).
