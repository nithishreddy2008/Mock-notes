EIA STATUTORY ARCHITECTURE & STAGES
  • Origins: US NEPA 1969; India 1976–77 (Planning Commission); statutory under EPA 1986 (1994 & 2006 Notifications).
  • Categorization: Category A (MoEFCC & EAC, mandatory EIA); Category B (SEIAA & SEAC, screened into B1 [EIA] & B2 [No EIA]).
  • 4 Sequential Stages: 1. Screening (Cat B) • 2. Scoping (ToR & 1-season baseline) • 3. Public Consultation (45-day SPCB hearing) • 4. Appraisal.
  • EMP & Monitoring: Mitigation hierarchy, disaster plans; mandatory half-yearly compliance reports submitted to MoEFCC.
EIA IMPACT PREDICTION METHODOLOGIES
  • Checklists: Simple, descriptive, scaling, weighting; structured inventories preventing omission of sensitive parameters.
  • Leopold Matrix (1971): 100 actions × 88 environmental characteristics (8,800 cells); scored for Magnitude / Importance (1–10).
  • Sorensen Network: Cause-condition-effect trees explicitly tracing secondary and tertiary indirect ecological cascades.
  • Overlays & Battelle BEES: McHarg map layering (GIS corridors); Battelle BEES quantitative index across 78 parameters.
ENVIRONMENTAL AUDITING & RULE 14
  • Definition: Systematic, documented, periodic, and objective evaluation of environmental performance and regulatory compliance.
  • Rule 14 EPA 1986: Mandatory annual submission of Environmental Statement (Form V) by September 30 to State Pollution Control Board.
  • 3-Phase Methodology: 1. Pre-Audit (scoping, questionnaire) • 2. On-Site (inspection, sampling) • 3. Post-Audit (report, corrective actions).
  • Audit Typology: Compliance audits, liabilities audits, waste minimization audits, and energy audits.
ISO 14000, LCA & ECO-LABELING
  • ISO 14001 EMS: Environmental Management Systems based on Deming's Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) continual improvement cycle.
  • ISO 14040/14044 LCA: Life Cycle Assessment across 4 phases: Goal & Scope, Inventory (LCI), Impact Assessment (LCIA), Interpretation.
  • ISO 19011: Consolidated standard for auditing management systems (integrating quality and environment).
  • Eco-Mark Scheme (1991): Indian earthen pot (Matka) logo issued by CPCB and BIS for eco-friendly consumer goods.
