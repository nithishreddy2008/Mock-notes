Master Synthesis Mindmap: Radio Broadcasting, Audio Systems & Community Airwaves

Six-pillar conceptual synthesis covering nature of radio, spectrum physics, Indian milestones, community tiers & acoustics

CUET Mass Media · Unit 4 Synthesis
1. Nature & "Theatre of the Mind"
Audio-only, blind medium; listener actively paints visual imagination; intensely intimate and personal; portable, low-cost receiver; transcends literacy barriers; fleeting/linear; background companion.
Blind Medium Theatre of Mind Intimacy Linear
2. Modulation & Spectrum Physics
AM (MW 526–1606 kHz, SW 3–30 MHz): skywave ionospheric bounce, long range, static noise; FM (88–108 MHz): VHF line-of-sight, 50–70 km radius, high-fidelity stereo; DRM (Digital Radio Mondiale).
AM Skywave FM VHF 88-108 DRM Line-of-Sight
3. Evolution of Radio in India
Radio Club of Bombay (1923); IBC (1927); Lionel Fielden (1935); Renamed All India Radio (8 June 1936); Keskar's classical policy; Vividh Bharati (3 Oct 1957); Private FM Phases I–III; News restriction.
IBC 1927 AIR 1936 Vividh Bharati 1957 Private FM
4. Community Radio Movement
CAB 1995 Supreme Court verdict ("Airwaves are public property"); Pastapur Declaration 2000; MIB Policy 2002 (Campus) & 2006 (NGOs); Anna FM (2004); Sangham Radio (2008); 100W transmitter, 10–15 km radius, 5 min/hr ads.
Airwaves Public Anna FM 2004 Sangham Radio 100W ERP
5. Studio Acoustics & Microphones
Reverberation time (RT60); Sound locks; Dynamic vs Condenser (+48V); Polar patterns: Cardioid (solo presenter), Bi-directional / Figure-8 (interview), Omnidirectional; Pop filters; Proximity effect (bass boost).
Cardioid Figure-8 Proximity Effect Phantom Power
6. Radio Formats & Scripting
"Writing for the Ear": short sentences, conversational tone, active voice, signposting; Radio News (voice dispatch, actualities); Radio Features/Documentaries; Radio Drama (acoustic perspectives, Foley SFX); Jingles.
Writing for Ear Actuality Radio Drama Foley SFX
