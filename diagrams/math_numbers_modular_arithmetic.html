Section B2 Applied Math
1. Modular Congruences, Remainders & Fermat's Little Theorem
Modular Congruence Axiom & Power Reductions
a ≡ b (mod m) ⟺ m | (a - b) ⟺ a = k·m + b
Fermat's Little Theorem: aᵖ⁻¹ ≡ 1 (mod p) for prime p, gcd(a, p) = 1
Linear Invariance: If a ≡ b (mod m) and c ≡ d (mod m), then a + c ≡ b + d, a·c ≡ b·d, and aⁿ ≡ bⁿ (mod m).
Application Modulus Used Mathematical Technique
Units Digit (Last Digit) mod 10 Cyclicity of powers: 2, 3, 7, 8 have cycle 4; 4, 9 have cycle 2
Last Two Digits mod 100 Binomial expansion / Euler's Totient φ(100) = 40
Day of the Week Calculation mod 7 Odd days accumulation: Mon=1, Tue=2, ..., Sun=0
Large Power Remainder mod m Fermat's Theorem aᵖ⁻¹ ≡ 1 (mod p) or Wilson's (p - 1)! ≡ -1 (mod p)
Negative Modulo Property: If a dividend leaves remainder -r, its canonical positive residue is $(m - r) \pmod m$. For example, $23 \equiv -2 \equiv 3 \pmod 5$. Working with small negative residues simplifies power exponentiation drastically.
CUET Trap Alert (Modular Division Prohibition): You CANNOT arbitrarily divide both sides of a congruence: $a c \equiv b c \pmod m \implies a \equiv b \pmod{m / \gcd(c, m)}$. Division is valid without altering the modulus ONLY if $\gcd(c, m) = 1$.
2. Allegation Matrix, Repeated Dilution & Kinematic Models
Allegation & Repeated Dilution Master Formulas
Allegation Ratio: (Quantity Cheaper) / (Quantity Dearer) = (d - m) / (m - c)
Repeated Dilution: Q_liquid = a · (1 - b / a)ⁿ
Where a is initial pure liquid volume, b is volume withdrawn and replaced by water in each cycle, and n is total successive dilution cycles.
Domain Physical Variables Governing Equation
Boats & Streams Boat $u$, Stream $v$ $u = (v_{\text{down}} + v_{\text{up}})/2$, $v = (v_{\text{down}} - v_{\text{up}})/2$
Pipes & Cisterns Fill $T_1$, Empty $T_2$ Net work rate $= 1/T_1 - 1/T_2$; Net time $= (T_1 T_2) / (T_2 - T_1)$
Races (Distance Start) Course $D$, Start $x$ A covers distance $D$ while B covers $(D - x)$
Races (Time Start) Time $t$, Speed $s_B$ If A beats B by $x$ meters or $t$ seconds, Speed of B $= x / t$
Stream Round-Trip Principle: Total round-trip time upstream and downstream over distance $d$ is: $T = \frac{d}{u+v} + \frac{d}{u-v} = \frac{2 d u}{u^2 - v^2}$. Notice that average speed for the round-trip $\frac{u^2 - v^2}{u} < u$; a current ALWAYS increases total round-trip travel time compared to still water!
CUET Trap Alert (Game of 100 Points): "A gives B 20 points in a 100-point game" means when A scores 100, B starts with 20 (or needs only 80). If A can give C 30 points, when A scores 100, C scores 70. Do NOT simply subtract points to find B vs C; multiply scoring ratios: $(C/B) = (70/80) \implies$ in 80 points, B gives C 10 points!
