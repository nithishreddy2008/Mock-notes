The Order of Settlement on Dissolution
Section 48, Indian Partnership Act 1932 — who gets paid first, and who absorbs the loss
1

Outside liabilities

Creditors, bills payable, bank loans — the firm's debts to strangers are cleared first, in full.

2

Partners' loans

Money partners lent to the firm is repaid in full — before any capital comes back.

3

Partners' capitals

What the firm still owes each partner's capital account — after absorbing the realisation result.

4

Surplus in PSR

Anything left after every payment is shared by the partners in their old profit-sharing ratio.

AND IF THE DISSOLUTION ENDS IN A LOSS — THE LOSS IS APPLIED IN THIS ORDER
5

From profits

First the loss is absorbed out of any profits still available for distribution.

6

From capitals

Next the loss eats into the partners' capital balances, partner by partner.

7

By partners in PSR

Still short? The partners bear the balance in their old profit-sharing ratio.
