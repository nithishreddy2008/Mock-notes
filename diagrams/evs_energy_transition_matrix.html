Energy Source Conversion Mechanism EROI & Capacity Factor Ecological Footprint CUET Core Invariant
Solar PV & Thermal Photovoltaic effect (silicon p-n junction); Concentrated Solar Power (CSP mirrors heating molten salts). EROI: ~10-15:1
Capacity Factor: 18-25%		 Land occupation, mineral mining (silver, cadmium, tellurium), toxic waste in end-of-life disposal. India target: 500 GW non-fossil by 2030; National Solar Mission flagship.
Wind Energy Aerodynamic lift on airfoil rotor blades driving induction generators; Betz limit constraint. EROI: ~18-25:1
Capacity Factor: 25-40%		 Avian/bat collision mortality, low-frequency acoustic noise, rare-earth magnets (neodymium). Theoretical maximum power extraction limit is 59.3% (Betz's Law).
Hydroelectric Power Gravitational potential energy of impounded water driving hydraulic turbines (Francis, Pelton, Kaplan). EROI: ~40-100:1
Capacity Factor: 35-50%		 Reservoir submergence, seismic risk (reservoir-induced seismicity RIS), methane from drowned organic decay. Small hydro (≤ 25 MW) classified as renewable in India; minimizes displacement.
Nuclear Fission Induced thermal neutron fission of Uranium-235; Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWR) in India. EROI: ~15-20:1
Capacity Factor: 85-90%		 High-level radioactive waste (actinides), thermal water pollution, decommissioning challenges. India's 3-Stage Program: PHWR (Natural U) → Fast Breeder (Pu) → Thorium-232 breeders.
Biomass & Biofuels Anaerobic digestion (biogas: 55-70% CH4); Fermentation (1G sugarcane/corn ethanol); Pyrolysis (bio-oil). EROI: 1.3:1 (1G) to 5:1 (2G)
Capacity Factor: 60-80%		 Food vs fuel land diversion (1G ethanol), monoculture fertilizer runoff, water footprint. 2G ethanol uses non-food lignocellulose (paddy straw); 3G uses algae; 4G uses engineered microbes.
Green Hydrogen Electrolysis of purified water (PEM / Alkaline) powered strictly by renewable solar or wind electricity. Efficiency: ~60-70%
High storage energy density		 Requires high purity demineralized water (9 liters H2O per kg H2); embrittlement of pipeline steel. National Green Hydrogen Mission: 5 MMT production per annum by 2030.
