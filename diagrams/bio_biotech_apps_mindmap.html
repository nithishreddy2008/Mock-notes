1. Agricultural Biotechnology

Green Biotech
Transgenic Crops & GMO Goals: Abiotic stress tolerance (cold, drought, salt), reduced chemical dependency, diminished post-harvest loss, enhanced mineral efficiency, nutritional fortification (Golden Rice enriched with provitamin A).
Bt Cotton (Cry Toxins): Crystal protoxins of Bacillus thuringiensis activated in insect alkaline midgut (pH 9–10) $arrow$ pore formation & osmotic lysis.
• cryIAc & cryIIAb: Cotton bollworms | • cryIAb: Corn borer (Top NTA trap)
RNA Interference (RNAi): Nematode Meloidogyne incognita root resistance in tobacco. Sense + antisense RNA $arrow$ dsRNA $arrow$ Dicer $arrow$ siRNAs $arrow$ RISC $arrow$ mRNA cleavage & post-transcriptional gene silencing.
Trap: RNAi occurs in all eukaryotic organisms as cellular defense, NOT in prokaryotes!

2. Medical Applications

Red Biotech
Recombinant Human Insulin (Humulin): Synthesized by Eli Lilly (1983). Two separate artificial DNA sequences for Chain A (21 aa) and Chain B (30 aa) expressed in E. coli plasmids; linked in vitro by disulfide bonds.
• Mature insulin has 51 aa; completely lacks the connecting C-peptide (33 aa).
Gene Therapy (ADA Deficiency): 1990 clinical trial on 4-yr-old girl with SCID. Retroviral vector transduced functional ADA cDNA into patient lymphocytes.
• Periodic infusion needed (mortal cells) | Permanent cure: stem cells at early embryo.
Molecular Diagnosis:
• PCR: Amplifies trace viral/bacterial DNA (early HIV & cancer mutation screening).
• ELISA: Antigen-antibody interaction detection.
• Autoradiography: Radioactive ssDNA/ssRNA probe fails to hybridize to mutated genes.
Trap: Bovine/porcine insulin caused immune allergies; Humulin causes zero immune rejection.

3. Transgenic Animals

In Vivo Models
Model Organisms: Over 95% of all existing transgenic animals are mice. Others include transgenic rats, rabbits, pigs, sheep, and cows.
Five Standard Biological Uses:
1. Normal Physiology & Development: Studying insulin-like growth factor (IGF).
2. Study of Human Diseases: Transgenic animal models for cancer, cystic fibrosis, rheumatoid arthritis, and Alzheimer's disease.
3. Biological Products: Human protein $alpha$-1-antitrypsin (treats emphysema); transgenic cow Rosie (1997) produced human protein-enriched milk (2.4 g/L $alpha$-lactalbumin).
4. Vaccine Safety: Transgenic mice test batch safety of polio vaccine before monkeys.
5. Chemical Safety Testing: Toxicity testing using animals engineered to be hypersensitive.
Trap: Rosie's milk contained alpha-lactalbumin (nutritionally balanced), NOT alpha-1-antitrypsin!

4. Ethical Issues, GEAC & Biopiracy

Legal & Regulatory
GEAC (Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee): Statutory government body under MoEFCC responsible for:
1. Evaluating the safety and validity of GM research.
2. Approving the commercial release of GMOs into public environments.
Biopiracy: Use of bio-resources and traditional indigenous knowledge by multinational companies without proper authorization or compensatory benefit-sharing.
Famous Controversies & Patent Battles:
• Basmati Rice: US company (RiceTec, 1997) patented semi-dwarf Indian Basmati crossed with American varieties; revoked after legal contestation.
• Turmeric (curcumin wound healing) & Neem (fungicidal): Traditional Indian uses successfully defended against foreign patent claims.
• Indian Patents Bill: Amendment passed by Parliament including research emergency terms.
Trap: India alone has 200,000 varieties of rice; 27 documented distinct varieties of Basmati!
