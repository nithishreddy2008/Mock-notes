1. Displacement Current
Ampere-Maxwell Law
Id = ε₀ (dΦE / dt) = ε₀ A (dE / dt)
∮ B · dl = μ₀ (Ic + Id) [Ampere-Maxwell Law]
  • Continuity of Current: Outside plates, conduction current Ic exists (Id = 0); inside gap, displacement current Id exists (Ic = 0). Across any boundary, Ic = Id.
  • Source of B: Magnetic fields are generated by both conduction currents (charges in motion) and displacement currents (time-varying electric fields).
  • Steady DC State: When fully charged, E is constant (dE/dt = 0), so Id = 0 and current ceases.

2. Transverse EM Wave
Orthogonal Fields
Ey = E₀ sin(kx - ωt) | Bz = B₀ sin(kx - ωt)
c = 1 / √(μ₀ ε₀) = ω / k = E₀ / B₀
  • Transverse Nature: E and B vectors oscillate mutually perpendicular to each other and perpendicular to direction of wave propagation (k ∥ E × B).
  • Phase Coherence: E and B reach their maximum and zero values at the same instant and position (phase difference = 0).
  • Medium Independence: Propagate through vacuum without mechanical medium; speed in medium: v = 1/√(μ ε) = c/n.

3. Energetics & Momentum
Radiation Pressure
u_avg = ½ ε₀ E₀² = B₀² / (2μ₀) [Equipartition]
I = u_avg · c = ½ ε₀ c E₀² | p = U / c
  • Equipartition of Energy: Total energy is divided equally between electric and magnetic fields: u_avg,E = u_avg,B.
  • Radiation Pressure: Exerts pressure on surfaces: Prad = I/c for complete absorption; Prad = 2I/c for perfect reflection.
  • Optical Effect: Electric field vector E is primarily responsible for optical sensations and photographic effects (referred to as the light vector).
