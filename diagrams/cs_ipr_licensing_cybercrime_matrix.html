Copyright Protects original literary, artistic, musical, dramatic, and software source code works. Copyright Act, 1957. Grants exclusive rights to reproduce, distribute, and adapt. Plagiarism & Piracy: Unauthorized copying or republishing of source code/text. Protects the expression of an idea, NOT the abstract idea or algorithm itself!
Patent Protects novel, non-obvious industrial inventions and manufacturing processes. Patents Act, 1970. Grants monopoly protection for strictly 20 years from filing. Patent Infringement: Commercial exploitation of patented claims without license. Pure software algorithms are generally non-patentable unless tied to hardware utility.
Trademark Protects distinctive brand names, logos, slogans, and symbols distinguishing goods. Trade Marks Act, 1999. Renewable indefinitely every 10 years upon fee payment. Infringement & Passing Off: Using deceptively similar brand marks to mislead buyers. Protects commercial reputation and market brand identity, not technical mechanics.
FOSS / GPL Free and Open Source Software granting 4 fundamental freedoms (run, study, modify, share). GNU General Public License (GPL), MIT, Apache, BSD copyleft / permissive licenses. Violating copyleft by closing derived source code in commercial distributions. FOSS is free as in 'freedom' (speech), NOT necessarily free of monetary cost (beer)!
IT Act Sec 66 Penal code for unauthorized hacking, system damage, identity theft, and cyber crimes. Information Technology Act, 2000 (Amended 2008): Sec 43, 66, 66C, 66D, 66E, 67. Hacking (66), Identity Theft (66C), Impersonation Cheating (66D), Privacy Violation (66E). Section 66A was struck down as unconstitutional by Supreme Court in Shreya Singhal (2015).
