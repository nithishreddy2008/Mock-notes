Master Accounting Ratios Matrix

Comprehensive mathematical formulas, benchmarks, and interpretation across the four standard ratio families

UNIT V · FINANCIAL RATIOS
1. Liquidity Ratios (Short-Term Solvency) Short-Term Debt Service
Current Ratio Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities Ideal 2 : 1
Quick / Liquid Ratio Quick Assets ÷ Current Liabilities Ideal 1 : 1
• Quick Assets: Current Assets − Inventory − Prepaid Expenses.
• Loose Tools Rule: Loose Tools, Stores & Spares are excluded from Current Assets while calculating Current and Quick Ratios!
CUET Effect Rule: Paying a current liability improves Current Ratio if original CR > 1; decreases CR if original CR < 1!
2. Solvency Ratios (Long-Term Solvency) Capital Leverage & Safety
Debt-Equity Ratio Long-Term Debt ÷ Shareholders' Funds Ideal 2 : 1
Total Assets to Debt Total Assets ÷ Long-Term Debt Higher is Safer
Proprietary Ratio Shareholders' Funds ÷ Total Assets Equity Cushion
Interest Coverage Profit before Interest & Tax ÷ Interest Charges In Times (e.g. 6x)
Debt Composition: Long-Term Debt includes Debentures, Mortgages, and Long-Term Bank Loans. Excludes Current Liabilities!
3. Activity / Turnover Ratios (Asset Efficiency) Velocity of Assets
Inventory Turnover Cost of Revenue from Ops (COGS) ÷ Average Inventory In Times (Speed)
Trade Receivables T/O Net Credit Revenue from Ops ÷ Avg Trade Receivables Debt Collection
Trade Payables T/O Net Credit Purchases ÷ Avg Trade Payables Creditor Payment
Working Capital T/O Revenue from Operations ÷ Working Capital (CA − CL) Working Cap Turn
Average Rule: When opening and closing figures are both given, Average = (Opening + Closing) ÷ 2.
4. Profitability Ratios (Earning Capacity) Margin & Return Metrics
Gross Profit Ratio [ Gross Profit ÷ Net Revenue from Operations ] × 100 Core Margin %
Operating Ratio [ (COGS + Operating Expenses) ÷ Net Revenue ] × 100 Cost Ratio %
Operating Profit Ratio [ Operating Profit ÷ Net Revenue from Operations ] × 100 EBIT Margin %
Return on Investment [ Net Profit before Interest & Tax ÷ Capital Employed ] × 100 ROCE %
Master Identity: Operating Ratio + Operating Profit Ratio = 100%! (If Operating Ratio is 78%, Operating Profit is exactly 22%).
