1. Everyday Physics

Physical Laws

Optics & Wave Phenomena Light

TIR: Mirage, Optical Fibres, Diamonds
Scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴: Blue sky, Red sunset

Total Internal Reflection occurs when light travels from denser to rarer medium beyond critical angle.

Mechanics, Force & Motion Dynamics

Newton I (Inertia), II (F = dp/dt), III (Action-Reaction)
Friction: Static > Sliding > Rolling

Seatbelts counteract inertia of motion. Recoil of gun illustrates momentum conservation.

Thermodynamics & Heat Energy

Latent Heat: 0°C ice cools faster than 0°C water
Evaporation causes cooling (sweating, earthen pots)

Heat transfers: Conduction (solids), Convection (fluids), Radiation (vacuum/electromagnetic).

Electricity & Domestic Circuits Circuits

Household Wiring: Parallel connection (220V, 50Hz)
Fuse Wire: High resistance, Low melting point

Earthing wire (green) drains leakage currents to ground safely, preventing lethal electrical shocks.

CUET Trap: Centrifugal force is a pseudo-force observed only in rotating (non-inertial) reference frames.

2. Everyday Chemistry

Matter & Tech

Acids, Bases & Daily pH pH Scale

pH < 7 Acidic | pH = 7 Neutral | pH > 7 Basic
Blood: 7.35–7.45 | Stomach: 1.5–2.0 (HCl)

Tooth decay starts when mouth pH falls below 5.5. Antacids (Mg(OH)₂, baking soda) neutralize excess acid.

Household Chemical Salts Salts

Baking Soda: NaHCO₃ | Washing Soda: Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
Plaster of Paris: CaSO₄·½H₂O | Bleach: CaOCl₂

Heating Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) at 373 K loses water of crystallisation to form Plaster of Paris.

Metals, Alloys & Corrosion Metallurgy

Brass: Cu + Zn | Bronze: Cu + Sn | Solder: Pb + Sn
Stainless Steel: Fe + Cr + Ni + C (Rust resistant)

Galvanisation coats iron with a thin layer of Zinc (sacrificial protection against air and moisture).

Polymers & Green Environment Materials

Teflon: Non-stick cookware | Bakelite: Switches
Greenhouse Gases: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, Water vapour

CFCs deplete stratospheric ozone (O₃) via chlorine free radical chain reactions over polar regions.

Chemical Rule: Adding water to concentrated acid is dangerously exothermic; always add acid to water slowly!

3. Life Science & Health

Biosystems

Circulation & Blood Types Physiology

Universal Donor: O negative (No A/B/Rh antigens)
Universal Recipient: AB positive (No antibodies)

Arteries carry oxygenated blood away from heart (except Pulmonary Artery). Veins have valves.

Vitamins & Deficiency Diseases Nutrition

Vit A (Night Blindness) | Vit B1 (Beriberi)
Vit C (Scurvy) | Vit D (Rickets) | Vit K (Clotting)

Water-soluble: Vitamins B & C (excreted in urine). Fat-soluble: Vitamins A, D, E, K (stored in liver).

Infectious Diseases & Pathogens Pathology

Viral: COVID-19, Polio, Dengue, Rabies, Measles
Bacterial: Tuberculosis, Cholera, Typhoid, Tetanus

Antibiotics kill bacteria by targeting cell walls/ribosomes; they are completely ineffective against viruses.

Genetics, Hormones & Biotech Biotech

Insulin: Pancreas beta-cells (regulates glucose)
DNA: Double helix (A=T, G≡C) | Master Gland: Pituitary

CRISPR-Cas9 acts as molecular scissors for targeted genome editing; mRNA vaccines teach spike synthesis.

Biological Fact: Mitochondria possess their own circular DNA and 70S ribosomes (endosymbiosis).
