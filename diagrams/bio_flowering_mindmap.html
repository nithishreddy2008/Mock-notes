Stamen & Pollen

Stamen = anther + filament; anther is bilobed with 4 microsporangia (dithecous)
Wall layers: epidermis → endothecium → middle layers → tapetum (feeds pollen)
Microsporogenesis: pollen mother cell (2n) —meiosis→ 4 microspores in a tetrad
Pollen: tough exine (sporopollenin) + thin intine; at shedding 60% two-celled, 40% three-celled

Pistil & Embryo Sac

Pistil = stigma + style + ovary; ovule parts: funicle, hilum, integuments, micropyle, chalaza, nucellus
Megasporogenesis: megaspore mother cell (2n) → 4 megaspores in a row — the chalazal one survives
Embryo sac: egg + 2 synergids (micropylar end), 2 polar nuclei in the centre, 3 antipodals (chalazal end)
Count it as 7 cells, 8 nuclei; filiform apparatus of synergids guides the pollen tube

Endosperm & Embryo

Endosperm (3n): free-nuclear divisions first, then cellular — coconut water vs coconut meat
Albuminous seeds keep endosperm (castor, coconut); non-albuminous use it up (pea, bean, groundnut)
Dicot embryo: 2 cotyledons, plumule up, radicle down; monocot: scutellum, coleoptile, coleorrhiza
Seed coats: testa outside, tegmen inside; perisperm = leftover nucellus (castor)

Pollination & Agents

Autogamy (same flower; cleistogamy in Viola, Commelina) · geitonogamy (same plant) · xenogamy (different plant — the only true cross)
Wind: light, dry, non-sticky pollen; feathery stigmas (grasses)
Water: rare hydrophily — Vallisneria, Zostera; pollen shielded from wetting
Insects: colour, scent, nectar; Yucca–moth obligate pair; pollen robbers take the reward, bring nothing

Double Fertilisation

Pollen tube grows through the style and enters a synergid via the micropyle
Syngamy: sperm + egg → zygote (2n), the first cell of the new plant
Triple fusion: second sperm + 2 polar nuclei → primary endosperm nucleus (3n)
Both together = double fertilisation — only in angiosperms; food is made only after fertilisation succeeds

Apomixis & Polyembryony

Apomixis: seeds without meiosis or fusion — a clone of the mother (diploid egg or nucellar cells)
Examples: Citrus and Mango (nucellar embryony); apomictic hybrids keep their edge year after year
Polyembryony: many embryos in one seed (Citrus, from nucellar cells)
Fruit = mature ovary; parthenocarpy = seedless fruit without fertilisation (banana, seedless grapes)

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Endosperm is 3n, not 2n — one sperm + two polar nuclei, all haploid
Syngamy uses one sperm, triple fusion the other — the two fuse events together are "double" fertilisation
The chalazal megaspore survives — the three micropylar ones degenerate
Coconut water = free-nuclear endosperm; coconut meat = cellular endosperm — do not flip them
