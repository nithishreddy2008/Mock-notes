1. Gametophyte Genesis & Architecture

Micro- & Megasporogenesis
Anther & Tapetum 4 wall layers: Epidermis, Endothecium (α-cellulose bands), Middle, Tapetum (multinucleate, polyploid, nourishes microspores, secretes callase & sporopollenin).
Pollen Grain (Male) Exine (Sporopollenin + germ pores) + Intine (pectin-cellulose). Shed at 2-celled (60%) or 3-celled (40%) stage. Cryopreserved at -196°C.
Ovule & Nucellus Anatropous ovule: Funicle, Hilum, Integuments, Micropyle, Chalaza, Nucellus. 1 MMC (2n) → Meiosis → 4 megaspores → 1 functional (chalazal).
Embryo Sac (Polygonum) 7-celled, 8-nucleate: 3 antipodals (chalaza), 1 central cell (2 polar nuclei, n+n), egg apparatus (2 synergids with Filiform apparatus + 1 egg, micropyle).

2. Pollination Types & Outbreeding Devices

Transfer & Inbreeding Prevention
Types of Pollination • Autogamy: Same flower.
• Geitonogamy: Different flower, same plant (ecologically cross, genetically autogamous).
• Xenogamy: Genetically different flowers.
Cleistogamous Flowers Closed flowers, never open; assured seed set without pollinators (Viola, Oxalis, Commelina). Chasmogamous: open flowers.
Abiotic Agents • Wind (Anemophily): Light, non-sticky pollen, feathery stigma, corn cob tassels.
• Water (Hydrophily): Vallisneria, Zostera, Hydrilla. (Water lily & hyacinth = insects!).
Outbreeding Devices Dichogamy, Herkogamy, Self-incompatibility (genetic block). Dioecy (Papaya prevents autogamy & geitonogamy; Castor is monoecious).

3. Double Fertilization & Endosperm Engine

Nawaschin's Dual Event
Pollen Tube Guidance Enters micropyle guided chemotropically by Filiform apparatus; penetrates one synergid; discharges 2 male gametes.
Syngamy & Triple Fusion • Syngamy: 1 sperm + egg → Zygote (2n).
• Triple Fusion: 2nd sperm + 2 polar nuclei → PEN (3n).
Together = Double Fertilization.
Endosperm Ontogeny Precedes embryo development. Free-nuclear (repeated mitoses without walls, e.g. coconut water) → Cellular (white coconut meat).
Albuminous vs Non-Albuminous • Non-albuminous: Endosperm consumed (Pea, Bean, Groundnut).
• Albuminous: Endosperm persists (Wheat, Maize, Barley, Castor).

4. Embryogeny, Perisperm & Apomixis

Post-Fertilization & Asexual Mimicry
Embryo Morphology • Dicot: 2 cotyledons, epicotyl → plumule, hypocotyl → radicle.
• Monocot: 1 cotyledon (Scutellum), Coleoptile (shoot cap), Coleorhiza (root cap).
Fruit Types & Perisperm • True fruit: Ovary alone.
• False fruit: Thalamus contributes (Apple, Strawberry, Cashew).
• Perisperm: Persistent nucellus (Black Pepper, Beet).
Parthenocarpy Development of fruit without fertilization; seedless fruits (e.g. Banana); induced by application of auxins and gibberellins.
Apomixis & Polyembryony • Apomixis: Seed formation without fertilization (mimics sex; e.g. Asteraceae).
• Polyembryony: Multiple embryos from nucellar cell intrusion (Citrus, Mango).
