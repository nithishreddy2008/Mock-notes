Books & Authors

Evergreen pairs: Gitanjali — Tagore; Discovery of India — Nehru; Godan — Premchand; Wings of Fire — Kalam
Link trick: attach each book to ONE hook (theme, author's role, or era)

Awards — Who Gets What

Civilian: Bharat Ratna is India's highest — the peak of the pyramid
Military: Param Vir Chakra sits at the top for wartime valour
Fields: Jnanpith (literature), Dadasaheb Phalke (cinema), Nobel (world, six fields)

Important Days

National: 26 Jan Republic · 15 Aug Independence · 5 Sept Teachers' · 14 Nov Children's
International: 8 Mar Women's · 22 Apr Earth · 5 Jun Environment · 1 Dec AIDS
Memory rule: learn days in month order, five at a time — never as a random list

The Current-Affairs System

Daily: 15-minute news diet — headlines, one deep read, note grid
Monthly: consolidate the grids into one revision page per month
Categories: national, international, sports, awards, science-tech, appointments

Geography & Polity Anchors

Geography: longest river Ganga; India's highest peak Kanchenjunga; largest state Rajasthan
Polity: President — head of the State; PM — head of the Government; Parliament = Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha
Firsts: first President Rajendra Prasad; first woman President Pratibha Patil; first PM Nehru

Scoring Habits

Grids over rereading: every fact gets the 5-field note treatment once
Revise in cycles: weekly 15-minute grid scans beat pre-exam cramming
Verify currency: officeholders and award names change — check a current source near exam time

Traps that catch most students

Award-pyramid swaps — Bharat Ratna (civilian) vs Param Vir Chakra (military) vs Khel Ratna (sports)
Day-date slips — Teachers' (5 Sept) vs Children's (14 Nov); Earth Day (22 Apr) vs Environment Day (5 Jun)
Stale current affairs — last year's facts in this year's exam are distractors, not answers
Reading without noting — the grid is what converts reading into marks
