UNIT IX · COMPLETE NCERT ARCHITECTURE
1. Restriction Enzymes & Gel Analysis Molecular Tools
• First Restriction Endonuclease: Hind II; recognizes and cuts at a specific 6 bp palindromic sequence.
• EcoRI Cleavage Site: 5'-GAATTC-3' / 3'-CTTAAG-5'; cuts between G and A leaving cohesive sticky ends.
• Nomenclature Rule: E = Escherichia, co = coli, R = strain RY13, I = order of discovery.
• Gel Electrophoresis: Negatively charged DNA migrates to anode (+) through agarose; smaller bands travel farthest.
• Staining & Elution: Stained with Ethidium Bromide (EtBr), glows bright orange under UV light. Excision = Elution.
2. Cloning Vectors & pBR322 Plasmid Vehicles
• Essential Features: ori (initiates replication & controls copy number); rop (replication of plasmid proteins).
• Selectable Markers: Differentiate transformants from non-transformants (ampr and tetr).
• Cloning Sites on pBR322: PstI and PvuI inside ampr; BamHI and SalI inside tetr; EcoRI, ClaI, HindIII elsewhere.
• Insertional Inactivation (BamHI): Inserting foreign DNA into BamHI disrupts tetr; cell stays ampicillin-resistant but becomes tetracycline-sensitive.
• Blue-White Selection: Inactivation of β-galactosidase (lacZ); white colonies = recombinants; blue = non-recombinant.
3. Host Competence & Gene Delivery Transformation
• Chemical Competence (Bacteria): Divalent cation (Ca2+) treatment + heat shock (42°C) and rapid ice cooling.
• Micro-injection: Recombinant DNA directly injected into the nucleus of an animal cell using micro-pipettes.
• Biolistics (Gene Gun): High-velocity microscopic pellets of gold or tungsten coated with DNA fired into plant cells.
• Plant Vector: Disarmed Ti plasmid of Agrobacterium tumefaciens (natural genetic engineer of dicot plants).
• Animal Vector: Disarmed retroviruses used to introduce beneficial genes into mammalian host cells.
4. Isolation & Purification of Genetic Material Extraction
• Cell Wall Digestion: Lysozyme for bacteria, Cellulase for plant cells, Chitinase for fungi.
• Macromolecule Removal: RNA digested by Ribonuclease (RNase); proteins digested by Proteases.
• Alcohol Precipitation: Addition of chilled ethanol precipitates pure DNA as a collection of fine threads.
• Spooling: Physical extraction and spooling of purified DNA threads onto a glass rod.
5. Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) In Vitro Synthesis
• Inventor: Developed by Kary Mullis (1983) for in vitro billion-fold gene amplification ($2^n$).
• Step 1 (Denaturation, 94°C): Thermal separation of double-stranded DNA into single template strands.
• Step 2 (Annealing, 54°C): Two sets of synthetic oligonucleotide primers anneal to 3' ends of templates.
• Step 3 (Extension, 72°C): Thermostable Taq polymerase synthesizes strands using dNTPs and Mg2+.
• Taq Source: Isolated from thermophilic bacterium Thermus aquaticus; withstands 95°C temperatures.
6. Bioreactors & Downstream Processing Scaling Up
• Bioreactors (100–1000 L): Provide optimal growth conditions (pH, temperature, oxygen, salts, substrate).
• Simple vs Sparged Tank: Simple stirred tank has curved base & flat agitator; sparged tank bubbles air to boost surface area.
• Upstream Processing: Inoculation, media preparation, fermentation, and recombinant protein synthesis.
• Downstream Processing: Separation + Purification of the target product, followed by addition of preservatives.
• Quality Control (QC): Stringent QC testing and clinical trials (for therapeutics) before commercial release.
⚠️ High-Yield CUET Pitfalls & Traps to Avoid
1. EcoRI cuts specifically between G and A ($5'-"G ↓ AATTC"-3'$), NOT in the exact center!
2. Thermus aquaticus is a thermophilic bacterium, NOT an archaebacterium.
3. In Blue-White screening, white colonies are the recombinants (inactivated lacZ), blue are non-recombinants.
4. Gene gun (biolistics) microprojectiles are made of gold or tungsten, used for plant cells.
