READING COMPREHENSION
CUET UG ENGLISH · LANGUAGE TEST

The Three Passage Types

Factual: facts, figures and findings — news-report style, up to 300 words
Narrative: a story told in order — events, motives, changes
Literary: poem or prose with mood and images — feelings matter as much as facts

The Reading Method

Step 1: read the passage once at normal speed — paragraph main ideas only
Step 2: read the questions and note what each one wants
Step 3 + 4: return to the exact lines, eliminate options, then answer

Question Families

Main idea / title: what covers the WHOLE passage, not one paragraph
Detail: locate the exact line — never answer from memory
Vocabulary in context: replace the word, keep the sentence's meaning
Inference and tone: what the lines suggest; how the writer sounds

How Each Type Feels

Factual: underline numbers and dates as you pass them
Narrative: keep a who → what → why timeline in your head
Literary: track the writer's mood — happy, sad, curious, critical

The Elimination Skill

Out of scope: true in real life but never said in the passage — out
Half-true: three right words, one wrong word — the option is wrong
Extreme words: always, never, only — almost always the wrong choice
Too narrow or too wide: one detail is not the main idea

Scoring Habits

Evidence first: every answer must point back to a line
Moderate options win: calm, measured wording is usually safest
Attempt everything: +5 right, −1 wrong — skip only when truly clueless

Traps that catch most students

Answering from general knowledge instead of the passage's own lines
Picking the first option that sounds nice — read all four, then choose
Treating one paragraph as the main idea — the main idea covers everything
Swapped dates and names in factual options — verify at the exact line
