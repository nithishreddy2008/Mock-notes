The E-Waste Hazard Matrix & Sustainable Recycling Lifecycle

Scientific recycling trajectory from collection to precious metal recovery and toxic heavy metal containment

CUET Code 308 · Ecological Protocol
1
Collection & Sorting
Formal take-back collection points, segregation of obsolete computers, mobile phones, servers, and CRTs.
Prevents unauthorized dumping into municipal landfills.
2
Dismantling & De-manufacturing
Manual disassembly isolating printed circuit boards (PCBs), batteries, display panels, and plastics.
Safe extraction of mercury lamps and lithium cells.
3
Shredding & Separation
Mechanical shredding followed by magnetic (iron), eddy-current (aluminum/copper), and density separation.
Recovers raw plastics and ferrous metals.
4
Smelting & Leaching
Certified hydrometallurgical and pyrometallurgical recovery of gold, silver, palladium, and platinum.
Slag encapsulated in secure hazardous waste landfills.

Lead (Pb)

Found in CRT screens and solder. Causes severe central nervous system, kidney, and brain damage.

Mercury (Hg)

Found in flat-panel backlights and switches. Causes chronic bioaccumulation and neural impairment.

Cadmium (Cd)

Found in SMD chip resistors and Ni-Cd batteries. Toxic to kidneys and causes bone fragility.

Chromium (Cr-VI)

Used for corrosion protection on chassis. Hexavalent form causes DNA mutations and respiratory cancer.
