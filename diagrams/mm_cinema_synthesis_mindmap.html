Master Synthesis Mindmap: Cinema History, Film Aesthetics & Language

Six-pillar conceptual synthesis covering early cinema, global movements, Indian film history, mise-en-scène, editing & sound

CUET Mass Media · Unit 5 Synthesis
1. Early Cinema & Pioneers
Persistence of vision (Roget, Wertheimer); Muybridge galloping horse (1878); Edison Kinetoscope (1891); Lumière Brothers Cinématographe (28 Dec 1895, Paris); Georges Méliès trick films (1902); Porter cross-cutting; D.W. Griffith.
Lumière 1895 Méliès 1902 Kinetoscope D.W. Griffith
2. World Cinema Movements
German Expressionism (Caligari, chiaroscuro, painted shadows); Soviet Montage (Kuleshov effect, Eisensteinian collision editing, Potemkin); Italian Neorealism (Bicycle Thieves, non-actors); French New Wave (Godard, jump cut, Auteur theory).
Expressionism Soviet Montage Neorealism Auteur Theory
3. Indian Cinema Milestones
Dadasaheb Phalke (Raja Harishchandra, 3 May 1913); Ardeshir Irani (Alam Ara, 14 March 1931); Golden Age 1950s (Mother India, Pyaasa, Awaara); Parallel Cinema / New Wave: Satyajit Ray (Pather Panchali, 1955), Ritwik Ghatak, Mrinal Sen.
Phalke 1913 Alam Ara 1931 Pather Panchali Mother India
4. Mise-en-scène & Cinematography
French "placing on stage": Setting/decor, costumes, lighting (chiaroscuro, high/low key), actor blocking & spatial proxemics; Aspect ratios: Academy (1.37:1), Widescreen (1.85:1), CinemaScope (2.39:1); Focal length & depth of field.
Mise-en-scène Chiaroscuro Aspect Ratios Blocking
5. Film Editing & Continuity
Continuity Editing (180° rule, eyeline match, match-on-action, shot-reverse-shot, cross-cutting); Disjunctive editing: Jump Cut; Eisenstein's 5 Montages: Metric, Rhythmic, Tonal, Overtonal, Intellectual (abstract concept synthesis).
180° Rule Jump Cut Cross-Cutting 5 Montages
6. Film Sound & Regulation
Diegetic sound (inside story world: dialogue, footsteps) vs Non-diegetic sound (outside: score, narrator VO); Foley effects; Cinematograph Act 1952; CBFC certification (U, U/A 7+/13+/16+, A, S); National Film Development Corp (NFDC), FTII.
Diegetic Sound Foley CBFC / 1952 Act NFDC / FTII
