Architecture & Devices

🌐 Network Scales & Classification

  • PAN (Personal Area): Span $< 10$ m. Bluetooth, Zigbee, infrared personal devices.
  • LAN (Local Area): Office, school building, lab ($< 1$–10 km). High data rate, low error rate.
  • MAN (Metropolitan Area): City-wide scope (10–50 km). Cable TV networks, municipal fiber.
  • WAN (Wide Area): Country/global scale. Connects disparate LANs/MANs via routers & satellites.
  • Architectures: Client-Server (centralized service) vs Peer-to-Peer (equal node rights, torrents).

📡 Transmission Media (Guided & Unguided)

  • Twisted Pair (UTP/STP): Copper wires twisted to cancel EMI and crosstalk. Cheap, RJ-45 jack.
  • Coaxial Cable: Solid copper core, dielectric insulator, braided shield. BNC connectors.
  • Optical Fiber: Total Internal Reflection of light. Immense bandwidth, immune to EMI, low loss.
  • Radio Waves: Omni-directional, penetrates walls (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth).
  • Microwaves: Unidirectional, line-of-sight required. Relay towers every 25–50 km.
  • Infrared & Satellite: Infrared cannot penetrate walls (secure). Satellite for global WAN links.

📐 Network Topologies & Layouts

  • Star Topology: Central hub/switch. Easy fault isolation. Central hub failure halts network.
  • Bus Topology: Single linear backbone wire with terminators. Cheap cable; cable break disables all.
  • Tree Topology: Hierarchical star-bus combination. Scalable; root failure impacts whole branch.
  • Mesh Topology: Point-to-point links between all nodes ($n(n-1)/2$ cables). Highest fault tolerance.
  • Ring Topology: Circular unidirectional loop. Token passing protocol. Cable break halts network.

⚙️ Network Connecting Devices

  • Repeater & Hub (L1): Amplifies signals. Hub broadcasts to all ports (1 collision domain).
  • Bridge & Switch (L2): Filters frames by physical 48-bit MAC address. Isolates collision domains.
  • Router (L3): Forwards packets using logical 32-bit IP addresses. Blocks broadcasts.
  • Gateway (L4–L7): Protocol converter linking incompatible network architectures.
  • Modem: Modulates digital bits to analog carrier waves and demodulates returning analog.
