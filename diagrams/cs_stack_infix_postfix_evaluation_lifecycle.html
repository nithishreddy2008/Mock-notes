Conversion Invariants:

• Operands: Sent immediately to Postfix output.
• Left Parenthesis `(`: Pushed directly to stack.
• Right Parenthesis `)`: Pop and output operators until matching `(` is popped and discarded.
• Operator $op$: Pop stack while $Top$ has $\ge$ precedence (or $>$ if right-associative).

CRITICAL OPERAND POP ORDER TRAP:
When an operator is encountered:
first_pop = op2 (Right Operand)
second_pop = op1 (Left Operand)
Calculation is op1 - op2 or op1 / op2. Inverting this order to op2 - op1 produces catastrophic sign and division errors!

Exponentiation Trap (`**`):

2 ** 3 ** 2 evaluates from right-to-left as 2 ** (3 ** 2) = 2 ** 9 = 512, NOT (2 ** 3) ** 2 = 64!

When pushing ** to stack, an incoming ** does not pop an existing ** because of Right-to-Left associativity.
