1. The Spectral Landscape
Wavelength Ladder
c = ν · λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s | E = hν = hc / λ
λ_gamma < λ_X < λ_UV < λ_Vis < λ_IR < λ_Micro < λ_Radio
  • Continuity: No sharp boundary exists between adjacent regions of the electromagnetic spectrum; overlapping regions are classified by method of production.
  • Energy Hierarchy: Gamma rays have highest photon energy ($E \sim \text{MeV}$); Radio waves have lowest energy ($E \sim 10^{-6}\text{ eV}$).
  • Visible Octave: Spans narrow band from $400\text{ nm}$ (violet, $7.5 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$) to $700\text{ nm}$ (red, $4.3 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$).

2. Sources & Detectors
Physical Mechanisms
LC Resonance: ν = 1 / (2π√(LC)) [Radio Waves]
Duane-Hunt Law: λ_min = hc / (e·V) [X-ray Cutoff]
  • Bremsstrahlung X-Rays: Decelerating electrons emit continuous X-rays with cut-off $\lambda_{\text{min}} = 12400 / V\text{ (\AA)}$.
  • Ozone Absorption: Stratospheric ozone ($O_3$) strongly absorbs solar UV below $300\text{ nm}$, protecting organic life.
  • Greenhouse Trap: Earth absorbs visible light and re-emits long-wavelength IR; atmospheric $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ vapour absorb IR, trapping thermal energy.

3. High-Yield CUET Facts
Technological Uses
Rayleigh Scattering: I_scattered ∝ 1 / λ⁴
Radio Bands: AM (530-1710 kHz) | FM (88-108 MHz) | Cellular UHF
  • Microwave Radar: Short wavelength ($1\text{ mm} - 30\text{ cm}$) produces narrow directive beams without spreading, ideal for aircraft navigation and speed guns.
  • Infrared Remote Controls: Light emitting diodes (LEDs) emit pulses of infrared radiation picked up by phototransistors in televisions.
  • Welders Mask: Special glass goggles with high UV absorption are worn by welders to shield retinas from harmful UV radiation produced in electric arcs.
