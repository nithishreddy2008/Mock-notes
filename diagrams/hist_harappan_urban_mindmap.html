Key Exam Focus · Urban Architecture

Standardized Bricks & The Great Bath

Across all Harappan settlements, baked bricks preserved a rigid ratio of 1 : 2 : 4 (thickness : width : length). The Great Bath on Mohenjodaro's Citadel was waterproofed with edge-laid bricks sealed by gypsum mortar for collective ceremonial cleansing.

Municipal Infrastructure

Grid Drainage & Visual Privacy

Streets intersected at strict 90° right angles and drains were engineered before houses. Residential ground-level perimeter walls had zero windows, and the front entrance never allowed passersby a direct line of sight into the courtyard.

Agricultural & Industrial Sites

Crucial Site Disambiguations

NTA traps frequently test exact site locations: Kalibangan has the ploughed field; Banawali has terracotta plough models; Shortughai (Afghanistan) holds canals and lapis lazuli; and Chanhudaro has no fortified citadel.
