Harappan Settlement Anatomy · Five Structural Tiers

From the elevated ritual and governance summit of the Citadel down to municipal sanitary arteries, residential privacy, and frontier resource-extraction outposts across ancient South Asia.

Key Exam Focus · Urban Architecture

Standardized Bricks & The Great Bath

Across all Harappan settlements from Baluchistan to Gujarat, baked bricks preserved a rigid ratio of 1 : 2 : 4 (thickness : width : length). The Great Bath on Mohenjodaro's Citadel was waterproofed with edge-laid bricks sealed by gypsum mortar, indicating highly organized collective ceremonial cleansing.

Municipal Infrastructure

Grid Drainage & Visual Privacy

Streets intersected at strict 90° right angles. Streets and drains were engineered before houses were built along them. In residential houses, ground-level perimeter walls had zero windows, and the front entrance never allowed passersby a direct line of sight into the central courtyard.

Agricultural & Industrial Sites

Crucial Site Disambiguations

NTA traps frequently test exact site locations: Kalibangan possesses the ploughed field; Banawali and Cholistan have terracotta plough models; Shortughai (Afghanistan) holds irrigation canals and lapis lazuli; and Chanhudaro is the only site with no fortified citadel.
