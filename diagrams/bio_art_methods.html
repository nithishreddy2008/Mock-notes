Infertility Definition

Clinical Criterion: Inability to conceive or produce children despite 1 to 2 years of regular, unprotected coitus.
Underlying Causes: Physical, congenital, hormonal, drug-induced, immunological, or psychological defects in either partner.
Primary Indian Bias: In India, the female is unfairly blamed, yet in more than 50% of couples, the defect lies in the male partner.
Ultimate Solution: If ART is financially or biologically unfeasible, legal child adoption represents the most humane path forward.

IVF & Embryo Transfer

"Test-Tube Baby" · Outside-Body Fertilization
Step 1: Oocyte & Sperm Harvesting
Ova from wife/donor + sperm from husband/donor induced to fuse under sterile lab conditions.
Step 2A: ZIFT Protocol
Zygote or early embryo with up to 8 blastomeres transferred.
TARGET: FALLOPIAN TUBE
Step 2B: IUT Protocol
Embryo with more than 8 blastomeres transferred.
TARGET: UTERINE CAVITY
IN VIVO FERTILIZATION (GIFT)

GIFT Procedure

Gamete Intra-Fallopian Transfer · Natural Fusion
Clinical Indication:
Female partner is unable to produce an ovum (anovulatory) but possesses healthy patent fallopian tubes and a normal receptive uterus.
Surgical Technique:
Unfertilized ovum collected from a healthy donor female is transferred into the patient's fallopian tube alongside husband's sperms.
Physiological Key:
Fertilization and early cleavage take place in vivo (naturally inside the patient's fallopian tube).
MICROMANIPULATION & AI

ICSI & IUI Modalities

Male Factor Infertility & Structural Barriers
ICSI (Intra-Cytoplasmic Sperm Injection):
Specialized micromanipulation where a single sperm is injected directly into ovum cytoplasm under microscope. Used for severe oligospermia/motility defects.
AI / IUI (Artificial Insemination):
Semen collected from husband or donor is artificially introduced into the vagina or uterus (IUI). Indicated for low sperm count or erectile dysfunction.
Amniocentesis & Statutory Ban: Foetal cells and biochemicals in amniotic fluid sampled at 14–16 weeks. Legitimate purpose: diagnosing chromosomal disorders (Down syndrome, Turner's, Klinefelter's, sickle-cell). Statutory ban enforced in India to curb female foeticide.
MTP Act (1971 & 2017 Amendment): Medical Termination of Pregnancy is legalized to avoid illegal quack abortions. Safest in 1st Trimester (up to 12 weeks). 1 RMP opinion required up to 12 weeks; 2 RMP opinions for 12–20 weeks (up to 24 weeks for rape survivors / minors).
NTA CUET Critical Threshold: Remember the blastomere count rule for ET: $\le 8$ blastomeres $\to$ ZIFT (Fallopian Tube); $> 8$ blastomeres $\to$ IUT (Uterus). GIFT is strictly an in vivo fertilization technique.
