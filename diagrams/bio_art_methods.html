ASSISTED REPRODUCTIVE TECHNOLOGIES AT A GLANCE
UNIT VI · INFERTILITY SPECIALISTS' TOOLKIT

IVF

In Vitro Fertilisation — "in glass", outside the body
TRANSFERRED
Embryo grown in glassware, moved at 8 or more blastomeres.
WHERE IT GOES
INTO THE UTERUS
BEST WHEN
Tubes are blocked or counts are very low — the famous test-tube baby method.

ZIFT

Zygote Intrafallopian Transfer
TRANSFERRED
Zygote or early embryo with up to 8 blastomeres.
WHERE IT GOES
INTO THE FALLOPIAN TUBE
BEST WHEN
Tubes are healthy — the zygote then rides naturally down to the uterus.

GIFT

Gamete Intrafallopian Transfer
TRANSFERRED
Unfertilised gametes — egg + sperm; no lab fertilisation step.
WHERE IT GOES
INTO THE FALLOPIAN TUBE
BEST WHEN
Fertilisation should happen inside the body, in the tube itself.

ICSI

Intra Cytoplasmic Sperm Injection
TRANSFERRED
One sperm injected straight into the egg's cytoplasm — a lab step added to IVF.
WHERE IT GOES
EMBRYO INTO THE UTERUS
BEST WHEN
Sperm cannot enter the egg on its own.

IUI

Intra Uterine Insemination
TRANSFERRED
Prepared semen — washed and concentrated in the lab.
WHERE IT GOES
INTO THE UTERUS
BEST WHEN
Low sperm count or poor sperm quality; a simpler first option.
