Quantity effect: Q ALWAYS expands unambiguously (Q₂ > Q₁). • If ΔD > ΔS: Price rises (P₂ > P₁). • If ΔD = ΔS: Price is CONSTANT (P₂ = P₁). • If ΔS > ΔD: Price falls (P₂ < P₁).

Equilibrium Foundation Theorems

  • Market Clearance: Equilibrium is the unique price where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied ($q_D(P^*) = q_S(P^*)$). Zero unsold surplus, zero unmet shortage.
  • Walrasian Price Adjustment: If $P > P^* \implies$ Excess supply induces price cuts; if $P < P^* \implies$ Excess demand bids price up.

The 4 Core Simultaneous Shift Theorems

  • Both Demand & Supply Increase: Quantity definitely rises; Price depends on which shift is larger.
  • Both Demand & Supply Decrease: Quantity definitely falls; Price depends on which shift is larger.
  • Demand Increases & Supply Decreases: Price definitely rises; Quantity change is indeterminate.
  • Demand Decreases & Supply Increases: Price definitely falls; Quantity change is indeterminate.

CUET Trap: Extreme Elasticity Shifts

Fatal Trap: Believing shifts always change both P and Q. Reality: If supply is perfectly elastic (horizontal), a demand shift changes only quantity; price is 100% fixed! If supply is perfectly inelastic (vertical), a demand shift changes only price; quantity is 100% fixed!
