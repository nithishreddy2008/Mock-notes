Levels of Biodiversity

Genetic: variety within one species — 50,000+ rice strains, ~1,000 mango varieties in India
Species: variety of species in a place — Western Ghats beat Eastern Ghats for amphibians
Ecological: variety of ecosystems — India's deserts-to-coral-reefs range beats Norway
Counted so far: ~1.7–1.8 million species described; Robert May estimates ~7 million exist

Patterns of Richness

Latitudinal gradient: richness rises towards the tropics — Colombia ~1,400 birds vs Greenland 56
Why tropics win: long evolutionary time · constant climate · more solar energy
Species–area: S = C·Az — log S = log C + z log A
Slope z: 0.1–0.2 for small regions; 0.6–1.2 for whole continents

Causes of Loss — the Evil Quartet

Habitat loss & fragmentation (biggest): Amazon cut for soy and cattle; rain forest 14% → 6%
Over-exploitation: Steller's sea cow and the passenger pigeon hunted to extinction
Alien invasions: Nile perch killed 200+ cichlids; Lantana, water hyacinth, Parthenium in India
Co-extinction: parasites and pollinators die with their partners

Why It Matters — 3 Views

Narrowly utilitarian: food, fibre, firewood; ~25% of world drugs come from plants
Broadly utilitarian: free ecosystem services — Amazon ≈ 20% of atmospheric oxygen; services ≈ US $33 trillion/yr
Ethical: every species has intrinsic value; pass nature on intact
Bioprospecting: searching biodiversity for useful products

In Situ Conservation

Idea: protect species inside their natural home — the whole ecosystem stays alive
Tools: national parks · wildlife sanctuaries · biosphere reserves · sacred groves
Sacred groves: Khasi & Jaintia Hills (Meghalaya); Bishnoi villages (Rajasthan)
Hotspots: very high endemism + very high habitat loss — India has 3 (see strip below)

Ex Situ Conservation

Idea: protect the species away from its natural home, under human care
Tools: zoos (captive breeding) · wildlife safari parks · seed & pollen banks
Lab routes: tissue culture · in vitro fertilisation · cryopreservation at −196°C
Used when: habitat is gone or wild numbers are too small to survive alone

COMMON ERRORS & TRAPS

Hotspot needs BOTH: high endemism AND high habitat loss — not just many species
Nile perch = alien invasion, not over-exploitation
Sacred groves are in situ — the forest is protected exactly where it stands
Endemic ≠ extinct: endemic means "found nowhere else", not "already gone"
