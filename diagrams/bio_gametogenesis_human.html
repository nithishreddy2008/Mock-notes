♂ Spermatogenesis (Seminiferous Tubules)

Continuous from Puberty

1. Endocrine Regulation & Niche Somatic Cells: 2n

Pubertal hypothalamic GnRH stimulates anterior pituitary LH and FSH:

Leydig Cells (LH target) Interstitial endocrine cells; synthesize and secrete androgens (Testosterone).
Sertoli Cells (FSH target) Nurse cells; provide nutrition, form blood-testis barrier, secrete ABP and Inhibin.

2. Meiotic Progression & Differentiation 1 Primary (2n) → 4 Sperms (n)

Spermatogonia (2n, 46) mitotically proliferate on basement membrane. Some grow into Primary Spermatocytes (2n, 46). Undergo Meiosis I to yield 2 Secondary Spermatocytes (n, 23). Meiosis II yields 4 Spermatids (n, 23).

• Spermiogenesis: Transformation of non-motile circular spermatids into flagellated motile spermatozoa.
• Spermiation: Release of mature spermatozoa from Sertoli cells into the seminiferous tubule lumen.

3. Mature Sperm Morphology (Spermatozoon) 200–300M per Ejaculate

Head Haploid nucleus; capped by Acrosome (Golgi-derived, hyaluronidase/enzymes).
Neck Contains Proximal Centriole (initiates zygote cleavage) & distal centriole.
Middle Piece Spiral mitochondria (Nebenkern); ATP engine for vigorous motility.
Tail Axial axoneme (9+2 microtubule array); facilitates whip-like movement.

Normal Fertility Criteria: ≥60% sperms must have normal morphology; ≥40% of those must exhibit vigorous motility.

♀ Oogenesis (Ovarian Cortex)

Embryonic Origin · Discontinuous

1. Prenatal Origin & Diplotene Arrest Arrest I: Prophase I

~2 million Oogonia (2n, 46) form in fetal ovary; zero oogonia added after birth. Form Primary Oocytes (2n, 46), which enter Meiosis I and arrest at Prophase I (Diplotene) until puberty.

Follicular Atresia: Only 60,000–80,000 primary follicles survive in each ovary at menarche.

2. Meiosis I Completion & Ovulation Arrest II: Metaphase II

Tertiary follicle completes Meiosis I just before ovulation: unequal cytokinesis yields 1 Secondary Oocyte (n, 23) + First Polar Body (n). Enters Meiosis II and arrests at Metaphase II. Ovulated at this stage under LH surge!

Meiosis II Completion: Triggered only by sperm entry in ampulla, forming haploid Ovum (Ootid, n) + 2nd Polar Body (n).

3. Ovulated Ovum Protective Envelopes Polyspermy Barrier

Corona Radiata Outer layer of radiating follicular granulosa cells cemented by hyaluronic acid.
Zona Pellucida Non-cellular glycoproteinaceous coat; mediates sperm binding & cortical block to polyspermy.
Perivitelline Space Fluid-filled space between plasma membrane and zona pellucida where polar bodies lie.

Cortical Reaction: Upon sperm fusion, cortical granules release exocytic enzymes that harden the zona pellucida, permanently preventing polyspermy.
