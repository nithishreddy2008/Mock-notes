1. Commercial Arithmetic

Core 35%

Percentages & Net Change Rule

Net% = a + b + (ab / 100)%

Two successive changes of a% and b%. If equal increase/decrease of x%: always loss = -(x/10)²%.

Profit, Loss & Mark-up Trading

P% = (SP - CP)/CP × 100
SP = MP × (1 - D1/100)(1 - D2/100)

Dishonest dealer selling at CP using weight w instead of W: Gain% = (Error / True Value - Error) × 100.

Simple & Compound Interest Finance

SI = (P·R·T)/100 | A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ
Diff (2 Yrs) = P(R / 100)²

Diff for 3 Yrs: P(R/100)² × (3 + R/100). Rule of 72: Doubling time ≈ 72 / R years.

Partnership & Profit Share Ratio

Profit Ratio = (C₁ × T₁) : (C₂ × T₂)

Working partner commission is deducted before net profit is distributed by capital-time weights.

CUET Pro-Tip: Multiplying factors save 40s. A 15% hike = ×1.15; 20% discount = ×0.80.

2. Rates & Movement

High Yield

Time & Work Dynamics LCM Model

Total Work = LCM(days)
Efficiency = Work / Days

If A takes x days and B takes y days: Combined = (xy) / (x + y) days. MDH: (M₁·D₁·H₁)/W₁ = (M₂·D₂·H₂)/W₂.

Pipes & Cisterns Inflow/Drain

Net Rate = Σ(Inlets) - Σ(Outlets)

If leak empties full tank in L hrs while pipe fills in F hrs: Net time = (F·L) / (L - F) hrs.

Speed, Time & Distance Motion

1 km/h = 5/18 m/s | 1 m/s = 18/5 km/h
Avg Speed = (2·u·v) / (u + v)

Harmonic mean applies strictly when distance is equal. If time is equal, Avg Speed = (u + v)/2.

Trains & Relative Velocity Linear

Same Dir: |u - v| | Opp Dir: (u + v)
Time = (L₁ + L₂) / Relative Speed

Crossing pole/man: Distance = L_train. Crossing platform/bridge: Distance = L_train + L_platform.

Boats & Streams Currents

Down = u + v | Up = u - v
Boat (u) = (D + U)/2 | Stream = (D - U)/2

Current always accelerates downstream and decelerates upstream equally.

3. Numbers & Algebra

Foundation

HCF, LCM & Divisibility Number Law

HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b
HCF(Fractions) = HCF(Num) / LCM(Den)

Divisibility by 7, 11, 13: Group digits in 3s from right, alternating sum difference divisible.

Unit Digit & Cyclicity Power

Cyclicity 4: (2, 3, 7, 8) | Cyclicity 2: (4, 9)
Power mod 4 gives position (0 ➔ 4)

Any integer ending in 0, 1, 5, 6 maintains identical unit digit across all natural exponents.

Algebraic Identities Algebra

If x + 1/x = k ➔ x² + 1/x² = k² - 2
x³ + 1/x³ = k³ - 3k

a³ + b³ + c³ - 3abc = (a+b+c)(a²+b²+c² - ab - bc - ca). If a+b+c = 0, a³+b³+c³ = 3abc.

Progressions: AP & GP Sequences

AP: Tₙ = a + (n-1)d | Sₙ = (n/2)[2a + (n-1)d]
GP: Tₙ = a·rⁿ⁻¹ | S_∞ = a / (1 - r)

Arithmetic Mean = (a+b)/2. Geometric Mean = √(ab). AM ≥ GM strictly holds for positive reals.

Alligation Cross: (Q₁ / Q₂) = (Cheaper diff) / (Dearer diff) to find mixture ratios in 10s.

4. Mensuration & Geometry

Formula-Heavy

2D Plane Figures Perimeter & Area

Equilateral △ = (√3 / 4)a² | Circle = πr²
Trapezium = 1/2(a + b)h | Rhombus = 1/2·d₁d₂

Heron's Formula: √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)] where s = (a+b+c)/2. Scalene triangle area.

3D Solids: Surface Area & Volume Spatial

Cylinder: V = πr²h | TSA = 2πr(r + h)
Cone: V = 1/3·πr²h | CSA = πrℓ
Sphere: V = 4/3·πr³ | Surface = 4πr²

Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr², Volume = (2/3)πr³. Slant height ℓ = √(r² + h²).

Geometric Theorems & Angles Geometry

Sum Int Angles = (n - 2) × 180°
Angle at center = 2 × Angle at circumference

Pythagorean Triplets: (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (7, 24, 25), (8, 15, 17), (9, 40, 41), (20, 21, 29).

Coordinate Geometry Cartesian

d = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
Midpoint = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2) | Slope = Δy/Δx

Two lines are perpendicular iff m₁ × m₂ = -1; parallel iff slopes are equal (m₁ = m₂).

Dimensional Scaling: If linear dimension scales by k, Area scales by k², Volume scales by k³.
