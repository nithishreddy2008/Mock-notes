STAGE 1 · INDUSTRIAL CRISIS
1. Green Revolution Limits
  • NPK Chemical Imbalance: Distorted application ratios deplete micronutrients (Zn, Fe, B) and acidify soils.
  • Pesticide Treadmill: Broad-spectrum insecticides destroy beneficial predators, triggering secondary pest resurgence.
  • Aquifer Over-draft: Deep tube wells collapse groundwater tables across Punjab and Haryana.
  • Soil Salinization: Canal waterlogging and capillary evaporation deposit mineral salts.
CUET Invariant: High-yield varieties (HYVs) require responsive chemical fertilizers and intensive irrigation.
STAGE 2 · AGROECOLOGICAL INPUTS
2. Biofertilizers & Biocontrol
  • Nitrogen Fixers: Symbiotic Rhizobium (legumes), free-living Azotobacter, and Anabaena-Azolla (paddy).
  • Phosphorus Solubilizers: Vesicular-Arbuscular Mycorrhizae (VAM) mobilize bound soil phosphorus.
  • Botanical Biopesticides: Neem seed extract (Azadirachtin) acts as antifeedant and insect growth disruptor.
  • Microbial Biocontrol: Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt Cry toxin) targets lepidopteran larvae.
Certification: NPOP (third-party export certification) and PGS-India (participatory domestic guarantee).
STAGE 3 · IPM DECISION MATRIX
3. Integrated Pest Management
  • General Equilibrium Position (GEP): Mean population density of pest over long periods.
  • Economic Injury Level (EIL): Lowest pest population that will cause economic damage exceeding control costs.
  • Economic Threshold (ET): Pest density at which control action MUST be initiated to prevent reaching EIL (ET < EIL).
  • Tiered Hierarchy: Cultural → Physical/Mechanical → Biological → Targeted bio-rational chemical spray.
Golden Rule: ET is ALWAYS numerically lower than EIL (Action Threshold < Damage Threshold).
STAGE 4 · CIRCULAR BIOECONOMY
4. Valuation & Circularity
  • Total Economic Value (TEV): Direct use, Indirect functional ecosystem services, Option, and Non-use Existence values.
  • Valuation Methods: Contingent Valuation (WTP/WTA), Travel Cost Method, Hedonic property pricing.
  • Pigouvian Instruments: Carbon taxation internalizing social costs; Cap-and-trade emissions trading.
  • Industrial Symbiosis: Kalundborg model where one industry's waste becomes another's raw material.
Costanza Baseline: Global biosphere services valued at $33 trillion/yr, vastly exceeding global GDP.
