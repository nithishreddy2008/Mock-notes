Magnetic Effects of Current: Biot-Savart, Ampere's Law & Lorentz Force

1. Biot-Savart Law & Circular Coil
Current Elements
dB = (μ₀ / 4π) · (I dl × r̂) / r²
B_axial = [μ₀ N I R²] / [2 (R² + x²)^(3/2)]
B_center = (μ₀ N I) / (2R)
  • Current Element (I dl): Vector source of magnetic field; direction along instantaneous current.
  • Inverse Square Attenuation: Field scales as 1/r²; strictly perpendicular to both dl and position vector r.
  • Axial Behavior: Near center (x ≪ R), B varies quadratically; far along axis (x ≫ R), B mimics magnetic dipole: B = (μ₀ / 4π)·(2M / x³).
2. Ampere's Law & Solenoid
Circuital Theorems
∮ B · dl = μ₀ · I_enc
Straight Wire: B = (μ₀ I) / (2π r)
Solenoid: B_core = μ₀ n I | B_end = ½ μ₀ n I
  • Ampere's Circuital Law: Valid for steady currents; requires high geometric symmetry for analytic solution.
  • Long Straight Wire: Concentric circular field lines; right-hand thumb rule sets orientation.
  • Core Uniformity: Inside a long solenoid, field lines are intensely dense, parallel, and uniform; exterior leakage field is virtually zero.
3. Lorentz Force & Motion
Particle Dynamics
F = q (E + v × B)
Circular Radius: r = (m v_⊥) / (q B)
Period: T = (2π m) / (q B) | Pitch: p = v_∥ · T
  • Magnetic Work is Zero: Since F_m ⊥ v, instantaneous power P = F · v = 0; magnetic field alters direction only, never kinetic energy.
  • Helical Trajectory: When v is at angle θ to B, component v_∥ = v cos θ yields translation, v_⊥ = v sin θ yields gyration.
  • Cyclotron Frequency: f = (q B) / (2π m) is independent of particle speed and orbit radius.
