1. Restriction Enzymes & Gel Tools

Molecular Scissors (Endonucleases)

First restriction enzyme: Hind II (cleaves at specific 6 bp recognition site). Cuts sugar-phosphate backbone at palindromic sequences.

Hind II = First Enzyme

EcoRI Palindrome & Sticky Ends

5' - G ↓ A A T T C - 3'
3' - C T T A A ↑ G - 5'

Asymmetric cleavage between G and A leaves overhanging, single-stranded Sticky Ends that hydrogen-bond with complementary inserts.

Cohesive / Sticky Ends

Agarose Gel Electrophoresis

• DNA is negatively charged and migrates toward the positive anode.
• Sieving effect: Smaller fragments migrate faster & farther.
• Stained with Ethidium Bromide (EtBr); emits bright orange under UV.
• Elution: Extracting the excised DNA band from agarose gel.

EtBr + UV = Orange Bands
2. Cloning Vector pBR322 Vector

pBR322 Plasmid Anatomy

Engineered E. coli plasmid containing essential regulatory and resistance loci:

• ori (Origin of Replication): Initiates replication; dictates vector copy number.
• rop: Codes for proteins involved in plasmid replication control.

ori Controls Copy Number

Antibiotic Resistance Markers

1. ampr (Ampicillin Resistance): Contains unique recognition sites for Pst I and Pvu I.
2. tetr (Tetracycline Resistance): Contains unique recognition sites for BamHI and Sal I.
• Other single sites: EcoRI, Cla I, and Hind III.

ampr (PstI, PvuI) · tetr (BamHI, SalI)

Insertional Inactivation (tetr gene)

Ligation of foreign gene into the BamHI site disrupts the tetr gene.
• Recombinant: Resistant to ampicillin, but sensitive to tetracycline.
• Selected via duplicate replica plating.

Insertional Inactivation
3. Selection & Transformation Methods

Blue-White Screening (lacZ Selection)

• Foreign gene ligated into the coding sequence of β-galactosidase (lacZ).
• Non-Recombinants: Intact enzyme converts X-gal into blue colonies.
• Recombinants: Inactivated enzyme produces white colonies (single-step selection without duplicate plates).

White Colonies = Recombinants

Competent Host Uptake

• Chemical (Bacteria): Divalent cation (Ca2+) treatment creates pores, followed by heat shock (42°C) and rapid ice chilling.
• Micro-injection: Direct DNA injection into animal cell nucleus.
• Biolistics / Gene Gun: Microscopic gold/tungsten particles coated with DNA fired into plant cells.

Heat Shock & Gene Gun

Vectors for Plants & Animals

• Plants: Disarmed Ti plasmid of Agrobacterium tumefaciens.
• Animals: Disarmed retroviruses deliver therapeutic genes.

Agrobacterium (Ti Plasmid)

DNA Isolation Enzymes

Cell walls digested enzymatically: Lysozyme (bacteria), Cellulase (plants), Chitinase (fungi). Pure DNA precipitates upon adding Chilled Ethanol as fine spoolable threads.

DNA Ligase Function

Catalyzes the formation of covalent phosphodiester bonds between adjacent 3'-OH and 5'-phosphate ends of cohesive or blunt termini, sealing recombinant DNA molecules.

EcoRI Nomenclature Breakdown

E = Genus Escherichia; co = Species coli; R = Strain RY13; I = First endonuclease isolated in Roman numeral chronological order.
