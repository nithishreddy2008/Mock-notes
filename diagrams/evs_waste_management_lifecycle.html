PROCESS ARCHITECTURE · FIG 2
Stage 01 · Physical Treatment
Preliminary & Primary STP
  • Bar Screens: Intercepts coarse rags, plastics, floating debris.
  • Grit Chambers: Removes dense inorganic sand, gravel, eggshells.
  • Primary Clarifiers: Gravitational sedimentation settles 50-70% SS and 30-40% BOD as primary sludge.
Screening Sedimentation
Stage 02 · Biological Treatment
Secondary Biological STP
  • Aeration Tank: Cultivates aerobic microbial flocs (bacteria + fungi) digesting dissolved organic matter.
  • Activated Sludge: Sludge return preserves continuous biomass kinetics.
  • Secondary Clarifier: Sinks biological flocs; drops total BOD by >90%.
Aerobic Flocs BOD > 90% Removal
Stage 03 · Advanced Finishing
Tertiary Polishing & Disinfection
  • Nutrient Removal: Nitrification-denitrification for N; chemical precipitation for P.
  • Filtration: Dual-media sand and activated carbon adsorption of toxic micropollutants.
  • Disinfection: Chlorination, Ozonation, or UV irradiation to eradicate fecal pathogens.
UV Irradation Zero Fecal Coliform
Special Waste · Legal Protocol
Biomedical & Hazardous Waste
  • Yellow: Anatomical, soiled, expired drugs (Incineration).
  • Red: Contaminated recyclable plastics (Autoclaving).
  • White (Translucent): Needles, scalpels (Autoclave + Shred).
  • Blue: Glassware, metallic implants (Disinfection).
