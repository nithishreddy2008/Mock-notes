Radio Studio Acoustic Architecture & Broadcast Audio Chain

Audio flow: Live acoustic performance studio, audio console mixing, processing & multi-tier transmission

CUET Mass Media · Acoustic Lifecycle
1
Acoustic Live Studio
1. Studio Acoustic Design
Sound-lock double doors, floating floors, acoustic baffling panels, bass traps, controlled Reverberation Time (RT60).
2. Microphone Transducers
Studio Condenser (large diaphragm) & Dynamic Broadcast mics (e.g. Shure SM7B); Pop filters & shockmounts.
3. Polar Pattern Placement
Cardioid for solo presenter (rejects room echo); Figure-8 (Bi-directional) for intimate face-to-face interviews.
➔
2
Audio Control Console
Mixing Desk Operations
Rotary gain pots, channel faders (potentiometers), high-pass EQ filters, +48V Phantom Power, PFL (Pre-Fade Listen).
Digital Audio Workstation
DAW multi-tracking (Pro Tools, Adobe Audition); non-destructive speech editing, cross-fades, noise reduction.
Sound FX & Jingle Playout
Cart machine automation (RCS Master Control / Dalet) firing station IDs, voice actualities, and musical sweepers.
➔
3
Audio Signal Processing
1. Multi-Band Compression
Tames dynamic peaks, raises quiet vocal whispers, delivers signature punchy "radio loudness" curve.
2. Peak Limiting & De-Essing
Strict brickwall limiting preventing FM over-modulation (>75 kHz deviation); de-essers suppress harsh sibilance.
3. Stereo Multiplex & RDS
Generates 19 kHz pilot tone for FM stereo; encodes RDS (Radio Data System) scrolling text for receiver displays.
➔
4
Transmission & Playout
1. Terrestrial FM / AM Tower
RF power amplifier excites mast radiator; FM dipole antennas propagate line-of-sight VHF signals across city.
2. DRM Digital Radio
Digital Radio Mondiale encoders replacing legacy Medium Wave transmitters with CD-quality digital broadcasts.
3. Web & Podcasting Feeds
Icecast/Shoutcast live audio streaming; RSS XML feeds distributed to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and Newsonair app.
Core Invariant: Acoustic isolation prevents bleed; Pre-Fade Listen (PFL) auditions audio privately; Multiband limiters prevent FM carrier over-modulation.
